Sensitivity and convergence tests of the assumption ledger¶

Companion of OPEN_ITEMS.md. Part 1 holds the tests that were RUN on the login node (scripts in internal/ledger_tests/, raw output in $TNGXMM_DEV/LEDGER2/); part 2 is the precise specification of the tests that need the node. The ledger entries that cite a result below name this file as their report:.

Common conventions of part 2:

  • Metric of fit-level tests: the median over views of ln_br_1 (ln of blue over red mass inside 1.0 R500c(z_obs), tngxmm.analysis.tables.mock_table), after the QC exclusion of fit_qc.qc_flags at the production fraction, as paired differences per view against the production arm. Also reported: the excluded count and the median ln_br_so.

  • Inputs: the 43 views of CHECKS D1 ($TNGXMM_DEV/CHECKS/fits/d1, sample in CHECKS/plan/sample_d1.csv), with the production configuration v4s1 as the reference arm.

  • Pre-registered tolerance, with its reason: a variation is immaterial when the median paired change of ln_br_1 is below 0.015. The reason is measured: three re-seeds (v4s1, v4s2, v4s3) of the same 43 views give medians -0.072, -0.064 and -0.089 (sd 0.013) with the 2.5 % flags, so a smaller shift cannot be told from the optimiser and chain noise on 43 views. A larger shift is reported as a systematic with its size, and the paper states it.

  • Cost unit: one 43-view arm is 43 independent quick fits, measured on the D1 queue as 3112 s mean and 13701 s maximum per fit (CHECKS/plan/queue_fits_d1_d3_d2_d4_progress.jsonl), that is about 37 core-hours, about 4 hours of wall time on one node with 43 cores. One node per job.

Status and configuration¶

Every test of part 1 and every run of part 3 was measured on the V4 configuration (the 43 D1 views with the reference arm v4s1, or the V4 tables), before the V4.1 metal-axis and pattern fixes, the release 1.1 instrument switches (donor footprint, three-component background, total Galactic column, anomalous MOS CCDs, ellbeta AGN PSF) and the release 1.1.1 conditions donor. A test whose subject has not changed since stays valid; the others have to be repeated on the current configuration. The last column says whether the code under test has changed since the measurement; it is not a re-measurement.

Result: between 1 % and 5 % the excluded set changes by 7 of 172 fits (27 % to 31 %) and the median of ln_br_1 by +0.016, which equals the run-to-run noise of the median (0.018 to 0.027) and the tolerance of part 2 (0.015). Of the 29 fits that change status between 1 % and 5 %, 21 are two halos at the c200 upper bound (c200 of 9.2 to 9.7 in the range 1 to 10: snap 72 halos 14025806 and 6775821, in every arm); the others sit at the low-Z bound (Z below 0.1 at 5 %) or at c200 near 1. The threshold is therefore not tuned on the outcome in any way that matters for the median, but it does decide whether about one fit in thirty is excluded. Not yet answered: that about 30 % of all fits are flagged at any fraction is a property of these 43 views and of the c200 and Z priors, not of the threshold.

R2. T_vir and constants.mu_tvir (sim.tvir_mu)¶

Script internal/ledger_tests/ecf_tvir.py. t_vir_keV(M500, R500) is used only as the T of ecf_lookup(T, z, nH), the count-rate to flux factor of the campaign Lx (measure_lx, seed_lx; V4 spec: tvir_frac = 0, so no gas cut reads it). The ECF in 0.5-2 keV changes for T x1.1 by less than 0.1 % at z = 0.05, 0.3 to 0.4 % at z = 0.2, 0.7 to 1.3 % at z = 0.5 and 1.3 to 2.9 % at z = 0.9 (T = 2 to 8 keV, nH = 0.03e22). A mean molecular weight 2 % different (0.588 against 0.60) moves T by 2 %, hence the ECF by at most 0.6 % at z = 0.9. The result feeds the fit only through the Lx seed (first-guess mass and c200, see R6 and S6).

R3. HSE crossing rule (method.hse_crossing_rule)¶

Scripts hse_multiple_crossings.py, hse_inner_exclusion.py.

  • Production results (9 snapshots, 7389 views): hse_crossing is single for 7371, multiple for 9 (0.12 %), fit-failed for 9, none none. The 9 are 3 halo-snapshots (3 views each).

  • For these 9 the outermost crossing would give a mass larger than the innermost by ln 0.73 to 1.20 (median 0.94): the rule matters for them, and only for them. They are flagged in the column, not cut.

  • Inner exclusion 0.2, 0.3, 0.4 (production), 0.5, 0.6 R500c on the 2463 truth files: the headline mass is identical for 0.2 to 0.5 (no profile changes), and 0.6 loses the crossing of 3 more profiles. The 0.4 R500c exclusion is therefore inert.

  • Note for the author: truth_estimands (black line, m500_at_z) takes the OUTERMOST crossing while the red HSE headline takes the INNERMOST. For single-crossing profiles (99.9 %) they coincide in definition.

R4. Merged canvas (instrument.merged_npix)¶

Script internal/ledger_tests/canvas_margin.py. 150 production views drawn at random (seed 1) out of 7389: the exposed region of image_merged_cov.fits stays at least 63 pixels (4.2 arcmin) away from the edge of the 648 x 648 canvas (median 70), no view touches it. The canvas does not clip any counts, so no number depends on 648. (A census of all 7389 views is a 10-minute login-node job if a referee asks.)

R5. Combined size of the three deviations from Barnes et al. (2021)¶

Script internal/ledger_tests/hse_barnes_deviations.py. The results carry barnes21 (finite-difference slopes, mu = 0.59, no gas cut) and the headline barnes21_nosf_fit_mu (fitted slopes, no star-forming cells, each cell’s mu). Over 7380 views, ln(headline / barnes21) at R500c has median +0.024, 16/84 percentiles -0.085/+0.141, 1/99 percentiles -0.38/+0.41, and a median per snapshot between +0.013 and +0.056. This is the COMBINED effect of the slope method, the star-forming cut and the own mu. S8 separates them: the star-forming cut and the own mu together move the median by -0.0045, so nearly all of the +0.024 is the slope method (finite differences in barnes21 against the Vikhlinin fits of Barnes et al. 2021, Sect. 2.4, which the headline follows); the slope method is not a deviation from Barnes et al.

R6. Pattern fractions of the EPIC-pn CCF (instrument.pattern_loss)¶

Script internal/ledger_tests/ccf_pn_pattern_fractions.py. EPN_QUANTUMEF_0018.CCF, extension FRACTION_ENERGY, Full Frame, first region: at 1 keV FRAC_S 0.712, FRAC_D 0.273, FRAC_T = FRAC_Q = 0, FRAC_SD = FRAC_SDTQ = 0.984; at 0.3, 0.5, 2, 5, 8 keV FRAC_SD is 0.995, 0.993, 0.974, 0.954, 0.946. The table has no triple and quadruple events, so FRAC_SD / FRAC_SDTQ = 1 at every energy and apply_pattern_loss returns 1.0000 in 0.5-2 keV for the three cameras. The spec value pattern_loss_factor = 0.92 is read only as a switch (!= 1 selects pattern_mode = "ccf", simulate_instrument.py), never as a multiplier. The 0.985-0.988 quoted in the apply_pattern_loss docstring is FRAC_SD itself, not the ratio the code applies.

R7. Matched-sampler kernel widths (S4, method.matched_kernel_widths)¶

Script internal/ledger_tests/matched_kernel_widths.py; inputs campaigns/v4_max/targets_v4_max.csv (9615 views) and the packaged observation table; the production main draw (K’ = 5, R = 12, dz_max 0.25, TNG-Cluster + TNG300, seed 20261008) is reproduced exactly (3111 draws, 2053 unique views, z D 0.036, mass D 0.026). Each arm is drawn with 20 seeds (the seed orders the observations that are equally hard to match, so the spread over seeds is the production draw’s own). The pick of a halo-snapshot minimises (dlogM / sigma_logm)^2 + (dz / sigma_z)^2, which depends on the two widths only through their ratio: halving sigma_z is the same draw as doubling sigma_logm (the runs agree to the last digit), so two arms cover the four variations.

R11. Annulus of the Lx seed (S6, photometry.legacy_apertures)¶

Script internal/ledger_tests/lx_seed_annulus.py. photometry.bkg_annulus_arcmin (11-14 arcmin) is read only by the fixed-aperture photometry of the legacy angbkg seed, which no v4 fit uses. The v4 seed uses the aperture-radius annulus (1.1, 1.6); the photometry takes it from _settings.default_settings(), which is the v32 preset’s in every spec. Re-measuring the 1 Mpc seed Lx of the 43 D1 views with the annuli (1.2, 1.8), (1.4, 2.0), (1.6, 2.4) and (1.0, 1.3) moves the first-guess R500 (R500 ~ L^0.191 with the seed calibration slope 1.742) by at most 1.5, 4.4, 5.6 and 6.0 % (medians below 1 %). The S6 arms perturb R500 by -20 % and +25 %: 3.7 times the largest change an annulus produces.

Part 2. Tests that need the node (specifications)¶

S1. Photon-generator grid and area (sim.phot_nbins, sim.area_cm2)¶

  • Parameter: sim.phot_nbins 5000; sim.area_cm2 4000.

  • Variation: bins 1250, 2500, 5000 (production), 10000, 20000. Area: 4000 and 8000 at fixed bins.

  • Inputs: the generation stage of 12 of the 43 D1 views (two per snapshot class: z about 0.1, 0.3, 0.6), same seeds, same donors; compare the products, not the fits.

  • Metric: (a) the band counts of the five PPS bands in image_merged.fits against the 20000-bin arm, per view ratio; (b) the APEC emissivity integral over 0.5-2 keV of the binned table against a direct integration; (c) for the area, the assertion area_cm2 >= max_E sum_cameras ARF(E) for all 3 cameras on-axis and the number of photons rejected by it. Then refit the 12 views of the 1250-bin arm only if (a) exceeds the tolerance.

  • Tolerance: counts per band within 0.5 % (Poisson 1 sigma at the faintest accepted view is about 1 %; a systematic below half of that is invisible in a fit) and the area test exact. The area cannot change a number if the inequality holds, because the draw is a thinning.

  • Cost: generation only (12 views x 5 bin arms, the per-view generation time of the production campaign; not measured here). The assertion in (c) and the emissivity integral in (b) are login-node jobs and are done first.

S2. Fit crop radius and block-centre convention (fit.crop)¶

  • Parameter: massfit.crop_r500 3.0; crop_cap_arcmin None. (The block-centre convention is a geometric identity, see the ledger entry, and is not varied.)

  • Variation: crop_r500 2.0, 2.5, 3 (production), 4; cap 13.5 arcmin (the G3 value).

  • Inputs: the 43 D1 views, fit arms crop2, crop2p5, crop4, cap13p5 with everything else as v4s1.

  • Metric: the paired change of the median ln_br_1, the excluded count (bg_fallback rises when the crop is smaller than 2 R500: report it separately) and the median fit time.

  • Tolerance: 0.015 (common). crop2 is expected to be flagged by bg_fallback by construction (background annulus beyond 2 R500, the rule of method.background_fallback_fov), so its comparison is restricted to the views without the flag.

  • Cost: 4 arms x 43 fits, 4 node-jobs of the standard size; the 4 R500 arm is the slowest (more pixels).

S3. Point-source thresholds (fit.point_source_thresholds)¶

  • Parameter: massfit.source_det_ml_min 10; protect_core_arcsec 90.

  • Variation: DET_ML 6 (the 4XMM catalogue cut, Webb et al. 2020), 10 (production), 14 (their 5 sigma “clean” threshold); core protection 45, 90, 135 arcsec and 0.

  • Inputs: the 43 D1 views; the SAS source lists already exist in the products, so each arm only changes the mask (except DET_ML 6: the lists stop at 10, the arm needs a new detection, see R10; run by the SENS job). The matching real-data comparison uses the 5XMM-DR15 fallback target set of massfit.build_realfit_targets (the same code path).

  • Metric: median ln_br_1, n_sources_masked, and for the core protection the number of views that lose a central source (the BCG) at 45 or 0.

  • Tolerance: 0.015. The same rule is applied to mocks and real data, so a bias would cancel in the real-to-mock comparison: the test reports that cancellation by repeating a real-data subset with DET_ML 6 and 14.

  • Cost: 5 arms x 43 fits (the 90 arcsec arm is production); the core-protection arms can share the DET_ML 10 fits for the views where no source lies within 135 arcsec (check from the source list first, login node).

S4. Matched-sampler kernel widths (method.matched_kernel_widths)¶

  • Parameter: params:donors.matched.sigma_z 0.05, sigma_logm_dex 0.05.

  • Variation: x0.5 and x2 of each separately (0.025/0.1 in z, 0.025/0.1 dex), 4 variations plus production.

  • Inputs: the full-snapshot maximal list (v4_max, 9615 views) and the observation table that the production sample_matched run used (K’ = 5, R = 12).

  • Metric: gate P of validate_population (the strict population-match statistics), the effective number of distinct halos, the reuse histogram, and the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance of the drawn (z, log M) against the reference sample.

  • Tolerance: no variation changes the gate P verdict, and the KS distance changes by less than the production value’s own bootstrap spread over 20 seeds. The widths decide who is drawn, not how a drawn halo is analysed, so no fit is needed.

  • Cost: pure numpy on tables; each draw takes 30 s. RUN 2026-10-18 on the login node: R7.

S5. Maximal-list donor assignment (method.maximal_list_donors)¶

  • Parameter: params:donors.max.n_nearest 10, cap_factor 2.0.

  • Variation: n_nearest 5, 10, 20; cap_factor 1.5, 2, 3 (3 is what the box runs use).

  • Inputs: as S4 (build_max_targets.assign_nearest_donors on the same unit list).

  • Metric: the donor-use histogram (maximum use), the median donor distance donor_dist and donor_dz, the number of distinct donors, and the share of views whose donor is farther than the 10th nearest.

  • Tolerance: the median donor_dist changes by less than 10 % and no donor exceeds the cap; the quantity that matters for the claim is the donor’s instrument and background, and the population gates of S4 already check its distribution.

  • Cost: numpy, minutes; login node. RUN 2026-10-18: R8.

S6. Seed constants of the Lx photometry (photometry.legacy_apertures: bkg_annulus_aperture_frac, bkg_annulus_arcmin)¶

  • Where it acts: the Lx seed gives the first-guess mass and c200 (fit.seed_lx), and the first-guess R500 sets the crop (3 R500) and the fit pixel (R500 / 40 when coarser than 8 arcsec). No other result reads these constants.

  • Variation: instead of changing the constants one by one, perturb their only channel: scale the seed R500 by x0.8 and x1.25 (the largest change of the seed Lx that either annulus can produce is smaller than that; check on the 43 views from the table, login node) on the D1 views.

  • Metric and tolerance: common (0.015); also report the change of crop and pixel.

  • Cost: 2 arms x 43 fits.

S7. Baryon fraction of the matching mass (donors.matching_mass)¶

  • Parameter: (1-b) of the PSZ2 scale, 0.78 (Planck 2015 XXIV).

  • Variation: 0.84 (low end of the later calibrations) and 0.90.

  • Inputs: the production observation table and sample_matched.

  • Metric: the matched population (S4’s metrics) and, for the 43 D1 views re-drawn, the shift of the median halo mass M500c; a refit is not needed, the claim depends on the population mass range only.

  • Tolerance: the shift of the median matched mass is stated; no tolerance is pre-registered because the choice is a prior on the observations, not a numerical parameter.

  • Cost: login node, minutes. Run in release 1.1.3: part 1, R12.

S8. Isolating the deviations from Barnes et al. (2021) (truth.star_forming_gas, truth.own_mu)¶

  • Variation: on the truth step alone, the headline barnes21_nosf_fit_mu against (i) the same fit with the star-forming cells kept (barnes21_fit_mu), (ii) the same fit with mu = 0.59 (barnes21_nosf_fit) and (iii) both, Barnes’s estimator as published with the same fits (barnes21_fit). All three are computed on request only (compute_hse_truth(..., mu059_fit=True, s8_variants=True), compute_hse_mass.S8_ESTIMATORS); none is a column.

S9. Background seed and F5 (fit.background_seed)¶

Not a numerical parameter: PLAN_V41 decision 5 replaces the typical-EPIC seed by each observation’s own QPB.
