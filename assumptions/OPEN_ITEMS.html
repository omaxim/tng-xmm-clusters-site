B. Justified, but the evidence is incomplete¶

These entries have a citation or a validation; the item says what a referee can still attack.

  • donors.matching_mass: Planck 2015 XXIV gives (1-b) = 0.78 as a sample-level prior for a count likelihood; we use it per cluster. 0.78 is the low end of later calibrations (0.84-0.95, Herbonnet et al. 2020 and others), and the built-in offset between the PSZ2 (0.78) and the ACT DR6 (A10/WL = 0.65, already calibrated) scales is about 1.20. The sensitivity test S7 ((1-b)_PSZ2 = 0.84 and 0.90) was run on the login node in release 1.1.3 (SENSITIVITY_TESTS.md, R12): the replica list fails gate P in mass at every value (D = 0.241, 0.229, 0.207 against 0.061-0.067), the coverage rises from 0.929 to 0.933 and 0.948, the median halo mass of the views moves by -0.008 and -0.018 dex, and almost every individual pairing changes (8.5 and 5.6 per cent of the production views are drawn again). What depends on it: which halo is paired with which real observation (the replica draw and gate P), and through that every mock-to-real comparison at fixed observation (count matching, realism, the comparison of mock and real fits). The bias estimands of a mock (M_X, M_HSE and M500c of the same halo) do not depend on it.

  • truth.star_forming_gas: excluding star-forming gas is our deviation from Barnes et al. (2021), who state no gas cut for the 3-D profiles. The three deviations together (fitted slopes, no star-forming gas, own mu) move ln M_HSE(R500c) by +0.024 in the median against the literature-faithful barnes21 column (16/84 percentiles -0.085/+0.141, 7380 views; SENSITIVITY_TESTS.md R5). The individual sizes were measured on 2026-11-04 on 24 TNG-Cluster halo-snapshots (SENSITIVITY_TESTS.md S8): keeping the star-forming cells moves the median of ln M_HSE(< R500c) / M500c by -0.0003, mu = 0.59 by -0.0041, both by -0.0045; the +0.024 is therefore the slope method of the barnes21 column.

  • truth.own_mu: the second deviation from Barnes et al.; with mu = 0.59 the median of ln M_HSE(< R500c) / M500c moves by -0.0041 (S8, 2026-11-04; the mu = 0.59 twin is not a column, compute_hse_truth(mu059_fit=True) computes it on request).

  • fit.background_seed: closed on 2026-11-03: the presets fit the ESAS background (corner-fixed QPB, free sky and soft protons, no prior); the typical-EPIC level calibrated on mocks is removed. Only the diagnostic mixture model keeps fallback_bg_prior_dex = 0.3, without a separate test.

  • fit.priors_low_signal: closed on 2026-11-03: flat priors at every signal level (Sanders et al. 2018); the 20 000-count threshold and the informative priors are diagnostics only. Since 2026-11-12 the concentration has the published relation of fit.cm_prior as its prior, again at every signal level; the metallicity prior stays flat. Open: what the low-signal fits lose, measured by closure B1.

  • fit.metallicity: the flat 0-2 Z_sun range has no separate test. Its lower edge 0 is the physical bound of an abundance and also a hard bound of the code: mbproj2d’s ApecRateCalc.get returns NaN for Z < 0 and the fit turns that into a log-likelihood of -inf. The X-COP abundances were fitted with the same kind of bound (APEC in XSPEC, Ghizzardi et al. 2021, p. 2) and it did not bias them (p. 6), but the authors warn that it can bias small, poorly measured abundances, the regime of the low-count fits here, and recommend allowing negative values, as Mernier et al. (2017, p. 3) do; following that advice would need a change to mbproj2d. A published hydrostatic MBProj2 fit uses a flat 0-1 Z_sun prior (Sanders et al. 2022, p. 18). The code tabulates the emissivity at 0 and 1 Z_sun and scales it linearly (Sanders et al. 2018, p. 21), so the upper edge 2 extrapolates that scaling and is a choice of the prior: about twice the edge of Sanders et al. (1 Z_sun of Asplund et al. 2009 is about 1.07 in lodd units), with no published value, a design parameter (section P). The mean and the average core abundances stay well below it (the mean ICM abundance within r180 is at most 0.26 Z_sun of Anders and Grevesse 1989, about 0.41 in lodd units, Molendi et al. 2016, p. 1-2; the average core about 0.8 proto-solar, Mernier et al. 2017, Table 2); single cores are not covered by these averages.

  • fit.molecular_weight: the fully ionised count departs from mbproj2d’s built-in constant and from the 1.83 particles per electron of Sanders et al. (2018, Appendix B); effect +0.03 to +0.05 dex. Since 2026-11-03 (section Q) the departure has its sources: a fully ionised plasma holds about 1.92 particles per electron (Yeung et al. 2024, p. 15), and published hydrostatic masses of real (Martino et al. 2014, p. 6) and mock clusters (Nagai et al. 2007, p. 4) use mu ~ 0.6, consistent with full ionisation; a mock XMM-Newton analysis takes the molecular weight from its abundance table (Seppi et al. 2026, p. 13). The review of X-ray masses (Ettori et al. 2013) quotes mu ~ 0.6 for the hydrostatic mass but backs neither count cleanly: its own pressure, P_gas = n_gas kT (p. 8), takes n_gas = n_e + n_p ~ 1.826 n_e (p. 9), the count of mbproj2d. Still open: the correction changes the published code, so the paper has to state it.

  • fit.outer_pressure: freezing P_out at the floor changed one real cluster (MACS J1347) by 20 %, so the choice matters; the free outer pressure has the precedent of Sanders et al. (2018). Since 2026-11-03 (section Q) also of a hydrostatic MBProj2 fit with a flat prior on log P_out (Sanders et al. 2022, p. 17). The X-COP code fits the boundary pressure free within a Gaussian prior on the measured SZ pressure (Eckert et al. 2022, p. 5) and starts its mock profiles at 5 R500 to keep it out of the fitted range (p. 6); the earlier backward method fixed it at the pressure measured at the outermost data point, and freeing it over a grid of values from the best-fit density and temperature moved the mass by about 3 per cent (Ettori et al. 2010, p. 6 and 9, Table 4 on p. 19). No paper read (Sanders et al. 2018, 2022; Scheck et al. 2023; Ettori et al. 2010, 2019; Eckert et al. 2022) freezes it at a floor. The run-away level -30 has no source and is a design parameter (section P).

  • fit.mock_agn: resolved in release 1.1 (instrument.agn_lognlogs = 'moretti03-soft'): production draws the field AGN from the soft-band (0.5-2 keV) counts of Moretti et al. (2003), p. 11 (alpha1 = 1.82, alpha2 = 0.60, S0 = 1.48e-14, N = 6150 per deg^2, BOXED). The hard-band set of the upstream xmm_simulator is used only under the stage option moretti03-hard, which no v1 preset selects.

  • method.estimand_fixed_radius: the estimand of claim C was fixed after seeing the v2 results (disclosed in DESIGN v3).

  • method.multistart_optimiser: the grid values (M500 x 0.5, 1, 2; c200 = 3, 7) have no source; the optimiser is not adopted by any preset until the J1 diagnostic.

  • method.background_fallback_fov: “beyond 2 R500” now has its precedent in the ledger (Ghirardini et al. 2019, sky level from R > 2 R500, BOXED, p. 4; also Eckert et al. 2022, p. 3); the 50-exposed-pixel minimum is the fitter’s constant, a design parameter since 2026-11-03 (section P). A mock XMM-Newton analysis replaces a background region that falls off the field by a corner region chosen by hand (Seppi et al. 2026, p. 5): support for the case, not for the number.

  • instrument.response: the presets now generate and fit every image with the SAS 22.1 responses of its own exposure (a mock: its conditions donor’s; instrument.responses, massfit.responses) and the SAS selection of the calibration files (ccf_selection). Findings for the author’s decision¶

  1. apply_pattern_loss’s docstring reports “0.985-0.988 at 1 keV” as the efficiency; that is FRAC_SD. The ratio the code applies is 1 because the CCF table carries no triple or quadruple events. Whether the image selection should lose the 1.5 % of events not in S+D at 1 keV (about 5 % at 8 keV) is a question for the SAS pattern convention, not settled here. The spec field is also better a boolean than 0.92 (changing it changes the spec hash, so it was not done).

  2. The red headline takes the innermost crossing, the black SO line (m500_at_z) the outermost; they differ only for the 0.12 % multiple-crossing views.

  3. About 30 % of the 514 D1/J1 fits are flagged excluded at every prior-edge fraction tried (R1); this is a property of the c200 and Z priors on these 43 views, not of the threshold.

  4. The tolerance of the part-2 tests (0.015 in ln M ratio) is derived from three re-seeds of the same views (sd 0.013). The author may prefer a different bar; the tests report the measured change either way.

F. Decisions taken on the recommended options (2026-10-17)¶

  1. Mock AGN band (superseded in release 1.1, see section B, fit.mock_agn). The decision of 2026-10-17 kept the upstream hard-band constants, consistent end to end with the hard-band template. Release 1.1 moved production to the soft-band counts (instrument.agn_counts_band). The mass effect of the AGN measured before that change is E = +0.007 [-0.007, +0.010]: removing the AGN from 43 mock views changes ln M_X by +0.028 (off minus on), and E is the quarter of that change attributed to the excess of mock AGN detections (x1.33).

  2. Pattern loss. Keep the behaviour (the applied ratio is 1.0000 in 0.5-2 keV). Fix the docstring. The boolean is not replaced, because that would change the spec hash for no effect.

  3. Crossing rule. The paper’s estimands (analysis.estimands) take red AND black at their outermost 500 ρc(z_obs) crossing with the same function (m500_at_z), so they are consistent by construction. The production headline column (innermost crossing) is not used by the estimands; the 0.12 % multiple-crossing views stay flagged.

  4. Tolerance of the part-2 sensitivity tests. 0.015 in the median ln(blue/red) at 1.0 R500c, the measured scatter of the median between three re-seeds of the same views (sd 0.013). It is a measured noise floor, not a chosen number.
