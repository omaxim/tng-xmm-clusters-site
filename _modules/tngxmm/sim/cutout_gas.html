Source code for tngxmm.sim.cutout_gas

"""Gas of the stored spherical truth cutout: ``sim.loader = 'cutout'``.

The spherical truth stage (:mod:`tngxmm.sim.truth_sphere`) has written, for every target
halo-snapshot, the snapshot indices of all particles within ``r_cut = max(3 R200c,
1.02 max(3.15 R500c, 2 R200c))`` of GroupPos (``<tree>/truth_cutouts/<sim>/snap_<snap:03d>.h5``).
With ``loader = 'cutout'`` the mock emits from exactly the gas cells (PartType0) of that
set: the particle set of the hydrostatic truth, not the halo's FoF members ('fof') and not
the whole original zoom ('zoom'), and without reading a chunk of the snapshot that holds
none of them (a TNG-Cluster sphere is two contiguous ranges of the zoom; a box sphere is
read chunk by chunk, :meth:`~tngxmm.sim.truth_sphere.SnapshotStore.read`).

:class:`CutoutGasSource` wraps a HaloSource (:mod:`tngxmm.sim.read_halo_source`) and serves
``gas(halo_id, fields, selection='cutout')``; every other call is the wrapped source's.  The
campaign source (:func:`tngxmm.sim.generate_view.campaign_source`) puts the periodic wrap
and the gas-load slot around it, so the cutout cells carry the same minimum-image
coordinates, native dtypes and units as the other loaders (positions in comoving ckpc/h,
converted by :func:`~tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas.load_halo_gas`).  The field set is the one the
photon generation needs (``GFM_Metals`` in all nine columns, which the truth reads as two).

Refusals (``ValueError`` / ``FileNotFoundError``; never a silent fallback): no cutout file for
the snapshot, no sphere for the halo, a sphere that is incomplete or truncated, a cutout
of another snapshot or centre than the source's, a corrupted index array (sha256); a
snapshot that lacks a requested field raises ``KeyError`` (so that optional fields can be dropped).

Low-resolution gas.  A TNG-Cluster zoom simulates the gas only inside its high-resolution
region, whose cells have a nearly uniform mass (median 1.19e7 Msun in every sphere; the
largest cell of a sphere without low-resolution dark matter is 3.9-6.4 x the median, 30
spheres checked).  The spheres that reach low-resolution dark matter (PartType2, 3.9e9 Msun,
24 per cent of the target spheres, ``CUTOUT/contamination_v1.csv``) also hold a few coarser
gas cells: 15287 cells in 108 of the 593 contaminated spheres are heavier than 8 x the median
(up to 512 x), at 1.9-5 R500c, with at most 0.3 per cent of the gas mass of a sphere; none in
the 30 clean spheres (``CUTOUT/lowres_gas_v1.csv``).  The loader identifies, for a TNG-Cluster
sphere (``sphere_source == 'zoom'``) the cells heavier than ``HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR`` x the
sphere's median gas cell mass and reports their number.  The threshold is empirical (it
separates the high-resolution fluctuation from the coarse tail; the distribution of the tail
is continuous) and the identification of these cells with the low-resolution gas of the zoom
is inferred from the data, not taken from a reference.  The snapshot's integer
``AllowRefinement`` is NOT the marker (cells with 0 occur in clean spheres and inside the
nearest low-resolution dark matter, and mostly not on the heavy cells) and is not used.  The
report (``CutoutGasSource.cutout_report``, ``obs_meta.json`` ``gas_cutout``) carries the
counts and the nearest low-resolution dark matter so a view can be flagged afterwards.

Box cutouts (``shape='box'``, ``sim.mock_cutout_shape = 'box'``).  The cube files of
:mod:`tngxmm.sim.mock_cutout` (``<tree>/mock_cutouts/<sim>/snap_<snap:03d>.h5``) replace the
spheres: the source serves ALL the gas of the cube of half-side ``box_r500`` R500c about GroupPos,
with no further spherical clip (:func:`~tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas.load_halo_gas` is given no
clip radius; the emitting cylinder of :func:`~tngxmm.sim.generate_view.photon_switches` is the one
inscribed in the cube, ``sim.mock_gas_region = 'cylinder'``, or the one that encloses it, 'cube').  The coarse cells (> 8 x the median of the cutout, the low-resolution gas of a TNG-Cluster
zoom) are KEPT, in a cube and in a sphere: they emit like any cell and are only unresolved, and at
the corners (5-7 R500c) their emission is far below the background, so dropping them would leave
empty corners (author's decision; the first version of the sphere loader dropped them).  They are counted and their mass share reported
(``n_coarse``, ``coarse_mass_frac``); :func:`~tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas.load_halo_gas` adds their share
of the 0.5-2 keV luminosity (``coarse_lum_frac``; every cell projects into the square, so it is
the share of the projected emission of each of the three views).  A missing file, a halo without a group, a
cube that is incomplete, truncated, of another half-side, snapshot or centre than requested, a
corrupted index array or a file that is not a cube file is refused.  The report carries
``half_side_kpc`` and ``half_side_r500c`` (``obs_meta.json`` ``gas_cutout`` records the shape and
half-side) and the cut-time diagnostics of the file (nearest low-resolution dark matter, coverage).

References
----------
tngcluster : original zooms, resolution levels (offsets ``OriginalZooms``).
illustristng : snapshot gas fields.
h5py, numpy : cutout files and row reads.

def check_sphere(sphere: Any, path: Path, halo_id: int) -> None:
    """Refuse an incomplete or truncated sphere (a stored sphere of a periodic box was cut
    by a complete pass over the chunk files and carries no ``sphere_truncated``)."""
    a = sphere.attrs
    if not bool(a.get("sphere_complete", False)):
        raise ValueError(f"{path}: the sphere of halo {halo_id} is not complete "
                         "(sphere_complete is false); loader 'cutout' needs every particle "
                         "of the sphere")
    truncated = a.get("sphere_truncated", a.get("sphere_source") != "box-stream")
    if bool(truncated):
        raise ValueError(f"{path}: the sphere of halo {halo_id} is truncated "
                         f"(at {a.get('sphere_truncated_at_kpc', 'unknown')} kpc, short of "
                         f"r_cut = {sphere.r_cut_kpc:.0f} kpc); loader 'cutout' needs the whole sphere")

def check_box(cube: Any, path: Path, halo_id: int, box_r500: float) -> None:
    """Refuse a cube that is not the requested one, or is incomplete or truncated."""
    a = cube.attrs
    if cube.shape != "box":
        raise ValueError(f"{path}: the cutout of halo {halo_id} is a {cube.shape}, not a cube")
    r = cube.r_cut_kpc / cube.r500_kpc
    if abs(r - float(box_r500)) > 1e-6 * float(box_r500):
        raise ValueError(f"{path}: the cube of halo {halo_id} has half-side {r:.6g} R500c, not "
                         f"sim.mock_box_r500 = {float(box_r500):g}")
    if not bool(a.get("box_complete", False)):
        raise ValueError(f"{path}: the cube of halo {halo_id} is not complete (box_complete is "
                         "false); loader 'cutout' needs every gas cell of the cube")
    if bool(a.get("box_truncated", True)):
        raise ValueError(f"{path}: the cube of halo {halo_id} is truncated (at "
                         f"{a.get('box_truncated_at_kpc', 'unknown')} kpc, short of its corners at "
                         f"{cube.rmax_kpc:.0f} kpc); loader 'cutout' needs the whole cube")

class CutoutGasSource:
    """A HaloSource whose ``gas(..., selection='cutout')`` is the stored sphere's gas.

    ``tree``: the campaign tree holding ``truth_cutouts``; ``sim``: the simulation label of
    its directory (``spec.sim.sim_name``); ``store``: the snapshot store of the
    sphere indices (default: opened from the wrapped source, a
    :class:`~tngxmm.sim.read_halo_source.SnapshotHaloSource` or a fixture
    :class:`~tngxmm.sim.read_halo_source.CutoutHaloSource`).  ``reports[halo_id]``: the
    report of the last gas read of that halo (cells, drops, contamination, radii)."""

    kind = "cutout-gas"

    def __init__(self, source: Any, tree: str | Path, sim: str, store: Any = None, *,
                 shape: str = "sphere", box_r500: float | None = None) -> None:
        """Wrap ``source``.  ``tree``: the campaign tree holding the cutout files; ``sim``: the simulation label of
        their directory; ``store``: the snapshot store (default: opened from the wrapped source); ``shape``:
        ``'sphere'`` (the spherical truth cutouts) or ``'box'`` (the mock cubes of half-side ``box_r500`` R500c,
        required for ``'box'``)."""
        if shape not in ("sphere", "box"):
            raise ValueError(f"shape must be 'sphere' or 'box', not {shape!r}")
        if shape == "box" and not (box_r500 is not None and float(box_r500) > 0):
            raise ValueError("shape 'box' needs the half-side box_r500 [R500c]")
        self._src = source
        self.shape, self.box_r500 = shape, (None if box_r500 is None else float(box_r500))
        self.tree, self.sim = Path(tree), str(sim)
        self._store = store
        self._spheres: dict[int, Any] = {}
        self.reports: dict[int, dict] = {}

    def __getattr__(self, name: str) -> Any:
        if name.startswith("__") or name in ("_src",):
            raise AttributeError(name)
        return getattr(self._src, name)

    @property
    def wrapped_source(self) -> Any:
        """The wrapped source."""
        return self._src

    def store(self) -> Any:
        """The snapshot store the sphere indices refer to."""
        if self._store is None:
            from tngxmm.sim.read_halo_source import CutoutHaloSource, SnapshotHaloSource
            from tngxmm.sim.truth_sphere import CutoutStore, SnapshotStore

            src = self._src
            while hasattr(src, "wrapped_source"):
                src = src.wrapped_source
            if isinstance(src, SnapshotHaloSource):
                self._store = SnapshotStore(src.base_path, src.snap)
            elif isinstance(src, CutoutHaloSource):
                self._store = CutoutStore(src.path)
            else:
                raise ValueError(f"loader 'cutout' reads a simulation snapshot or a fixture "
                                 f"cutout, not a {type(src).__name__}")
        return self._store

    def sphere(self, halo_id: int) -> Any:
        """The halo's stored sphere (gas indices only), checked against the source."""
        import numpy as np

        from tngxmm.sim.truth_sphere import read_cutouts

        hid = int(self._src.halo_id if halo_id is None else halo_id)
        if hid in self._spheres:
            return self._spheres[hid]
        snap = int(self._src.snap)
        box = self.shape == "box"
        if box:
            from tngxmm.sim.mock_cutout import mock_cutouts_path

            path = mock_cutouts_path(self.tree, self.sim, snap)
        else:
            path = cutout_file(self.tree, self.sim, snap)
        if not path.is_file():
            raise FileNotFoundError(
                f"loader 'cutout': no {'box' if box else ''} cutout file {path} ("
                + ("`campaign mock-cutout` writes it; sim.mock_cutout_root names the tree)" if box
                   else "the spherical truth stage, `campaign truth-sphere`, writes it; "
                        "sim.cutout_root names the tree)"))
        import h5py

        with h5py.File(path, "r") as f:
            if int(f.attrs.get("snap", -1)) != snap:
                raise ValueError(f"{path} is snapshot {f.attrs.get('snap')}, not {snap}")
            if f"halo_{hid}" not in f:
                raise ValueError(f"{path}: no {'cube' if box else 'sphere'} for halo {hid}")
        sph = read_cutouts(path, [hid], ptypes=(0,))[hid]
        if box:
            check_box(sph, path, hid, float(self.box_r500))
        else:
            if sph.shape != "sphere":
                raise ValueError(f"{path}: the cutout of halo {hid} is a {sph.shape}, not a sphere")
            check_sphere(sph, path, hid)
        centre = np.asarray(self._src.group(hid)["GroupPos"], np.float64)
        if not np.allclose(sph.centre, centre, rtol=0.0, atol=1e-3):
            raise ValueError(f"{path}: the centre of the sphere of halo {hid} "
                             f"({sph.centre}) is not the catalogue GroupPos ({centre})")
        if len(sph.gidx[0]) == 0:
            raise ValueError(f"{path}: the {'cube' if box else 'sphere'} of halo {hid} holds no "
                             "gas cell")
        self._spheres[hid] = sph
        return sph

    def particles(self, ptype: int, fields: Sequence[str], selection: str = "fof",

    def _cutout_gas(self, halo_id: int | None, fields: list[str], radius_ckpc_h: float | None
                    ) -> dict[str, Any]:
        import numpy as np

        if radius_ckpc_h is not None:
            raise ValueError("selection 'cutout' takes no radius: the sphere is the cutout's")
        hid = int(self._src.halo_id if halo_id is None else halo_id)
        sph = self.sphere(hid)
        st = self.store()
        present = st.fields_present(0)
        lack = [f for f in fields if f not in present]
        if lack:                                   # as the snapshot reader: KeyError (mini snapshots)
            raise KeyError(f"fields {lack} absent from the snapshot")
        zoom = sph.attrs.get("box_source" if self.shape == "box" else "sphere_source") == ZOOM_SOURCE
        read = list(dict.fromkeys([*fields, "Masses"]))
        got = st.read(0, sph.gidx[0], read, metals=None)
        n_sphere = len(sph.gidx[0])
        if len(got.get("Masses", ())) != n_sphere:
            raise RuntimeError(f"halo {hid}: {n_sphere} cells selected, "
                               f"{len(got.get('Masses', ()))} read")
        m = np.asarray(got["Masses"], np.float64)
        med = float(np.median(m))
        # the coarse cells of a zoom are KEPT (counted and reported, module docstring)
        keep = np.ones(n_sphere, bool)
        coarse = (m > HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR * med) if zoom else np.zeros(n_sphere, bool)
        gc = st.groupcat_header()
        if self.shape == "box":
            self.reports[hid] = self._box_report(sph, hid, zoom, n_sphere, keep, med, m, gc, coarse)
            return self._assemble(got, fields, keep)
        self.reports[hid] = dict(
            r_cut_kpc=float(sph.r_cut_kpc), r_cut_ckpc_h=float(sph.r_cut_ckpc_h),
            r500c_kpc=float(sph.r500_kpc), n_sphere=int(n_sphere),
            n_lowres_gas_dropped=0, coarse_gas_kept=True, n_coarse=int(coarse.sum()),
            coarse_mass_frac=float(m[coarse].sum() / m.sum()),
            lowres_gas_rule=(f"mass > {HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR:g} x median" if zoom else None),
            median_cell_mass_code=med, max_over_median=float(m.max() / med),
            lowres_dm_rmin_kpc=float(sph.attrs.get("sphere_lowres_rmin_kpc", float("nan"))),
            lowres_dm_n_rcut=int(sph.attrs.get("sphere_lowres_n_rcut", 0)),
            sphere_source=str(sph.attrs.get("sphere_source", "")),
            h=float(gc["HubbleParam"]), a=float(gc["Time"]))
        return self._assemble(got, fields, keep)

    @staticmethod
    def _assemble(got: dict, fields: list[str], keep: Any) -> dict[str, Any]:
        out: dict[str, Any] = {f: (got[f] if keep.all() else got[f][keep]) for f in fields}
        out["count"] = int(keep.sum())
        return out

    def _box_report(self, sph: Any, hid: int, zoom: bool, n_cube: int, keep: Any, med: float,
                    m: Any, gc: dict, coarse: Any) -> dict:
        """The report of a cube read: geometry, the drops, and the cut-time diagnostics."""
        a = sph.attrs
        rep = dict(
            shape="box", half_side_kpc=float(sph.r_cut_kpc), half_side_ckpc_h=float(sph.r_cut_ckpc_h),
            half_side_r500c=float(sph.r_cut_kpc / sph.r500_kpc), r500c_kpc=float(sph.r500_kpc),
            n_cube=int(n_cube), n_lowres_gas_dropped=0,
            coarse_gas_kept=True, n_coarse=int(coarse.sum()),
            coarse_mass_frac=float(m[coarse].sum() / m.sum()),
            lowres_gas_rule=(f"mass > {HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR:g} x median" if zoom else None),
            median_cell_mass_code=med, max_over_median=float(m.max() / med),
            lowres_dm_rmin_kpc=float(a.get("box_lowres_rmin_kpc", float("nan"))),
            lowres_dm_rmin_r500c=float(a.get("box_lowres_rmin_kpc", float("nan")) / sph.r500_kpc),
            lowres_dm_n_box=int(a.get("box_lowres_n_box", 0)),
            box_source=str(a.get("box_source", "")), snap_is_mini=bool(a.get("snap_is_mini", False)),
            h=float(gc["HubbleParam"]), a=float(gc["Time"]))
        for k in ("box_cover_v0", "box_cover_v1", "box_cover_v2", "box_voxel_fill",
                  "box_coarse_mass_frac", "box_allowref0_n"):
            if k in a:
                rep[k] = float(a[k])
        return rep
