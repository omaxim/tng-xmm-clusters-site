Source code for tngxmm.sim.outskirts_convergence

"""Projected-emission convergence test of the uniform gas sphere (``sim.gas_radius_r500``).

Question.  For every projected aperture of the analysis and of the realism features, what
fraction of the 0.5-2 keV emission of a TNG-Cluster halo comes from gas beyond the 3D radius
``X`` R500c, and how does the emission beyond ``X`` R500c compare with the XMM background in
the same aperture?  The answer decides whether a sphere of 4 R500c about GroupPos (the gas
and the emitting cylinder of ``loader = 'cutout'`` with ``gas_radius_r500 = los_depth_r500 =
4``) loses emission that matters.

Decision rule (fixed before any production result; see the note on its history).  A radius
``X`` is converged if, at the 90th percentile over the halos and the three views,

* (A) in the signal annuli (``SIGNAL_ANNULI``: projected R/R500c < 1, the count-matching and
  fit core, where the cluster emission is the measured quantity), the emission from beyond
  ``X`` R500c is < 1 per cent of the total emission of the annulus, AND
* (B) in every annulus (all eight), the surface brightness of the emission beyond ``X`` R500c
  is < 1 per cent of the XMM total background in 0.5-2 keV (the median of the real donors).

The stricter background (10th percentile of the donors) is also reported, and so is the
literal rule of the request (A in every one of the eight annuli, ``converged_literal``).

History.  The rule first written applied (A) in every annulus; it cannot be met by any
``X <= 6``: in an annulus that starts at or beyond ``X`` all the emission is beyond ``X`` by
geometry, and in an annulus ending at ``X`` a large share lies beyond ``X`` along the line of
sight (login test, one halo: 45 per cent in 2.5-4 R500c for ``X`` = 4).  In the outer annuli
the cluster emission is also far below the background (0.1-0.7 per cent of it for that halo),
so the fraction of the total says nothing there and the comparison with the background (B)
decides; (A) is kept where the cluster is the signal.  This was changed after the login test
of one halo, before the production run.

Cube cut (``shape = 'cube'``, the box cutouts of the mock gas, ``sim.mock_cutout_shape = 'box'``).  The mock
gas of the box path is ALL the gas of the axis-aligned cube of half-side ``H`` R500c about GroupPos (``H`` = 4 in
production), viewed along the simulation axes, so what it loses is the gas OUTSIDE the cube: beyond ``H`` along the line
of sight and beyond ``H`` in the sky coordinates.  In this shape the "radius" of a cell is its Chebyshev radius
``max(|x|, |y|, |z|)`` / R500c, binned with the same list ``X_R500`` (now read as ``H``); the annuli, the depth, the
views, the ECF, the background reference and the decision rule are exactly those of the sphere.  The histograms of the
sphere run are binned by the Euclidean radius and cannot be re-binned into the Chebyshev radius, so the zooms are streamed
again (4 minutes on a node for the 20 halos).  Baseline: the cylinder of the sphere test, ``|offset along the line of
sight| <= DEPTH_R500`` (6) R500c and projected radius < 10 R500c, which encloses the cube of ``H`` = 6 (its corners are at
projected 8.49, depth 6, 3D radius 10.39 R500c; the 3D radius of the farthest corner of the baseline is 11.7).  Emission
beyond ``H`` along the line of sight past the baseline depth is not in the total, so the fractions of the cube cut are
lower bounds for ``H`` < 6.  Where the zoom does not fill the baseline there is no gas to count: per halo
``r_cell_max_r500`` (outermost kept cell), ``covers_h6_corner`` (it reaches the corner distance ``CORNER_H6_R500``), and
``cube6_voxel_fill`` (fraction of the 24^3 voxels of the cube of ``H`` = 6 with at least one kept cell centre) say whether
the high-resolution region covers the baseline (cells excluded as star-forming or below the temperature floor leave voxels
empty, so the fill is a conservative measure).  Rule of the cube run (stated before it is run; identical to the sphere
rule with ``X`` -> ``H``): at the 90th percentile over the halos and the three views, (A) the emission outside the cube of
half-side ``H`` is < 1 per cent of the emission of the annulus in 0-0.15 and 0.15-1 R500c, AND (B) its surface brightness
is < 1 per cent of the median XMM total background in 0.5-2 keV in all eight annuli.  Annuli that start beyond ``H`` hold
only emission from outside the cube by geometry, so (B) there is a statement about the cluster emission beyond ``H`` in
the sky plane against the background; ``criterion_b_annuli_within_h`` (annuli ending at or inside ``H``) is reported as
information only and does not enter the verdict.

Geometry.  The annuli in projected R/R500c are ``ANNULI_R500`` (0-0.15, 0.15-1, 1-1.5, 1.5-2.5,
2.5-4, 4-6, 6-8, 8-10); the projection is along the three view axes of the campaign
(``generate_photons.LOS_AXIS_IDX``) with the depth ``|offset| <= DEPTH_R500`` (6) R500c about
GroupPos.  The total of an annulus is the emission of that cylinder, the beyond-X part is the
cells with 3D radius ``r > X`` R500c.  The ``X`` list is ``X_R500``.

Gas.  Every gas cell of the original zoom of the TNG-Cluster halo (``loadOriginalZoom``: the
FoF group range and the outer-fuzz range of the offsets file, :func:`zoom_gas_ranges`), read
in blocks of ``BLOCK_ROWS`` rows through the truth stage's row reader, so the memory does not
grow with the zoom (the whole gas of a zoom is up to 1.9e8 cells, ~77 GB).  The cells come
through :func:`tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas.load_halo_gas` itself, so the per-cell n_e n_H V
Lambda(T, Z) (rest-frame 0.5-2 keV, the TNG reference APEC table of ``sim.apec_version``)
is the one the photons use, the star-forming and heated cells are excluded and the
temperature floor is ``sim.t_min_k``.  The coarse (low-resolution) cells, heavier than
``cutout_gas.HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR`` x the median gas cell mass of the zoom, are not part of the
emission here (the cutout loader of the mocks KEEPS them) and are accumulated separately so the share of the
emission they would carry is reported.  The zoom has no gas beyond its high-resolution
region, so a halo that reaches low-resolution dark matter inside the cylinder has too little
emission there: ``r_cell_max_r500`` (the outermost kept cell) is reported to judge it.

Counts.  The count rate per cell is ``ECF(T, z_obs, N_H) L_rest / (4 pi d_L(z_obs)^2)``,
with the EPIC per-camera energy conversion factor of the pipeline
(:func:`tngxmm.instrument.compute_ecf.ecf_lookup`, ``ecf_table_v4``: counts in the 0.5-2 keV
channels per rest-frame unabsorbed 0.5-2 keV energy flux, on-axis ARF x filter x QE x RMF x
TBabs(N_H), APEC at Z = 0.3 Z_sun), averaged over MOS1, MOS2 and pn, i.e. the rate of a camera
of the mean effective area.  This is the quantity of the realism feature ``log_bkg_rate``
(``validation.realism.image_features``): summed counts over summed exposure of the three
cameras in 0.5-2 keV, per arcmin^2, with the vignetted exposure map (the rate per unit
on-axis exposure), measured in the background region (beyond 1.5 R500 or the outer 15 per cent
of the field) with the compact sources clipped.  Each view takes the redshift and N_H of the
campaign row of that view (the donor's), the gas the snapshot's physical state; the
temperature and the redshift are clamped to the ECF grid (T 1-15 keV, z 0-1).
The ratios of the emission beyond X to the total of an annulus, and the ratio of two rates of
the same camera mix, do not depend on the absorption or the K-correction; the absolute
surface brightness, and so the comparison with the background, does (N_H enters through the
ECF and the redshift through the distance and the ECF).

References
----------
tngcluster : original zooms, resolution levels (offsets ``OriginalZooms``).
illustristng : snapshot gas fields.
atomdb, xmm_ccf_sas : the energy conversion factor (ARF, filter, RMF).
"""

#: annuli in projected R/R500c: the aperture of the central count rate, the fit core
#: (0.15-1), the outskirts and the background region of large clusters, and the field of
#: small clusters (a 15 arcmin field edge is 5-10 R500c for theta500 of 1.5-3 arcmin)
ANNULI_R500 = ((0.0, 0.15), (0.15, 1.0), (1.0, 1.5), (1.5, 2.5),
               (2.5, 4.0), (4.0, 6.0), (6.0, 8.0), (8.0, 10.0))
#: 3D radii [R500c] beyond which the emission is counted
X_R500 = (2.0, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5, 5.0, 6.0)
#: geometry of the cut: 'sphere' (Euclidean radius, the zoom/cutout sphere) or 'cube' (Chebyshev radius, the box cutout)
SHAPES = ("sphere", "cube")
#: 3D distance [R500c] of the corners of the cube of half-side 6 R500c: the baseline must reach it
CORNER_H6_R500 = 6.0 * math.sqrt(3.0)
#: voxels per side of the occupancy grid of the cube of half-side 6 R500c (0.5 R500c each)
N_VOX = 24
#: annuli where the cluster emission is the measured quantity (criterion A of the decision rule)
SIGNAL_ANNULI = ((0.0, 0.15), (0.15, 1.0))
#: half-depth of the projected cylinder [R500c]
DEPTH_R500 = 6.0
VIEWS = (0, 1, 2)
#: rows of a gas block read and processed at once
BLOCK_ROWS = 2_000_000
#: the percentile of the decision rule and the tolerated fraction
PERCENTILE = 90.0
TOLERANCE = 0.01
#: nominal Galactic column [1e22 cm^-2] when a view row carries none
NH_DEFAULT = 0.03
#: ECF table of the per-camera ECF (``build_table_v4``); filter of the energy conversion
ECF_FILTER = "Medium"
ECF_CAMERAS = ("MOS1", "MOS2", "PN")
#: the realism features of the real donors (``log_bkg_rate`` column)
BKG_COLUMN = "log_bkg_rate"
#: kpc per Mpc, erg per keV, cm per kpc
KPC_CM = 3.0856775814913673e21

def zoom_gas_ranges(offsets_path: str | Path, zoom_id: int) -> list[tuple[int, int]]:
    """Global ``(start, length)`` rows of the gas (PartType0) of the original zoom
    ``zoom_id``: its FoF range and its outer-fuzz range (what ``loadOriginalZoom`` reads)."""
    import h5py
    import numpy as np

    with h5py.File(offsets_path, "r") as f:
        z = f["OriginalZooms"]
        i = int(np.flatnonzero(z["HaloIDs"][()] == int(zoom_id))[0])
        g0, gl = z["GroupsSnapOffsetByType"][i, 0], z["GroupsTotalLengthByType"][i, 0]
        f0, fl = z["OuterFuzzSnapOffsetByType"][i, 0], z["OuterFuzzTotalLengthByType"][i, 0]
    return [(int(g0), int(gl)), (int(f0), int(fl))]

def zoom_gas_cells(offsets_path: str | Path, zoom_id: int) -> int:
    """Number of gas cells of the original zoom (read from the offsets file only)."""
    return sum(n for _, n in zoom_gas_ranges(offsets_path, zoom_id))

def row_blocks(ranges: Sequence[tuple[int, int]], block_rows: int = BLOCK_ROWS
               ) -> Iterator[tuple[int, int]]:
    """``(first, last+1)`` global rows of consecutive blocks of at most ``block_rows`` rows
    that tile ``ranges`` in order."""
    for start, length in ranges:
        for a in range(int(start), int(start) + int(length), int(block_rows)):

def region_radius(rel_r500: Any, shape: str = "sphere") -> Any:
    """Radius [R500c] of cells at ``rel_r500`` (N, 3) [R500c]: Euclidean for 'sphere',
    Chebyshev ``max(|x|, |y|, |z|)`` for 'cube'."""
    import numpy as np

    if shape == "sphere":
        return np.sqrt((rel_r500 * rel_r500).sum(axis=1))
    if shape == "cube":
        return np.abs(rel_r500).max(axis=1)
    raise ValueError(f"shape must be one of {SHAPES}, not {shape!r}")

def mark_voxels(occupied: Any, rel_r500: Any, half: float = 6.0) -> None:
    """Set the voxels of ``occupied`` (``N_VOX``^3 bool) that hold a cell centre of ``rel_r500``
    (N, 3) [R500c]; the grid tiles the cube of half-side ``half``."""
    import numpy as np

    n = occupied.shape[0]
    idx = np.floor((np.asarray(rel_r500) + half) / (2.0 * half) * n).astype(np.int64)
    ok = ((idx >= 0) & (idx < n)).all(axis=1)
    occupied[idx[ok, 0], idx[ok, 1], idx[ok, 2]] = True

def accumulate(hist: dict[str, Any], rel_kpc: Any, lum: Any, rate_per_view: Sequence[Any],
               r500_kpc: float, prefix: str = "", shape: str = "sphere") -> None:
    """Add cells at ``rel_kpc`` (N, 3) [kpc, relative to GroupPos] to ``hist``.

    ``lum`` (N,) rest-frame 0.5-2 keV luminosity [erg/s]; ``rate_per_view[v]`` (N,) count
    rate [ct/s] of the cell for view ``v``.  A cell belongs to an annulus of the view when
    its projected radius lies in it (lower edge inclusive) and its offset along the line of
    sight is within ``DEPTH_R500``; its radius bin is ``r3d_edges()`` of :func:`region_radius`
    (``shape``).  ``prefix`` ('heavy_') selects the accumulators of the dropped cells."""
    import numpy as np

    from tngxmm.sim.generate_photons import LOS_AXIS_IDX

    rel = np.asarray(rel_kpc, np.float64) / float(r500_kpc)
    r3 = region_radius(rel, shape)
    b3 = np.searchsorted(np.asarray(r3d_edges()), r3, side="right") - 1      # 0 .. len(X)
    ann_edges = np.array([a for a, _ in ANNULI_R500] + [ANNULI_R500[-1][1]])
    nb = len(X_R500) + 1
    for v in VIEWS:
        los = LOS_AXIS_IDX[v]
        sky = [i for i in range(3) if i != los]
        rp = np.sqrt(rel[:, sky[0]] ** 2 + rel[:, sky[1]] ** 2)
        ia = np.searchsorted(ann_edges, rp, side="right") - 1
        ok = (ia >= 0) & (ia < len(ANNULI_R500)) & (np.abs(rel[:, los]) <= DEPTH_R500)
        idx = ia[ok] * nb + b3[ok]
        size = len(ANNULI_R500) * nb
        for name, w in (("lum", lum), ("rate", rate_per_view[v])):
            hist[prefix + name][v] += np.bincount(
                idx, weights=np.asarray(w, np.float64)[ok], minlength=size).reshape(
                    len(ANNULI_R500), nb)
        hist[prefix + "n"][v] += np.bincount(idx, minlength=size).reshape(len(ANNULI_R500), nb)

def ecf_curve(z_obs: float, nh_1e22: float, table: str | Path | None = None,
              filter: str = ECF_FILTER) -> tuple[Any, Any]:
    """``(T_keV grid, ECF)``: the mean over MOS1, MOS2 and pn of the per-camera ECF
    [ct cm^2 / erg] at ``z_obs`` and ``nh_1e22`` on the table's temperature grid (z and N_H
    clamped to the table, :func:`tngxmm.instrument.compute_ecf.ecf_lookup`)."""
    import numpy as np

    from tngxmm.instrument.compute_ecf import _interpolator, resolve_table_path, TABLE_V4_NAME

    interps, tg, zg, ng = _interpolator(str(table or resolve_table_path(TABLE_V4_NAME)))
    pts = np.array([[t, np.clip(z_obs, zg[0], zg[-1]), np.clip(nh_1e22, ng[0], ng[-1])]
                    for t in tg])
    ecf = np.mean([interps[(c, filter)](pts) for c in ECF_CAMERAS], axis=0)
    return np.asarray(tg, np.float64), ecf

def cell_rate(lum_erg_s: Any, T_K: Any, curve: tuple[Any, Any], z_obs: float,
              d_l_cm: float) -> Any:
    """Count rate [ct/s] of cells of rest luminosity ``lum_erg_s`` and temperature ``T_K``:
    ``ECF(T) L / (4 pi d_L^2)`` with ``ECF`` from :func:`ecf_curve` (log-linear in T,
    clamped to its grid)."""
    import numpy as np

    kev = np.asarray(T_K, np.float64) * 8.617333262e-8        # k_B [keV/K]
    tg, ecf = curve
    e = np.interp(np.log(np.clip(kev, tg[0], tg[-1])), np.log(tg), ecf)
    return e * np.asarray(lum_erg_s, np.float64) / (4.0 * math.pi * d_l_cm ** 2)

def halo_emission(base_path: str | Path, snap: int, halo_id: int, views: dict[int, dict],
                  *, opts: Any, ecf_table: str | Path | None = None,
                  block_rows: int = BLOCK_ROWS, store: Any = None, source: Any = None,
                  zoom_ranges: Sequence[tuple[int, int]] | None = None,
                  shape: str = "sphere", verbose: bool = False) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """The emission histograms of one halo's original zoom (module docstring).

    ``views[v]``: ``{'z_obs': ..., 'nh_1e22': ...}`` for each view of :data:`VIEWS`.
    ``opts``: a :class:`~tngxmm.sim.generate_view.SimOptions` (temperature floor, APEC table
    version and abundance table, band, cosmology).  ``store`` / ``source`` /
    ``zoom_ranges`` (tests): the snapshot store, the HaloSource and the gas ranges;
    default opened from ``base_path``.  ``shape``: 'sphere' or 'cube' (module docstring).  Returns the accumulators (``hist``), the catalogue
    values and the counts, JSON-ready except ``hist`` (numpy arrays)."""
    import resource

    import numpy as np

    from tngxmm.core.cosmology import flat_lcdm
    from tngxmm.sim.cutout_gas import HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR
    from tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas import load_halo_gas, t_vir_from_catalog
    from tngxmm.sim.read_halo_source import cosmo_units, minimum_image, read_halo_source
    from tngxmm.sim.truth_sphere import SnapshotStore

    if shape not in SHAPES:
        raise ValueError(f"shape must be one of {SHAPES}, not {shape!r}")
    t0 = time.time()
    source = source or read_halo_source(base_path, int(snap), int(halo_id))
    store = store or SnapshotStore(base_path, int(snap))
    h, a, z_snap, UL, UM, UV = cosmo_units(source)
    grp = source.group(int(halo_id))
    box = float(source.groupcat_header()["BoxSize"])
    centre_code = np.asarray(grp["GroupPos"], np.float64)
    centre_kpc = centre_code * (a / h)
    r500_kpc = float(grp["Group_R_Crit500"]) * (a / h)
    m500_msun = float(grp["Group_M_Crit500"]) * 1e10 / h
    if zoom_ranges is None:
        zoom_ranges = zoom_gas_ranges(store.offsets_path(), int(grp["GroupOrigHaloID"]))
    n_cells = sum(n for _, n in zoom_ranges)
    t_vir = t_vir_from_catalog(m500_msun, r500_kpc)

    # median gas cell mass of the zoom (the coarse cells are those heavier than a factor of it)
    masses = np.concatenate([store.read(0, np.arange(a0, b0, dtype=np.int64), ["Masses"])["Masses"]
                             for a0, b0 in row_blocks(zoom_ranges, block_rows)])
    m_med = float(np.median(masses))
    n_heavy_cells = int((masses > HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR * m_med).sum())
    del masses

    cosmo = flat_lcdm(opts.h0, opts.om0)
    curves, d_l = {}, {}
    for v in VIEWS:
        z_o = float(views[v]["z_obs"])
        curves[v] = ecf_curve(z_o, float(views[v].get("nh_1e22", NH_DEFAULT)), ecf_table)
        d_l[v] = float(cosmo.luminosity_distance(z_o).to("cm").value)

    hist = new_histograms()
    block_src = _BlockGas()
    fields = ["Coordinates", "Masses", "Density", "InternalEnergy", "ElectronAbundance",
              "StarFormationRate", "GFM_CoolingRate", "GFM_Metals", "GFM_Metallicity"]
    r_max = 0.0
    occupied = np.zeros((N_VOX,) * 3, dtype=bool)
    n_read = 0
    for a0, b0 in row_blocks(zoom_ranges, block_rows):
        got = store.read(0, np.arange(a0, b0, dtype=np.int64), fields, metals=None)
        got["Coordinates"] = minimum_image(got["Coordinates"], centre_code, box)
        heavy = np.asarray(got["Masses"]) > HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR * m_med
        n_read += len(heavy)
        for name, sel in (("", ~heavy), ("heavy_", heavy)):
            if not sel.any():
                continue
            block_src.block = {k: (v[sel] if sel.size == len(v) else v) for k, v in got.items()}
            try:
                g = load_halo_gas(block_src, int(halo_id), None, h, a, UM, UL, UV,
                                  opts.emin_lx_kev, opts.emax_lx_kev, verbose=False,
                                  tvir_frac=opts.tvir_frac, t_vir_K=t_vir, selection="zoom",
                                  t_min_k=opts.t_min_k, abund_table=opts.abund_table,
                                  apec_version=opts.apec_version,
                                  z_solar_ag89=opts.table_metal_unit)
            except ValueError:              # a block without hot gas
                continue
            rel = g.p_kpc - centre_kpc
            rates = [cell_rate(g.lum_erg_s, g.T_K, curves[v], float(views[v]["z_obs"]), d_l[v])
                     for v in VIEWS]
            accumulate(hist, rel, g.lum_erg_s, rates, r500_kpc, prefix=name, shape=shape)
            if name == "":
                r_max = max(r_max, float(np.sqrt((rel * rel).sum(axis=1)).max()) / r500_kpc)
                mark_voxels(occupied, rel / r500_kpc)
        if verbose:
            print(f"    halo {halo_id}: {n_read:,}/{n_cells:,} cells  {time.time() - t0:.0f} s",
                  flush=True)
    return dict(hist=hist, snap=int(snap), halo_id=int(halo_id), z_snap=float(z_snap),
                m500c_msun=m500_msun, r500c_kpc=r500_kpc, n_cells=int(n_cells),
                median_cell_mass_code=m_med, n_heavy_cells=n_heavy_cells,
                r_cell_max_r500=r_max, shape=shape,
                covers_h6_corner=bool(r_max >= CORNER_H6_R500),
                cube6_voxel_fill=float(occupied.mean()), views={int(v): dict(views[v]) for v in VIEWS},
                d_l_cm={int(v): d_l[v] for v in VIEWS}, seconds=round(time.time() - t0, 1),
                peak_rss_gb=resource.getrusage(resource.RUSAGE_SELF).ru_maxrss / 1e6,
                snap_is_mini=False)

def select_halos(table: Any, n: int = 20, m_min: float = 1e14, m_max: float = 2e15) -> Any:
    """``n`` halos spanning M500c, z_snap and relaxation: farthest-point sampling in
    (log10 M500c, z_snap, log10 com_offset_r500) scaled to unit range, started from the halo
    nearest the median, among the full-snapshot halos with ``m_min <= M500c <= m_max``."""
    import numpy as np

    t = table[(table.m500c_msun >= m_min) & (table.m500c_msun <= m_max)
              & ~table.snap_is_mini].reset_index(drop=True)
    x = np.column_stack([np.log10(t.m500c_msun), t.z_snap,
                         np.log10(np.maximum(t.com_offset_r500, 1e-3))])
    x = (x - x.min(axis=0)) / np.maximum(np.ptp(x, axis=0), 1e-12)
    chosen = [int(np.argmin(((x - np.median(x, axis=0)) ** 2).sum(axis=1)))]
    d = ((x - x[chosen[0]]) ** 2).sum(axis=1)
    while len(chosen) < min(n, len(t)):
        i = int(np.argmax(d))
        chosen.append(i)
        d = np.minimum(d, ((x - x[i]) ** 2).sum(axis=1))
    return t.iloc[chosen].sort_values("n_gas_zoom", ascending=False).reset_index(drop=True)

def annulus_area_arcmin2(r500_kpc: float, z_obs: float, h0: float, om0: float) -> Any:

def summarize(long: Any, bkg: dict[str, float], pct: float = PERCENTILE,
              tol: float = TOLERANCE) -> tuple[Any, Any]:
    """``(per annulus and X table, decision table)``.

    The first table: median and ``pct`` percentile over halos and views of ``f_beyond``, of
    the beyond-X surface brightness and of its ratio to the background (median donor and
    10th percentile donor).  The second: per X, the two criteria of the decision rule
    (module docstring), the verdict and the verdict of the literal rule."""
    import numpy as np
    import pandas as pd

    d = long.copy()
    d["ratio_bkg"] = d.sb_beyond / bkg["median"]
    d["ratio_bkg_p10"] = d.sb_beyond / bkg["p10"]
    d["sb_total_over_bkg"] = d.sb_total / bkg["median"]
    g = d.groupby(["x_r500", "annulus_lo", "annulus_hi"])
    out = g.agg(n=("f_beyond", "size"),
                f_median=("f_beyond", "median"),
                f_pct=("f_beyond", lambda s: float(np.nanpercentile(s, pct))),
                sb_total_median=("sb_total", "median"),
                sb_beyond_median=("sb_beyond", "median"),
                sb_beyond_pct=("sb_beyond", lambda s: float(np.nanpercentile(s, pct))),
                ratio_bkg_median=("ratio_bkg", "median"),
                ratio_bkg_pct=("ratio_bkg", lambda s: float(np.nanpercentile(s, pct))),
                ratio_bkg_p10_pct=("ratio_bkg_p10", lambda s: float(np.nanpercentile(s, pct))),
                total_over_bkg_median=("sb_total_over_bkg", "median"),
                heavy_share_median=("heavy_share", "median")).reset_index()
    out["criterion_a_applies"] = [(lo, hi) in SIGNAL_ANNULI
                                  for lo, hi in zip(out.annulus_lo, out.annulus_hi)]
    dec = []
    for x, g2 in out.groupby("x_r500"):
        a_rows = g2[g2.criterion_a_applies]
        a_ok = bool((a_rows.f_pct < tol).all())
        a_all = bool((g2.f_pct < tol).all())
        b_ok = bool((g2.ratio_bkg_pct < tol).all())
        b10_ok = bool((g2.ratio_bkg_p10_pct < tol).all())
        dec.append(dict(x_r500=x, criterion_a_fraction=a_ok, criterion_b_background=b_ok,
                        converged=a_ok and b_ok, converged_with_p10_background=a_ok and b10_ok,
                        converged_literal=a_all and b_ok,
                        criterion_b_annuli_within_h=bool(
                            (g2[g2.annulus_hi <= x + 1e-12].ratio_bkg_pct < tol).all()),
                        worst_fraction_pct=float(a_rows.f_pct.max()) if len(a_rows) else np.nan,
                        worst_annulus_a=(f"{a_rows.loc[a_rows.f_pct.idxmax(), 'annulus_lo']}-"
                                         f"{a_rows.loc[a_rows.f_pct.idxmax(), 'annulus_hi']}"
                                         if len(a_rows) else ""),
                        worst_ratio_bkg_pct=float(g2.ratio_bkg_pct.max()),
                        worst_annulus_b=(f"{g2.loc[g2.ratio_bkg_pct.idxmax(), 'annulus_lo']}-"
                                         f"{g2.loc[g2.ratio_bkg_pct.idxmax(), 'annulus_hi']}")))
    return out, pd.DataFrame(dec)

def _options() -> Any:
    from tngxmm.config import get_preset
    from tngxmm.sim.generate_view import SimOptions

    return SimOptions.from_spec(get_preset("v1"))


def _run_one(job: dict) -> dict:
    """Worker: one halo of the list to its ``halo_*.npz`` (skipped when it exists)."""
    os.environ["OMP_NUM_THREADS"] = "1"
    out = Path(job["out_dir"]) / f"halo_s{job['snap']:03d}_h{job['halo_id']}.npz"
    if out.exists():
        return dict(snap=job["snap"], halo_id=job["halo_id"], status="exists")
    views = {v: dict(z_obs=job[f"z_obs_v{v}"], nh_1e22=job[f"nh_v{v}"]) for v in VIEWS}
    res = halo_emission(job["base_path"], job["snap"], job["halo_id"], views, opts=_options(),
                        ecf_table=job["ecf_table"], block_rows=job["block_rows"],
                        shape=job.get("shape", "sphere"), verbose=True)
    save_halo(res, job["out_dir"])
    return dict(snap=job["snap"], halo_id=job["halo_id"], status="ok",
                seconds=res["seconds"], peak_rss_gb=res["peak_rss_gb"])

def main(argv: Sequence[str] | None = None) -> int:
    """``python -m tngxmm.sim.outskirts_convergence {select,run,collect} ...``"""
    import argparse

    ap = argparse.ArgumentParser(prog="outskirts_convergence", description=__doc__.split("\n")[0])
    sub = ap.add_subparsers(dest="cmd", required=True)
    s = sub.add_parser("select", help="write the halo list")
    s.add_argument("out_csv")
    s.add_argument("--results-dir", required=True, help="campaign tree with results_tng-cluster_snap_*.csv")
    s.add_argument("--base-path", required=True, help="TNG-Cluster output directory")
    s.add_argument("--n", type=int, default=20)
    s.add_argument("--all-csv", default=None, help="also write the full table of candidates")
    r = sub.add_parser("run", help="emission histograms of the listed halos")
    r.add_argument("halo_csv")
    r.add_argument("out_dir")
    r.add_argument("--workers", type=int, default=1)
    r.add_argument("--block-rows", type=int, default=BLOCK_ROWS)
    r.add_argument("--max-halos", type=int, default=None)
    r.add_argument("--shape", choices=SHAPES, default="sphere",
                   help="'sphere': Euclidean radius (default); 'cube': Chebyshev radius, the box cutout")
    r.add_argument("--ecf-table", required=True, help="ecf_table_v4.npz (per-camera ECF)")
    r.add_argument("--base-path", required=True, help="TNG-Cluster output directory")
    c = sub.add_parser("collect", help="tables and figures from the halo files")
    c.add_argument("hist_dir")
    c.add_argument("out_dir")
    c.add_argument("--features", required=True, help="features_real.csv of the real donors")
    a = ap.parse_args(argv)

    if a.cmd == "select":
        import pandas as pd

        tab = halo_table(a.results_dir, SNAPS, Path(a.base_path).parent / "postprocessing" / "offsets")
        if a.all_csv:
            tab.to_csv(a.all_csv, index=False)
        sel = select_halos(tab, a.n)
        sel.to_csv(a.out_csv, index=False)
        with pd.option_context("display.width", 200):
            print(sel[["snap", "halo_id", "z_snap", "m500c_msun", "com_offset_r500",
                       "n_gas_zoom"]])
        return 0
    if a.cmd == "run":
        import multiprocessing as mp

        import pandas as pd

        tab = pd.read_csv(a.halo_csv).sort_values("n_gas_zoom", ascending=False)    # largest first
        if a.max_halos:
            tab = tab.head(a.max_halos)
        jobs = [dict(r._asdict(), out_dir=a.out_dir, base_path=a.base_path,
                     ecf_table=a.ecf_table, block_rows=a.block_rows, shape=a.shape)
                for r in tab.itertuples(index=False)]
        Path(a.out_dir).mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        if a.workers <= 1:
            results = [_run_one(j) for j in jobs]
        else:
            with mp.get_context("fork").Pool(a.workers, maxtasksperchild=1) as pool:
                results = list(pool.imap_unordered(_run_one, jobs, chunksize=1))
        for x in results:
            print(x, flush=True)
        return 0
    if a.cmd == "collect":
        files = sorted(Path(a.hist_dir).glob("halo_s*_h*.npz"))
        if not files:
            raise SystemExit(f"no halo files in {a.hist_dir}")
        opts = _options()
        shapes = {load_halo_file(f)[0].get("shape", "sphere") for f in files}
        if len(shapes) != 1:
            raise SystemExit(f"the halo files of {a.hist_dir} mix the shapes {sorted(shapes)}")
        shape = shapes.pop()
        out = Path(a.out_dir)
        out.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        bkg = background_reference(a.features)
        long = long_table(files, opts.h0, opts.om0)
        summary, decision = summarize(long, bkg)
        decision.insert(0, "shape", shape)
        long.to_csv(out / "per_halo_view.csv", index=False)
        summary.to_csv(out / "summary_by_annulus_x.csv", index=False)
        decision.to_csv(out / "decision.csv", index=False)
        (out / "background_reference.json").write_text(json.dumps(
            dict(bkg, column=BKG_COLUMN, source=str(a.features),
                 unit="counts / s / arcmin^2 per camera (mean of MOS1, MOS2, pn), 0.5-2 keV"),
            indent=1))
        make_figures(summary, long, bkg, out, shape=shape)
        print(decision.to_string())
        return 0
    return 2
