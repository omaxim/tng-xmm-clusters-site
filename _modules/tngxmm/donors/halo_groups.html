Source code for tngxmm.donors.halo_groups

"""Halo groups: every view of one physical halo, the unit of the folds and of the bootstrap.

A halo is observed many times: at every campaign snapshot at which it passes the mass cut, and along several lines
of sight (the three simulation axes in ``v1``, six icosahedral axes in release 1.2).  In the 60-snapshot target list
of ``v1_common`` one zoom target has up to 89 views at neighbouring epochs (DECISIONS "P4: the TNG mini snapshots
under the common mode").  These views are not independent clusters.  A model tested on a halo it was trained on, or
a bootstrap that draws the views of one halo independently, claims more than the sample holds.  This module makes
the halo the unit of both.

**Halo group** (:func:`halo_group_key`): ``(sim, <identity column>)``, by default the split unit of the view table,
``zoom_target_id``.

* TNG-Cluster: the zoom target (``zoom_target_id`` = ``GroupOrigHaloID``, the same at every snapshot);
* TNG300, TNG100, TNG50: the z = 0 object that :mod:`tngxmm.sim.build_box_halos` writes in ``zoom_target_id``: the
  z = 0 FoF group in which the halo's central subhalo ends, so it joins every halo whose central ends in that group,
  as its central or as a satellite (a central whose descendants stop before z = 0 is its own object);
* FLAMINGO, SIMBA, EAGLE: their target tables so far carry the halo id of one snapshot in ``zoom_target_id`` (the
  view schema's "the halo's own id in a box").  Such a column names a halo-snapshot, and one halo seen at several
  snapshots would fall into several groups, the leak this module removes.  So a simulation whose identity column
  equals ``halo_id`` on every row while its rows span several snapshots is refused, unless ``identity`` names a
  column that follows a halo (for FLAMINGO L1_m8 the HBT+ ``track_id`` of the sample table) or accepts the
  halo-snapshot explicitly (``{sim: None}``).

The identity rule of every simulation is stored in the halo-fold table (column ``identity``) and read back by every
path that attaches folds or groups (:func:`attach_halo_folds`, the bank, the analysis tables); a request for another
rule than the table's raises.  This module names no simulation: what names a halo comes from the table and the code
that wrote it (one graph for every simulation, ``tests/unit/test_single_graph.py``).  The key is ``<sim>:<id>``, or
``<sim>:s<snap>:h<halo_id>`` for a halo-snapshot.  ``halo_id`` alone is never a halo identity: it is the group index
of one snapshot's catalogue.  The 3,258 halo-snapshots of the maximal and box lists (``campaigns/v4_max``,
``campaigns/v4_box``) hold 2,657 distinct (sim, halo_id) pairs but 535 halos (352 TNG-Cluster zoom targets, 171
TNG300, 11 TNG100 and 1 TNG50 objects).

**Halo folds** (:func:`assign_halo_folds`): every group in one of ``n_folds`` folds.  Groups are sorted by stratum
(the simulation, and the frozen ``split`` where the table has one), then by decreasing log of their largest M500c,
then by key; consecutive blocks of ``n_folds`` groups receive a random permutation of the fold labels
(``numpy.random.default_rng(seed)``), the block rule of the donor folds (:mod:`tngxmm.donors.assign_donor_folds`).
The blocks run on across strata, so fold sizes differ by at most one group, and by at most two within a stratum.
The result depends on the set of groups and on their largest M500c, so the folds are assigned once, on the whole
halo list, and every later table takes them from that table (:func:`attach_halo_folds`), as the donor folds are
taken from ``donor_folds.csv``; a view whose group has no fold is refused, and new halos are added to the table
without moving the others (``frozen``).  Assigned separately, the maximal-and-box lists and the 60-snapshot list
give the same fold to 68 of their 292 common zoom targets, about what chance gives.  A table is read back only
through :func:`load_halo_folds`, against its pinned sha256 (as the frozen split is,
:mod:`tngxmm.bank.attach_splits`); the candidate table of the maximal and box lists is
:data:`CANDIDATE_HALO_FOLDS` (not used by any preset or parameter).

**Cross-fitting with the donor folds** (:func:`cross_fit_masks`).  The donor folds are unchanged: the observations
are grouped by cluster and linked through the donors of the mock views (:mod:`tngxmm.bank.assign_donor_folds`), and
the conditions donor of a view is drawn from its physics donor's fold (``fold_restricted``).  The two must have the
same number of folds.  The views of one halo have donors in several donor folds (285 of the 290 zoom targets of the
replica list), and the donors of a halo also serve other halos: in every committed target list the graph of halos
and donors joined by views is one connected component, so no fold assignment can hold every halo and every donor
together.  Fold f therefore trains on the views whose halo and whose donor are both outside fold f, and tests on
the views whose halo and donor are both in fold f (with the real observations of donor fold f); a view with only one
of the two in fold f is used in neither.  No halo and no donor is then in training and test at once.  With halo
folds drawn independently of the pairing, a fold trains on about (1 - 1/K)^2 of the views and tests on about 1/K^2
(64 and 4 per cent for K = 5; 62-65 and 3.3-4.0 per cent per fold on the maximal and box lists).  Only the views
whose halo fold equals their donor fold are ever tested: 1,860 of the 9,774 views of the maximal and box lists
(19 per cent), and 35 of their 535 halos are never tested (66 of the 290 zoom targets of the replica list and 91 of
the 292 of ``v4_common``, with the folds of the maximal and box lists).  :func:`cross_fit_coverage` gives these
numbers for any table.  A pairing that draws the physics donors of a halo from the halo's own fold makes the two
folds equal on every view, and no view and no halo is left out.

**Halo bootstrap** (:func:`halo_bootstrap`): a resample draws halo groups with replacement and keeps every row of a
drawn group, as often as the group is drawn; the statistic is computed on all rows of the resample.  The views and
snapshots of one halo are never resampled apart.  This is the bootstrap of Efron (1979) with the halo as the
independent unit, as :mod:`tngxmm.realism.c2st` resamples donors.  Fewer than two halos give no interval (NaN), and
a row without a group label is refused.

References
----------
tngcluster : the zoom-target identity ``GroupOrigHaloID`` (Nelson et al. 2024).
illustristng, sublink : the z = 0 objects of the periodic boxes (SubLink ``RootDescendantID``).
efron1979 : the bootstrap estimate of the standard error, here with halo groups as the resampled units.
"""

class HaloFoldError(ValueError):
    """Halo groups or folds cannot be formed (a view without a halo identity, an identity that names a
    halo-snapshot, a group in two folds or two splits, a view whose group has no fold, a table that is not the
    pinned one or whose identity rule differs from the one asked for)."""




def _rule(col: str | None) -> str:
    return col or HALO_SNAPSHOT_RULE


def _col(rule: str | None) -> str | None:
    return None if rule in (None, HALO_SNAPSHOT_RULE) else str(rule)

def identity_rule(sim: str, identity: Mapping[str, str | None] | None = None) -> str:
    """The column that names a halo of ``sim`` across snapshots, or ``'halo-snapshot'``."""
    ident = dict(identity or {})
    return _rule(ident[str(sim)] if str(sim) in ident else HALO_IDENTITY_COLUMN)

def table_identity(folds: Any) -> dict[str, str | None]:
    """The identity rule of every simulation of a halo-fold table, ``{sim: column or None}`` (None = the
    halo-snapshot).  A simulation with two rules raises :class:`HaloFoldError`."""
    if folds is None or not len(folds):
        return {}
    if "identity" not in folds.columns:
        raise HaloFoldError("the halo-fold table has no identity column")
    per = folds.groupby(folds["sim"].astype(str))["identity"].unique()
    bad = {s: sorted(map(str, r)) for s, r in per.items() if len(r) > 1}
    if bad:
        raise HaloFoldError(f"simulations under two identity rules in the halo-fold table: {bad}")
    return {s: _col(str(r[0])) for s, r in per.items()}

def resolve_identity(identity: Mapping[str, str | None] | None = None, frozen: Any = None) -> dict[str, str | None]:
    """The identity rules to use: those of the halo-fold table ``frozen`` for its simulations, ``identity`` for the
    others.  ``identity`` may repeat a table's rule; another rule for a simulation of the table raises
    :class:`HaloFoldError` (a table is never read, nor extended, under another rule than its own)."""
    out = table_identity(frozen)
    for s, c in (identity or {}).items():
        c = _col(c)
        if s in out and out[s] != c:
            raise HaloFoldError(f"{s}: the halo-fold table names halos by {_rule(out[s])}, not by {_rule(c)}")
        out[s] = c
    return out

@cites("tngcluster", "illustristng", "sublink")
def halo_group_key(views: Any, identity: Mapping[str, str | None] | None = None) -> Any:
    """The halo group of every row of ``views`` (a Series of strings on the same index).

    ``views`` needs ``sim`` and the identity column (:data:`HALO_IDENTITY_COLUMN`, ``zoom_target_id``) or, for a
    simulation that ``identity`` maps to None, ``snap`` and ``halo_id``.  ``identity`` names another column per
    simulation (``{'flamingo_l1m8': 'track_id'}``) or None (the halo-snapshot).  Raises :class:`HaloFoldError` for a
    row whose identity is missing or negative, and for a simulation whose identity column equals ``halo_id`` on every
    row while its rows span several snapshots (that column names halo-snapshots: one halo would be several groups;
    pass the column that follows a halo, or ``None`` to accept halo-snapshot groups).  Never a silent fallback."""
    import pandas as pd

    if "sim" not in views.columns:
        raise HaloFoldError("the view table has no sim column")
    ident = dict(identity or {})
    sim = views["sim"].astype(str)
    out = pd.Series(pd.NA, index=views.index, dtype=object)
    have_hs = {"snap", "halo_id"} <= set(views.columns)
    for s in sorted(sim.unique()):
        rows = (sim == s).to_numpy()
        col = _col(ident[s]) if s in ident else HALO_IDENTITY_COLUMN
        if col is None:
            if not have_hs:
                raise HaloFoldError(f"{s}: halo-snapshot groups need the snap and halo_id columns")
            snap = pd.to_numeric(views.loc[rows, "snap"], errors="coerce")
            hid = pd.to_numeric(views.loc[rows, "halo_id"], errors="coerce")
            if snap.isna().any() or hid.isna().any():
                raise HaloFoldError(f"{s}: {int((snap.isna() | hid.isna()).sum())} rows without snap or halo_id")
            out.loc[rows] = [f"{s}:s{int(a):03d}:h{int(b)}" for a, b in zip(snap, hid)]
            continue
        if col not in views.columns:
            raise HaloFoldError(f"{s}: the identity column {col!r} is not in the view table")
        v = pd.to_numeric(views.loc[rows, col], errors="coerce")
        bad = v.isna() | (v < 0)
        if bad.any():
            raise HaloFoldError(f"{s}: {int(bad.sum())} rows without a valid {col}")
        if have_hs:
            snaps = pd.to_numeric(views.loc[rows, "snap"], errors="coerce").nunique()
            hid = pd.to_numeric(views.loc[rows, "halo_id"], errors="coerce")
            if snaps > 1 and bool((v == hid).all()):
                raise HaloFoldError(
                    f"{s}: {col} equals halo_id on every row at {snaps} snapshots, so it names halo-snapshots and one "
                    f"halo would be several groups; name the column that follows a halo across snapshots (identity "
                    f"{{'{s}': '<column>'}}, e.g. an HBT+ track id) or accept halo-snapshot groups ({{'{s}': None}})")
        out.loc[rows] = [f"{s}:{int(x)}" for x in v]
    return out

@cites("tngcluster", "illustristng")
def halo_group_table(views: Any, identity: Mapping[str, str | None] | None = None,
                     mass_col: str | None = None) -> Any:
    """One row per halo group of ``views``: ``halo_group, sim, identity, stratum, log_m500c_max, n_halo_snaps,
    n_views``.

    ``identity``: :func:`halo_group_key`; the rule of each simulation is written in ``identity``.  ``mass_col``: the
    M500c column [Msun] (default ``m500c_msun``, else ``M500c``).  ``stratum`` is the simulation, and
    ``<sim>/<split>`` when the table has a ``split`` column; the rows of one group must share one split (the frozen
    split is by zoom target or object), else :class:`HaloFoldError`.  ``n_halo_snaps`` counts the distinct
    (snap, halo_id) of the group (two halos of one z = 0 object at one snapshot are two)."""
    import numpy as np
    import pandas as pd

    mc = _mass_column(views, mass_col)
    v = pd.DataFrame({"halo_group": halo_group_key(views, identity).to_numpy(),
                      "sim": views["sim"].astype(str).to_numpy(),
                      "m": pd.to_numeric(views[mc], errors="coerce").to_numpy(float)})
    bad_m = ~np.isfinite(v["m"].to_numpy()) | (v["m"].to_numpy() <= 0)
    if bad_m.any():
        raise HaloFoldError(f"{int(bad_m.sum())} rows without a positive {mc}")
    have_hs = {"snap", "halo_id"} <= set(views.columns)
    v["hs"] = ((views["snap"].astype("int64").astype(str) + ":" + views["halo_id"].astype("int64").astype(str))
               .to_numpy() if have_hs else np.arange(len(v)).astype(str))
    if have_hs:                       # the views of one halo-snapshot must name one halo
        per_hs = v.groupby(v["sim"] + ":" + v["hs"])["halo_group"].nunique()
        if (per_hs > 1).any():
            bad = sorted(per_hs[per_hs > 1].index)
            raise HaloFoldError(f"{len(bad)} halo-snapshots in more than one halo group (their views disagree on the "
                                f"identity column): {bad[:5]}")
    have_split = "split" in views.columns
    v["split"] = (views["split"].astype(object).where(views["split"].notna(), "none").astype(str).to_numpy()
                  if have_split else "")
    agg = v.groupby("halo_group", sort=True).agg(
        sim=("sim", "first"), n_sims=("sim", "nunique"), m=("m", "max"), n_halo_snaps=("hs", "nunique"),
        n_views=("sim", "size"), split=("split", "first"), n_splits=("split", "nunique")).reset_index()
    if (agg["n_sims"] > 1).any():
        raise HaloFoldError(f"{int((agg['n_sims'] > 1).sum())} halo groups in more than one simulation")
    if (agg["n_splits"] > 1).any():
        bad = sorted(agg.loc[agg["n_splits"] > 1, "halo_group"])
        raise HaloFoldError(f"{len(bad)} halo groups in more than one split: {bad[:5]}")
    out = pd.DataFrame({
        "halo_group": agg["halo_group"].astype(str), "sim": agg["sim"].astype(str),
        "identity": [identity_rule(s, identity) for s in agg["sim"]],
        "stratum": (agg["sim"] + "/" + agg["split"]) if have_split else agg["sim"],
        "log_m500c_max": np.log10(agg["m"].to_numpy(float)),
        "n_halo_snaps": agg["n_halo_snaps"].astype(int), "n_views": agg["n_views"].astype(int)})
    return out.reset_index(drop=True)

@cites("tngcluster", "illustristng")
def assign_halo_folds(views: Any, n_folds: int = N_FOLDS, seed: int = HALO_FOLD_SEED, *,
                      identity: Mapping[str, str | None] | None = None, mass_col: str | None = None,
                      frozen: Any = None) -> Any:
    """The halo-fold table of ``views`` (one row per halo group, columns :data:`HALO_FOLD_COLUMNS`).

    Every view of a group, at every snapshot and along every line of sight, has the group's fold.  The rule is in
    the module docstring.  Deterministic for a given ``seed`` and independent of the row order of ``views``.

    ``frozen`` (a halo-fold table of the same ``n_folds``): its rows are kept as they are, and only the groups of
    ``views`` that it lacks are assigned, by the same rule among themselves.  A release's table is extended this way
    when a list brings new halos; assigning again on a larger list would move groups that already have a fold (the
    blocks depend on every group's place in the sorted list).  The simulations of ``frozen`` keep its identity rules
    (:func:`resolve_identity`); asking for another raises."""
    import numpy as np
    import pandas as pd

    if n_folds < 2:
        raise HaloFoldError("n_folds must be >= 2")
    rules = resolve_identity(identity, frozen)
    g = halo_group_table(views, rules, mass_col)
    if frozen is not None:
        if len(frozen) and set(frozen["n_folds"].astype(int)) != {int(n_folds)}:
            raise HaloFoldError(f"the frozen table has {sorted(set(frozen['n_folds']))} folds, not {n_folds}")
        if frozen["halo_group"].duplicated().any():
            raise HaloFoldError("a halo group appears twice in the frozen table")
        g = g[~g["halo_group"].isin(set(frozen["halo_group"].astype(str)))]
    g = g.sort_values(["stratum", "log_m500c_max", "halo_group"], ascending=[True, False, True],
                      kind="mergesort").reset_index(drop=True)
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    labels = np.empty(len(g), int)
    for start in range(0, len(g), n_folds):
        blk = np.arange(start, min(start + n_folds, len(g)))
        labels[blk] = rng.permutation(n_folds)[: len(blk)]
    g["halo_fold"] = labels
    g["fold_seed"] = int(seed)
    g["n_folds"] = int(n_folds)
    if frozen is not None:
        g = pd.concat([frozen[list(HALO_FOLD_COLUMNS)], g[list(HALO_FOLD_COLUMNS)]], ignore_index=True)
    return g.sort_values("halo_group").reset_index(drop=True)[list(HALO_FOLD_COLUMNS)]

def load_halo_folds(path: str | Path, *, expected_sha256: str | None = CANDIDATE_HALO_FOLDS_SHA256) -> Any:
    """The halo-fold table at ``path``, checked against its pinned sha256 (default the candidate's,
    :data:`CANDIDATE_HALO_FOLDS_SHA256`) and for its form: the columns of :data:`HALO_FOLD_COLUMNS`, one row per
    group, one fold count, folds in range, one identity rule per simulation.  ``expected_sha256=None`` skips the
    checksum (synthetic tables in tests only)."""
    import pandas as pd

    from tngxmm.core.hashing import sha256_file

    p = Path(path)
    if expected_sha256 is not None:
        got = sha256_file(p)
        if got != expected_sha256:
            raise HaloFoldError(f"{p}: sha256 {got} != pinned {expected_sha256}; a halo-fold table is read only "
                                "with the checksum it was pinned with")
    t = pd.read_csv(p, dtype={"halo_group": str, "sim": str, "identity": str, "stratum": str})
    missing = [c for c in HALO_FOLD_COLUMNS if c not in t.columns]
    if missing:
        raise HaloFoldError(f"{p}: missing columns {missing}")
    if t["halo_group"].duplicated().any():
        raise HaloFoldError(f"{p}: a halo group appears twice")

        raise HaloFoldError(f"{p}: several fold counts {sorted(k)}")
    if len(t) and ((t["halo_fold"] < 0) | (t["halo_fold"] >= t["n_folds"])).any():
        raise HaloFoldError(f"{p}: a fold label outside 0..n_folds-1")
    table_identity(t)
    return t[list(HALO_FOLD_COLUMNS)]

def attach_halo_folds(views: Any, folds: Any, *, identity: Mapping[str, str | None] | None = None,
                      kind_col: str = "kind") -> Any:
    """``views`` with ``halo_group`` (str) and ``halo_fold`` (Int64) from the halo-fold table ``folds``.

    The groups are formed under the identity rules the table records (:func:`resolve_identity`; ``identity`` may
    repeat them, another rule raises).  Mock rows (``kind == 'mock'``, or every row when there is no ``kind``
    column) take the fold of their group; a mock whose group is not in ``folds`` raises :class:`HaloFoldError`.
    Observation rows get ``<NA>``.  A pre-existing ``halo_fold`` is replaced (the table is the only source).  Row
    order and index are kept."""
    import pandas as pd

    rules = resolve_identity(identity, folds)
    out = views.copy()
    mock = _is_mock(out, kind_col)
    key = pd.Series(pd.NA, index=out.index, dtype=object)
    if mock.any():
        key.loc[mock] = halo_group_key(out.loc[mock], rules).to_numpy()
    if folds["halo_group"].duplicated().any():
        raise HaloFoldError("a halo group appears twice in the halo-fold table")
    fmap = dict(zip(folds["halo_group"].astype(str), folds["halo_fold"].astype("int64")))
    fold = key.map(lambda k: fmap.get(k, pd.NA) if isinstance(k, str) else pd.NA)
    lost = mock & fold.isna().to_numpy()
    if lost.any():
        raise HaloFoldError(f"{int(lost.sum())} views have a halo group without a fold "
                            f"(e.g. {key[lost].head(3).tolist()}): assign the folds on the whole halo list")
    out["halo_group"] = key
    out["halo_fold"] = pd.array(fold.tolist(), dtype="Int64")
    return out

def check_halo_folds(views: Any, folds: Any = None, *, n_folds: int | None = None,
                     kind_col: str = "kind") -> list[str]:
    """Problems of the halo folds of a view table with ``halo_group`` and ``halo_fold`` (empty list = clean): mock
    rows without a fold, a group in more than one fold, a fold label outside 0..n_folds-1 and, with ``folds`` (the
    halo-fold table), a view whose fold differs from its group's."""
    problems: list[str] = []
    mock = _is_mock(views, kind_col)
    m = views[mock]
    if m["halo_fold"].isna().any() or m["halo_group"].isna().any():
        problems.append(f"{int((m['halo_fold'].isna() | m['halo_group'].isna()).sum())} mock rows without a halo fold")
    ok = m.dropna(subset=["halo_group", "halo_fold"])
    n_per = ok.groupby("halo_group")["halo_fold"].nunique()
    split = n_per[n_per > 1]
    if len(split):
        problems.append(f"{len(split)} halo groups in more than one fold: {sorted(split.index)[:5]}")
    k = n_folds if n_folds is not None else (int(folds["n_folds"].iloc[0]) if folds is not None and len(folds)
                                             and "n_folds" in folds.columns else None)
    if k is not None:
        out_of = ok[(ok["halo_fold"].astype(int) < 0) | (ok["halo_fold"].astype(int) >= k)]
        if len(out_of):
            problems.append(f"{len(out_of)} mock rows with a fold outside 0..{k - 1}")
    if folds is not None:
        fmap = dict(zip(folds["halo_group"].astype(str), folds["halo_fold"].astype(int)))
        want = ok["halo_group"].astype(str).map(fmap)
        diff = want.notna() & (want != ok["halo_fold"].astype(int))
        if diff.any():
            problems.append(f"{int(diff.sum())} mock rows disagree with the halo-fold table")
        unknown = want.isna()
        if unknown.any():
            problems.append(f"{int(unknown.sum())} mock rows with a halo group missing from the halo-fold table")
    return problems

def cross_fit_masks(views: Any, fold: int, *, halo_fold_col: str = "halo_fold",
                    donor_fold_col: str = "donor_fold") -> tuple[Any, Any]:
    """``(train, test)`` boolean arrays of the mock views for fold ``fold``.

    ``train``: halo fold and donor fold both differ from ``fold``; ``test``: both equal ``fold``.  The views with
    one of the two equal to ``fold`` are in neither (module docstring).  A view without either fold raises."""
    import numpy as np
    import pandas as pd

    hf = pd.to_numeric(views[halo_fold_col], errors="coerce")
    df = pd.to_numeric(views[donor_fold_col], errors="coerce")
    if hf.isna().any() or df.isna().any():
        raise HaloFoldError(f"{int((hf.isna() | df.isna()).sum())} views without a halo fold or a donor fold")
    hf, df = hf.to_numpy(int), df.to_numpy(int)
    return (hf != fold) & (df != fold), np.asarray((hf == fold) & (df == fold))

def fold_usage(views: Any, n_folds: int = N_FOLDS, *, halo_fold_col: str = "halo_fold",
               donor_fold_col: str = "donor_fold") -> Any:
    """Per fold: views and halo groups in training and in test under :func:`cross_fit_masks`, the views left
    out, and the fractions of all views (one row per fold; the input's mock views only)."""
    import pandas as pd

    rows = []
    n = len(views)

                     "train_halos": int(views.loc[tr, "halo_group"].nunique()) if "halo_group" in views else -1,
                     "test_halos": int(views.loc[te, "halo_group"].nunique()) if "halo_group" in views else -1})
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

def cross_fit_coverage(views: Any, *, halo_fold_col: str = "halo_fold", donor_fold_col: str = "donor_fold") -> dict:

def check_cross_fit_leakage(views: Any, donor_folds: Any, n_folds: int | None = None, *,
                            donor_roles: tuple[str, ...] = DONOR_ROLES, halo_fold_col: str = "halo_fold",
                            donor_fold_col: str = "donor_fold") -> list[str]:
    """Problems of the cross-fitting of the mock ``views`` (empty list = clean).

    ``donor_folds``: the donor-fold table (``obsid`` or ``donor_id``, and ``donor_fold``).  ``n_folds``: the number
    of halo folds (the halo-fold table's); it must equal the number of donor folds (:func:`donor_n_folds`), else
    the real observations of the extra donor folds would never be tested (a problem).  For every fold f: no training
    view borrowed an observation of donor fold f in any role of ``donor_roles`` that the views carry (physics,
    conditions, redshift donor; a target list carries the physics donor only, the bank metadata the conditions donor
    too), no halo group has views in both training and test, and every view's ``donor_fold`` is its physics
    donor's.  A donor without a fold is a problem, never skipped."""
    import numpy as np
    import pandas as pd

    from tngxmm.schemas.ids import normalise_obsid

    idc = "obsid" if "obsid" in donor_folds.columns else "donor_id"
    fmap = {normalise_obsid(o): int(f) for o, f in zip(donor_folds[idc], donor_folds["donor_fold"])}
    problems: list[str] = []
    k_donor = donor_n_folds(donor_folds)
    k_halo = k_donor if n_folds is None else int(n_folds)
    if k_halo != k_donor:
        problems.append(f"{k_halo} halo folds against {k_donor} donor folds: the two fold counts must be equal")
    roles = [c for c in donor_roles if c in views.columns]
    folds_of = {}
    for c in roles:
        ids = [normalise_obsid(v) if pd.notna(v) else None for v in views[c]]
        unknown = [i for i in ids if i is not None and i not in fmap]
        if unknown:
            problems.append(f"{len(unknown)} views have a {c} without a donor fold (e.g. {unknown[:3]})")
        folds_of[c] = np.array([fmap.get(i, -1) if i is not None else -1 for i in ids], int)
    own = pd.to_numeric(views[donor_fold_col], errors="coerce").to_numpy(float)
    if "donor_id" in folds_of:
        mism = (folds_of["donor_id"] >= 0) & (folds_of["donor_id"] != own)
        if mism.any():
            problems.append(f"{int(mism.sum())} views whose donor_fold is not their physics donor's")
    for f in range(max(k_halo, k_donor)):
        tr, te = cross_fit_masks(views, f, halo_fold_col=halo_fold_col, donor_fold_col=donor_fold_col)
        for c, fo in folds_of.items():
            lent = int((fo[tr] == f).sum())
            if lent:
                problems.append(f"fold {f}: {lent} training views borrowed a {c} of the test fold")
        if "halo_group" in views.columns:
            both = set(views.loc[tr, "halo_group"]) & set(views.loc[te, "halo_group"])
            if both:
                problems.append(f"fold {f}: {len(both)} halo groups in training and test: {sorted(both)[:5]}")
    return problems




def _codes(groups: Any) -> tuple[Any, Any]:

    import pandas as pd

    g = pd.Series(np.asarray(groups, dtype=object))

        raise ValueError(f"{int(g.isna().sum())} rows without a group label: every row needs its halo group")

@cites("efron1979")
def halo_bootstrap_indices(groups: Any, n_boot: int, seed: int = HALO_BOOT_SEED) -> Iterator[Any]:
    """``n_boot`` resamples of the rows of a table whose rows belong to ``groups``, one index array each.

    A resample draws as many groups as there are, with replacement, and holds every row of a drawn group as often
    as the group is drawn (rows of one group are never resampled apart).  The draw does not depend on the row
    order: the groups are numbered in sorted order.  A missing group label (None, NA, NaN) raises ``ValueError``."""
    import numpy as np

    uniq, codes = _codes(groups)
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    n_g = len(uniq)
    rows = np.arange(len(codes))
    for _ in range(int(n_boot)):
        counts = np.bincount(rng.integers(0, n_g, n_g), minlength=n_g) if n_g else np.zeros(0, int)
        yield np.repeat(rows, counts[codes])

@cites("efron1979")
def halo_bootstrap(x: Any, groups: Any, stat: Callable[[Any], float] | None = None, n_boot: int = 10000,
                   seed: int = HALO_BOOT_SEED) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """The statistic ``stat`` (default the median) of ``x`` and its bootstrap over the halo groups ``groups``.

    Non-finite values are dropped first; a group without a finite value is not a unit, and a row with a finite value
    but no group label raises ``ValueError``.  Returns ``value`` (the statistic of all rows), ``lo68, hi68, lo95,
    hi95`` (percentiles 16, 84, 2.5, 97.5 of the resampled statistic), ``se`` (its standard deviation, ddof 1),
    ``n_groups``, ``n_rows`` and ``n_boot``.  With fewer than two groups the interval and ``se`` are NaN: the
    resamples of one halo all repeat it, and their spread says nothing about the sampling error."""
    import numpy as np

    f = stat or np.median
    x = np.asarray(x, float)
    g = np.asarray(groups, dtype=object)
    if len(g) != len(x):
        raise ValueError("x and groups differ in length")
    ok = np.isfinite(x)
    x, g = x[ok], g[ok]
    nan = float("nan")
    if not len(x):
        return dict(value=nan, lo68=nan, hi68=nan, lo95=nan, hi95=nan, se=nan, n_groups=0, n_rows=0,
                    n_boot=int(n_boot))
    uniq, _ = _codes(g)
    n_g = len(uniq)
    if n_g < 2:
        return dict(value=float(f(x)), lo68=nan, hi68=nan, lo95=nan, hi95=nan, se=nan, n_groups=int(n_g),
                    n_rows=int(len(x)), n_boot=int(n_boot))
    b = np.array([f(x[idx]) for idx in halo_bootstrap_indices(g, n_boot, seed)], float)
    lo68, hi68, lo95, hi95 = (float(q) for q in np.percentile(b, [16, 84, 2.5, 97.5]))
    return dict(value=float(f(x)), lo68=lo68, hi68=hi68, lo95=lo95, hi95=hi95,
                se=float(np.std(b, ddof=1)) if len(b) > 1 else nan, n_groups=int(n_g),
                n_rows=int(len(x)), n_boot=int(n_boot))




def _identity_is_halo_id(g: Any, col: str) -> bool:
    """True when the identity column of a simulation's rows equals their ``halo_id`` on every row (one snapshot, or
    groups accepted as halo-snapshots)."""
    import pandas as pd

    if col == HALO_SNAPSHOT_RULE or col not in g.columns or "halo_id" not in g.columns:
        return False
    a, b = pd.to_numeric(g[col], errors="coerce"), pd.to_numeric(g["halo_id"], errors="coerce")
    return bool(len(g)) and bool((a == b).all())

def halo_fold_report(views: Any, folds: Any, donor_folds: Any = None, *,
                     identity: Mapping[str, str | None] | None = None) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Summary of a halo-fold table on its views.

    Groups, halo-snapshots and views per simulation and per fold; the identity rule of each simulation (with
    ``identity_is_halo_id`` when its identity column holds the snapshot's halo id on every row); the problems of
    :func:`check_halo_folds`; and, when the views carry ``donor_fold``, the cross-fitting use per fold
    (:func:`fold_usage`), what is ever tested (:func:`cross_fit_coverage`), the donor roles the views carry
    (``leak_check_roles``: the roles :func:`check_cross_fit_leakage` can check on them; a target list carries the
    physics donor only) and, with ``donor_folds``, the fold counts of both and the problems of
    :func:`check_cross_fit_leakage`."""
    rules = resolve_identity(identity, folds)
    v = attach_halo_folds(views, folds, identity=identity)
    hs = (v.drop_duplicates(["sim", "snap", "halo_id"]) if {"snap", "halo_id"} <= set(v.columns) else v)
    rep: dict[str, Any] = {
        "n_views": int(len(v)), "n_halo_snaps": int(len(hs)), "n_groups": int(v["halo_group"].nunique()),
        "n_sim_halo_id_pairs": (int(len(hs.drop_duplicates(["sim", "halo_id"]))) if "halo_id" in hs.columns else None),
        "per_sim": {s: {"identity": identity_rule(s, rules),
                        "identity_is_halo_id": _identity_is_halo_id(g, identity_rule(s, rules)),
                        "groups": int(g["halo_group"].nunique()),
                        "halo_snaps": int(len(hs[hs["sim"] == s])), "views": int(len(g))}
                    for s, g in v.groupby("sim", sort=True)},
        "per_fold": {int(f): {"groups": int(g["halo_group"].nunique()), "views": int(len(g))}
                     for f, g in v.groupby("halo_fold", sort=True)},
        "max_views_per_group": int(v.groupby("halo_group").size().max()) if len(v) else 0,
        "fold_seed": int(folds["fold_seed"].iloc[0]) if len(folds) else None,
        "n_folds": int(folds["n_folds"].iloc[0]) if len(folds) else None,
        "problems": check_halo_folds(v, folds),
    }
    if "donor_fold" in v.columns and v["donor_fold"].notna().all():
        k = rep["n_folds"] or N_FOLDS
        rep["cross_fit"] = fold_usage(v, k).to_dict("records")
        rep["cross_fit_coverage"] = cross_fit_coverage(v)
        rep["leak_check_roles"] = [c for c in DONOR_ROLES if c in v.columns]
        if donor_folds is not None:
            rep["n_donor_folds"] = donor_n_folds(donor_folds)
            rep["cross_fit_problems"] = check_cross_fit_leakage(v, donor_folds, k)
    return rep
