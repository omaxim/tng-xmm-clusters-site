Written with the first build of `docs/assumptions/assumptions.yml`. Nothing here has an invented
justification: where a number has neither a citation nor a validation recorded in the repository it is
listed, and its ledger entry carries `open:`. `tests/unit/test_ledger.py` fails when this list and the
`open:` entries of the ledger disagree.

## B. Justified, but the evidence is incomplete

These entries have a citation or a validation; the item says what a referee can still attack.

- `donors.matching_mass`: Planck 2015 XXIV gives (1-b) = 0.78 as a sample-level prior for a count
  likelihood; we use it per cluster. 0.78 is the low end of later calibrations (0.84-0.95, Herbonnet et
  al. 2020 and others), and the built-in offset between the PSZ2 (0.78) and the ACT DR6 (A10/WL = 0.65,
  already calibrated) scales is about 1.20. The sensitivity test S7 ((1-b)_PSZ2 = 0.84 and 0.90) was run on the login
  node in release 1.1.3 (SENSITIVITY_TESTS.md, R12): the replica list fails gate P in mass at every value (D = 0.241,
  0.229, 0.207 against 0.061-0.067), the coverage rises from 0.929 to 0.933 and 0.948, the median halo mass of the
  views moves by -0.008 and -0.018 dex, and almost every individual pairing changes (8.5 and 5.6 per cent of the
  production views are drawn again). What depends on it: which halo is paired with which real observation (the
  replica draw and gate P), and through that every mock-to-real comparison at fixed observation (count matching,
  realism, the comparison of mock and real fits). The bias estimands of a mock (M_X, M_HSE and M500c of the same
  halo) do not depend on it.
- `truth.star_forming_gas`: excluding star-forming gas is our deviation from Barnes et al. (2021), who
  state no gas cut for the 3-D profiles. The three deviations together (fitted slopes, no star-forming gas, own
  mu) move ln M_HSE(R500c) by +0.024 in the median against the literature-faithful `barnes21` column (16/84
  percentiles -0.085/+0.141, 7380 views; SENSITIVITY_TESTS.md R5). The individual sizes were measured on 2026-11-04
  on 24 TNG-Cluster halo-snapshots (SENSITIVITY_TESTS.md S8): keeping the star-forming cells moves the median of
  ln M_HSE(< R500c) / M500c by -0.0003, mu = 0.59 by -0.0041, both by -0.0045; the +0.024 is therefore the slope
  method of the `barnes21` column.
- `truth.own_mu`: the second deviation from Barnes et al.; with mu = 0.59 the median of ln M_HSE(< R500c) / M500c
  moves by -0.0041 (S8, 2026-11-04; the mu = 0.59 twin is not a column, `compute_hse_truth(mu059_fit=True)`
  computes it on request).
- `fit.background_seed`: closed on 2026-11-03: the presets fit the ESAS background (corner-fixed QPB, free sky and
  soft protons, no prior); the typical-EPIC level calibrated on mocks is removed. Only the diagnostic mixture
  model keeps `fallback_bg_prior_dex` = 0.3, without a separate test.
- `fit.priors_low_signal`: closed on 2026-11-03: flat priors at every signal level (Sanders et al. 2018); the
  20 000-count threshold and the informative priors are diagnostics only. Since 2026-11-12 the concentration has the
  published relation of `fit.cm_prior` as its prior, again at every signal level; the metallicity prior stays flat.
  Open: what the low-signal fits lose, measured by closure B1.
- `fit.metallicity`: the flat 0-2 Z_sun range has no separate test. Its lower edge 0 is the physical bound of an
  abundance and also a hard bound of the code: mbproj2d's ApecRateCalc.get returns NaN for Z < 0 and the fit turns
  that into a log-likelihood of -inf. The X-COP abundances were fitted with the same kind of bound (APEC in XSPEC,
  Ghizzardi et al. 2021, p. 2) and it did not bias them (p. 6), but the authors warn that it can bias small, poorly
  measured abundances, the regime of the low-count fits here, and recommend allowing negative values, as Mernier et
  al. (2017, p. 3) do; following that advice would need a change to mbproj2d. A published hydrostatic MBProj2 fit
  uses a flat 0-1 Z_sun prior (Sanders et al. 2022, p. 18). The code tabulates the emissivity at 0 and 1 Z_sun and
  scales it linearly (Sanders et al. 2018, p. 21), so the upper edge 2 extrapolates that scaling and is a choice of the
  prior: about twice the edge of Sanders et al. (1 Z_sun of Asplund et al. 2009 is about 1.07 in lodd units), with no
  published value, a design parameter (section P). The mean and the average core abundances
  stay well below it (the mean ICM abundance within r180 is at most 0.26 Z_sun of Anders and Grevesse 1989, about 0.41
  in lodd units, Molendi et al. 2016, p. 1-2; the average core about 0.8 proto-solar, Mernier et al. 2017, Table 2);
  single cores are not covered by these averages.
- `fit.molecular_weight`: the fully ionised count departs from mbproj2d's built-in constant and from the
  1.83 particles per electron of Sanders et al. (2018, Appendix B); effect +0.03 to +0.05 dex. Since 2026-11-03
  (section Q) the departure has its sources: a fully ionised plasma holds about 1.92 particles per electron (Yeung
  et al. 2024, p. 15), and published hydrostatic masses of real (Martino et al. 2014, p. 6) and mock clusters (Nagai
  et al. 2007, p. 4) use mu ~ 0.6, consistent with full ionisation; a mock XMM-Newton analysis takes the molecular
  weight from its abundance table (Seppi et al. 2026, p. 13). The review of X-ray masses (Ettori et al. 2013) quotes
  mu ~ 0.6 for the hydrostatic mass but backs neither count cleanly: its own pressure, P_gas = n_gas kT (p. 8), takes
  n_gas = n_e + n_p ~ 1.826 n_e (p. 9), the count of mbproj2d. Still open: the correction changes the published code,
  so the paper has to state it.
- `fit.outer_pressure`: freezing P_out at the floor changed one real cluster (MACS J1347) by 20 %, so the
  choice matters; the free outer pressure has the precedent of Sanders et al. (2018). Since 2026-11-03 (section Q)
  also of a hydrostatic MBProj2 fit with a flat prior on log P_out (Sanders et al. 2022, p. 17). The X-COP code fits
  the boundary pressure free within a Gaussian prior on the measured SZ pressure (Eckert et al. 2022, p. 5) and starts
  its mock profiles at 5 R500 to keep it out of the fitted range (p. 6); the earlier backward method fixed it at the
  pressure measured at the outermost data point, and freeing it over a grid of values from the best-fit density and
  temperature moved the mass by about 3 per cent (Ettori et al. 2010, p. 6 and 9, Table 4 on p. 19). No paper read
  (Sanders et al. 2018, 2022; Scheck et al. 2023; Ettori et al. 2010, 2019; Eckert et al. 2022) freezes it at a floor.
  The run-away level -30 has no source and is a design parameter (section P).
- `fit.mock_agn`: resolved in release 1.1 (`instrument.agn_lognlogs = 'moretti03-soft'`): production draws the
  field AGN from the soft-band (0.5-2 keV) counts of Moretti et al. (2003), p. 11 (alpha1 = 1.82, alpha2 = 0.60,
  S0 = 1.48e-14, N = 6150 per deg^2, BOXED). The hard-band set of the upstream `xmm_simulator` is used only under the
  stage option `moretti03-hard`, which no v1 preset selects.
- `method.estimand_fixed_radius`: the estimand of claim C was fixed after seeing the v2 results (disclosed in
  DESIGN v3).
- `method.multistart_optimiser`: the grid values (M500 x 0.5, 1, 2; c200 = 3, 7) have no source; the
  optimiser is not adopted by any preset until the J1 diagnostic.
- `method.background_fallback_fov`: "beyond 2 R500" now has its precedent in the ledger (Ghirardini et al. 2019,
  sky level from R > 2 R500, BOXED, p. 4; also Eckert et al. 2022, p. 3); the 50-exposed-pixel minimum is the fitter's
  constant, a design parameter since 2026-11-03 (section P). A mock XMM-Newton analysis replaces a background region
  that falls off the field by a corner region chosen by hand (Seppi et al. 2026, p. 5): support for the case, not for
  the number.
- `instrument.response`: the presets now generate and fit every image with the SAS 22.1 responses of its own
  exposure (a mock: its conditions donor's; `instrument.responses`, `massfit.responses`) and the SAS selection of the
  calibration files (`ccf_selection`). The response sets are built (`tngxmm instrument obs-responses`):
  `responses_obs_v1` for the bank of 638 observations, with which pass 1 was generated and fitted, and
  `responses_obs_v2` for the 808 observations, whose files of the 638 are those of the first set. The shared responses they replace (one RMF per camera made in 2020 with SAS 16.1,
  CCF ARFs with a MOS quantum efficiency SAS no longer selects) read the three-camera temperature of a real
  observation +1.0 / +2.2 / +4.1 per cent high at 3 / 5 / 8 keV (median of 13 epochs), the sum of the EPIC-to-NuSTAR
  correction (+0.9 / +1.9 / +3.5), the withdrawn quantum efficiency (-1.2 / -1.7 / -2.4) and the contamination and
  other MOS terms (rising with the date), each measured on its own; the three add to the total of every
  epoch within 0.06 points (0.3 at 8 keV in XSPEC). With cifbuild's files the shared set would read +2.2 / +3.9 / +6.7
  per cent instead. The calibration error itself is sized only by closure
  arms that
  have not run: `v1_xcallo` (the ACIS/PN spline of Schellenberger et al. 2015 applied to every camera, MOS included;
  per camera it misses the paper's Table 2; the range is [v1_xcallo, nominal])
  and `v1_nustarlo` / `v1_nustarhi` (the SOC's EPIC-to-NuSTAR correction); `v1_obsresp_shared` measures the
  real-side error of the shared responses through the whole fit.
- `instrument.pn_soft_protons_inout`: the pn IN/OUT soft-proton excess is a lower bound (the pn corners receive
  soft protons and photons, Marelli et al. 2021) and is not validated: no source calibrates a pn IN/OUT excess
  against a soft-proton level, and the pn uses the MOS quiescent soft-proton spectrum. One replay (job 1891399) moved
  the pn field background from 0.08-0.10 dex below the donors' to within 0.03 dex above 1 keV, left the pn / MOS
  background ratio below the real one (1.18 against 1.32) and made the background slightly too hard (1.72 against
  1.68). A size of the bias, e.g. from the pn level predicted by the MOS2 corners (Marelli et al. 2021, Sect. 5.2),
  is not measured.
- `fit.particle_background_esas`: the level errors d of the closure arms `v1_qpbhi`, `v1_qpblo`, `v1_qpbsoft` and
  `v1_qpbhard` (per camera and band, `closure_grid.QPB_LEVEL_ERROR`) are measured on the CCF filter-wheel-closed
  exposures (tools/fwc_qpb_level.py, docs/dev/reports/QPB-LEVEL-FWC.md) and are a lower bound. Choices of ours: the
  circle inside each square crop about the detector centre standing for the fit region (the circle holding the crop
  gives up to 0.010 less; a high-z crop within 5 arcmin sees up to 1.7 times the value); every FWC exposure weighted
  alike (the real observations are later on average, median 2013); NFLAG 1-2 of the FWC exposure table read as the
  anomalous state. Not in d: the pn corners lit by the cluster's own photons (Marelli et al. 2021 advise against the
  pn corners under bright extended sources and give no size); soft protons in the pn corners beyond the 2.3 per cent
  bound measured on cleaned 10-14 keV data (our pn level in each band comes from that band's own corners, 0.5-12 keV;
  only the relative scale of the quadrants uses 5-12 keV), and the out-of-time events the pn model leaves there; the
  part of the corners of MOS CCDs 2 and 7 that X-ray photons and low-energy particles reach (De Luca & Molendi 2004,
  Sect. 3.1, mask it from their corner data; our corner selection keeps it). Masking that region is a proposed code
  change, not made: the paper gives no outline, so the region must first be found in the corner images of the
  donors against the FWC data, and the change touches the donor tables, the mocks and the real templates.
- The closure test fails with a realistic background: on the 120 fits of the closure factorial (2026-10-17, the
  configuration before the release 1.1 instrument switches) the median M500_fit / M500_true is 0.970 for clean cells
  and 0.845 for cells with sky, particle background and AGN (0.921 background only, 0.779 AGN only). The gate as
  first registered (1.00 +- 0.03 in every cell) fails; it was moved to the clean cells and the catastrophic tail of
  the real cells after the results were seen. The deficit is not subtracted from any mock result. The
  `validation:` blocks of the ledger quote measurements, not passes. Status page: docs/validation.md.
- Journal page numbers of every cited paper are NOT verified: the proofs use arXiv PDF pages.

### Findings for the author's decision

1. `apply_pattern_loss`'s docstring reports "0.985-0.988 at 1 keV" as the efficiency; that is `FRAC_SD`. The
   ratio the code applies is 1 because the CCF table carries no triple or quadruple events. Whether the
   image selection should lose the 1.5 % of events not in S+D at 1 keV (about 5 % at 8 keV) is a question for
   the SAS pattern convention, not settled here. The spec field is also better a boolean than 0.92 (changing
   it changes the spec hash, so it was not done).
2. The red headline takes the innermost crossing, the black SO line (`m500_at_z`) the outermost; they differ
   only for the 0.12 % multiple-crossing views.
3. About 30 % of the 514 D1/J1 fits are flagged excluded at every prior-edge fraction tried (R1); this is a
   property of the c200 and Z priors on these 43 views, not of the threshold.
4. The tolerance of the part-2 tests (0.015 in ln M ratio) is derived from three re-seeds of the same views
   (sd 0.013). The author may prefer a different bar; the tests report the measured change either way.

## F. Decisions taken on the recommended options (2026-10-17)

1. **Mock AGN band** (superseded in release 1.1, see section B, `fit.mock_agn`). The decision of 2026-10-17 kept the
   upstream hard-band constants, consistent end to end with the hard-band template. Release 1.1 moved production to the
   soft-band counts (`instrument.agn_counts_band`). The mass effect of the AGN measured before that change is
   E = +0.007 [-0.007, +0.010]: removing the AGN from 43 mock views changes ln M_X by +0.028 (off minus on), and E is
   the quarter of that change attributed to the excess of mock AGN detections (x1.33).
2. **Pattern loss.** Keep the behaviour (the applied ratio is 1.0000 in 0.5-2 keV). Fix the docstring. The boolean is not
   replaced, because that would change the spec hash for no effect.
3. **Crossing rule.** The paper's estimands (`analysis.estimands`) take red AND black at their outermost 500 ρc(z_obs) crossing
   with the same function (`m500_at_z`), so they are consistent by construction. The production headline column (innermost
   crossing) is not used by the estimands; the 0.12 % multiple-crossing views stay flagged.
4. **Tolerance of the part-2 sensitivity tests.** 0.015 in the median ln(blue/red) at 1.0 R500c, the measured scatter of the
   median between three re-seeds of the same views (sd 0.013). It is a measured noise floor, not a chosen number.

**Counter-evidence and cautions (not evidence):**

- `method.closure_factorial`: Meneghetti 2010 p. 6 leaves the background out because Rasia 2006 found no systematic on the
  bias; Rasia 2006 p. 1 itself finds the estimated mass changes when the background is reduced by a factor of 100
  (attributed to bulk motions and temperature bias, not to the background). Our closure grid (real/clean 0.916, CHECKS)
  finds that the background does shift the mass here, so these papers are context for the question, not agreement with the
  answer. Nagai 2007 p. 3 omits background subtraction in its main analysis.
- `method.multistart_optimiser`: Gelman 2020 p. 60 says that ignoring non-converging chains is poor practice and
  recommends examining all of them first; the ledger code records every start's optimum, but picking the highest log
  posterior without that examination has no support. The same paper (p. 60) shows that sampling the starts from the prior
  made the chains converge quickly, a different design from a grid of dispersed M500 and c200 values.
- `method.qc_flags`: no cluster or X-ray paper was found that flags or excludes fits whose posterior sits at a hard prior
  bound; the prior-edge flags and the outer-pressure runaway remain our own diagnostics. Gelman 2020 p. 60 also warns
  against discarding non-converging results without diagnosis (the flags report the excluded fits).
- `method.maximal_list_donors`: Stuart 2010 and Austin 2011 concern matching in observational studies of treatment effects;
  they support the idea of a reuse count and a caliper, not any numerical value, and no astronomy paper was found that caps
  the reuse of a real observation for a mock.

**Searched, nothing found:**

- `fit.pixel`: no XMM-Newton surface-brightness or forward-model paper read states an image pixel size or a minimum number
  of pixels across R500. X-COP (Ghirardini 2019 p. 4, Eckert 2016 p. 6) uses Voronoi bins of 20 counts, CHEX-MATE 2 arcsec
  bins (section I), Sanders 2018 0.492 arcsec pixels. Nothing supports 8 arcsec or 40 pixels.
- `fit.point_source_thresholds`, the 90 arcsec protection: no paper found that leaves the central source or BCG unmasked
  (Pacaud 2016, Seppi 2022 in section K only support the premise). Rasia 2012 and Vikhlinin 2006 do the opposite (section K).
  Bulbul 2024, Clerc 2012 and Pacaud 2006 were not read (not in the references area).
- `photometry.lx_background_clip`: no paper was found that clips azimuthal sectors of an annulus at a multiple of a Poisson
  or MAD error to estimate a background; X-COP clips in time and uses the azimuthal median instead.
- Not read, not cited: Le Brun 2014 (arXiv 1312.5462) and Eckert 2016 (arXiv 1606.05657, A2142) were fetched into the
  references area; a text search found no use for them. Bahar 2022 was not identified, Feigelson and Nelson 1985 (upper

### N addendum: counter-evidence moved out of `photometry.lx_core_protection`

Mantz et al. 2010b (abstract) and Pratt et al. 2009 (p. 7) treat the central 0.15 R500 as a distinct, cool-core
dominated region, but they EXCISE it from the luminosity; REXCESS removes point sources with no core exemption
(Pratt 2009 p. 14). That is the opposite of protecting core sources from masking, so they are recorded here as
counter-evidence and not as support. The entry rests on its validation (the masked-core cases of the campaign
Lx background check).
