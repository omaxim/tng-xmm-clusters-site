# Sensitivity and convergence tests of the assumption ledger

Companion of `OPEN_ITEMS.md`. Part 1 holds the tests that were RUN on the login node (scripts in
`internal/ledger_tests/`, raw output in `$TNGXMM_DEV/LEDGER2/`); part 2 is the
precise specification of the tests that need the node. The ledger entries that cite a result below name this
file as their `report:`.

Common conventions of part 2:

* Metric of fit-level tests: the median over views of `ln_br_1` (ln of blue over red mass inside
  1.0 R500c(z_obs), `tngxmm.analysis.tables.mock_table`), after the QC exclusion of
  `fit_qc.qc_flags` at the production fraction, as paired differences per view against the production arm.
  Also reported: the excluded count and the median `ln_br_so`.
* Inputs: the 43 views of CHECKS D1 (`$TNGXMM_DEV/CHECKS/fits/d1`, sample in
  `CHECKS/plan/sample_d1.csv`), with the production configuration `v4s1` as the reference arm.
* Pre-registered tolerance, with its reason: a variation is immaterial when the median paired change of
  `ln_br_1` is below 0.015. The reason is measured: three re-seeds (`v4s1`, `v4s2`, `v4s3`) of the same 43
  views give medians -0.072, -0.064 and -0.089 (sd 0.013) with the 2.5 % flags, so a smaller shift cannot be
  told from the optimiser and chain noise on 43 views. A larger shift is reported as a systematic with its
  size, and the paper states it.
* Cost unit: one 43-view arm is 43 independent quick fits, measured on the D1 queue as 3112 s mean and
  13701 s maximum per fit (`CHECKS/plan/queue_fits_d1_d3_d2_d4_progress.jsonl`), that is about 37 core-hours,
  about 4 hours of wall time on one node with 43 cores. One node per job.

## Part 1. Tests run

kept. The 2.5 % column reproduces the stored `qc_excluded` of every fit.

Result: between 1 % and 5 % the excluded set changes by 7 of 172 fits (27 % to 31 %) and the median of
`ln_br_1` by +0.016, which equals the run-to-run noise of the median (0.018 to 0.027) and the tolerance of

### R2. T_vir and `constants.mu_tvir` (`sim.tvir_mu`)

Script `internal/ledger_tests/ecf_tvir.py`. `t_vir_keV(M500, R500)` is used only as the T of
`ecf_lookup(T, z, nH)`, the count-rate to flux factor of the campaign Lx (`measure_lx`, `seed_lx`; V4 spec:
`tvir_frac` = 0, so no gas cut reads it). The ECF in 0.5-2 keV changes for T x1.1 by less than 0.1 % at z = 0.05,
0.3 to 0.4 % at z = 0.2, 0.7 to 1.3 % at z = 0.5 and 1.3 to 2.9 % at z = 0.9 (T = 2 to 8 keV, nH = 0.03e22). A mean molecular
weight 2 % different (0.588 against 0.60) moves T by 2 %, hence the ECF by at most 0.6 % at z = 0.9. The result
feeds the fit only through the Lx seed (first-guess mass and c200, see R6 and S6).

### R3. HSE crossing rule (`method.hse_crossing_rule`)

Scripts `hse_multiple_crossings.py`, `hse_inner_exclusion.py`.

* Production results (9 snapshots, 7389 views): `hse_crossing` is `single` for 7371, `multiple` for 9
  (0.12 %), `fit-failed` for 9, none `none`. The 9 are 3 halo-snapshots (3 views each).
* For these 9 the outermost crossing would give a mass larger than the innermost by ln 0.73 to 1.20 (median

* Inner exclusion 0.2, 0.3, 0.4 (production), 0.5, 0.6 R500c on the 2463 truth files: the headline mass is
  identical for 0.2 to 0.5 (no profile changes), and 0.6 loses the crossing of 3 more profiles. The 0.4 R500c
  exclusion is therefore inert.
* Note for the author: `truth_estimands` (black line, `m500_at_z`) takes the OUTERMOST crossing while the red HSE
  headline takes the INNERMOST. For single-crossing profiles (99.9 %) they coincide in definition.

### R5. Combined size of the three deviations from Barnes et al. (2021)

Script `internal/ledger_tests/hse_barnes_deviations.py`. The results carry `barnes21` (finite-difference slopes,
mu = 0.59, no gas cut) and the headline `barnes21_nosf_fit_mu` (fitted slopes, no star-forming cells, each cell's mu).
Over 7380 views, `ln(headline / barnes21)` at R500c has median +0.024, 16/84 percentiles -0.085/+0.141, 1/99
percentiles -0.38/+0.41, and a median per snapshot between +0.013 and +0.056. This is the COMBINED effect of the
slope method, the star-forming cut and the own mu. S8 separates them: the star-forming cut and the own mu together
move the median by -0.0045, so nearly all of the +0.024 is the slope method (finite differences in `barnes21` against
the Vikhlinin fits of Barnes et al. 2021, Sect. 2.4, which the headline follows); the slope method is not a deviation
from Barnes et al.

### R6. Pattern fractions of the EPIC-pn CCF (`instrument.pattern_loss`)

Script `internal/ledger_tests/ccf_pn_pattern_fractions.py`. `EPN_QUANTUMEF_0018.CCF`, extension `FRACTION_ENERGY`,
Full Frame, first region: at 1 keV FRAC_S 0.712, FRAC_D 0.273, FRAC_T = FRAC_Q = 0, FRAC_SD = FRAC_SDTQ = 0.984;
at 0.3, 0.5, 2, 5, 8 keV FRAC_SD is 0.995, 0.993, 0.974, 0.954, 0.946. The table has no triple and quadruple
events, so `FRAC_SD / FRAC_SDTQ` = 1 at every energy and `apply_pattern_loss` returns 1.0000 in 0.5-2 keV for the

`apply_pattern_loss` docstring is FRAC_SD itself, not the ratio the code applies.

### R7. Matched-sampler kernel widths (S4, `method.matched_kernel_widths`)

Script `internal/ledger_tests/matched_kernel_widths.py`; inputs `campaigns/v4_max/targets_v4_max.csv` (9615 views) and the
packaged observation table; the production main draw (K' = 5, R = 12, dz_max 0.25, TNG-Cluster + TNG300, seed
20261008) is reproduced exactly (3111 draws, 2053 unique views, z D 0.036, mass D 0.026). Each arm is drawn with 20 seeds
(the seed orders the observations that are equally hard to match, so the spread over seeds is the production draw's own).
The pick of a halo-snapshot minimises `(dlogM / sigma_logm)^2 + (dz / sigma_z)^2`, which depends on the two widths only
through their ratio: halving `sigma_z` is the same draw as doubling `sigma_logm` (the runs agree to the last digit), so
two arms cover the four variations.

| arm | gate P (20 seeds) | z D (crit 0.069) | mass D (crit 0.067) | unique views | effective halo-snapshots | views drawn 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+ times |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| production (0.05, 0.05) | 20 PASS | 0.0375 +- 0.0009 | 0.0259 +- 0.0007 | 2050 | 545 | 1440 / 322 / 123 / 164 |
| sigma_z x0.5, or sigma_logm x2 | 20 PASS | 0.0346 | 0.0309 | 2061 | 544 | 1467 / 306 / 119 / 169 |
| sigma_z x2, or sigma_logm x0.5 | 20 PASS | 0.0443 | 0.0202 | 2068 | 553 | 1462 / 323 / 127 / 155 |

Result: no variation changes the gate P verdict (all 100 draws pass strictly) and the number of draws and the coverage
(0.9827) are the same. The KS distances move by 0.003 to 0.007, in opposite directions for z and mass (the width ratio
trades one against the other), which is 3 to 8 times the seed spread of the production arm (0.0009 and 0.0007): by the
pre-registered rule the widths are not immaterial, but the largest shift is a tenth of the critical distance.

### R9. Photon generator: the area and the spectral grid (S1 b and c, `sim.photon_sampling_numerics`)

Scripts `internal/ledger_tests/photon_area_assertion.py` and `photon_spectral_grid.py`.

## Part 2. Tests that need the node (specifications)

### S1. Photon-generator grid and area (`sim.phot_nbins`, `sim.area_cm2`)

* Parameter: `sim.phot_nbins` 5000; `sim.area_cm2` 4000.
* Variation: bins 1250, 2500, 5000 (production), 10000, 20000. Area: 4000 and 8000 at fixed bins.
* Inputs: the generation stage of 12 of the 43 D1 views (two per snapshot class: z about 0.1, 0.3, 0.6), same
  seeds, same donors; compare the products, not the fits.
* Metric: (a) the band counts of the five PPS bands in `image_merged.fits` against the 20000-bin arm, per view
  ratio; (b) the APEC emissivity integral over 0.5-2 keV of the binned table against a direct integration;
  (c) for the area, the assertion `area_cm2 >= max_E sum_cameras ARF(E)` for all 3 cameras on-axis and the number
  of photons rejected by it. Then refit the 12 views of the 1250-bin arm only if (a) exceeds the tolerance.
* Tolerance: counts per band within 0.5 % (Poisson 1 sigma at the faintest accepted view is about 1 %; a
  systematic below half of that is invisible in a fit) and the area test exact. The area cannot change a
  number if the inequality holds, because the draw is a thinning.
* Cost: generation only (12 views x 5 bin arms, the per-view generation time of the production campaign; not
  measured here). The assertion in (c) and the emissivity integral in (b) are login-node jobs and are done
  first.

### S2. Fit crop radius and block-centre convention (`fit.crop`)

* Parameter: `massfit.crop_r500` 3.0; `crop_cap_arcmin` None. (The block-centre convention is a geometric
  identity, see the ledger entry, and is not varied.)
* Variation: `crop_r500` 2.0, 2.5, 3 (production), 4; cap 13.5 arcmin (the G3 value).
* Inputs: the 43 D1 views, fit arms `crop2`, `crop2p5`, `crop4`, `cap13p5` with everything else as `v4s1`.
* Metric: the paired change of the median `ln_br_1`, the excluded count (`bg_fallback` rises when the crop is
  smaller than 2 R500: report it separately) and the median fit time.
* Tolerance: 0.015 (common). `crop2` is expected to be flagged by `bg_fallback` by construction (background
  annulus beyond 2 R500, the rule of `method.background_fallback_fov`), so its comparison is restricted to the
  views without the flag.
* Cost: 4 arms x 43 fits, 4 node-jobs of the standard size; the 4 R500 arm is the slowest (more pixels).

### S3. Point-source thresholds (`fit.point_source_thresholds`)

* Parameter: `massfit.source_det_ml_min` 10; `protect_core_arcsec` 90.
* Variation: DET_ML 6 (the 4XMM catalogue cut, Webb et al. 2020), 10 (production), 14 (their 5 sigma "clean"
  threshold); core protection 45, 90, 135 arcsec and 0.
* Inputs: the 43 D1 views; the SAS source lists already exist in the products, so each arm only changes the mask
  (except DET_ML 6: the lists stop at 10, the arm needs a new detection, see R10; run by the SENS job).
  The matching real-data comparison uses the 5XMM-DR15 fallback target set of `massfit.build_realfit_targets`
  (the same code path).
* Metric: median `ln_br_1`, `n_sources_masked`, and for the core protection the number of views that lose a
  central source (the BCG) at 45 or 0.
* Tolerance: 0.015. The same rule is applied to mocks and real data, so a bias would cancel in the real-to-mock
  comparison: the test reports that cancellation by repeating a real-data subset with DET_ML 6 and 14.
* Cost: 5 arms x 43 fits (the 90 arcsec arm is production); the core-protection arms can share the DET_ML 10
  fits for the views where no source lies within 135 arcsec (check from the source list first, login node).

### S4. Matched-sampler kernel widths (`method.matched_kernel_widths`)

* Parameter: `params:donors.matched.sigma_z` 0.05, `sigma_logm_dex` 0.05.
* Variation: x0.5 and x2 of each separately (0.025/0.1 in z, 0.025/0.1 dex), 4 variations plus production.
* Inputs: the full-snapshot maximal list (v4_max, 9615 views) and the observation table that the production
  `sample_matched` run used (K' = 5, R = 12).
* Metric: gate P of `validate_population` (the strict population-match statistics), the effective number of
  distinct halos, the reuse histogram, and the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance of the drawn (z, log M) against the
  reference sample.
* Tolerance: no variation changes the gate P verdict, and the KS distance changes by less than the production
  value's own bootstrap spread over 20 seeds. The widths decide who is drawn, not how a drawn halo is
  analysed, so no fit is needed.
* Cost: pure numpy on tables; each draw takes 30 s. RUN 2026-10-18 on the login node: R7.

### S5. Maximal-list donor assignment (`method.maximal_list_donors`)

* Parameter: `params:donors.max.n_nearest` 10, `cap_factor` 2.0.
* Variation: n_nearest 5, 10, 20; cap_factor 1.5, 2, 3 (3 is what the box runs use).
* Inputs: as S4 (`build_max_targets.assign_nearest_donors` on the same unit list).
* Metric: the donor-use histogram (maximum use), the median donor distance `donor_dist` and `donor_dz`, the
  number of distinct donors, and the share of views whose donor is farther than the 10th nearest.
* Tolerance: the median `donor_dist` changes by less than 10 % and no donor exceeds the cap; the quantity that
  matters for the claim is the donor's instrument and background, and the population gates of S4 already check
  its distribution.
* Cost: numpy, minutes; login node. RUN 2026-10-18: R8.

### S6. Seed constants of the Lx photometry (`photometry.legacy_apertures`: `bkg_annulus_aperture_frac`, `bkg_annulus_arcmin`)

* Where it acts: the Lx seed gives the first-guess mass and c200 (`fit.seed_lx`), and the first-guess R500 sets
  the crop (3 R500) and the fit pixel (R500 / 40 when coarser than 8 arcsec). No other result reads these
  constants.
* Variation: instead of changing the constants one by one, perturb their only channel: scale the seed R500 by
  x0.8 and x1.25 (the largest change of the seed Lx that either annulus can produce is smaller than that; check
  on the 43 views from the table, login node) on the D1 views.
* Metric and tolerance: common (0.015); also report the change of crop and pixel.
* Cost: 2 arms x 43 fits.

### S7. Baryon fraction of the matching mass (`donors.matching_mass`)

* Parameter: (1-b) of the PSZ2 scale, 0.78 (Planck 2015 XXIV).
* Variation: 0.84 (low end of the later calibrations) and 0.90.
* Inputs: the production observation table and `sample_matched`.
* Metric: the matched population (S4's metrics) and, for the 43 D1 views re-drawn, the shift of the median
  halo mass M500c; a refit is not needed, the claim depends on the population mass range only.
* Tolerance: the shift of the median matched mass is stated; no tolerance is pre-registered because the
  choice is a prior on the observations, not a numerical parameter.
* Cost: login node, minutes. Run in release 1.1.3: part 1, R12.

### S8. Isolating the deviations from Barnes et al. (2021) (`truth.star_forming_gas`, `truth.own_mu`)

* Variation: on the truth step alone, the headline `barnes21_nosf_fit_mu` against (i) the same fit with the
  star-forming cells kept (`barnes21_fit_mu`), (ii) the same fit with mu = 0.59 (`barnes21_nosf_fit`) and (iii)
  both, Barnes's estimator as published with the same fits (`barnes21_fit`).  All three are computed on request only
  (`compute_hse_truth(..., mu059_fit=True, s8_variants=True)`, `compute_hse_mass.S8_ESTIMATORS`); none is a column.
* Inputs: 24 TNG-Cluster halo-snapshots of the validation campaign (`campaign_v113`, 590 halo-snapshots), every
  24th in M500c rank (1.05e14-1.65e15 Msun, snapshots 50-99), the sphere read from the snapshot as the spherical
  truth stage reads it.  Script `internal/ledger_tests/s8_barnes_deviations.py`; login node, 4 workers, 9.5 minutes
  (outputs in the dev study folder `TODO_RUN/d-sims/s8`).
* Metric: `ln` of each variant over the headline, M_HSE(< R500c) / M500c, median and 16/84 percentiles.
* Result (2026-11-04):

  | variant | median | 16 / 84 per cent | range |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | (i) star-forming cells kept | -0.0003 | -0.0024 / -0.0001 | -0.045 / +0.004 |
  | (ii) mu = 0.59 | -0.0041 | -0.0044 / -0.0036 | -0.014 / +0.005 |
  | (iii) both (Barnes as published, fitted) | -0.0045 | -0.0066 / -0.0039 | -0.050 / +0.005 |

  Each deviation is below half a per cent in the median; the star-forming cut matters for single halos only (the
  -0.045 is a 2.6e14 Msun halo at z = 1).  The +0.024 of R5 is therefore the slope method of the `barnes21` column.
* Tolerance: the effects are reported, not judged; a run on every halo-snapshot is a node job if a referee asks.

### S9. Background seed and F5 (`fit.background_seed`)

Not a numerical parameter: PLAN_V41 decision 5 replaces the typical-EPIC seed by each observation's own QPB.
The test is the J1 F5 refit already planned (D1 views and the low-z real targets), with the same metric and
tolerance; the circularity of the typical-EPIC level is closed by that arm, not by a variation.

## Part 3. The SENS job (S1, S3, S6)

One node, `$TNGXMM_DEV/SENS/` (plan builder `scripts/build_sens.py`, analysis `scripts/analyse_sens.py`,
job `slurm/sens_vera.sh`), built only from package commands and the node queue:

| arm | what | views | fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ctl` v4c | the production fit `v4s1` repeated with the code under test (reproducibility of the reference) | 43 | 43 |
| `s3` ml14, pc0, pc45, pc135 | `--det-ml-min 14`; `--fitfix protect_core_as=0 / 45 / 135` | 43 | 172 |
| `s3b` ml6 | DET_ML 6 on lists re-detected with `likemin` 5, `mlmin` 6 (`sas-queue`, entry field `detect`) | 43 | 43 |
| `s6` r0p8, r1p25 | first-guess R500 of the Lx seed x0.8 and x1.25 (Lx scaled by f^(3 slope), core ratio kept: the mass seed moves by f^3, the c200 seed not at all) | 43 | 86 |
| `s1` p1250, p20000 | 12 views regenerated with the presets `v4_phot1250` / `v4_phot20000` (production's random streams), SAS-detected, fitted with v4 defaults | 12 | 24 |
| S1 products | the presets `v4_phot2500`, `v4_phot10000`, `v4_area8000` are generated and compared at the product level only | 12 | - |

All fits use the production v4 options of `v4s1` (preset v4, seed 1, `--mass-seed-lx lx_1mpc`, crop 3 R500, long chains),
each in its own work directory with its own copy of the settled rate cache. The analysis pairs every arm with the `v4s1` fits
of the same views (`tngxmm.analysis.tables.mock_table`; QC exclusion at the production fraction on either side) and reports
the median over views of the paired change of `ln_br_1` with a bootstrap 95 % interval over the views and the verdict
(PASS: interval inside +-0.015; MATTERS: entirely outside, with the size; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise). The two other re-seeds of
the same views (`v4s2`, `v4s3`) go through the same code as the null of the statistic.
