# tngxmm documentation

`tngxmm` builds mock XMM-Newton EPIC observations of simulated galaxy clusters from IllustrisTNG (TNG-Cluster, and the
TNG300, TNG100 and TNG50 boxes) and FLAMINGO, with SIMBA and EAGLE as internal validation runs
({doc}`simulations`), each one observed under the conditions of a real archival XMM observation, and
measures hydrostatic masses from the mocks and from the real observations with the same code. Its purpose is to
measure how the hydrostatic mass of an X-ray analysis departs from the true mass on simulated clusters, and to test
whether those mocks are close enough to real data for the comparison to transfer. The test has not been carried
out yet; the status of every validation, including the ones that fail, is on {doc}`validation`.

What it does now:

* generates the mocks reproducibly: one hashed configuration, deterministic random streams per view, every product
  stamped with the configuration it was made with ({doc}`overview`, {doc}`configuration`);
* traces every number of the configuration to a citation, a measurement or an instrument document, and lists the
  ones that have neither ({doc}`assumptions`);
* runs the same photometry, source detection rules and hydrostatic fit on mocks and on real observations
  ({doc}`definitions`);
* takes every background level from calibration data or from the observation's own measurement, with no factor
  measured on replays or fitted on donors ({doc}`background`).

What is not established yet ({doc}`validation`): the mass fit recovers idealised clusters to 3 per cent on clean data
but is about 15 per cent low with a realistic background; the replica sample is lighter than the real one; a
classifier still separates mocks from real observations (AUC 0.88-0.97 on image statistics); the pre-registered realism
evaluation on held-out donors has not been run; in the pilot of November 2026 the mock fits lie above the simulation's
own hydrostatic mass, and the cause is not established.

  (what runs without the TNG data and the cluster);
* the words and labels: {doc}`glossary`; the masses, radii and bias ratios: {doc}`definitions`;
* what the pipeline has shown and what it has not: {doc}`validation`, {doc}`samples`, {doc}`figures`;
* the simulations, the ingest stage and the TNG-Cluster L_X excess: {doc}`simulations`;
* the instrument background and the factors removed from it: {doc}`background`;
* running a stage: {doc}`PIPELINE` and {doc}`pipelines/index`;
* a production run from the target list to the fitted masses, and what to do when a job stops: {doc}`production`,
  {doc}`when-a-run-stops`;
