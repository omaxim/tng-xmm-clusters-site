# The assumptions ledger

Every assumption of the pipeline is an entry of `docs/assumptions/assumptions.yml`, traced to a published number or
method (`literature`: citation key, page, the quoted text), a decision or measurement of this project (`validation`),
an instrument document (`instrument`), or a convention with no physical effect.  An entry that has neither a citation
nor a validation is marked `open` and listed in the open items; nothing is justified that is not.

`tngxmm ledger check` verifies that every hashed spec field of the production presets is named in the `used_by` of at
least one entry and that every reference resolves.  The proofs (each quoted text boxed on its page of the cited
paper) are made by a tool in the private references area, which is not in the repository; `tngxmm ledger html`
builds the review page from the ledger and those proofs, one self-contained HTML file organised by pipeline and
node, with links to Kedro-Viz.

Beside the generated list below: {doc}`assumptions/OPEN_ITEMS` (what is unjustified or only partly justified) and
{doc}`assumptions/SENSITIVITY_TESTS` (the measured effect of the choices); the decisions behind the validations are
in {doc}`dev/DECISIONS`.
