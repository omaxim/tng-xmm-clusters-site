# Glossary

The terms, labels and version names used on this site and in the code, each defined once. The physical quantities
(masses, radii, bias ratios) are defined with their table columns in {doc}`definitions`.

## Samples and views

halo-snapshot
: One halo of one simulation at one snapshot (`sim`, `snap`, `halo_id`).

descriptor
: The YAML file that describes one simulation for the `ingest` stage (`src/tngxmm/ingest/descriptors/<name>.yml`):
  family (`tng`, `swift`, `gizmo`, `eagle`), cosmology, snapshots, halo catalogue and the conversion of its fields
  to canonical gas cells. A preset that names one (`sim.source`) carries its sha256 in the spec hash.

canonical cube
: The particles of one halo-snapshot inside a cube about the halo centre, in physical units and the field names
  of `tngxmm.ingest.schema`, whatever the simulation. FLAMINGO, SIMBA and EAGLE are generated from stored cubes;
  TNG converts its cells to the same form in memory (`runtime.cube_store`).

view
: One projection of a halo-snapshot along a simulation axis (`view` 0, 1, 2 = line of sight along the simulation z, y, x axes), observed as
  one mock XMM observation. `view_id` names it; the products live in `<tree>/<preset>/<sim>/<snap>/<halo>/view<v>/`.
  The pass-2 presets have six views per halo-snapshot, along the six icosahedral axes
  (`sim.projection = 'icosahedral6'`, `view` 0 to 5).

reference observation, donor
: One of the real XMM-Newton observations of SZ- or lensing-selected clusters (`obs.observations`, 808 rows). A view
  is matched to one of them and borrows its redshift and its observing conditions. "Donor" is the short name used in
  the code.

bank, added observations
: The bank is the 638 observations the table held until 2026-11-25 (`in_bank` True, `donors.reference_sample =
  'bank638'`). The 170 observations added on that day from an archive search have `in_bank` False and `sample` and
  `mass_class` = `extra`; a preset reads them only with `donors.reference_sample = 'bank638+extra'`
  (`v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass2_extra`). The hash of every other preset covers the bank rows only.

physics donor
: The reference observation a view replicates: it fixes the redshift z_obs, the matching mass and the real
  cluster the mock is compared with.

conditions donor
: The observation whose observing conditions (live times, filters, pn mode, exposure maps, background, N_H, roll,
  core offset) the instrument stage uses for a view. Since release 1.1.1 it is drawn among the N = 5 nearest
  observations of the physics donor in a space of observing conditions, with jitter of exposure, roll and pointing
  (`donors.observing_conditions`); before, it was the physics donor itself. The pass-1 preset uses the rule `donor`:
  the view's donor as it is, without neighbours or jitter.

pass 1, pass 2
: The two passes of the pairing on the fit mass (PREREGISTRATION section 6). Pass 1 observes projection 0 of every
  candidate halo-snapshot under one reference observation and fits it; that fit mass is the halo-snapshot's first
  matching mass. Pass 2 observes every halo-snapshot with a pass-1 fit along the six icosahedral axes, each view
  under a different observation of its window in redshift and fitted mass; the mass is the fitted hydrostatic mass
  on both sides (`tngxmm donors pair-projections`; with `--assignment least-used` a halo-snapshot takes the
  observations of its window that serve the fewest views so far). For FLAMINGO L1_m8 pass 1 is the list
  `campaigns/v4_flamingo_l1m8_pass1/` (5,001 views under observation 0762440401) and the preset
  `v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass1`; {doc}`production` follows it step by step. It has been generated and fitted with short
  chains. The pass-2 presets are `v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass2` and `v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass2_extra`; the pass-2 list with
  the 170 added observations is not built yet, because it needs their fits.

replica, K
: The K views matched to one reference observation (K = 5 in the replica list). `rep` numbers them.

target list
: A table with one row per view to generate (`campaigns/v4/targets_v4.csv`, the replica list of 2,840 views;
  `campaigns/v4_max/targets_v4_max.csv`, the maximal list; `campaigns/v4_box/`, the box list).

replica list, maximal list, matched draw (K')
: The replica list draws K = 5 TNG-Cluster views per reference observation, matched in mass and redshift. The
  maximal list uses every eligible halo-snapshot of TNG-Cluster and TNG300 with a donor each. A matched draw takes K'
  views per observation from the maximal list so that the mass distribution matches the real sample. See
  {doc}`samples`.

donor fold
: A group of reference observations used for cross-validation; an observation and every mock made under its
  conditions are in the same fold, so no model is tested on a cluster whose conditions it saw in training.

halo group, halo fold
: Every view of one physical halo, at every snapshot and along every line of sight: the split unit
  `zoom_target_id`, which is the TNG-Cluster zoom target and the z = 0 object of a halo of a TNG box. The FLAMINGO,
  SIMBA and EAGLE tables carry a snapshot's halo id there; such a table is refused until a column that follows the
  halo (FLAMINGO's HBT+ track) or halo-snapshot groups are asked for. A halo group sits in one halo fold and is one
  unit of the bootstrap
  (release 1.2, `tngxmm donors halo-folds`), so no model is tested on a halo it was trained on and the views of a
  halo are never resampled apart. `halo_id` is an index of one snapshot's catalogue and does not name a halo.

count matching, f
: The ratio f of a real cluster's net counts in 0.15-1 R500 to its replica's, reported by the realism evaluation as
  a measure of the luminosity difference. No production mock or frozen realism replay is thinned by it; a thinned
  replay is a diagnostic ({doc}`definitions`).

## The fit and a run

concentration prior
: The prior on the NFW concentration c200 of the hydrostatic fit (`massfit.cm_prior`). The default `dutton14` is
  log-normal about the c200(M200, z) relation of Dutton & Maccio (2014), 0.11 dex wide, evaluated at the M200 of the
  sampled halo, with c200 bounded at 0.5-20; `flat` is flat in ln c200 over 1-10 (Sanders et al. 2018). Ledger
  entry `fit.cm_prior`.

domain, domain prediction
: A fit is inside the domain of the measurement when it carries none of the four flags that are known before it
  runs: `centre` (the centre search was flagged), `bg_fallback` (an image has fewer than 50 exposed pixels beyond 2 R500:
  the cluster fills the field), `field_edge` (the first guess is limited by the field) and `no_seed` (no first
  guess). They come from the target and the geometry of the images, not from what the fit returns, so cutting on
  them cannot select on the result. Fits outside the domain are reported apart. `tngxmm massfit predict-domain`
  evaluates the flags before the fit; in pass 1 the prediction is a column of the fit targets and decides nothing.

fit target, fit plan, sweep
: A fit target is one row of the table the fitter reads: a view or an observation with its centre, redshift and
  first-guess mass. A fit plan is the file a fit job reads (`slurm/fits.sh`): one stream per line with its target
  table, image root, work directory, workers and rate cache. `tngxmm massfit fit-plan jobs` splits a long target
  table over several jobs and adds a last plan, the sweep, that lists every target.

done-marker
: The file that marks an item as finished: `obs_meta.json` of a view, `sas_detect/provenance.json` of a SAS
  detection, `provenance.json` of a fit. It is written last and atomically, and removed first when the item is made
  again, so an item cut by a kill or a full disk is never taken for a finished one ({doc}`when-a-run-stops`).

exit 28, `interrupted: disk`
: A stage driver stopped by a full disk or an exhausted quota prints one line starting with `DISK FULL:` and exits
  with code 28. The items it could not finish have the status `interrupted: disk`, are recorded in no table and are
  made again by the same command.

## Masses and the three curves

M500c, M_true
: The true total mass inside the radius where the mean density is 500 times the critical density, from the
  particles. "black" in older notes and in figure legends of the mass profiles.

M_HSE
: The intrinsic hydrostatic mass of the simulated gas, from its 3-D profiles (Barnes et al. 2021 estimator).
  "red" in older notes.

M_X
: The hydrostatic mass measured by the X-ray fit (mbproj2d) on an image. "blue" in older notes.

The colour names blue, red and black come from the mass-profile figures (`viz.plot_mock_truth`) and survive in column
names: `ln_br` = ln(M_X / M_HSE), `ln_bt` = ln(M_X / M_true), `ln_rt` = ln(M_HSE / M_true).

## Claims of the paper plan

claim A
: On real clusters, the X-ray fit reproduces published XMM hydrostatic masses (Lovisari et al. 2020), cluster by
  cluster, with the same mass definition.

claim B
: The ratio of the X-ray mass of real clusters to their lensing mass is consistent with M_X / M_true of the mocks
  (spherical-overdensity masses).

claim C
: On mocks, the bias of the X-ray method alone: M_X / M_HSE inside the true R500c (a fixed radius).

claim D
: A model trained on mock images predicts the masses of real clusters; tested on held-out donor folds. Not part of
  the first paper: the `ml` stage is not implemented, and claims A to C do not rest on it.

None of the four is established yet; {doc}`validation` gives the status of each.

## Gates and tests

gate
: A pass/fail rule fixed before the measurement it judges. A failed gate is reported, not relaxed silently; where a
  rule was changed after the data were seen, the page says so.

gate P (population)
: Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests of the views of a target list against the reference observations in matching mass,
  redshift, live times, N_H and theta500, with a critical distance from a bootstrap. The replica list fails it in mass
  (D = 0.241 against 0.061); see {doc}`samples`.

closure test, closure grid
: Idealised clusters in exact hydrostatic equilibrium (two profiles, three masses; two redshifts in the grid of 2026-10-17, three in the grid of the rules of 2026-11-04) put through the
  whole chain and fitted; M_fit / M_true measures the bias of the measurement itself. Data modes: `clean` (no sky,
  particle background or AGN), `agnonly`, `bgonly`, `real` (all three).

C2ST
: Classifier two-sample test: a classifier is trained to tell mock from real images; the cross-validated area under
  the ROC curve (AUC) is 0.5 when it cannot. Grouped by donor, with a donor bootstrap and a permutation null.

frozen evaluation
: The realism test on 84 held-out donors, with every setting registered before the run (`realism.frozen`); the
  code refuses a run that differs. Not run yet.

campaign check C1-C17
: Acceptance checks of a generated campaign tree (completeness, spec hash, offsets, products, ...), `campaign check`.

R1-R12, S1-S9
: Sensitivity tests of the assumption ledger: R are measurements made on the login node, S are tests that need new
  generations or fits ({doc}`assumptions/SENSITIVITY_TESTS`). They are unrelated to the rule labels R3, R4, R7, R8
  of the certification rules (`conf/base/parameters/certify.yml`).

## Versions and labels

The project uses one version number for the package and keeps older labels in file and key names. What each means:

| label | what it is |
|---|---|
| 1.0.0 ... 1.1.3 | package releases (`tngxmm.__version__`, `CHANGELOG.md`); the current one is in the page title |
| `v1`, `v1_tng300`, `v1_tng100`, `v1_tng50` | the TNG presets (`tngxmm.config.PRODUCTION_PRESETS`); `v1` (TNG-Cluster) is the production campaign |
| `v1_flamingo`, `v1_flamingo_l1m8`, `v1_simba`, `v1_eagle`, `v1_common`, `v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass1`, `v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass2`, `v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass2_extra` | the presets of the other simulations (`config.SOURCE_PRESETS`), of TNG-Cluster under the `common` emission, of pass 1 of the pairing on the fit mass (`config.PASS1_PRESETS`: one projection under one reference observation) and of pass 2 (`config.PASS2_PRESETS`: six projections, each under a paired observation); every preset is in `tngxmm.config.PRESETS`, one spec hash each |
| V3.2, V4, V4.1 | configurations before the package releases, listed as 3.2, 4.0 and 4.1.0 in `CHANGELOG.md` (V3.2: the earlier code base; V4: the first campaign of this package; V4.1: the configuration that release 1.0.0 froze) |
| `v4` in `campaigns/v4/`, `targets_v4.csv`, `sim.rng_campaign = 'v4'`, `obsid_style 'v4'`, `header_style 'v4'` | the target lists drawn for V4 and the random-stream key they fix; the v1 presets keep them so that the same views get the same random draws |
| `golden_v1`, `golden_v1.1`, `golden_v1.1.1` | the golden products of the fixture halo, named by the last release that regenerated them |
| DESIGN v3, PLAN_V41, CHECKS, D1, J1c | internal planning documents and study names of the development, not part of the repository; where a number comes from one of them, the page states the number and its configuration |
| spec hash, generation hash | sha256 of the preset (all hashed fields) and of the part that determines the generated products (without the fit settings), stamped on every product |
| seed hash | hash of the seed procedure of the fit (its code, the seed sections of the preset and the tables it reads); the real seed table and the guarded centres are read only when they carry the current one |
