# What the pipeline does and produces

The pipeline tests whether a hydrostatic mass measured from an XMM-Newton image of a *simulated* cluster scatters
and is biased in the way the same measurement is on a *real* cluster. Every mock is therefore observed like a real
observation, measured like a real observation, and compared with the masses the simulation knows exactly.

```{image} figures/pipeline_overview.png
:width: 100%
```

## The data flow

```text
snapshots and halo catalogues ──ingest──> gas cells of each halo in one canonical form (TNG, FLAMINGO, SIMBA, EAGLE)
              ──sim──> photons of each projection (three along the simulation axes in `v1`, six icosahedral
                       axes in pass 2; pyXSIM / SOXS, APEC), truth profiles
real observations ──obs, donors──> which real XMM observation each view replicates (physics donor) and is
                                   observed under (conditions donor)
              ──instrument──> EPIC pn and MOS images under the conditions donor's observation: exposure maps,
                              vignetting, PSF, particle and sky background, field AGN, out-of-time events;
                              SAS source detection
              ──photometry──> L_X in apertures, centres, fixed-aperture fluxes
              ──massfit──> mbproj2d hydrostatic M500 of mock views and of the real observations (the same code)
              ──viz──> figures of fits and of mocks against their simulation
              ──bank──> image renders of mocks and real clusters ──embed──> DINOv3 embeddings
extra pipelines: donor_tables (the per-donor tables of the data package), validation (the closure grid),
                 analysis (fit tables), certify (pre-registered rules), realism (the classifier test of mocks
                 against real observations), setup (the downloads of a machine away from the cluster)
```

A machine-learning stage that estimates masses from the embeddings (a conditional invertible network, `ml`) is
planned and not implemented: the stage name exists in the package, no code or pipeline does. It is outside the scope
of the first paper, whose claims (the real fits against published masses, the mock and real mass ratios, and the bias
of the method on mocks) rest on the fits alone.

Only `ingest` reads a simulation's own files; every later stage receives gas cells and halo rows in the same
canonical form, whatever the simulation, and only the cosmology of the generation follows the simulation. TNG-Cluster
and FLAMINGO L1_m8 are the production pair; TNG300 is the volume-limited comparison sample for TNG-Cluster, TNG100
and TNG50 test resolution, and SIMBA and EAGLE are validation runs.

The stages and their order (`tngxmm.STAGES`) are the Kedro namespaces, the CLI groups, the catalog fragments
(`conf/base/catalog/<stage>.yml`) and the citation fragments (`citations/`). {doc}`pipelines/index` lists the
nodes of every pipeline, generated from the registry.

## What it produces

For every view (one projection of one halo-snapshot, observed as one real observation; {doc}`glossary`):

* the photon list and the absorbed events (not kept by a preset that sets `runtime.drop_events`, such as the pass-1
  preset); per camera and PPS band the image and exposure map; a merged 0.5-2 keV
  image and its coverage; the list of field AGN; the SAS source list; `obs_meta.json` with the provenance and the
  observing conditions;
* for the TNG presets, a temet-exact intrinsic surface-brightness map, which reproduces the official TNG-Cluster
  X-ray products;
* one row of the results table: the halo, the redshifts, L_X measured on the image and intrinsic, temperatures, and
  the true and intrinsic hydrostatic masses ({doc}`definitions`);
* per halo-snapshot, the 3-D truth profiles behind the masses.

Then, on request: hydrostatic fits of mocks and of the real observations, the closure grid, the realism evaluation and
the image bank with its embeddings. A mock beside the real observation it replicates (golden fixture products of the
current configuration):


The figures of the current results, with their sources and caveats, are on {doc}`figures`; what the results do and
do not show is on {doc}`validation`.

## Principles of the code

* Every number is a field of `tngxmm.config.CampaignSpec` with value, unit, reading module and citation
  ({doc}`configuration`); a preset is the whole physics of a run and its sha256 is stamped on every product.
  `campaign check` (C1-C17) refuses products of another spec.
* Mocks and real data go through the same code: the photometry, centring, source masking and fitting functions do
  not know whether an image is simulated.
* Each function that uses a published method or code carries `@cites(...)`; `CREDITS.md`, the
  bibliography (`tngxmm cite`) and the Kedro node tags read the keys. Every assumption is traced to a citation, a
  measurement or an instrument document, or listed as open ({doc}`assumptions`).
* Each view has random streams keyed by (campaign, snapshot, halo, projection, replica, purpose)
  through sha256 (`tngxmm.core.rng`), so a rerun, a different number of workers or a Kedro run write the same bytes.

Units and conventions: {doc}`definitions`.
