# Validation status

What has been tested, against which rule, with which result, and on which configuration. The mock-making and the
provenance are checked in detail; the three tests a reader needs before trusting a mock hydrostatic bias are not
passed. The mass fit is biased low by about 15 per cent once a realistic background is
present, the replica sample does not match the real sample in mass, and a classifier separates mocks from real
observations. The pre-registered realism evaluation has not been run. Terms: {doc}`glossary`; quantities:
{doc}`definitions`; figures: {doc}`figures`.

Every number below names the configuration it was measured on. Most come from release 1.1.1 (`198b80e9d4563eec`)
or older, and the last section lists which. None was measured on the configuration of the integration of
2026-10-30 with the pn out-of-time energies from the CTI calibration (spec hash `12641be3938b849d` of preset `v1`:
`full` emission abundances, the corner QPB level of every camera, the pn Cu-line gap, no RGA dipole, the
out-of-time energies from EPN_CTI instead of the removed factor; {doc}`background`).

The configuration has changed further since ({doc}`changelog`): the MOS vignetting carries the RGA obscuration of
the calibration since 2026-11-03 (`instrument.mos_rga_vignetting = 'ccf'`), and the spec hash of `v1` is
`c2e6b282f6aab356` since 2026-11-12. Two measurements exist on the newer configurations: the closure grid of
2026-11-10 and the pilot of 60 real and 60 mock fits. They are in the section "The closure grid and the pilot of
November 2026". No other number on this page has been measured again. Pass 1 of the pairing on the fit mass (5,001
FLAMINGO L1_m8 views) has been generated and fitted since; its fits use short chains and serve the pairing
({doc}`production`), and no number of this page comes from them.

## The closure test

Idealised clusters in exact hydrostatic equilibrium (cool-core and non-cool-core profiles; M500 = 2, 4, 8e14 Msun;
z = 0.1 and 0.6) are put through the whole chain and fitted with the production fit (the cell's own SAS source
list, arm `v4sas`). Data modes: `clean` (cluster only), `agnonly`, `bgonly` (sky and particle background) and `real`
(all of them). 120 fits, 2026-10-17, figure "closure":

| data mode | median M_fit / M_true | fits |
|---|---|---|
| clean, background fixed in the model | 0.970 | 24 |
| clean, background free in the model | 1.032 | 24 |
| background only | 0.921 | 24 |
| AGN only | 0.779 | 24 |
| real (background and AGN) | 0.845 | 24 |

* The rule registered in `PIPELINE.md` before the grid was run, 1.00 +- 0.03 in every cell, fails in every data mode.
  After the results were seen the gate was moved to the clean cells and to the catastrophic tail of the real cells
  (the certification rules `R4_clean` and `R4_real_tail`); the change is recorded with its date in the decision log
  ("Fit crop stays 3 R500; closure background bias carried as a measured calibration").
* The deficit of the real cells is not the fit crop (0.845 / 0.854 / 0.845 at crops of 3 / 5 / 8 R500), the spatial
  shape of the background, the SAS detection, the AGN light or the optimiser; it needs the sky and particle
  background. The AGN-only cells are lower still, which points at the background model of the fit when the data
  hold no background (the background-free fit of the clean cells gives 1.032).
* The deficit is a measured property of the method. It is not subtracted from any mock or real result on this
  site. Its dependence on redshift, mass, profile and S/N is in the figure; with 24 fits per mode it is not
  measured well enough to be a calibration. An M_X / M_true of a mock is therefore the product of the hydrostatic
  bias of the gas and a method bias of about 10-15 per cent that depends on the background.
* The grid was run before the release 1.1 instrument changes (donor background components, footprint, Galactic
  column). It has to be rerun on the current configuration (`slurm/closure_grid.sh`, about 3 node-hours).
* The rerun (STATUS B1) is judged by DESIGN v4, written on 2026-11-04 before any of its fits
  ({doc}`dev/PREREGISTRATION`): the clean cells gate the fitter (pooled median within 0.95-1.05, every redshift within
  0.90-1.10), the real cells gate the catastrophic tail (at most 5 per cent outside 0.67-1.5), and the real-cell median
  (within 0.92-1.08, every redshift within 0.85-1.15, DESIGN v2's rule) decides whether a mock M_X / M_true may be
  quoted as a hydrostatic bias without the method term. Every study arm is judged by one paired rule at 0.03 in ln M.
  The per-cell rule above is kept as a report.

## The closure grid and the pilot of November 2026

Two runs on the configurations after the integration. Both used the flat concentration prior; the prior was changed
afterwards (below). The fits of pass 1 ran under the new prior.

The closure grid (job 1896730, 2026-11-10; gate arm with the model averaged over the pixel). Under the rule
registered on 2026-11-04 the grid fails: the pooled ratio of the usable clean fits is 1.046 (27 usable of 54,
interval about 1.010 to 1.084; the 54 ok fits give 1.015), and the real-background cells give 0.89. The rule was not
changed. On 2026-11-11 the author decided, knowing this result, that the grid is reported as the measured error of
the method on clusters in exact equilibrium and no longer gates the freeze ({doc}`dev/PREREGISTRATION`, section 1).
Nothing is subtracted for it.

The pilot ({doc}`dev/PILOT`): 60 real observations with a hydrostatic M500 of Lovisari et al. (2020) and 60 FLAMINGO
L1_m8 views, fitted with the configuration that is to be frozen, quick chains.

* A real fit is read by the tension between the fit's 68 per cent interval and the published one, with the
  published mass and its errors carried to our cosmology, not by a percentage band about 1. Since the amendment of
  2026-11-11 three things are read together: the common offset inside the domain, the scatter against the scatter
  between published analyses of the same clusters, and every cluster beyond 3 sigma traced and listed.
* About half of each list lies outside the domain of the fit (a flag known before the fit: the centre,
  a cluster that fills the field, a first guess limited by the field or missing). Those fits are reported apart.
* Real fits inside the domain and without a flag from the fit's own result: 22 fits, median M_fit / M_published
  1.020 (0.878 to 1.185), scatter 0.32 in ln, 9 beyond 2 sigma and 5 beyond 3 sigma. With every ok fit inside the
  domain: 39 fits, median 0.978, intervals overlapping for 14, 19 beyond 2 sigma and 12 beyond 3 sigma. The size of
  the scatter between published analyses has not been entered in the ledger yet, so the pilot's scatter has no
  verdict. The fit intervals come from quick chains and may be too narrow; a pull is then too large.
* One cluster was traced. MACS J0717.5+3745 (pull -10.7) had been fitted without a particle background because its
  templates were empty. After the fix it gives 11.87 (11.28-12.42) e14 Msun against 12.91 (12.22-13.46), pull -1.2.
  The counts above still contain the old fit.
* The mock fits lie above the simulation's own hydrostatic mass, where the reading asks for a ratio of 1. The
  cause is not established ({doc}`dev/STATUS`, B15), so no M_fit / M_HSE or M_fit / M_true of the pilot is quoted
  here as a bias.
* 18 of the 67 in-domain fits ended at a bound of the flat concentration prior. On 2026-11-12 the prior
  became the Dutton & Maccio (2014) relation (ledger `fit.cm_prior`). The fits that were not at a bound move by a
  median of 0.3 per cent (real) and 0.0 per cent (mock) when their chains are weighted by the new prior, at most 10
  per cent; the 18 fits at a bound are not refitted.

The freeze of the configuration is judged on the pilot. The pilot is not the science result: 60 views of one
projection are too few for a bias to two digits, and its views were not paired on the fit mass. Pass 1 of that
pairing has been run since; the pass-2 list with the 170 added observations is not built yet ({doc}`samples`).

## Hydrostatic bias of the mocks (preview)

Two measurements exist; neither is on the current configuration and neither is a result yet.

* The intrinsic hydrostatic bias of the whole population (no X-ray fit, figure "hse_bias_truth"): M_HSE(< R500c) /
  M500c has a median of 0.883 (16-84 per cent 0.781-1.008) over 2,460 TNG-Cluster halo-snapshots and 0.914 over 742
  TNG300 halo-snapshots, nearly flat in mass and redshift (v1.0.1 trees; the truth stage has not changed since).
* X-ray fits of 43 mock views (the development sample "D1", V4 configuration, figure "hse_bias_fits"): at the
  true R500c, M_X / M_true = 0.825, M_HSE / M_true = 0.866, M_X / M_HSE = 0.847 (medians of all 43). Without the 12
  fits flagged by the QC rules the medians are 0.835, 0.881 and 0.931. The QC flags (posterior at a prior edge,
  runaway outer pressure, centre, background fallback) select on the outcome, and the method-bias median moves by 8
  per cent when they are applied, so a result has to be given with and without them. Three re-seeds of the same
  views give ln(M_X / M_HSE) medians of -0.072, -0.064 and -0.089 without the flagged fits (a 7-8 per cent method
  bias).
* Three numbers on this page measure the bias of the method, and they are not the same quantity: the closure ratio
  M_fit / M_true of idealised clusters with a realistic background (0.845; exact hydrostatic equilibrium, smooth
  gas), and M_X / M_HSE of TNG mocks with every fit (0.847) or without the QC-flagged fits (0.931), where M_HSE is
  itself an estimate from the 3-D profiles. None is adopted as a correction: the closure grid has to be rerun on the
  current configuration first, and the QC choice has to be fixed before the result is seen. The closure ratio is the
  method bias without clumping and non-thermal pressure; the mock ratio M_X / M_HSE is the one the paper's claim C
  refers to. What the mocks represent of the physics behind the bias (thermal against total pressure, clumping,
  temperature weighting, the absorbing column, the epoch) is listed in {doc}`definitions` ("Scope of the bias
  measurement").

See {doc}`samples`. The replica list fails gate P in mass (the mocks are lighter) and in coverage. Population

## Realism

A classifier can tell a mock from the real observation it replicates. The rule
AUC <= 0.75 was proposed before any machine-learning result on real clusters (`method.realism_domain_gap`); it is a
requirement for claim D, not for the hydrostatic bias. Claim D is not part of the first paper; there the realism tests
are reported as a description of how far the mocks are from the real observations.

| sample, configuration | statistics (logistic / boosting) | DINOv3 PCA-2 / 8 / 32 |
|---|---|---|
| 195 donors, v1.0.1 products as stored | 0.993 / 0.999 | 0.973 / 0.998 / 1.000 |
| 195 donors, count-matched | 0.985 / 0.998 | |
| 195 donors, instrument fixes of 2026-10-20 | 0.796 / 0.958 | 0.512 / 0.773 / 0.918 |
| 465 donors, round 3 (2026-10-21) | 0.777 / 0.865 | 0.540 / 0.765 / 0.913 |
| 194 donors, conditions donor, before the final 1.1.1 instrument | 0.883 / 0.970 | |

Real against real (`internal/ledger_tests/realism_real_null.py`): two disjoint random halves of the real
observations of the development donors, split by cluster and compared with the same grouped test on the same
statistics, give AUC 0.49 +- 0.03 (logistic) and 0.49 +- 0.04 (boosting) at 194 against 194 over 20 splits, with a 95th
percentile of 0.53-0.56 (also at 242 against 242). The mock-against-real AUCs above are therefore far outside what two
real samples of this size give.

The samples differ between rows, so the rows are not a sequence of improvements on one set. What drives the
separation (round 3, 465 donors; figure "realism"):

* background features: 0.718 / 0.796; gas features alone (surface-brightness shape and band ratios): 0.664 / 0.677;
  counts, point sources and footprint: 0.53-0.57;
* DINOv3 on the cluster region alone 0.560 / 0.691 / 0.825, on the field alone 0.502 / 0.615 / 0.791;
* single statistics (194 donors): the exposed fraction of the field (AUC 0.28, mocks lower), the background ring ratio
  (0.68), the number of compact sources (0.36, mocks fewer), the hardness of the source (0.37) and of the background
  (0.62).

So the separation comes from both sides. The background and field statistics point at the instrument and sky model
(the fixed sky foreground, `instrument.sky_background`; the pn soft background, which is not measured per donor). The
gas statistics point at TNG itself: TNG-Cluster halos are brighter than real clusters of the same mass (next
section), and the band ratio of a mock relative to its donor grows as 0.46 ln(T_sl,mock / T_donor) (465 donors,
17 sigma; `instrument.galactic_column_total`), i.e. the mock clusters are hotter than the real clusters they are
matched to. These are properties of the simulation, not of the instrument model.

The frozen evaluation (84 held-out donors never used to tune the instrument stage, every setting registered in
`realism.frozen`, code that refuses a deviating run; {doc}`realism`) has not been run. It needs one node
(`slurm/realism_frozen.sh`).

## The luminosity of TNG-Cluster

At fixed true M500c (5e14 Msun, z = 0.2) the soft-band luminosity of TNG-Cluster is +0.28 dex above observed
relations (Bulbul et al. 2019 +0.41, Lovisari et al. 2020 +0.28, Pratt et al. 2009 +0.20; the references differ by
+-0.1 dex among themselves; each 0.1 of hydrostatic bias in the observed masses adds about +0.09 dex). About 70 per
cent is explained by the gas fraction, clumping, the density-profile shape and the cool-core mix (+0.19 +- 0.06 stat
+- 0.07 lensing-mass systematic); the remainder, +0.09 +- 0.09 dex, is consistent with zero. The pipeline is ruled
out as the cause (its intrinsic L_X matches temet's to +0.0095 dex). The excess is reported as a property of the
simulation and is not calibrated away. Figure "lx_mass" shows the measured L_X of the mocks and of the real
observations (+0.31 dex at 4-7e14 Msun, with the true mass on one side and the catalogue mass on the other). In the
median a measured mock L_X is below the intrinsic one: by 0.077 dex inside R500 and 0.071 dex in L_X(1 Mpc) (6,634
and 6,967 TNG-Cluster views of the v1.0.1 trees, an earlier configuration; 0.052 dex in L_X(1 Mpc) on the 193
TNG-Cluster views of the L_X gap test), though not in every aperture and sample ({doc}`definitions`, L_X). The next
section gives its steps. Whether it cancels between mocks and real data is not measured.

FLAMINGO L1_m8, put through the same mock pipeline in 193 pairs matched in true mass, donor, redshift and line of
sight, is 0.33 dex fainter than TNG-Cluster in L_X(1 Mpc) (0.35 dex intrinsic; the estimator reads FLAMINGO 0.041 dex
and TNG-Cluster 0.052 dex below their intrinsic L_X(1 Mpc), medians over the 193 pairs) and sits on the real pool
(-0.04 dex on true mass, +0.03 on its 3-D hydrostatic mass), while TNG-Cluster sits +0.31 and +0.41 dex above it. An
independent recomputation (-0.36 dex) and a catalogue-level comparison of temet and SOAP luminosities (-0.42 dex)
confirm the difference; about 60 per cent of it is the gas fraction. No step of the pipeline raises the TNG-Cluster
luminosities by more than 0.022 dex. The pipeline therefore does not create the excess ({doc}`simulations`).

Consequence for the mass fits: a mock has about twice the net counts of its real counterpart (the count-matching
factor has a median of 0.54), and no mock is thinned. The S/N of a mock fit (QC exclusions, prior-edge flags)
therefore differs from that of the real fit of the same observation; the priors themselves are the same at every
signal level (the concentration-mass relation for c200, flat for the metallicity).

## The L_X estimator against the intrinsic luminosity

The measured L_X of a mock is the net result of every step between the gas and the number in the table: the count
image (vignetting, chip gaps and inactive CCDs, PSF, pn out-of-time events), the background from the annulus at
1.1-1.6 aperture radii, the on-axis live time t_eff and the ECF (on-axis response with the Medium filter for every
camera, APEC at T_vir and Z = 0.3, TBabs on the angr abundances, a table that stops at z = 1). Each step was measured
on its own for L_X(1 Mpc) of the TNG-Cluster views of the v1.0.1 trees (`internal/ledger_tests/lx_truth_ratio_terms.py`):
the steps of the image on 135 views (15 per snapshot) from their photon lists and exposure maps, the ECF steps from the
ECF calculation itself (`tngxmm.instrument.compute_ecf`). Each entry is the median over the 135 views of log10 of the
luminosity with the step against without it.

| step | median (dex) | 16-84 per cent |
|---|---|---|
| gas beyond +-R200c along the line of sight (photons inside 1 Mpc against the intrinsic cylinder) | -0.000 | -0.002 to +0.002 |
| cluster light in the background annulus | -0.003 | -0.012 to -0.000 |
| exposure map: vignetting, chip gaps and inactive CCDs, against the on-axis live time | -0.059 | -0.093 to -0.035 |
| PSF: light scattered out of the aperture and into the annulus (on-axis King profile of each camera) | -0.018 | -0.025 to -0.006 |
| pn out-of-time events moved along their readout column | -0.005 | -0.012 to -0.002 |
| ECF temperature: T_vir of the halo mass against the emission-weighted T_ew of the view | -0.003 | -0.012 to +0.001 |
| ECF beyond z = 1, where the table is clamped (the 448 views at z_obs > 1 of all 6,967) | +0.002 | -0.002 to +0.009 |
| ECF absorption: the mock photons absorbed with TBabs on lodd, the ECF on angr, at the same column | +0.011 | +0.006 to +0.022 |
| ECF response: the donor's filter and exposure of each camera, against Medium at equal exposure | +0.007 | -0.000 to +0.010 |
| sum of the steps | -0.078 | -0.120 to -0.051 |
| measured on the same 135 views | -0.079 | -0.139 to -0.045 |
| not explained (measured minus the sum, view by view) | -0.001 | -0.039 to +0.019 |

* Most of the ratio comes from the image and not from the background estimate: the exposure map, the PSF and the
  out-of-time events applied together give -0.085 dex (16-84 per cent -0.117 to -0.060). The ECF leaves these losses out on
  purpose, as losses the real images share (the provenance record of `ecf_table_v2.npz`). The cluster light in the
  annulus is -0.003 dex on the photons and -0.005 dex over all 6,967 views from the intrinsic cylinders (the median
  light at 1-1.5 Mpc is 1.9 per cent of that inside 1 Mpc).
* What is not explained depends on redshift. The remainder is +0.008 and +0.003 dex at z_obs below 0.2 and 0.2-0.5
  (43 and 35 views) but -0.022 and -0.030 dex at 0.5-0.8 and above 0.8 (25 and 32 views). Of the deeper ratio above
  z_obs = 0.8 (-0.112 dex over 851 views), the PSF (-0.025 dex in the sample there) and the ECF temperature (-0.008
  dex over all 851) explain part; the rest is not explained.
* The 65 views with the Thick filter on every camera have a ratio of -0.173 dex, of which the
  response step gives -0.078; the 2,001 views with Medium on every camera have -0.077 dex.
* From Z = 0.3 to 0.1 the metallicity of the ECF's spectrum changes the ECF by 0.2 per cent (+0.001 dex), so it is
  not a term.
* Some steps differ between a mock and the real observation it replicates. In the campaign columns the ECF
  temperature is T_vir of the halo's true mass; for a real cluster it is T_vir of an estimated mass (the catalogue
  mass, or the first guess of the seed procedure); the real-side step is not measured. The mock photons are absorbed
  with TBabs on lodd at the HI4PI column; modelled as TBabs on the ISM abundances (wilm) at the total column N_HI + 2
  N_H2 (Willingale et al. 2013), the same step for the donor observations is +0.006 dex, so the mock exceeds its
  donor by +0.003 dex in this step (16-84 per cent +0.001 to +0.011). The other steps are the same in kind on both
  sides, but their size depends on the cluster's profile and position on the detector. Whether the net ratio cancels
  between mocks and real data is not measured.
* The table holds for the v1.0.1 configuration only (since release 1.1 the mocks are absorbed at the total column
  and the instrument stage has changed) and for L_X(1 Mpc) only (inside R500 the same steps act, but were not
  measured one by one).

FLAMINGO, SIMBA and EAGLE go through the same graph after the `ingest` stage. Measured so far:

* Ingest: every gas particle that SIMBA's original snapshots and the TNG-format conversion share is
  bit-identical (two halos); the originals add the decoupled wind particles, which do not emit either way. EAGLE
  agrees with its conversion to rounding; its electron abundance of full ionisation changes L_X by +0.0027 dex
  (one halo). Campaign checks C1-C17 passed on the fixture halos of FLAMINGO L1_m10 and L1_m8, and of SIMBA and
  EAGLE as read from the conversions.
* Emission: our L_X(0.5-2 keV, R500c) of FLAMINGO L1_m8 (legacy emission) is +0.028 dex (16-84 per cent +0.018,
  +0.040) above the sum of FLAMINGO's own per-particle luminosities (20 clusters, z = 0.2).
* Luminosity through the mock pipeline (199 pairs of TNG-Cluster and FLAMINGO L1_m8 views at matched true M500c,
  each pair under the same donor, z = 0.1-0.5): the median log10 of L_FLAMINGO / L_TNG-Cluster is -0.325 [95 per
  cent: -0.375, -0.292] for L_X(1 Mpc) measured on the images, -0.351 for the intrinsic L_X in R500c, and grows in
  size with mass (-0.24 at 1-2e14 Msun, -0.39 above 4e14 Msun); `full` emission on both sides. Indicative only:
  against the median real L_X(1 Mpc) of the observations within 0.1 dex in catalogue M500 and 0.1 in z, with the
  mocks at their true M500c, TNG-Cluster lies +0.31 dex and FLAMINGO L1_m8 -0.04 dex from the real data (the real
  masses are mostly SZ-based catalogue masses; no conversion between the definitions is applied).
* Resolution: FLAMINGO L1_m10 represents the integrated quantities (luminosity, spectral temperature, hydrostatic
  truth) and image statistics at the scale of R500c above about 2e14 Msun, but not structure below about 100 kpc.
  L1_m8 (gas particles of 1.3e8 Msun) is the production FLAMINGO source.

Of these simulations only FLAMINGO L1_m8 has hydrostatic fits: the 60 views of the pilot and the 5,001 views of
pass 1 (short chains). No fit of SIMBA or EAGLE has been run.
