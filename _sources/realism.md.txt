# The realism evaluation and frozen mode

A hydrostatic mass from a mock behaves like one from a real cluster only if the mock looks like the real
observation.  The `realism` pipeline measures this: for a real donor observation and the mock replicas observed
under its conditions, classifier two-sample tests measure how well the two can be told apart.  An AUC near 0.5 means they
cannot.  The pipeline is not part of `__default__`.

## Results so far

The mocks are not yet indistinguishable from the real observations. On the development donors a classifier on 17
image statistics separates them with AUC 0.88 (logistic regression) to 0.97 (gradient boosting), and one on DINOv3
embeddings with AUC 0.91; the gate proposed before any real-cluster machine-learning result is AUC <= 0.75. The
separation comes from the background and field statistics and from the gas itself (TNG-Cluster halos are brighter and
hotter than the real clusters they are matched to). Every number so far is of a development configuration, measured on
donors that were used to develop the instrument model; the frozen evaluation on 84 untouched donors, which measures the
current configuration, has not been run. Numbers, samples and the drivers of the separation: {doc}`validation`; figure:
{doc}`figures` (realism).

This page is therefore the description of the test, not of its result.

## What is compared

* Real side: the donor's own PPS images and exposure maps.
* Mock side: the replica views of the donor (`donors.targets`), re-observed by the instrument stage
  (`instrument.replay_views`, environment `realism`) under the preset's observing conditions, with the luminosity of
  the simulation's gas, as the production mocks. The count-matching factor f of each view (the real net counts in
  0.15-1 R500 over the replica's, `realism.measure_count_matching`; median 0.54 for TNG-Cluster) is reported, not
  applied: thinning a replica to the real counts would hide the simulation's luminosity excess from the classifier
  with a factor measured on the data. A replay thinned by f exists as a diagnostic (`instrument.replay.thin_table`)
  and the frozen evaluation refuses it.
* Tests: a classifier on image statistics (`tngxmm.realism.features`, `tngxmm.realism.c2st`: all features, each
  group alone and without it, a univariate table) and one on DINOv3 embeddings (`tngxmm.realism.dino`), each with a
  donor-bootstrap standard error and a permutation null.

The pipeline page is {doc}`pipelines/realism`.  The step order (the steps share files, so they are separate runs),
the commands and the environment each step needs (scikit-learn and torch AND Kedro in one interpreter for the DINO and
classifier nodes) are in the section "The realism evaluation" of {doc}`dev/KEDRO`; `slurm/realism_frozen.sh` runs the
chain on one node.

## The donor split

`realism.build_split` sorts every donor into `development` (donors on whose mocks a change of the instrument stage was
designed or tuned), `fresh` (evaluated by the independent audit, not used for tuning), `earlier` (appear only in earlier
evaluation runs), `heldout` (every other donor with at least `min_replicas` replica views: the donors of the frozen
evaluation) and `ineligible`.  The split is written once: the dataset that stores it
(`tngxmm.core.datasets.FrozenReportDataset`) refuses to replace a stored split by a different one, and the hash of its
rows goes into every report.

The registered split has 84 held-out donors; the sha256 of its rows is
`499e3e00581e7843ac74dc35572bcda996f9baffc38508905456a3a014e0066d` (`realism.frozen.n_heldout`, `split_sha256`).
It had 85 until 2026-11-12, when three observations were excluded from the donors because SAS cannot build a
response for them: 0780291001 (MOS2 in a fast mode with the cluster on its central CCD), which was a held-out donor
with five replica views, and 0747420101 and 0161560101 (the cluster outside or at the edge of the field), which had
no replica views and no role in the split. The author signed this split off on 2026-11-12, before any frozen run.

## Modes

`params:realism.mode` is `development` (the default) or `frozen`.  In `frozen` mode the run is checked against the
pre-registered section `realism.frozen` of the parameters before the selection and the count matching compute anything,
and again, with the replay record, before the report: the configuration, the preset and its spec hash, the conditions
rule (`donors.observing_conditions`) and the donor set must be the registered ones, and only the held-out donors are
evaluated.  What may differ in a frozen run is the mode itself, the registration, paths and numbers of processes
(`tngxmm.realism.report.FROZEN_MAY_VARY`); everything else is part of the registered configuration, identified by its
sha256 (`frozen_config_sha256`).  A mismatch raises an error listing the differences
(`tngxmm.realism.report.frozen_mismatches`, `require_frozen`).

The report (`realism.c2st_report`, JSON and Markdown) states the donors and views, the replicas per donor, the
count-matching factors, every AUC with its uncertainty, the list of configurations tried (`variants_tried`), the
preset with its spec hash, the commit, and that every view is of a full snapshot.
