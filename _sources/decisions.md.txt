# Decision log

One line per decision: the date, the decision, the reason in one line and the section of the long record,
{doc}`dev/DECISIONS`, that holds the reasoning and the measurements. The history of each version is in the {doc}`changelog`.
The decisions are the author's; a line does not replace its section.

## Gas geometry

| date | decision | reason | record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-22 | The cutout gas loader: the mock gas is the stored cutout of the halo (`sim.loader = 'cutout'`), the same for every simulation | one gas definition for TNG-Cluster, TNG300, TNG100 and TNG50, read from stored, checksummed index sets | [Geometry of the mock gas](dev/DECISIONS.md#geometry-of-the-mock-gas-authors-decisions-of-2026-10-22-and-2026-10-24) |
| 2026-10-24 | Box cutouts: all the gas in the cube of half-side 4 R500c about GroupPos; the emitting volume is the cylinder inscribed in the cube (radius and half-depth 4 R500c) | 4 R500c = 1.64 Mpc is the smallest round multiple that covers the 1.5 Mpc aperture of the smallest halo (R500c 0.41 Mpc); the cylinder has a circular edge and the same depth at every radius | same section |
| 2026-10-24 | Convergence result: inside 4 R500c the cylinder gives the same as the cube; the core criterion passes (< 0.12 per cent) and the background criterion passes at P90 <= 0.62 per cent in the footprint | the rule was stated before the run; it fails only beyond the footprint, from structure past about 6 R500c that no stored cutout holds | same section |
| 2026-10-24 | Coarse cells are kept: cells heavier than 8 times the median cell mass of the cutout emit like any cell and are counted and reported | no reference defines a cleaner rule; the share of the emission is recorded per view (`gas_cutout` of `obs_meta.json`) | same section |

## The fit, the pilot and the production run

| date | decision | reason | record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | The fit model is averaged over each pixel within 24 fit pixels of the centre (`massfit.model_pixel_average = 8`), for mocks and real observations alike | a pixel holds the integral of the surface brightness over its area; read at the pixel centre, a cool core is too peaked on coarse pixels | [The model averaged over the pixel](dev/DECISIONS.md#the-model-averaged-over-the-pixel-2026-11-10) |
| 2026-11-10 | A real fit is compared with the published mass of the same cluster by the tension between the two 68 per cent intervals, not by a percentage band about 1. Since 2026-11-11 the tension is read together with the common offset and the scatter, and every cluster beyond 3 sigma is traced | a band says nothing about the errors of either side; independent analyses of the same data differ by more than their statistical errors, so overlap of every pair is not asked for | {doc}`dev/PILOT`, "How the result is read" |
| 2026-11-11 | The closure grid is reported as the measured error of the method, with its verdict as it came out (FAIL), and no longer gates the freeze; the freeze is judged on the pilot.
