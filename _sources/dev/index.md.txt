# Development log

The working records of the development, kept for traceability. They are not user documentation: they were written
while the work happened, use the internal labels of that time (see the {doc}`../glossary`), and some numbers in them
were superseded later. Where a user page and a record here disagree, the user page and the code are current.

* {doc}`STATUS`: the open work after the integration of 2026-10-30, what decides each item, and what was closed.
  The item list was written between 2026-11-04 and 2026-11-12; the state paragraph of 2026-11-26 and the dated
  notes under the items say what has changed since.
* {doc}`INTEGRATION_REPORT`: the integration of 2026-10-30 for the author: branches, removed factors, the L_X
  verdict, the checks.
* {doc}`PREREGISTRATION`: the rules of 2026-11-04 (DESIGN v4), written before the runs they judge: the closure
  grid and its study arms, the QPB level, the sampler, the soft-proton bands, the matching mass, the bank filter,
  the field-edge targets and the protection radii.
* {doc}`PILOT`: the pilot run of 2026-11-10 before the freeze: the two lists, what is measured and how it is read,
  with the amendments of 2026-11-11 and the changes made after it on 2026-11-12.
* {doc}`DECISIONS`: the long record behind the one-line {doc}`decision log <../decisions>`: every decision with its
  options, measurements and date, in the order it was taken.
* {doc}`INTERFACES`: the contracts between stages (file layouts, column tables, roots, the spec-hash table).
* {doc}`KEDRO`: the node-to-command map of every Kedro pipeline, the environments and the acceptance runs.
* {doc}`LX_GAS_EXPLORATION`: the alternatives to the emission model tested against the soft-band luminosity
  excess of TNG-Cluster (branch `lx-gas-explore`, none adopted).
* {doc}`LX_CROSS_SIM`: the L_X-M500c relation of TNG-Cluster, TNG300, FLAMINGO, SIMBA and EAGLE with one emission code
  and one definition (branch `lx-gas-explore`).
* {doc}`REFERENCE_LEDGER`: the claims taken from the literature, with the state of their check by hand.
* {doc}`PSF_WINDOW_VALIDATION`: what the fit-fix switches `psf_eval = window` and `projection = matrix` change in a
  fit (rounding, direction, reproducibility) and how much time they save, measured on paired fits. The preset
  `v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass2_extra` sets both.
* {doc}`REVIEW_RESPONSE_v1.1.3`: the response to the review of the documentation of release 1.1.2, finding by finding.
