# Pre-registered rules of 2026-11-04 (DESIGN v4)

Written on 2026-11-04, before any fit under the configuration these rules judge (spec hash of preset `v1`
`66a72be359876779` and its successors from this commit on; on 2026-11-04 no closure cell, real refit or pass-1 view
existed for it).
Each rule says what is measured, how it is judged, and what a verdict does. Thresholds come from published practice
where it exists; where it does not, the number is ours, is said to be ours, and is listed in
`docs/assumptions/OPEN_ITEMS.md`, section P. On 2026-11-04 no result of the configuration these rules judge existed. Some
choices were made knowing results of earlier configurations; they are named where they are made (section 1). A rule
may say "decided by run X": then the rule, not the outcome, is what is fixed here.

The code that applies the rules: `validation.closure_grid` (`CLOSURE_RULE`, `ARM_RULE`, `ARM_BASE`, `ARM_ROLE`,
`SAMPLER_RULE`, `QPB_LEVEL_RULE`, the arm lists `B1_DECISION_ARMS`, `B1_BUDGET_ARMS`, `Q18_ARMS`; `analyse` writes
every verdict to `closure_verdict.json`), `conf/base/parameters/certify.yml` (the confirmation run), `massfit.fit_qc`
(the science-sample flags), `bank.filter_rows` (the training filter) and `donors.pair_projections` (the pairing on the
fit mass).

## Published precision these rules are set against

| source | what it quotes |
|---|---|
| Ettori et al. (2019, X-COP, abstract, p. 1) | hydrostatic masses "recovered with a relative (statistical) median error of 3 % at R500"; method systematics "of the order of 5% at both R500 and R200" |
| Pratt et al. (2019, review, Sect. 3.2.2, p. 13) | spherical symmetry and gas clumping "few %" each, the hydrostatic bias itself "< 10 - 30%", the absolute X-ray temperature calibration 15-20 per cent |
| CHEX-MATE Collaboration (2021, abstract, p. 1) | exposures "designed to obtain individual mass measurements accurate to 15 −20% under the hydrostatic assumption" |
| Nelson et al. (2014, p. 9) | on simulated clusters, a mass reconstruction that adds the gas motions to the hydrostatic term recovers the true mass to within 5 per cent at every radius, for relaxed subsamples of up to half the clusters (f_relax < 50 per cent) |
| Vehtari et al. (2021, pp. 4 and 6) | R-hat < 1.01 and a rank-normalised ESS above 400 as first checks; whether a Monte-Carlo error is small enough "requires domain expertise" |
| Foreman-Mackey et al. (2013, p. 11) | run the sampler for a few (say 10) autocorrelation times; too few steps is the common mistake |

No paper gives a pass threshold for a closure test of a mock-image pipeline, or a per-term budget of an XMM-Newton
hydrostatic mass below these sizes. The thresholds below are therefore ours, placed against these numbers.

## 1. The closure grid B1

**Amendment of 2026-11-11, made knowing the result.** Job 1896730 (gate arm with the pixel average) gave FAIL under
the rule below: 1.046 on the 27 usable clean fits, 0.89 on real backgrounds. The author decided that the grid is
reported as the measured error of the method, with its verdict as it came out, and that it no longer gates the
freeze. The freeze is judged on the pilot (`docs/dev/PILOT.md`), which measures the two things the grid cannot: the
agreement of real fits with published hydrostatic masses, and the ratio of the fit to the hydrostatic and true mass
on simulated gas. The rule below is left as written; nothing in it was changed to fit the result. No further closure
or sampler run is made before the freeze.

Grid: true M500 2, 4, 8 x 1e14 Msun, z 0.06, 0.2, 0.5, cool-core and non-cool-core, three seeds (donors), clean and
real background: 54 fits per background and arm. Gate arm `v1sas`.

* **Usable fit.** Status ok, a finite ratio, and no flag of `fit_qc.QC_EXCLUDING`, the flags that keep a mock or a real
  fit out of the science sample: a posterior at a prior edge of c200 or Z, the outer-pressure run-away, the centre
  flag, no background annulus (`bg_fallback`), and, new on 2026-11-04, a first guess limited by the field (`field_edge`) or
  none (`no_seed`, the pivot start). The same flags apply to every mock and real fit, so the grid judges the regime the
  science sample keeps (DESIGN v3 section 2 made the QC sample the science sample; the closure grid now follows it).
* **Statistic.** Per background, the median of ln(M500_fit / M500_true) over the usable fits, pooled over mass,
  profile, redshift and seed, with a bootstrap 95 per cent interval over the fits (10,000 resamples, seed 20261104).
  Pooled, not per cell: the per-cell rule 1.00 +- 0.03 with three seeds passes an unbiased cell 40-55 per cent of the
  time at a per-fit scatter of 0.07-0.1, and all 18 cells with a probability below 1e-4 (memo of 2026-11-03, item 2).
  It is kept as a report. This argument from power was made after the per-cell rule had failed for the earlier freeze
  candidate (13 of 72 cells); the windows themselves are DESIGN v2's, set before any v2 fit.
* **Domain.** A redshift with fewer than half of its 18 fits usable is out of the domain: it enters no median and is
  reported with its counts. A background needs two redshifts in the domain, else it is INCOMPLETE. This is how a
  cluster that fills the field at z = 0.06 is handled: by the same flags that remove such targets from the headline
  statistics of both sides (section 7), not by a closure rule of its own. Disclosed: the domain rule and the QC
  exclusion of closure fits were proposed (memo of 2026-11-03, item 2(c)) after the z = 0.06 cells of earlier
  configurations were known to fail (fitted background about 20 per cent low, DECISIONS "The background-cell deficit");
  every median is therefore also reported with all ok fits and every redshift, and the z = 0.06 cells are shown in the
  per-cell report.
* **Clean cells (the gate).** PASS when the 95 per cent interval of the pooled ratio lies inside [0.95, 1.05] and every
  redshift median in the domain lies in [0.90, 1.10]. These are the clean windows of DESIGN v2, set before any v2 fit,
  and the 5 per cent is the X-COP method systematic (Ettori et al. 2019) and the precision of the simulation method
  test of Nelson et al. (2014): the fitter on its own data must do as well as the methods it is compared with. At a
  per-fit scatter of 0.05 (the V4 clean cells) and 54 fits the interval is +-0.017, so an unbiased fitter passes with
  probability near 1, one 2 per cent high with 0.92 (2 per cent low 0.96), one 4 per cent high with 0.21 (low 0.27;
  bootstrap simulation with the three redshift windows). A clean FAIL is a fitter defect, whatever the real cells show.
* **Real cells, tail (the gate).** PASS when at most 5 per cent of the ok fits (2 of 54) lie outside [0.67, 1.5]
  (DESIGN v2 section 3; certification `R4_real_tail`).
* **Real cells, the hydrostatic-bias window (not the gate).** PASS when the pooled median itself lies in [0.92, 1.08]
  and every redshift median in the domain in [0.85, 1.15]: DESIGN v2's rule R4, set before any v2 fit and made a
  report in v3 after the v2 results; it is restored here before any fit it judges. A FAIL does not stop the
  production: in the matched mock-real comparison one fitter serves both sides, so a method term common to both
  cancels to first order. It does forbid quoting a mock M_X / M_true as a hydrostatic bias without the measured
  closure median beside it. At a per-fit scatter of 0.1 the median's standard error is 0.017: a true 0.95 passes with
  probability 0.97, a true 0.92 with 0.5, a true 0.90 with 0.1. The 0.08 is ours (DESIGN v2), between the 5 per cent
  method systematic of X-COP and the 10 per cent below which Pratt et al. (2019) place the hydrostatic bias of relaxed
  clusters.
* **Failed, missing and pivot fits.** More than 10 per cent failed fits in a background is a FAIL (DESIGN v2 section 3,
  certification `max_fail_frac`). Any missing or stale fit makes the background INCOMPLETE: a grid not finished is not
  judged. More than 10 per cent of fits started from the pivot (no first guess, or a failed seed step) makes it
  INCOMPLETE: such a fit is excluded from every statistic (it is `no_seed`), and when they are many the grid does not
  test the procedure the real targets go through. A closure record with status ok whose result.json cannot be read
  (its QC unknown) counts as stale. Order within a background: a missing or stale fit makes it INCOMPLETE first; else
  more than 10 per cent failed fits make it FAIL, even when pivot starts or the domain would make it INCOMPLETE; else
  INCOMPLETE for pivot starts or too few redshifts; else the windows decide. Across the two backgrounds INCOMPLETE wins
  over FAIL in the gate.
* **Exit codes** of `closure_grid analyse`: 0 PASS, 1 FAIL, 2 INCOMPLETE, from the clean and tail verdicts.

The confirmation run (`conf/base/parameters/certify.yml`, DESIGN v4) takes the same windows on its fresh grid, and
restores R3p's 95 per cent half-width of 0.07 (DESIGN v3; v3.1 had loosened it to 0.10 after the v2 results). At the
D1 scatter of 0.3 the half-width is 1.96 x 1.2533 x 0.3 / sqrt(n), 0.07 at n = 111 (where a bootstrap meets it about
half the time). The plan is 16 fresh views per cell (192), about 136 science fits at the D1 QC loss of 29 per cent
(half-width about 0.063). A width not met is INCOMPLETE (`max_blocks` 2): a block of views (two more per cell, a new
sample seed, no cluster drawn before) is added and the rule read again; only a miss after the second block FAILs. The
stop looks at the width, never at the median. One difference from the grid of B1: in the certification a redshift
rule below its minimum count is INCOMPLETE and keeps the gate closed, where B1 leaves such a redshift out of the domain;
the fresh grid's redshifts (0.15, 0.4, 0.75) hold no field-filling cell, so the stricter form costs nothing there.

## 2. Paired study arms

* **Statistic.** For an arm and its base (`ARM_BASE`: `v1` for the arms masked with the cell's simulated sources,
  `v1sas` for those masked with its SAS list, `v1` also for `v1_obsresp_shared`, the arm without the suffix for a
  re-seed), d = ln M500(arm) - ln M500(base) of the same cell and seed, over the pairs whose two fits are both usable.
  The verdict is taken on the real-background cells (54 pairs); the clean cells and all ok pairs without the QC
  exclusion are reported. Median and bootstrap 95 per cent interval as above; fewer than 27 pairs is INCOMPLETE.
* **Verdict**, with delta = 0.03:
  * NEGLIGIBLE: the interval lies inside +-0.03; the arm is reported and enters no budget.
  * SYSTEMATIC: the interval reaches beyond +-0.03 and excludes 0; MATERIAL as well when |median| > 0.03.
  * INCONCLUSIVE: the interval reaches beyond +-0.03 and includes 0; the term enters at the far end of the interval,
    or seeds are added before the paper.
* **Why 0.03.** It is DESIGN v2's MATERIAL line (R2, set before the v2 fits) and the old per-cell tolerance, and it
  equals the 3 per cent median statistical error of the X-COP hydrostatic masses at R500 (Ettori et al. 2019), the
  best-measured XMM-Newton masses: a term below it cannot change a comparison with them. Pratt et al. (2019) give the
  smaller systematic terms of a hydrostatic mass as "few %". The value is ours (OPEN_ITEMS P).
* **Power.** At n = 54 the interval half-width is about 0.33 s, s the scatter of the paired differences. At true zero
  an arm is NEGLIGIBLE with probability 1.0 / 0.89 / 0.44 / 0.15 for s = 0.03 / 0.05 / 0.07 / 0.09; a true shift of
  0.03 is SYSTEMATIC with probability 0.98 / 0.93 / 0.72 / 0.52, one of 0.05 with 1.0 / 1.0 / 0.99 / 0.90 (bootstrap
  simulation of the rule). The re-seed arms
  `v1sas_r2` and `v1sas_r3` (the gate arm with another chain seed, nothing else changed) measure the floor: if either
  is not NEGLIGIBLE, the grid has too few cells for this rule, seeds are added (three more donors per z), and delta
  does not move.
* **What a verdict does** (`ARM_ROLE`):

| role | arms | a SYSTEMATIC or INCONCLUSIVE verdict |
|---|---|---|
| error, one alternative calibration or model | `v1_psfon`, `v1_spflat`, `v1_psfell` | enters two-sided at the absolute value of its median (INCONCLUSIVE: at the far end of its interval) |
| pair, two directions | `v1_vighi` / `v1_viglo` (TN-0018 off-axis flux accuracy), `v1_nustarhi` / `v1_nustarlo` (EPIC-to-NuSTAR), `v1_qpbhi` / `v1_qpblo` and `v1_qpbsoft` / `v1_qpbhard` (section 3), `v1sas_seedhi` / `v1sas_seedlo`, `v1sas_prot0` / `v1sas_prot90`, `v1sas_crop2p5` / `v1sas_crop4` (section 8) | each arm judged alone; the pair enters as the asymmetric interval [min(0, a, b), max(0, a, b)] of its medians that are not NEGLIGIBLE |
| range | `v1_xcallo` (a correct ACIS calibration; `v1_xcalhi` a diagnostic) | enters one-sided, [median, 0] |
| bound, an assumption with no published error size | `v1_nhhi` (the whole molecular column one way), `v1_esasalt` (the oldest FWC set) | the shift is quoted two-sided as a stated bound, and a SYSTEMATIC verdict makes the assumption a stated limitation |
| decision | `v1_qpbfree` (section 3), `v1_sptie` (section 4) | decides a production choice by the rule of its section |
| reseed, sampler | `v1sas_r2`, `v1sas_r3`; the arms of section 5 | power check; the rule of section 5 |
| diagnostic | every other arm (`v1_obsresp_shared`, the shared responses against the per-observation ones the presets now use; `v1_mix`, `v1_grid`, `v1_long`, `v1_half`, `v1_deep`, `v1_vigcw`, `v1_fpmask`, `v1_axpt`, `v1_vigzero`, `v1_qpbflat`) | reported |

  Terms that enter combine in quadrature (each is a different calibration file or model choice); a pair or a range
  keeps its own sides (`closure_grid.budget`, written to `closure_verdict.json`). The budget is reported beside every
  result it applies to; it is never subtracted.
* **`v1_psfell` per band.** The verdict is on the mass of the joint four-band fit, as for every arm. Per band the
  analysis reports the counts within theta500 of the arm's cells over the base cells; no verdict is taken per band.
  If the arm is not NEGLIGIBLE, the term enters at its |median|, and one refit of its cells without the 4.5-12 keV
  images (the band that holds the ELLBETA rows above 10 keV, which Read et al. 2011 could not fit stably) is reported
  beside it; the refit does not replace the term.
* **Vignetting arms.** The size 1 +- 0.05 theta / 10' is the reading of XMM-SOC-CAL-TN-0018 Sect. 4.5 declared in
  OPEN_ITEMS P (one coherent radial error, all cameras alike); the MOS-to-pn difference of up to 20 per cent at about
  12' (Sect. 2.3) stays a stated real-side term, not an arm.

## 3. The QPB level in production (review 7, B1)

XMM-ESAS (Snowden et al. 2008, Sect. 3.1.1) and the X-COP imaging (Ghirardini et al. 2019, p. 3) fix the level of the
filter-wheel-closed template by the corners; the X-COP spectral fits leave it free within 10 per cent (Sect. 2.4.2,
p. 6). The rule (`QPB_LEVEL_RULE`, `qpb_level_decision`):

* `v1_qpbfree` NEGLIGIBLE under section 2: the level is free on both sides in production (a hashed `massfit` field
  then replaces the fit-fix, and the spec hash moves). Freeing it removes the one real-side term with no published
  size; its precision cost is reported, and bought back with R3p views if needed.
* SYSTEMATIC or INCONCLUSIVE: the level stays fixed by the corners, the published imaging practice, and the four level
  arms are sized from the real free-level fits, in both directions (`qpb_level_error_from_fits`): per image, d_up =
  q84 - m and d_down = m - q16, with q16 and q84 the percentiles of the fitted over corner level on the real refits made
  with the level free, and m the median of the same ratio on the `v1_qpbfree` cells, whose corners predict the field
  exactly (so m is the free fit's own offset). The noise of the real free fits is left in: the size is an upper
  estimate. Until those fits exist, the arms keep the lower bound of `QPB_LEVEL_ERROR` and are reported as such.
* If the level stays fixed, whether the mocks get pn-corner soft protons is decided before the regeneration.

## 4. Sky and soft-proton levels across bands (STATUS Q7)

* **Soft protons.** Fit-fix `sp_bands = 'tied'` (hashed field `massfit.sp_band_tie`, closure arm `v1_sptie`): one
  free level per camera, the bands in the ratios of the Leccardi & Molendi (2008) broken power law in counts
  (`esas_background.sp_band_factor`, the pn hard band without the Cu-line gap). X-COP models this component with a
  fixed spectral shape and a free normalisation (Ghirardini et al. 2019, Sect. 2.4.2, p. 7), and the mocks draw that
  spectrum, so the tie is exact for the mocks and an assumption for real data. Leccardi & Molendi (2008) measured the
  shape on the MOS; the pn takes the same shape, an extrapolation, in the mocks and in the tie alike. Rule
  (`sp_tie_decision`), in two steps:
  * mock side: `v1_sptie` must be SYSTEMATIC and bring the real-cell median nearer the truth than `v1`, by a gain g in
    |median ln(M_fit / M_true)|; otherwise the free level per image stays;
  * real side, before adoption: the real targets are refitted with the tie, and the paired shift tied - free is
    judged by the arm rule of section 2. The tie is adopted only if that shift is NEGLIGIBLE or smaller than g (its far
    end if INCONCLUSIVE); a shift that is not NEGLIGIBLE then enters the budget as a real-side error term. Beside it,
    `sp_band_ratio_check` reports, per camera and band, the median over the real free-level fits of the fitted
    soft-proton band ratio minus the Leccardi & Molendi ratio (0 if the real bands follow the assumed spectrum; the
    pn rows show the extrapolation). It is a report: the decision rests on the paired shift.
* **Sky.** Not tied. The X-COP imaging measures the sky level of each image from the regions beyond 2 R500 (Ghirardini
  et al. 2019, Sect. 2.2.4, p. 4), as the production fit's free level per image does. Tying it across bands would need
  the sky spectrum folded through each camera's response, which the X-COP imaging does not do.
* **Walkers inside the bounds.** Covered by `walker_start = 'scaled'`, which redraws starts outside a prior's support;
  decided with the sampler (section 5).

## 5. The sampler (STATUS Q18) and `chain_lp_rising`

The Q18 job fits `Q18_ARMS` on B1's views with B1's commit. Rule (`SAMPLER_RULE`, `sampler_verdict`), on all usable
pairs of both backgrounds:

* Reference: `v1sas_delong` (long chains, differential-evolution move, scaled start). It is itself not converged by
  R-hat, so agreement with it is agreement between samplers, not accuracy.
* A candidate (`v1sas`, `v1sas_de`, `v1sas_scaled`, `v1sas_long`) is **eligible** when (a) its paired difference from
  the reference is NEGLIGIBLE under section 2 and (b) its Monte-Carlo scatter, 1.4826 MAD of the candidate minus its
  re-seed (`*_r2`) over sqrt 2, is at most 0.015 in ln M500. Vehtari et al. (2021) leave the size of an acceptable
  Monte-Carlo error to the field; 0.015 is half the 3 per cent statistical error of the X-COP masses (it adds 12 per
  cent to that error in quadrature) and is the SENS tolerance, the scatter of the median between three re-seeds. Ours.
* A candidate is **converged** when every one of its fits has a rank-normalised split R-hat below 1.01 and a bulk ESS
  above 400 (Vehtari et al. 2021). B9 found none so; R-hat over the walkers of one ensemble is a mixing check, not a
  formal test.
* **Choice:** the cheapest converged eligible candidate first; else, among the eligible candidates whose scatter is
  within 0.005 of the smallest, the cheapest, equal costs going to the smaller scatter; if none is eligible, the
  reference itself if its own re-seed scatter is within 0.015; else no choice, the author decides, and the Monte-Carlo
  term is carried from the repeats. The chosen sampler serves the real refits, pass 1 and every later fit, mocks and
  real data alike. Costs on the 108 fits of the grid (`SAMPLER_RULE['cost_core_h']`, from the quick fits of the grid
  of 2026-10-28 and the long-over-quick ratio 2.4-2.7 of the release 1.1.3 grid):

  | arm | chains | core-hours per 108 fits |
  |---|---|---|
  | `v1sas` | quick, stretch move, ball start | 40 |
  | `v1sas_de` | quick, differential-evolution move | 40 |
  | `v1sas_scaled` | quick, scaled start | 40 |
  | `v1sas_long` | long, stretch move | 100 |
  | `v1sas_delong` (reference) | long, differential-evolution move, scaled start | 100 |
* **`chain_lp_rising` cuts nothing.** A chain whose log posterior still rises is a chain run for too few
  autocorrelation times; the remedy Foreman-Mackey et al. (2013, p. 11) give is to run it longer, which is the sampler
  rule above, not a cut. No chain passes Vehtari's R-hat, so leaving out the rising ones would select on a diagnostic
  that tracks the signal (17 per cent of the long chains rise by more than 2 sd, STATUS B9) without leaving converged
  chains. The flag's rate is compared between mocks and real fits (certification `Rqc_seed_chain`, reported), and
  every science median is given with and without the flagged fits.
* Cost: the eight arms are about 560 core-hours (the four arms of the Q18 job and their repeats), about two jobs of
  the current length on one node, each on its own go.

## 6. The matching mass (C1)

**Amendment of 2026-11-12, made before any pass-1 view exists and with the pilot results in hand.** Pass 1 is run
first on FLAMINGO L1_m8: 5,001 candidate halo-snapshots (`campaigns/v4_flamingo_l1m8_pass1`; the 3,258 of the rule
below are the halo-snapshots of the TNG campaign). The list is generated and fitted under a preset of its own,
`v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass1`: one projection, every view under the reference observation as it is, with no draw among
neighbours and no jitter. The reference is 0762440401, chosen by the rule below. Two points differ from the rule
as registered, both by the author's decision of that day. The cluster sits at the reference's core offset, 1.78
arcmin from the optical axis, as every production view sits at its donor's, and not on axis. Every view of the list
is fitted, whatever the domain prediction says (it puts 2,290 of the 5,001 views outside the domain): the
prediction is a column of the fit targets and decides nothing, because whether a fit is outside the domain is known
only when it has run. One point the rule did not foresee: the 665 views of the snapshot at z = 0 cannot be observed
at the halo's redshift and keep the redshift of their row in the source list (0.042 to 0.114). The fits carry the
concentration prior of the same day (ledger `fit.cm_prior`). The rule below is left as written.

* **Pass 1** (the design note of 2026-10-27, option c of the memo of 2026-11-03): one view of every candidate
  halo-snapshot (3,258), projection axis 0, under one reference observation, on axis at the halo's redshift, fitted
  with the production fit and the sampler of section 5; that fit mass is the halo-snapshot's first matching mass. The
  reference (`pair_projections.pass1_reference`): among the observations with all three cameras in full-frame modes,
  the one with the smallest summed |percentile rank - 0.5| over the effective exposure of each camera, the HI4PI
  column, N_H,tot / N_HI and the flare-filtered particle background; ties by obsid; its obsid and values are recorded
  before the job. One reference, not each halo's 0.78-matched donor, so that no catalogue mass or bias factor enters
  the first matching mass.
* **Pairing** on (ln M_fit, z) on both sides, with the window and distance of the matched draw (`PairingSpec`);
  catalogue masses (Planck, ACT, lensing) are cross-checks only.
* **Convergence** (`CONVERGENCE_MAX_CHANGED_FRAC`, `CONVERGENCE_MAX_ITERATIONS`, `convergence_state`): stop when fewer
  than 5 per cent of the paired halo-snapshots change any physics donor between two iterations, or after three
  iterations of pass 2. The history (share changed, median |d log M_fit| per iteration, `pairing_changes`) is reported;
  a pairing that does not converge is reported as NOT_CONVERGED and not extended.
* **Edge test** of a simulation streamed in a mass window about its targets (FLAMINGO L1_m8: log M500c(true) within log
  M_cat - 0.15 to + 0.25; `window_edge_share`): at most 5 per cent of its paired halo-snapshots in either outer tenth
  of the window, else its pairing is incomplete at that end and the mass range it covers is stated.
* Gate P is redone on M_fit. Until convergence, every realism verdict, gate P, the matched-draw figures and any L_X-M
  comparison are conditional on the 0.78 pairing and the frozen realism run waits. The 5 per cent, three iterations
  and the outer tenth are ours (OPEN_ITEMS P).

## 7. Training filter of the ML bank, and field-edge targets

* **Bank** (`bank.filter_rows`, rule `seed_unmeasured`, the default since 2026-11-04): a mock row whose seed photometry
  measured no positive luminosity (`lx500_seed` <= 0 or missing) is left out of training, and a real row of that kind
  is flagged out of the domain at prediction (`out_of_domain`). The seed photometry is the same code on a mock fit
  target and a real observation. The rule it replaces read the generation's `lx_r500_erg_s`, measured in the true
  R500 at the true centre, which no real observation has. A dropped row is a non-detection, a censored value (Kelly
  2007): an estimator that takes censored data keeps it as an upper limit instead, and then the rule is not applied.
  The fixture bank keeps the earlier rule by name.
* **Field-edge targets** (21 real: 17 PSZ2, 4 ACT, 19 of them at z <= 0.077) and the target without a source list:
  out of the headline statistics on both sides by one rule, the QC flags `field_edge` and `no_seed` read from each
  fit's own record (`mass_seed_source`), with `bg_fallback` as before. They stay in the tables and in training. The
  mock and real field-edge fractions are reported side by side (certification `Rqc_seed_chain`). The two field-edge
  targets at z = 0.33 and 0.72 are looked at by hand and reported; no rule changes for them.

## 8. Protection radii (STATUS Q14; memo of 2026-11-03, item 6)

* **Core protection of the point-source mask** (Q14): one PSF half-energy width, 16.487 arcsec, as since 2026-11-03;
  the sensitivities of Q14 were measured about the old 90 arcsec rule and are void. The arms `v1sas_prot0` and
  `v1sas_prot90` (sensitivity test S3) bracket it and enter as a pair under section 2.
* **Protection in the centre search**: kept, max(r90, HEW) about the nominal position (a published positional error
  used as the region the cluster may occupy; a cool core can be detected as point-like, Pacaud et al. 2016). The check,
  fixed now: per catalogue, the share of final centres within one HEW of a source the search left unmasked because of
  the protection, mocks (each physics donor weighted once) against the real share with a bootstrap 95 per cent
  interval over clusters. One-sided: it fails if the mock share lies below the real interval, that is, if real
  peaks are captured by such sources more often than mock ones (member AGN have no mock counterpart). A failure is
  answered by a refit of the affected real targets with those sources masked, its paired shift in ln M entering as a
  real-side term under section 2; the radius is not changed on that result. The search must record the protected
  sources of every target for this check. Not coded yet: the centre search does not record them, and the check is
  written before the regeneration.

## 9. Fit-level tests that become B1 arms (STATUS B4, B5, B7)

* **B4 and S6, the first guess.** `v1sas_seedhi` / `v1sas_seedlo`: the cell's first-guess mass times exp(+-0.143), one
  standard deviation of the Bulbul et al. (2019) relation's scatter at fixed luminosity (sigma_lnL 0.27 over the slope
  1.89, p. 22); the centre stays, the crop, pixel and start follow. A pair under section 2. If either is not
  NEGLIGIBLE, the slope d ln M_fit / d ln M_seed is reported, and with it the shift TNG-Cluster's luminosity excess
  induces in mock first guesses, beside every mock-real L_X-M_fit comparison; it is not subtracted.
* **S2, the crop.** `v1sas_crop2p5` / `v1sas_crop4`, a pair.
* **S3 and Q14.** `v1sas_prot0` / `v1sas_prot90` (section 8). The DET_ML 6 arm of S3 needs a new detection and is not
  part of B1.
* **R1, R10, R11** are login analyses of B1's output (the QC prior-edge fraction at 0.01 and 0.05 on the B1 fits, the
  SAS lists of the B1 cells, the seed annulus on the B1 cells), reported. **S1** compares generation products, not
  fits, and stays outside B1.
* **B7, the donor jitter**, is not an arm. It is a zero-mean jitter in ln t (0.15 per view, 0.05 per camera) and a
  random roll and pointing, which the three donor seeds of every cell already sample. To first order a zero-mean
  jitter adds scatter, not bias; the second-order bias is (1/2) c sigma^2, with c the curvature of ln M_fit in ln t
  from the three exposures of `v1_half`, `v1` and `v1_deep` (x 0.5, 1, 10) and sigma^2 = 0.15^2 + 0.05^2
  (`closure_grid.jitter_bias`, written to `closure_verdict.json`). It enters the budget only if above 0.03.

**Arm lists and cost.** `B1_DECISION_ARMS` (gate, base, re-seeds, `v1_qpbfree`, `v1_sptie`, `v1_half`,
`v1_deep`) are needed before the regeneration; `B1_BUDGET_ARMS` make the budget; `Q18_ARMS` follow on B1's views. At
about 0.8 node-hours per arm of 108 quick fits (the QPB level arms on the real cells only), B1 with the decision arms
is about 3 node-hours plus about 6, and the budget arms about 16 plus the generation of their cells. Each group runs
on its own go, one node at a time; the rules hold whenever a group runs.
