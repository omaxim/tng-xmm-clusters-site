# Response to the review of the documentation (release 1.1.2)

The documentation site of commit 82064df and its static Kedro-Viz graph were read independently of the code and the
development records. The read gave 53 findings: 12 major, 27 minor and 14 on the Kedro-Viz graph. Each finding was
checked against the code, the data products and the development records before anything was changed.

Since 2026-10-26 (state of 2026-11-26): this page is a record of release 1.1.3, the version of the package now. Four
of its statements were overtaken. The cluster PSF of the mocks is the CCF King interpolated in energy and off-axis
angle (`instrument.psf_model = 'king-energy-angle'`) and the fit uses the King at each pixel's off-axis angle
(`massfit.psf = 'king-angle'`), so M4 and recommendation 6 are closed. The six icosahedral projections are in the
pass-2 presets (`sim.projection = 'icosahedral6'`). No realism replay of the frozen evaluation is thinned
(DECISIONS, "No tuned factors: the last replacements"). S8 was run on 2026-11-04 (SENSITIVITY_TESTS, S8).

Verdicts:

* **a**: true, fixed in the documentation;
* **b**: true, needed a code change (made, with tests);
* **c**: true, needs new science or node time; stated plainly on the user pages, with what it would take;
* **d**: false, or already addressed; the evidence is given.

A row may carry two verdicts (the documentation part fixed, the science part open). Commits are labelled K, F, D and R
and listed at the end.

## Re-review

The rebuilt site and Kedro-Viz of release 1.1.3 (the documentation of commit 05ad75c, built with `-W`) were read a
second time, again without the development log and without a list of the changes. The write-up was found clearer.
The scientific case was found no more convincing than before: the pages now state the failed and pending tests
plainly, and the case was judged on them. The major issues of the second read, and what was done:

1. The closure deficit is not diagnosed; AGN only (0.779) is worse than AGN plus background; three estimates of the
   method bias (0.845, 0.847, 0.931) without a statement of which applies. Done after the re-review: the validation
   page explains that the three are different quantities and that none is adopted as a correction, and gives the
   per-fit scatter of the clean cells (0.057). Open: the diagnosis needs the closure rerun (memo 1).
2. No page lists what the mocks represent of the physics that sets the hydrostatic bias (thermal against total
   pressure, clumping, the Willingale against HI4PI columns). Partly answered by the definitions page; a dedicated
   "scope of the bias measurement" section is still to write.
3. The replica list fails gate P; the sample of the bias statement and its weighting are not stated; the samples page
   said S7 was not run while OPEN_ITEMS gave its result; the (1-b) values were unsourced. Done after the re-review:
   the S7 result and the sources are on the samples page. Open: the sample of the bias statement (memo 2).
4. All realism AUCs are from development rounds on tuned donors and different samples; no real-against-real null;
   no AUC for the unthinned production mocks. Open: the frozen evaluation; a real-against-real null is a new test.
5. Claims A and B are inconclusive or wide; claim C's estimand was chosen after the results; `LOCAL` still listed
   `ml`. Done after the re-review: `ml` removed from LOCAL. The rest is open science.
6. The Kedro defaults of the fit (quick chains) beside "Kedro and production write the same bytes". Done after the
   re-review: PIPELINE section 4 states the quick default, the long chains of the certification fits and the pixel
   size.
7. Reproducibility: the data package and fixtures are not public; the validation inputs sit in the development area.
   Open (author: data release and the TNG permission).

Kedro-Viz points of the re-review: the real-data branch and the truth comparison are not in the graph; the per-view
physics is one node; the donor tables are not edges (all memo 5); 13 of the 32 default nodes have no citation tag
(they are plumbing and table nodes that cite nothing; d); the photometry aperture tables are sinks (they are the
fixed-aperture inputs of the planned `ml` stage, not of the fit; d).

The report of the second read is kept with the development material of the release, outside the repository.
