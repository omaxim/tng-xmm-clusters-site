tngxmm.donors.halo_groups¶

Halo groups: every view of one physical halo, the unit of the folds and of the bootstrap.

A halo is observed many times: at every campaign snapshot at which it passes the mass cut, and along several lines of sight (the three simulation axes in v1, six icosahedral axes in release 1.2). In the 60-snapshot target list of v1_common one zoom target has up to 89 views at neighbouring epochs (DECISIONS “P4: the TNG mini snapshots under the common mode”). These views are not independent clusters. A model tested on a halo it was trained on, or a bootstrap that draws the views of one halo independently, claims more than the sample holds. This module makes the halo the unit of both.

Halo group (halo_group_key()): (sim, <identity column>), by default the split unit of the view table, zoom_target_id.

  • TNG-Cluster: the zoom target (zoom_target_id = GroupOrigHaloID, the same at every snapshot);

  • TNG300, TNG100, TNG50: the z = 0 object that tngxmm.sim.build_box_halos writes in zoom_target_id: the z = 0 FoF group in which the halo’s central subhalo ends, so it joins every halo whose central ends in that group, as its central or as a satellite (a central whose descendants stop before z = 0 is its own object);

  • FLAMINGO, SIMBA, EAGLE: their target tables so far carry the halo id of one snapshot in zoom_target_id (the view schema’s “the halo’s own id in a box”). Such a column names a halo-snapshot, and one halo seen at several snapshots would fall into several groups, the leak this module removes. So a simulation whose identity column equals halo_id on every row while its rows span several snapshots is refused, unless identity names a column that follows a halo (for FLAMINGO L1_m8 the HBT+ track_id of the sample table) or accepts the halo-snapshot explicitly ({sim: None}).

The identity rule of every simulation is stored in the halo-fold table (column identity) and read back by every path that attaches folds or groups (attach_halo_folds(), the bank, the analysis tables); a request for another rule than the table’s raises. This module names no simulation: what names a halo comes from the table and the code that wrote it (one graph for every simulation, tests/unit/test_single_graph.py). The key is <sim>:<id>, or <sim>:s<snap>:h<halo_id> for a halo-snapshot. halo_id alone is never a halo identity: it is the group index of one snapshot’s catalogue. The 3,258 halo-snapshots of the maximal and box lists (campaigns/v4_max, campaigns/v4_box) hold 2,657 distinct (sim, halo_id) pairs but 535 halos (352 TNG-Cluster zoom targets, 171 TNG300, 11 TNG100 and 1 TNG50 objects).

Halo folds (assign_halo_folds()): every group in one of n_folds folds. Groups are sorted by stratum (the simulation, and the frozen split where the table has one), then by decreasing log of their largest M500c, then by key; consecutive blocks of n_folds groups receive a random permutation of the fold labels (numpy.random.default_rng(seed)), the block rule of the donor folds (tngxmm.donors.assign_donor_folds). The blocks run on across strata, so fold sizes differ by at most one group, and by at most two within a stratum. The result depends on the set of groups and on their largest M500c, so the folds are assigned once, on the whole halo list, and every later table takes them from that table (attach_halo_folds()), as the donor folds are taken from donor_folds.csv; a view whose group has no fold is refused, and new halos are added to the table without moving the others (frozen). Assigned separately, the maximal-and-box lists and the 60-snapshot list give the same fold to 68 of their 292 common zoom targets, about what chance gives. A table is read back only through load_halo_folds(), against its pinned sha256 (as the frozen split is, tngxmm.bank.attach_splits); the candidate table of the maximal and box lists is CANDIDATE_HALO_FOLDS (not used by any preset or parameter).

Cross-fitting with the donor folds (cross_fit_masks()). The donor folds are unchanged: the observations are grouped by cluster and linked through the donors of the mock views (tngxmm.bank.assign_donor_folds), and the conditions donor of a view is drawn from its physics donor’s fold (fold_restricted). The two must have the same number of folds. The views of one halo have donors in several donor folds (285 of the 290 zoom targets of the replica list), and the donors of a halo also serve other halos: in every committed target list the graph of halos and donors joined by views is one connected component, so no fold assignment can hold every halo and every donor together. Fold f therefore trains on the views whose halo and whose donor are both outside fold f, and tests on the views whose halo and donor are both in fold f (with the real observations of donor fold f); a view with only one of the two in fold f is used in neither. No halo and no donor is then in training and test at once. With halo folds drawn independently of the pairing, a fold trains on about (1 - 1/K)^2 of the views and tests on about 1/K^2 (64 and 4 per cent for K = 5; 62-65 and 3.3-4.0 per cent per fold on the maximal and box lists). Only the views whose halo fold equals their donor fold are ever tested: 1,860 of the 9,774 views of the maximal and box lists (19 per cent), and 35 of their 535 halos are never tested (66 of the 290 zoom targets of the replica list and 91 of the 292 of v4_common, with the folds of the maximal and box lists). cross_fit_coverage() gives these numbers for any table. A pairing that draws the physics donors of a halo from the halo’s own fold makes the two folds equal on every view, and no view and no halo is left out.

Halo bootstrap (halo_bootstrap()): a resample draws halo groups with replacement and keeps every row of a drawn group, as often as the group is drawn; the statistic is computed on all rows of the resample. The views and snapshots of one halo are never resampled apart. This is the bootstrap of Efron (1979) with the halo as the independent unit, as tngxmm.realism.c2st resamples donors. Fewer than two halos give no interval (NaN), and a row without a group label is refused.

References

tngcluster : the zoom-target identity GroupOrigHaloID (Nelson et al. 2024). illustristng, sublink : the z = 0 objects of the periodic boxes (SubLink RootDescendantID). efron1979 : the bootstrap estimate of the standard error, here with halo groups as the resampled units.

The halo-fold table of views (one row per halo group, columns HALO_FOLD_COLUMNS).

Every view of a group, at every snapshot and along every line of sight, has the group’s fold. The rule is in the module docstring. Deterministic for a given seed and independent of the row order of views.

tngxmm.donors.halo_groups.halo_bootstrap_indices(groups, n_boot, seed=20261103)[source]¶

n_boot resamples of the rows of a table whose rows belong to groups, one index array each.

A resample draws as many groups as there are, with replacement, and holds every row of a drawn group as often as the group is drawn (rows of one group are never resampled apart). The draw does not depend on the row order: the groups are numbered in sorted order. A missing group label (None, NA, NaN) raises ValueError.

tngxmm.donors.halo_groups.halo_bootstrap(x, groups, stat=None, n_boot=10000, seed=20261103)[source]¶

The statistic stat (default the median) of x and its bootstrap over the halo groups groups.

Non-finite values are dropped first; a group without a finite value is not a unit, and a row with a finite value but no group label raises ValueError. Returns value (the statistic of all rows), lo68, hi68, lo95, hi95 (percentiles 16, 84, 2.5, 97.5 of the resampled statistic), se (its standard deviation, ddof 1), n_groups, n_rows and n_boot. With fewer than two groups the interval and se are NaN: the resamples of one halo all repeat it, and their spread says nothing about the sampling error.
