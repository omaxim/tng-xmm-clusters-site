tngxmm.massfit.build_realfit_targets¶

Targets, reference masses and the fit plan of the fits of the real XMM clusters.

Every observation of the reference sample (obs/catalogue/observations.csv, 638 rows, including the three donors the replica excludes for their core offsets, flagged excluded_donor) but those the preset does not fit (runtime.exclude_donors: listed with their reason under not_fitted in the summary) becomes one row of a fit-targets CSV for tngxmm massfit fit-hydrostatic and of the plan of slurm/fits.sh; every fit switch is the production preset’s (the plan passes --preset and no switch, fit-hydrostatic fills them from tngxmm.massfit.fit_hydrostatic.fit_switches_from_spec(), including the PSF and pn out-of-time terms and the fit pixel), with the crop of the closure-grid arm (tngxmm.validation.closure_grid.FIT_ARMS).

Centres: the guarded peak of the 16”-smoothed merged image (data-package table obs_centres_guarded_v4.csv, massfit.real_centre = 'xray-peak'), the one rule of the mocks; the frozen peak table within 4’ of the catalogue position (lowz_supplement/obs_centres.csv) is the 'legacy' option of load_observations(). N_H, redshift and the catalogue mass come from observations.csv.

Point sources (massfit.source_masking, real side): the XMM PPS EPIC source list P<obsid>_OBSMLI.FTZ (copied from --pps-source). Where the archive has none, --dr15-fallback builds the same columns (RA, DEC, EP_DET_ML, EP_EXTENT) from the detections of that observation in the 5XMM-DR15 serendipitous source catalogue (HEASARC table xmmssc; srcid 3<obsid><srcnum>; same emldetect quantities), recorded as pps_list_origin = 5xmm-dr15; an observation without either is fitted unmasked (mask_sources = False with the reason in mask_note). With massfit.source_list = 'sas' the fit masks the SAS edetect_chain list of the observation instead.

Mass seed: the first-guess M500 iterated on the observation’s own mosaic through the published L-M relation (m500_seed_msun of the seed table, tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.seed_lx_iterated()); the published mass enters only the reported ratio (column hse).

Seed photometry: under massfit.seed_lx_photometry = 'campaign' the seeds lx_0p35mpc, lx_r500, lx_1mpc come from the data-package table obs_seed_lx_v4.csv (tngxmm.photometry.build_obs_seed_lx: measure_lx.seed_lx, the masked-clipped campaign estimator of the mocks, at the same peak, with the PPS list the fit masks); their flags and the seed columns (m500_seed_msun, r500_seed_kpc, lx500_seed, seed_n_iter, seed_status) are carried. The table must be current for the preset (tngxmm.photometry.build_obs_seed_lx.check_seed_table()), and its seed hash is written to the provenance sidecar of the targets (targets_realfit_v4.provenance.json), where a run that fits mock targets next to these compares it with theirs (tngxmm.certify.queues.check_seed_pairs()).

Order (--order as-listed of the fitter): clusters with a literature hydrostatic or weak-lensing mass first, so that early results can be plotted, then the rest; each group longest-first (the fitter’s job_cost: the angular crop radius, then the mass).

Reference masses (references_realfit_v4.csv, long format, one row per observation and reference family): taken from the curated catalogue obs/catalogue/reference_masses.csv only, like-for-like (spherical M500c at the r500c of that mass, main table): Planck PSZ2 and ACT DR6 (SZ), Lovisari et al. 2020 (XMM hydrostatic, their Table A1), Okabe & Smith 2016, Hoekstra et al. 2015 and Herbonnet et al. 2020 (weak lensing, NFW fits). Wicker et al. 2023 Table A.1, a copy of the Lovisari table with 20 of 120 rows changed in the copying, is kept as a comparison family read from its superseded catalogue rows. See REF_FAMILIES for what is excluded and why.

References

psz2, act_dr6, wicker2023, lovisari2020, okabe2016, hoekstra2015, herbonnet2020 (reference masses); xmm_ccf_sas, xmm_mission, heasarc, webb2020 (source lists).

centres (massfit.real_centre): 'legacy' the 16 arcsec peak within 4 arcmin of the catalogue position (frozen table lowz_supplement/obs_centres.csv); 'guarded' the guarded peak, the one rule of the mocks (data-package table obs_centres_guarded_v4.csv, tngxmm.photometry.build_obs_centres_guarded; centres_table overrides its path): ra_peak, dec_peak, off_cat_peak, off_imgcentre_peak are then the guarded ones and the columns centre_flag (ok | edge | empty | no_image; rejected only with max_move_theta500 < 1, never at the default), centre_changed, centre_theta500_arcsec and ra_peak_legacy, dec_peak_legacy are added. The Lx seed columns are those of the seed table whatever the centre (build_realfit_targets checks that the table was measured at the centre used).

seed_photometry (massfit.seed_lx_photometry): 'angbkg' the peak-centred measure_apertures Lx (lx_*_peak, frozen table obs_lx_apertures_recentred.csv); 'campaign' additionally the columns of the seed table (tngxmm.photometry.build_obs_seed_lx, default $TNGXMM_DATA/tables/obs_seed_lx_v4.csv) prefixed seed_, which must be current for spec when given and measured from the observations table read here (tngxmm.photometry.build_obs_seed_lx.check_seed_table()).

tngxmm.massfit.build_realfit_targets.reference_masses(o, catalogue_csv=None)[source]¶

Long table: obsid, ref_key, ref_kind, ref_label, m500_ref_1e14 (in the pipeline Planck15 cosmology), m500_published_1e14, ref_name, match, ref_id, radius_definition, paper_key.

The rows come from the curated catalogue (obs/catalogue/reference_masses.csv, written by obs.check_reference_masses with its cross-match to our observations): for each family of REF_FAMILIES, the paper’s M500c rows of the table role the family names (the main table, the superseded rows for hse_wicker2023_xmm) whose radius is that mass’s own r500c (radius_definition), joined on the catalogue’s obsids. One value per observation and family; a duplicate (two main-table rows) raises, except a double cluster listed per component, which gets no value (recorded in EXCLUDED_REFERENCES).

tngxmm.massfit.build_realfit_targets.fit_arguments(out_root, pps_dir, img_root=PosixPath('$TNGXMM_SITE_OBS_IMAGES'), resp=PosixPath('$TNGXMM_MBPROJ_RESP'), seed=20261008, worker_caches=None)[source]¶

fit-hydrostatic arguments of a real fit (the EXTRA of the plan line; slurm/fits.sh adds –all –nprocesses 1 –per-fit-timeout-s –resume [–quick]).

Every fit switch comes from --preset (fit-hydrostatic fills each switch not given from fit_switches_from_spec(): free metallicity, lodd, full-ionisation mu, optical axis from the header, block-centre rebin, point-source masking, the King PSF and the pn out-of-time template in the model with the OoT CTI term, the 8” target pixel coarsened to R500/40 at low z), so the plan names none of them and cannot disagree with the preset; the arguments here are the run options (seed, crop, background prior, source lists, trees, order, mass seed, caches) with the crop of the closure grid’s arm.

tngxmm.massfit.build_realfit_targets.write_targets_provenance(targets, spec, seed_table=None)[source]¶

The provenance sidecar of the targets CSV targets: the preset, its spec hash and the seed hash of the seed table the rows were seeded from (seed_table, default the data package’s; load_observations() checked it against the preset), so a run that fits these targets next to mock fit targets can check that one procedure seeded both sides (tngxmm.certify.queues.check_seed_pairs()). Returns that seed hash.
