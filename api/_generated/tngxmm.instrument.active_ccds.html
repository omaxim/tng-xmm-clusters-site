tngxmm.instrument.active_ccds¶

EPIC active CCDs: image each camera on the CCDs its donor actually had.

Under instrument.active_ccds = 'donor' every camera images only the CCDs that have exposure in the donor’s own PPS exposure map (table $TNGXMM_DATA/tables/donor_active_ccds.csv, built once per donor list by tngxmm.donors.build_active_ccds), instead of the packaged detector masks of obsid 0827340901 (2018), whose MOS1 lacks CCD6 (lost 2005-03-09) and CCD3 (lost 2012-12-11) whatever the donor’s epoch. The CCD footprints are the per-CCD label maps of $TNGXMM_DATA/tables/epic_ccd_maps.fits on the 900 x 900 detector grid of CameraResponse (2.5” pixels): every active pixel of a packaged mask carries its CCD number, and MOS1 CCD3 and CCD6, absent from the packaged MOS1 mask, carry the silicon measured on the PPS exposure maps of the donors observed before 2005-03-09, as does the outer stripe of MOS1 CCD4 (segment 14, MOS1_CCD4_STRIPE), whose columns the PPS exposure maps no longer expose since the 2012-12-11 event, absent from the packaged mask too. The response of a donor (ccd_masked_response()) is a copy whose mask holds the donor’s CCDs, with its active pixels and a QPB normalisation scaled to the imaged area (the readout windows of tngxmm.instrument.readout_windows are applied on top). Every mask lookup of the instrument stage and both exposure-map constructions read resp.mask, so events and EXPMAPs share one footprint. A camera whose CCD set equals the packaged mask keeps the packaged response object, so a post-2012 donor gives the same products as 'mask' (the packaged mask). A donor without a PPS exposure map falls back to the epoch rule (date_rule_ccds()), recorded as such.

Geometry (ccd_label_map()): the CCDs are squares of 600 x 1.1” (MOS) in the CCD-aligned frame of the readout windows (rotation pn_readout_tilt_deg of the mask axes), centred on the CCD centres of EMOS1/2_LINCOORD_0019 (mm in the focal plane, 27.5” per mm) about the measured centre of the central CCD (readout_windows.MOS_CENTRAL_CCD_CENTRE_ARCMIN), with the focal-plane Y axis mirrored and rotated by 270 deg (MOS1) or 0 deg (MOS2) into the mask frame: the orientation whose squares hold every packaged-mask pixel (1.000 vs 0.990 for the unmirrored one) and whose MOS1 CCD3 and CCD6 squares are the two the packaged mask lacks, CCD3 the one the 2005-2012 PPS maps still expose. Every mask pixel takes the CCD of the nearest square (Chebyshev distance). The pn is twelve cells of 64 columns x one CCD row (two rows split at the measured row boundary, the column edges of CCD 4 from the small-window geometry); no donor lacks a pn CCD (the donor table shows it), so the pn numbering is a convention and never changes a product.

References

XMM-Newton Users Handbook section 3.3 (EPIC CCD layout); XMM-Newton CCF EMOS1_LINCOORD_0019, EMOS2_LINCOORD_0019 (CCD positions); ESA XMM-Newton news “EPIC MOS1 CCD6” (2005-03-09, revolution 961) and “EPIC MOS1 event of revolution 2382” (CCD3, 2012-12-11); Turner et al. 2001 (EPIC-MOS); Strueder et al. 2001 (EPIC-pn).

Module Attributes

ACTIVE_CCD_RULES

rules of instrument.active_ccds

MOS1_CCD4_STRIPE

the outer columns of CCD4 (a 6' x 2.6' stripe along its outer edge), exposed on every PPS map before the 2012-12-11 event and on none after (the Users Handbook records the raised noise of CCD4 after the event; the stripe is what the PPS exposure maps show); part of CCD4, its own segment so that a donor images it exactly when its own map exposes it

CCDS

the CCD numbers (and the MOS1 CCD4 stripe)

MASK_CCDS

segments the packaged masks (obsid 0827340901, 2018) image

CCD_LOSSES

MOS1 CCD6 at revolution 961; CCD3 and the CCD4 stripe at revolution 2382

MOS_CCD_CENTRES_MM

CCD centres [mm] in the focal plane relative to CCD1 (EMOS*_LINCOORD_0019, mean of the two readout nodes)

MOS_FP_ROTATION_DEG

Y mirrored, then rotated by this angle [deg] in the CCD-aligned (q, s) frame

LABEL_TOLERANCE_ARCMIN

pixels farther than this from every CCD square [arcmin] carry no label

PN_CCD_WIDTH_ARCMIN

64 columns of 4.1" per CCD; edges of CCD 4 (readout_windows.PN_SW_Q_RANGE_ARCMIN)

PN_ROW_NUMBERS

pn cell numbers by column (increasing q) of the upper and lower CCD row

Functions

apply_active_ccds(resps, camera_ccds[, ...])

Per-camera responses on the donor's CCDs (rule='donor'; camera_ccds: camera -> CCD list or {'ccds': ...}) or unchanged ('mask').

ccd_frame_arcmin(shape, cx, cy, pixsize, ...)

(q, s) [arcmin from the detector centre] of every pixel of the mask grid in the CCD-aligned frame (readout_windows.ccd_frame in arcmin).

ccd_label_map(inst, shape, cx, cy, pixsize, ...)

uint8 CCD number of every pixel of the mask grid (0: no CCD), from the geometry of the module docstring (no mask applied); MOS: ccds restricts the nearest-square assignment to those CCDs (the packaged MOS1 mask: no pixel of it goes to CCD3 or CCD6).

ccd_masked_response(resp, ccds, labels)

resp imaged on the CCDs ccds only (labels: the camera's label map): mask = label in ccds, its active pixels, the QPB spectrum scaled to the imaged area and ccds = the CCD list.

date_rule_ccds(inst, date_obs)

CCDs of inst working at date_obs (ISO UTC) by the permanent losses (CCD_LOSSES); raises ValueError without a date (no epoch is assumed).

default_maps_path()

$TNGXMM_DATA/tables/epic_ccd_maps.fits (data package).

default_table_path()

$TNGXMM_DATA/tables/donor_active_ccds.csv (data package).

donor_camera_ccds(table, obsid)

{camera: {'ccds': (...), 'source': 'pps-expmap' | 'date-rule'}} of one donor from the table; raises KeyError when the donor is absent.

format_ccds(ccds)

'1,2,4,5,7' (sorted, comma-separated): the table and CCDLIST form.

mos_ccd_centres_arcmin(inst)

CCD centres [arcmin, (q, s)] of a MOS camera in the mask frame.

mos_focal_to_frame_arcmin(inst, x_mm, y_mm)

(q, s) [arcmin, CCD-aligned frame of the mask] of the focal-plane point (x_mm, y_mm) measured from the centre of the central CCD (CCD1) of MOS camera inst: the focal plane is mirrored in Y, rotated by MOS_FP_ROTATION_DEG and set on the measured CCD1 centre.

package_camera_ccds(package[, table])

The active CCDs of a donor package (obsid / donor_id) from the donor table (read_active_ccds() when table is None); a donor absent from the table gets the epoch rule of its date_obs (source date-rule), and without a date the call fails.

parse_ccds(text)

Inverse of format_ccds() (also accepts ';' and blanks).

read_active_ccds([path])

The donor active-CCD table (obsid as 10-digit strings; cached per path).

read_ccd_maps([path])

{camera: uint8 (900, 900) label map} of the CCD-map product (cached).

tngxmm.instrument.active_ccds.read_ccd_maps(path=None)[source]¶

{camera: uint8 (900, 900) label map} of the CCD-map product (cached). The package copy is read only when its provenance says it is current (tngxmm.instrument.donor_tables.check_table()).

tngxmm.instrument.active_ccds.read_active_ccds(path=None)[source]¶

The donor active-CCD table (obsid as 10-digit strings; cached per path). The package copy is read only when its provenance says it is current (tngxmm.instrument.donor_tables.check_table()).

tngxmm.instrument.active_ccds.package_camera_ccds(package, table=None)[source]¶

The active CCDs of a donor package (obsid / donor_id) from the donor table (read_active_ccds() when table is None); a donor absent from the table gets the epoch rule of its date_obs (source date-rule), and without a date the call fails.

tngxmm.instrument.active_ccds.donor_camera_ccds(table, obsid)[source]¶

{camera: {'ccds': (...), 'source': 'pps-expmap' | 'date-rule'}} of one donor from the table; raises KeyError when the donor is absent.

tngxmm.instrument.active_ccds.ccd_masked_response(resp, ccds, labels)[source]¶

resp imaged on the CCDs ccds only (labels: the camera’s label map): mask = label in ccds, its active pixels, the QPB spectrum scaled to the imaged area and ccds = the CCD list. Returns resp itself when the mask equals the packaged one. Cached (the object identity keys the downstream caches).

Per-camera responses on the donor’s CCDs (rule='donor'; camera_ccds: camera -> CCD list or {'ccds': ...}) or unchanged ('mask').
