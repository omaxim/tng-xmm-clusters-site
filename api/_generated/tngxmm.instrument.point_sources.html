tngxmm.instrument.point_sources¶

Field point sources of the instrument stage: the number counts the AGN are drawn from, the band their flux refers to, and the off-axis PSF they are scattered with.

Three switches (agn_lognlogs, agn_galactic_nh, agn_psf) of InstrumentOptions select the behaviour; their defaults are the xmm_simulator behaviour (hard, off, king-onaxis); the v1 presets set soft, view and ellbeta.

agn_lognlogs

'moretti03-hard' (default; xmm_simulator 0.2.0 point_sources): fluxes S drawn from Eq. 2 of Moretti et al. (2003) with the 2-10 keV best-fit parameters, between 1e-15 and 1e-12 erg/s/cm^2; S is the 2-10 keV flux of the z = 0 absorbed template (templates_pts_hard.dat); log N_H uniform in [20.5, 23]. 'moretti03-soft': S drawn from the same Eq. 2 with the 0.5-2 keV best-fit parameters (the band of the images), between the same limits (1e-15 is also the soft-band flux limit below which the sky model’s unresolved CXB, cxb_m13_unres, holds the sources); S is the OBSERVED 0.5-2 keV energy flux of each source’s own model (PhAbs(N_H, z) x power law), integrated per source instead of read from a z = 0 template; log N_H uniform in [20.5, SOFT_NH_MAX]. Photon index and redshift as in the default.

Why: the images, the realism features and the fit are 0.5-2 keV. Drawn from the hard-band counts and converted with the population’s spectra, the mock AGN have 1.4-1.7 times the Moretti soft-band counts at 1-3e-15 erg/s/cm^2 (the fluxes near an XMM detection limit) and 0.55-0.8 times above 2e-14. The N_H cap keeps the hard band of the soft-drawn population within 15 % of the Moretti hard counts above 3e-14 (a cap of 23 gives 2.5-5 times too many bright hard sources, because a soft-normalised absorbed source is very bright above 2 keV).

agn_galactic_nh

'off' (default; xmm_simulator): the AGN spectra carry their intrinsic absorption only, while the cluster and the sky background are absorbed by the Galactic column of the view. 'view': the AGN photon spectra are also absorbed by the view’s Galactic N_H (PhAbs at z = 0), after the flux normalisation, so S stays the flux before Galactic absorption (the convention of number counts built from high-latitude fields, whose conversion factors are computed at each field’s column, Moretti et al. 2003 Sect. 6). At the median N_H of the 195 donors (2.8e20) this removes 8 % of the 0.5-2 keV energy flux of a Gamma = 1.9 source (17 % at the 90th percentile, 6.4e20).

agn_psf

'king-onaxis' (default): every AGN photon is scattered with the camera’s on-axis King profile (row 0 of KING_PARAMS, 225 eV), as the cluster. 'ellbeta': the SAS default 2-D PSF envelope (Read et al. 2011; ELLBETA_PARAMS of the XRT*_XPSF CCF): an elliptical King (beta2d) profile plus, for MOS, a Gaussian core, with the CCF core radius, slope, ellipticity, Gaussian FWHM and peak ratio at the photon’s energy (nearest tabulated energy) and off-axis angle (linear in theta), the major axis tangential (the Wolter-I off-axis stretch). The spokes and the large-scale azimuthal modulation of the full model are not drawn (they move no flux between radii). The cluster keeps the on-axis King profile (the fit convolves with that one).

References: moretti2003 (Eq. 2, Sects. 7.1 and 7.2), read2011 (Sects. 2.1 and 3.1), xmm_ccf_sas (XRT*_XPSF), xmm_simulator (the default generator).

tngxmm.instrument.point_sources.ellbeta_offsets(params, n, rng, tangential_rad, rmax_arcsec=900.0)[source]¶

n PSF offsets [arcsec] (dx, dy) from the ELLBETA envelope params.

Sherpa beta2d/gauss2d with ellipticity e: r^2 = x’^2 + y’^2 / (1 - e)^2, x’ along the major axis. Both components are integrated over the ellipse ((1 - e) times the circular area), so the Gaussian weight is N FWHM^2 / (4 ln 2) against r0^2 / (alpha - 1) for the King profile (King inside rmax_arcsec). A circular radius is drawn from the chosen component, compressed by (1 - e) along the minor axis and rotated so the major axis lies along tangential_rad (angle of the tangential direction in the output frame).
