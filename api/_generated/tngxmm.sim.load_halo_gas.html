tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas¶

Load gas cells, apply the X-ray gas selection and compute per-cell emission quantities.

Configuration is injected as keyword arguments whose defaults are the CampaignSpec values (sim.t_min_k, sim.sphere_r200c_factor, sim.apec_*, constants.*).

Gas selection (the standard literature criterion): a cell emits iff T > 3e5 K, its net cooling rate is negative (GFM_CoolingRate < 0) and it is not star forming (SFR == 0), as Chatzigiannakis et al. (2025). The optional tvir_frac > 0 raises the floor to tvir_frac * T_vir (core-excised variant; off by default).

Which cells are read (selection, CampaignSpec sim.loader): 'fof' the FoF members (loadHalo), 'zoom' the halo’s original zoom run (loadOriginalZoom, TNG-Cluster only), 'spatial' every cell within radius_ckpc_h, 'cutout' the gas of the halo’s stored spherical truth cutout (tngxmm.sim.cutout_gas). All but 'cutout' are then clipped to sphere_r200c_factor * R200c about GroupPos; the cutout cells are the sphere and are clipped again only when clip_radius_kpc is given. The clip itself is not periodic; with sim.periodic_wrap the campaign source has already moved every coordinate to its minimum image about GroupPos (tngxmm.sim.read_halo_source.CampaignHaloSource), so a halo at a box face keeps the cells across the face.

Reference luminosity (lx_reference, off by default): 'temet-exact' also returns, for EVERY loaded cell (no temperature, star-formation or heating cut), the luminosity exactly as temet computes it for the TNG official X-ray products (tngxmm.sim.temet_xray: temet’s table and line deposition, X_H = 0.76, star-forming gas at 1000 K, rest-frame band divided by (1+z)). The other arrays are unchanged. The official maps use all gas of the original zoom: with selection='zoom' the cell set is the same (inside the clip sphere); with 'fof' the outer-zoom gas is missing (1-2 % less in 1-1.5 Mpc cylinders on the z = 0.5 fixture halo).

References

IllustrisTNG (gas fields and units); Chatzigiannakis et al. (2025) gas selection; APEC table lookup as in temet (see compute_apec_spectra).

tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas.emission_masks(T_K, sfr, cooling_rate, t_cut)[source]¶

The mock’s emission selection: (keep, cold, star_forming, heated).

A cell is EXCLUDED if it is cold (T_K < t_cut), star forming (sfr > 0) or heated (cooling_rate >= 0); every other cell is kept.

Sign convention of TNG’s GFM_CoolingRate (the net cooling rate, du/dt of radiative cooling and heating): NEGATIVE when the cell loses energy (net cooling), POSITIVE when it gains energy (net heating, its temperature increasing). The mock keeps NET-COOLING cells (< 0) and drops heated ones (>= 0). Barnes et al. (2021, sec. 2.2.3) drop cells whose “net cooling rate is positive (i.e. it is increasing in temperature)”: the same heating criterion. cooling_rate=None (field absent): no cell is heated. Used by load_halo_gas() and by the truth estimators that reuse the mock’s selection (compute_hse_mass).

Load gas cells through a HaloSource and compute the emission arrays.

source.gas(halo_id, fields, selection) reads the cells. Temperature cut: max(tvir_frac * t_vir_K, t_min_k) if tvir_frac > 0 and t_vir_K > 0, else t_min_k.

clip_radius_kpc is the radius of the sphere about GroupPos the cells are clipped to, instead of sphere_r200c_factor * R200c (for selection='zoom' with a line-of-sight depth beyond 2 R200c); abund_table gives the solar mass fractions of Z_elements (must be the table of the photon spectra); apec_version selects the reference table built from that APEC version; z_solar_ag89 is the metal unit of the table’s metallicity axis (sim.table_metal_unit).

cell_resample_msun (sim.cell_resample_msun, a diagnostic arm; None = off): the cells are degraded to that particle mass before the emission (tngxmm.sim.emission.resample_cells()), with a Generator seeded by resample_key (integers, e.g. snapshot and halo); the record goes to cutout_info['resample'].

snapshot_fields (sim.snapshot_fields): None = every field the snapshot carries; 'mini' = the cells reduced to the fields of a TNG mini snapshot at every snapshot (tngxmm.ingest.loaders.mini_fields_fn(): X_H = 0.76 in the temperature and the emission measure, no net-heating cut), before the resampling and the emission.

tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas.load_reference_cells(source, halo_id, grp, h, a, UM, UL, UV, emin_kev, emax_kev, *, selection='zoom', clip_radius_kpc, temet_table=None, map_clip_radius_kpc=None, map_bands=(), verbose=False)[source]¶

Cells and temet-exact luminosities for the *_tngref columns and, with map_clip_radius_kpc, for the per-view reference maps, from ONE snapshot read (sim.lx_reference_gas ‘fof’ or ‘zoom’, read SEPARATELY from the mock’s gas, without the mock’s temperature, star-formation and cooling cuts).

selection='fof' reads every gas cell of the halo’s FoF group (loadHalo): the cell set used in every simulation.

selection='zoom' reads every gas cell of the halo’s original zoom (loadOriginalZoom: all its FoF groups and its outer fuzz), the cell set of the TNG official X-ray maps (temet sphMap_globalZoomOrig). Only the six fields temet needs plus the coordinates are read.

*_tngref cells: within clip_radius_kpc (physical) of GroupPos (the distance is not periodic, as load_halo_gas(); the campaign source wraps the coordinates at load with sim.periodic_wrap), luminosity in emin_kev-emax_kev from tngxmm.sim.temet_xray.temet_luminosity() on exactly those cells, so these arrays do not depend on the map options. Map cells: within map_clip_radius_kpc of GroupPos, one temet-exact luminosity per band of map_bands (e.g. '0.5-5.0') and the cell radius from Masses / Density. temet’s zero-luminosity floor (the smallest positive value of the set) is taken per cell set, as temet does for the set it is given.

Cost: one extra snapshot read per halo, of the WHOLE zoom region (TNG-Cluster: 1-7e7 gas cells, 20-30 s; about 6 GB peak at z = 1 for the snap-50 test halos, against about 1e6 FoF cells for the mock). All luminosities are temet’s: rest-frame band divided by (1+z).
