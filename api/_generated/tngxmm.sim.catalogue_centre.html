tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre¶

The catalogue position of a mock before its measurement error: where the catalogue of the cluster it replicates would place the projected gas.

A real target’s nominal position is its cluster’s entry in PSZ2 or ACT DR6 (three clusters in neither catalogue have X-ray positions); a mock’s is the position computed here, moved by an error drawn from the published positional error of the replicated cluster’s entry (tngxmm.photometry.find_centre.mock_nominal()). The rule follows the entry (tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.position_rule()).

SZ entries. Both catalogues find clusters as peaks of maps filtered with a matched filter whose signal template is the universal pressure profile of Arnaud et al. (2010), projected and convolved with the instrument beam, and report a position measured on the filtered map: PSZ2 (Planck Collaboration 2016, Sect. 2.1, “the locations of the peaks give the positions of our cluster candidates”; candidates closer than 10 arcmin are merged), ACT DR6 (ACT Collaboration 2025, Sect. 2.2.2) from the map filtered at the reference scale theta500 = 2.4 arcmin, on the segment of pixels above S/N 3 that makes the object. Here the Compton-y weight of every cell of the emitting cylinder (electron count x T, the cell’s integrated electron pressure) is binned on a map centred on the halo and filtered with the catalogue’s template (filtered_map(), sz_kernel()). The catalogue entry is the target’s own detection: the search starts at the local maximum of the filtered map reached from the halo centre by steepest ascent (steepest_ascent()), not at the highest point of the cylinder, which can be another object in the projection. From there each catalogue’s rule: PSZ2’s merge of candidates closer than 10 arcmin (Sect. 2.1), each maximum, from the highest down, taking the unassigned ones within reach, as the union of the three codes takes the highest detection as the reference position (Sect. 3.1, used for one code’s merge: a reading; merged_peak(); the 10’ of Sect. 2.1 against the 5’ of Sect. 7.1 is declared in OPEN_ITEMS P) and the peak, located to a fraction of a pixel by a quadratic fit to the 3 x 3 pixels about it (quadratic_peak()); ACT DR6’s S/N-weighted centre of the S/N > 3 segment that holds the detection, the map scaled so that the halo’s own maximum has the entry’s S/N (segment_centroid(): the ACT finder takes the centre of mass of the pixels above the S/N threshold of each object, Hilton et al. 2021, Sect. 2.2, and DR6 builds the segments and measures the positions above S/N 3, Sect. 2.2.2; DR6 does not say at which pixel fixed_SNR is read). A subcluster that is part of the detection moves the position, as in a catalogue (PSZ2, Sect. 4.5: for merging clusters the position of the most bound particle “can be significantly offset from the centre of the peak of the SZ distribution”). A view whose highest point lies outside the detection, whose detection is governed by a higher maximum than the one reached from the halo centre (a neighbour merged within 10 arcmin, or inside the same ACT segment; the S/N of the saddle between the two is recorded), whose position lies outside the halo’s projected R500 (after such a merge, or where the halo has no maximum of its own and the ascent ends on a neighbour), or whose position moves by more than a tenth of the entry’s published error when the map is cut to three quarters of the cylinder’s radius (the map holds only the cylinder’s gas; the test is made where the kernel about the position reaches beyond the cylinder), is flagged (global-elsewhere, merged, off-halo, edge; header CPFLAG, obs_meta.json). The flags select nothing: an off-halo view is what the catalogue would report for that projection, and it stays in every comparison (OPEN_ITEMS P). PSZ2’s filter scale here is the angular R500 of the entry’s mass MSZ, not the scale of highest S/N its detection chose (tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.position_rule()).

The filter. Template: the generalised NFW pressure profile of Arnaud et al. (2010, eqs. 11-12; c500 = 1.177, gamma = 0.3081, alpha = 1.0510, beta = 5.4905) in units of R500 (scale radius theta500 / c500, PSZ2 Sect. 2.1), integrated along the line of sight through the sphere of 5 R500, the radius of PSZ2’s Y5R500 (eq. 5). Beam: a Gaussian of the catalogue’s FWHM (ACT DR6 f150, PSZ2 143 GHz; tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions). The beam enters twice, once in the sky map and once in the template. Noise, ACT DR6: the filter is tau B / (C_l B^2 + N_w) (cmb_white_transfer()), a CMB + white model standing in for DR6’s map-estimated N, with the lensed CMB TT spectrum of the Planck 2018 best fit (packaged, CMB_TABLE) and the median f150 depth of the DR6 maps as N_w; DR6 forms its noise term from the maps themselves, “dominated by the CMB on large scales, and white noise on small scales” (Sect. 2.2). One band stands for the three DR6 combines, one median depth for the whole survey, and neither foregrounds nor the atmospheric 1/f noise are in the model (docs/assumptions/OPEN_ITEMS.md, section P). Noise, PSZ2: white, so the filtered map is the y map convolved with the template and with a Gaussian of sqrt(2) times the beam’s sigma; PSZ2’s matched multi-filter weights by the measured cross-channel spectrum of the maps (Sect. 2.1), which this one does not. The six channels let that filter take the primary CMB out by its spectrum (“multiband surveys remove the primary CMB signal”, Melin et al. 2006, p. 10), so what it leaves is closer to white than a single-channel CMB model would be; declared, with the measured effect of such a model, in section P.

X-ray positions (the three clusters in neither SZ catalogue): the local maximum reached from the halo centre on the projected rest-frame 0.5-2 keV emission of the cells smoothed with the Gaussian of the X-ray peak finder (photometry.peak_smoothing_arcsec, 16 arcsec): the X-ray peak without photon noise, flagged as the SZ positions are. The real positions of those clusters lie within 4.1 arcsec of the coordinates of Mantz et al. (2010b), who centre their clusters on the centroid of the 0.7-2 keV Chandra emission after masking point sources (Sect. 2) and give no aperture; the difference between the peak and an emission-weighted centroid is declared in docs/assumptions/OPEN_ITEMS.md, section P.

Numerics (design parameters, docs/assumptions/OPEN_ITEMS.md section P): the map pixel is the FWHM of the narrowest kernel scale (the beam; for the X-ray map the smoothing Gaussian) over PIXELS_PER_FWHM, coarsened when the cylinder would need more than MAX_MAP_PIXELS pixels per side; a cell’s weight is shared among the four pixels about its centre (cloud in cell, cic_map()).

References

arnaud2010 (template), psz2 and act_dr6 (filters, beams, merge rules, positions from the filtered maps), melin2006 (the multi-frequency filter of PSZ2’s MMF codes and its removal of the CMB), act_dr5 (the centre of mass of the segment), planck2018_params and camb (the CMB spectrum of the ACT noise model), naess2025 (its white level), mantz2010b (the X-ray positions of the three other clusters).

Functions

catalogue_position(rule, a_kpc, b_kpc, *, ...)

The catalogue position of a mock by rule (tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.position_rule()): on the filtered map (filtered_map(): the Compton-y weight y_weight for kind = 'sz', the band luminosity lum for kind = 'xray') the local maximum reached from the halo centre (steepest_ascent()), then the catalogue's estimator: peak (refined by quadratic_peak(); with merge_arcmin, PSZ2's merge of nearby candidates, merged_peak()) or snr-segment-centroid (ACT DR6, segment_centroid()).

cic_map(a_kpc, b_kpc, weight, pixel_kpc, half)

The cells' weight at (a, b) [kpc] on a map of (2 half + 1)^2 pixels of pixel_kpc, pixel (half, half) centred on (0, 0), each weight shared bilinearly among the four pixels about it (cloud in cell: the weighted mean position of the map is the cells'); img[iy, ix], weight off the map dropped.

cmb_cl()

(l, C_l [muK^2 sr]) of the packaged lensed TT spectrum (cmb_table_path(); the file holds D_l = l (l + 1) C_l / 2 pi).

cmb_table_path()

The packaged spectrum (CMB_TABLE).

cmb_white_transfer(n, pix_arcmin, ...)

The Fourier transfer (numpy.fft.rfft2 layout, side n) of the matched filter tau B / (C_l B^2 + N_w) applied to a y map seen through the beam B: tau the projected template of scale theta500_px (sum 1), B the Gaussian beam of beam_fwhm_arcmin, C_l the lensed CMB TT spectrum (cmb_cl()) and N_w the white noise of white_uk_arcmin [uK arcmin], both in uK^2 sr, so their ratio, the only thing the positions depend on, needs no tSZ spectral factor.

filtered_map(kind, a_kpc, b_kpc, weight, ...)

The filtered map of the cells' weight at the sky offsets (a_kpc, b_kpc) about the halo centre, binned over the cylinder of radius_kpc (cic_map()), at the angular-diameter distance d_a_kpc.

kernel_outside(kernel, inside, iy, ix)

The fraction of kernel (sum 1), centred on pixel (iy, ix), that falls outside the pixels inside: the share of the filtered value there that would come from beyond the cylinder, where the map has no gas.

local_maxima(F, inside)

(iy, ix) of the positive local maxima of F in inside: pixels equal to the largest value of their 3 x 3 neighbourhood.

map_pixel_kpc(fwhm_kpc, radius_kpc)

The map pixel [kpc]: fwhm_kpc / PIXELS_PER_FWHM, or, when that is larger, the pixel that fits the cylinder of radius_kpc into at most MAX_MAP_PIXELS pixels per side.

merged_peak(F, start, link_px, inside)

PSZ2's merge of candidates closer than link_px pixels (Planck Collaboration 2016, Sect.

quadratic_peak(img, iy, ix)

(dx, dy) [pixels] of the maximum of a quadratic surface fitted (least squares) to the 3 x 3 pixels of img about pixel (iy, ix), a local maximum.

saddle_snr(sn, a, b[, mask, steps])

The highest S/N t at which pixels a and b of the S/N map sn are still connected through pixels above t (within mask): the saddle of the bridge that joins two maxima in one segment (bisection to 1e-12 of the range in steps steps).

segment_centroid(F, start, snr, threshold[, ...])

ACT DR6's estimator (ACT Collaboration 2025, Sect.

steepest_ascent(F, iy, ix[, inside])

The local maximum of F reached from pixel (iy, ix) by steps to the highest of the eight neighbouring pixels (those in inside, default all) for as long as one is higher.

sz_kernel(theta500_px, beam_fwhm_px)

The kernel the y map is convolved with [pixels]: the projected template of scale theta500_px (upp_projected()) convolved with a Gaussian of sqrt(2) times the beam's sigma (the beam of the sky map and the beam of the template), sum 1, odd side, centred.

upp_pressure(x)

The shape of the universal pressure profile (Arnaud et al. 2010, eqs.

upp_projected(x)

The universal pressure profile integrated along the line of sight through the sphere of 5 R500, at the projected radii x [R500] (0 beyond the sphere); the shape of the SZ catalogues' signal template before the beam.

tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre.merged_peak(F, start, link_px, inside)[source]¶

PSZ2’s merge of candidates closer than link_px pixels (Planck Collaboration 2016, Sect. 2.1), with the rule Sect. 3.1 (p. 4) states for the union of the three codes, used here for one code’s merge (a reading, declared): the union “identifies the highest S/N detection as the reference position during the merge: any detections by other codes within 5 arcmin are identified with the reference position”. The positive local maxima of F are taken from the highest down; each one not yet assigned becomes a reference and takes every unassigned maximum within link_px of it. Returns the reference of the maximum start (start itself when it is no positive maximum).

In a chain A-B-C of maxima each within reach of the next, with C the highest and A out of C’s reach, C takes B and A stays a detection of its own, whatever the order of A and B; a merged candidate never merges again. The radius of the merge of one code’s candidates (Sect. 2.1, 10 arcmin) is kept although Sect. 7.1 (p. 17) gives 5 arcmin for the patch merge (docs/assumptions/OPEN_ITEMS.md, section P; rule version 4 replaced the greedy ascent of version 3, which no section states).

tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre.segment_centroid(F, start, snr, threshold, inside=None)[source]¶

ACT DR6’s estimator (ACT Collaboration 2025, Sect. 2.2.2): F scaled to an S/N map in which the halo’s own detection, the local maximum start, has the entry’s snr, the connected region of S/N > threshold (within inside, the cylinder, when given; side neighbours, the default of scipy.ndimage.label) that holds the start, and its S/N-weighted centre. The start always belongs to its segment; a higher maximum in the same segment then has an S/N above snr (flag merged, catalogue_position()). Returns (x, y) [px] and the region’s mask. ValueError when the start has no positive signal or snr is not above the threshold.

The catalogue position of a mock by rule (tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.position_rule()): on the filtered map (filtered_map(): the Compton-y weight y_weight for kind = 'sz', the band luminosity lum for kind = 'xray') the local maximum reached from the halo centre (steepest_ascent()), then the catalogue’s estimator: peak (refined by quadratic_peak(); with merge_arcmin, PSZ2’s merge of nearby candidates, merged_peak()) or snr-segment-centroid (ACT DR6, segment_centroid()).
