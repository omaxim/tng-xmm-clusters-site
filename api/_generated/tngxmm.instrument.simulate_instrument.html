tngxmm.instrument.simulate_instrument¶

Fast EPIC instrument stage: camera partition, PSF, masks, RMF, sky and particle backgrounds, AGN, pn out-of-time events, event-level roll and rebinning, per-camera products, merged image and coverage map.

Why a fast stage: the upstream xmm_simulator path spends 10-20 min per camera on machinery whose outputs are statistically identical to closed-form draws (494 FFT PSF convolutions of a flat sky background; a per-pixel loop over ~2e5 active pixels although the ARF cube is radially symmetric; a per-photon double loop for the cluster). The replacements are exact applications of Poisson thinning and splitting. Camera partitioning: each parent photon goes to at most ONE camera by a single categorical draw with p_i = ARF_i(theta, E)/A_gen x t_i/t_gen, so the per-camera streams are independent Poisson processes with the single-camera rates (needs A_gen >= sum_i max ARF_i, hence AREA_CM2 = 4000). Event-level merge: the roll and the 2.5”->4” rebin act on event coordinates, so the merged mock is integer Poisson counts like the real mosaics.

The stage follows the donor observation: the filter, the SUBMODE, the active CCDs and the readout window of every camera, and the cluster placement (the donor’s core offset) come from the donor package. The ARF and sky-background energy grid extends beyond upstream’s 10.01 keV (arf_energy_max_kev); the pn effective area carries the PATTERN efficiency; exposure maps are built by inverse mapping with a count-weighted vignetting energy; the headers are written from scratch (optical axis, filter, PSF model, vignetting energy, provenance); the AGN list is a FITS table; the gzip container is deterministic.

References

Eckert, D., xmm_simulator 0.2.0 (point_sources, event_file, imaging, background: the models and calibration products this stage re-implements). Moretti et al. 2003 (logN-logS and unresolved CXB of the sky model). XMM-Newton SOC CCF; Jansen et al. 2001; Strueder et al. 2001; Turner et al. 2001. Vianello et al. 2015 (threeML spectral models).

Module Attributes

CAMERA_OFFSET_ARCMIN

measured inter-camera boresight offsets [arcmin, detector frame] (tngxmm.instrument.calibrate_boresight).

PN_OOT_FRAC

pn out-of-time fraction, Full Frame (4.6 ms / 73.4 ms, Users' Handbook)

P_CUM_TOL

the camera-assignment probability sum may exceed 1 by this much (rounding) before the stage refuses the view

PN_COLUMN_ARCMIN

200 rows x 150 um = 200 x 4.1" [arcmin] (Strueder et al. 2001; Users' Handbook section 3.3).

PN_OOT_MODEL

every X-ray event is out-of-time with probability oot_frac and lands uniformly anywhere on its own CCD column

PN_BAND1_RAWY_MIN

PPS pn band 1 (0.2-0.5 keV) keeps single-pixel events (PATTERN == 0) with RAWY above PN_BAND1_RAWY_MIN, i.e. drops the rows next to the readout node.

PN_ROW_ARCSEC

pn pixel row pitch on the sky [arcsec] (150 um; Strueder et al. 2001; Handbook section 3.3).

PN_EDGE_ROWS

'drop' removes the events of the 12 rows inside the footprint's edge (pn_rawy counts from that edge); 'footprint' leaves the cut to the footprint, which already ends at the readout rows (the PPS exposure maps are zero at RAWY <= 12 and the cleaned event lists empty there)

PSF_MODEL

the on-axis King profile of the camera's XRT*_XPSF CCF, independent of energy and off-axis angle

DONOR_FILTERS

filters a donor camera may carry (CCF E*_FILTERTRANSX extensions)

VIG_AXIS_RULES

where each camera's vignetting is centred in the mask frame: 'pointing', or 'ccf' (the telescope axis of the XMM calibration files, tngxmm.instrument.calibration_axis.telescope_axis_arcmin())

REALISM_SWITCHES

the spec fields (InstrumentSpec) that InstrumentOptions.from_spec copies one for one, with vig_energy_rule

CLUSTER_PSF_MODELS

the on-axis King profile (the default, what the fit convolves with), the King parameters interpolated in energy and off-axis angle, or the ELLBETA envelope the AGN are drawn with (a second calibration of the same PSF; the closure arm v1_psfell)

SKY_MODELS

values of InstrumentOptions.sky_model

PN_HARD_BANDS

values of InstrumentOptions.pn_hard_band

QPB_UNITS

values of the ESAS particle-background options of InstrumentOptions

Functions

agn_channel_cdfs(resp, sources, t_live[, ...])

Per-source (x, y, expected counts, channel CDF) for one camera: the point_sources.pts_box model PhAbs(NH, z) x Powerlaw, flux normalised through templates_pts_hard.dat; off-axis angles in the detector frame.

apply_vignetting_calibration(resps, opt)

resps (camera -> response) with the vignetting centre that the options ask for: vig_axis = 'ccf' centres each camera on the telescope axis of the calibration files in opt.ccf_dir (tngxmm.instrument.calibration_axis); mos_rga_vignetting = 'ccf' adds to the MOS the azimuthal modulation by the RGA that the SAS calibration layer computes (tngxmm.instrument.rga_obscuration), about the same centre.

band_gaps(opt, inst)

Band code -> PI range [keV] left out of that band's image: the pn 4.5-12 keV band without 7.8-8.2 keV with pn_hard_band = 'pps' (tngxmm.instrument.donor_corner_qpb.PPS_BAND_GAPS_EV), else none.

camera_events(resp, eobs, x_pix, y_pix, ...)

All events for one camera: (X, Y, chan), X/Y in SKY coordinates (mask-pixel frame of the pointing axis).

camera_model_images(opt, inst, rp, t_live, ...)

The model images of one camera that the fit's ESAS background reads (qpb_model_images), per band code:

camera_qpb_maps(opt, inst, rp, ccf_dir, ...)

(QPB density maps or None, soft-proton detector map or None) of one camera on the mask grid.

catalogue_position_parameters(par)

The catalogue position a photon file's parameters group carries (tngxmm.sim.generate_photons.generate_photons(), tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre), as header keys: {'SZ': {deg, SZRULE, SZTHETA, SZBEAM[, SZSNR, SZNOISE], CATPOSV, CPFLAG}} for an SZ position, {'XR': {deg, XRSMOOTH, CATPOSV, CPFLAG}} for an X-ray position (deg the position in the frame of xsky/ysky; CATPOSV the rule version, CPFLAG the position's flag); empty for a file without one (a view made before the rule, whose centre search then refuses it).

channel_cdf_from_coarse(resp, coarse_spec)

(cdf, total) of the RMF-convolved channel distribution for a spectrum given as a DENSITY [per keV] on box_ene_mean (interp to the MC grid, x bin width, rmf.convolve), done once.

check_agn_switches(opt)

ValueError unless the AGN switches name implemented choices (agn_lognlogs, agn_psf, agn_galactic_nh and agn_norm, whose only choice is the published Moretti et al. counts).

check_camera_partition(p_cum, t_gen, a_gen, ...)

Raise ValueError when the camera-assignment probabilities of the parent photons sum to more than 1 (p_cum: the cumulative sum over the cameras per photon, ARF_c / A_gen x t_c / t_gen).

check_pn_gap_level(opt, background, corner_qpb)

pn_hard_band = 'pps' with a donor background needs qpb_level = 'corners': the pn FWC spectrum is drawn at the donor's corner scale (or at the FWC level when the donor has no pn corner level) and binned in the PPS PI ranges, with no further factor.

check_psf_model(opt)

ValueError unless psf_model names an implemented cluster PSF (CLUSTER_PSF_MODELS).

check_qpb_model_options(opt, ...)

Validate qpb_units, soft_proton_map and qpb_model_images; qpb_units = 'corners' needs the donor's unit factors for every camera of the view.

donor_core_offset(*records)

The donor's core offset (core_dx_mask_arcmin, core_dy_mask_arcmin, mask frame [arcmin]) from the first record (mapping or object) that carries both as finite numbers; None otherwise.

donor_filters(*records)

Per-camera donor filters from the first record that carries them.

donor_offset_displacement(core_offset_arcmin)

Cluster displacement [arcmin, mask frame] of the donor-offset placement: the donor's core offset, shrunk along its direction to max_offset_arcmin (the dither cap that keeps the largest aperture in the field; None = no cap; a cap <= 0 puts the cluster at the pointing).

donor_submodes(*records)

Per-camera donor submodes (camera -> SAS SUBMODE) from the first record that carries any: a submodes dict (camera -> mode), or the columns submode_mos1, submode_mos2 and pn_submode/pn_mode (donor package, targets row; empty/NaN = unknown).

drop_out_of_fov(resp, X, Y, chan[, off_px])

Remove the events (sky X/Y [mask px], channels) whose detector pixel round(sky - off) lies outside fov_map(): the OUT_OF_FOV selection of the PPS images.

efficiency_thinning(resp, X, Y, rng[, off_px])

Boolean keep-mask of the events at sky X, Y [mask px]: u < efficiency at the detector pixel round(sky - off) (0 off the grid).

ellbeta_offsets_energy_angle(resp, table, ...)

PSF offsets [mask pixels] of cluster photons of energy e_kev at detector mask pixel (x_det, y_det) from the ELLBETA envelope of the CCF (tngxmm.instrument.point_sources.ellbeta_table()): per photon the parameters at the nearest tabulated energy and linear in the off-axis angle from the vignetting centre, the major axis tangential, the profile of tngxmm.instrument.point_sources.ellbeta_offsets() (King inside its normalisation radius plus, where tabulated, the Gaussian core).

ellbeta_scatter(resp, table, xs, ys, chan, ...)

ELLBETA PSF offsets [mask px] of the photons of one point source at sky mask pixel (xs, ys): per tabulated energy nearest to the photons' energies chan [keV], the envelope at the source's off-axis angle (detector frame), major axis tangential.

expectation_image(resp, X, Y, w, off_px, ...)

An expectation carried to the camera's image grid like its events: the pixels outside the field of view (drop_out_of_fov()) and, for the pn, at RAWY <= PN_BAND1_RAWY_MIN dropped, the roll applied, weights w histogrammed on edges; each pixel split into 2 x 2 sub-positions (the events are spread +-0.5 px).

fov_map(resp)

Detector pixels inside the field of view: on the mask (mask == 1) AND with non-zero effective area at some energy (the vignetting CCF ends at 15'; the pn and MOS CCD corners beyond it are active silicon that sees only the particle background).

generate_agn_sources(resp, rng[, lognlogs, ...])

Draw the field AGN population (Moretti+03 logN-logS), as xmm_simulator.point_sources.gen_sources but Generator-driven.

in_band(chan, elo, ehi[, gap])

Channel mask of a band [elo, ehi) keV without the PI range gap [keV] (None: no gap).

king_offsets(resp, n, rng)

PSF offsets [mask pixels] from the analytic King EEF inverse r = r0 sqrt(u^(-1/(alpha-1)) - 1) (the corrected upstream sampler).

king_offsets_energy_angle(resp, table, ...)

PSF offsets [mask pixels] of photons of energy e_kev at off-axis angle theta_arcmin: the King EEF inverse of king_offsets() with r0 and alpha interpolated (bilinearly, clipped to the tabulated range) in energy and angle in the camera's KING_PARAMS (king_table()).

king_table(psf_file)

The KING_PARAMS extension of an XRT PSF calibration file as a grid: e_kev (energies), theta_arcmin (off-axis angles), r0_arcmin and alpha (energy x angle), without the energy rows whose slope alpha is not above 1 at every angle.

output_pixel(sky_deg, mask_pix_arcmin, ...)

0-indexed pixel of the merged output grid of a position sky_deg (x, y offsets [deg] in the frame of the photon list's xsky / ysky), carried as the photons are: onto the mask grid (mask_pix_arcmin per pixel), displaced by the placement (dx_px, dy_px), rolled (cos, sin = ca, sa) and binned on the output grid (wpix mask pixels per output pixel, n4 pixels).

pn_band1_single_pixel(resp, X, Y, chan, rng, ...)

Boolean mask of the pn events that the PPS band-1 image keeps (PATTERN == 0 && RAWY > 12), for the events (X, Y [sky mask px], chan [keV]) of the band.

pn_out_of_time(resp, X, Y, oot_frac, rng[, ...])

pn out-of-time events, full-column model.

pn_rawy(resp, X, Y[, off_px, readout_tilt_deg])

Row number RAWY (1 = the row next to the readout node of the event's CCD column) of events at sky positions X, Y [mask px]; inf where the column holds no active silicon.

qpb_expectation(resp, t_live, bands[, ...])

tngxmm.instrument.simulate_instrument.pn_out_of_time(resp, X, Y, oot_frac, rng, off_px=(0.0, 0.0), readout_tilt_deg=-24.434, column_arcmin=13.666666666666664, chan=None, cti=None, ccd_map=None)[source]¶

pn out-of-time events, full-column model.

A photon that arrives while the CCD is being read out (charge shifted along the column towards the node) is registered with a RAWY that has nothing to do with where it landed: the readout phase is uniform over the frame, so the assigned row is uniform over the whole column, on both sides of the source, and the event stays in its own column (RAWX) and on its own CCD (each pn CCD is read out through its own node at the outer edge of the camera; the two CCD rows meet at the boundary near the detector centre). This is how SAS epchain withoutoftime=Y simulates them: “a new RAWY is simulated by randomly shifting the pattern along the RAWY axis”. Each event is out-of-time independently with probability oot_frac (Poisson splitting: in-time and OoT streams are independent Poisson processes).

Energy (cti, a PnCtiLine): the charge of an OoT event is transferred over the rows between where it landed and the node, but epevents corrects it for the CTI of its assigned RAWY, so its energy becomes E x c(A, y_rec) / c(A, y_true) (tngxmm.instrument.pn_oot_energy, from the EPN_CTI calibration): too high for events assigned farther from the node than where they landed, too low for those assigned closer (Users’ Handbook 3.3.10). The row of an event is its distance to the node in rows plus one half (the centre of its RAWY); its CCD is read from ccd_map (the CCD label map of the camera on the mask grid) at its true position. cti=None: energies unchanged.

X, Y: event positions, sky mask-pixel frame (detector = sky - off_px); chan: event energies [keV] (optional, returned shifted when cti is given). Returns (X, Y, chan, kept): positions (OoT ones moved), energies, and the boolean mask of events still on active silicon (OoT events landing on a dead pixel, the row-boundary lane or outside the field are lost, as in the real camera); X, Y and chan are already filtered by kept.

Draw the field AGN population (Moretti+03 logN-logS), as xmm_simulator.point_sources.gen_sources but Generator-driven. Returns the source table (srcnum, X, Y, Fx, Gamma, NH, z, 0, 0) in the 512-px simulation-box frame.
