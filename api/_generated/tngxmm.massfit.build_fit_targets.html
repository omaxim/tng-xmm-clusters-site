tngxmm.massfit.build_fit_targets¶

Mock fit targets and product links in the fitter format.

Builds hydrostatic-fit target lists and symlink farms for mock views in the SAME format the observation fitter consumes (snapshot-qualified names and obsids, one view per physical cluster, theta500 strata and a theta500 floor):

tngxmm massfit build-fit-targets --root <campaigns>/<campaign> \
    --strata 5,7,10 --per-stratum 50 --one-per-cluster \
    --img-root <img_root> --out fit_targets.csv

Per selected view it

  • takes the catalogue entry of the cluster the view replicates (its physics donor, mock_entry()) and the redshift that entry would give the view: the redshift of the photons plus an error drawn from the entry’s published error when its redshift is photometric (tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.mock_redshift()), the target’s z; a view made before its entry’s catalogue-position rule is refused (regenerate it);

  • finds the centre and the first guess by the procedure of the real observations’ seed table (measure_seed(), tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.centre_and_seed()) at that redshift: from the view’s simulated catalogue position (its catalogue position before the error, tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre, moved by an error drawn from the published positional error of the entry), the guarded X-ray peak, the luminosity inside the iterated first-guess R500 measured with tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx.seed_lx() (masked-clipped, the view’s SAS list masked); the table has the columns lx_seed_photometry, lx_<ap>_flag, lx_n_sources_masked and the seed columns, a value is NaN unless its flag is ok, and a view whose measurement failed has seed_status = 'error';

  • links <img_root>/tngc/<snap>_<halo>_v<view>/P<obsid><tail>.FTZ to the per-camera products, obsid = f'{snap:02d}{halo:07d}v{view}' (11 chars; the snapshot is part of the id because halo_id 0 exists at all 16 snapshots; INTERFACES section 2);

  • writes the targets CSV: name (mock_<snap>_<halo>_v<view>), obsid, z, ra, dec, nh_1e22, hse (= true M500/1e14 so ratio_fit_over_pub is fit/truth), lx_0p35mpc, lx_1mpc, lx_r500, m500_true, snap, halo_id, view, theta500_arcmin, zoom_target_id, split, campaign, the entry columns (MOCK_ENTRY_COLUMNS: physics_donor_id, catalogue, r90_arcmin, position_rule, position_flag, z_obs, z_offset, z_type, z_err), view_dir, the seed columns and the offsets (MOCK_OFFSET_COLUMNS) that tngxmm.validation.catalogue_moves compares with the real catalogue-to-centre moves.

The Lx seed only sets the optimiser START, never the reported mass.

The campaign split tables (splits/zoom_target_ids.csv, splits/split_by_zoom_target.csv) are parameters whose defaults are the frozen copies in the data package ($TNGXMM_DATA/tables/splits). H0 and Om0 come from the CampaignSpec preset (the production preset by default).

SEED_COLUMNS

MOCK_OFFSET_COLUMNS

the mock's offsets [arcsec], for the comparison with the real table's catalogue-to-centre moves (tngxmm.validation.catalogue_moves): catalogue position before its error (the SZ position, or the X-ray position of an entry of neither SZ catalogue) - true centre, nominal (drawn catalogue) position - that position, centre found - nominal position

MOCK_ENTRY_COLUMNS

the physics donor, its catalogue (psz2 | act_dr6 | other), the 90 per cent radius of its position [arcmin], the rule the mock's position was made with (sz:<catalogue> or xray) and the position's flags (ok, or global-elsewhere, merged, off-halo, edge comma-joined, tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre), the redshift the photons were made at (z_obs), the drawn error of a photometric catalogue redshift (z_offset; the target's z is z_obs + z_offset) and the entry's redshift type and published error

measure_seed(view_dir, z_obs, texp, nh, spec)

The centre and first guess of a view by the procedure of the real observations (tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.centre_and_seed()) on image_merged.fits and its coverage, at the redshift the view's catalogue entry would give it (z_obs, the redshift the photons were made at, plus an error drawn from the physics donor's published error when its redshift is photometric, tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.mock_redshift()): the search starts at the simulated catalogue position (the view's catalogue position before its error moved by an error drawn from the published positional error of the physics donor's entry, tngxmm.photometry.find_centre.mock_nominal()), the view's SAS source list masked (massfit.source_list = 'sas').

mock_entry(view_dir, z_obs[, donor_id, hdr, ...])

MOCK_ENTRY_COLUMNS and z_fit of a view: the catalogue entry of its physics donor (donor_id, else from obs_meta.json), the flags of its catalogue position (CPFLAG: ok, or global-elsewhere when the filtered map's maximum over the cylinder lies outside the detection, merged when the detection's highest maximum is not the one reached from the halo centre, off-halo when the position lies outside the halo's projected R500, edge when the filter support crosses the cylinder edge; tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre) and the redshift that entry would give it (tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.mock_redshift(), keyed by the view's VIEW_ID).

mock_position_rule(donor[, spec])

The catalogue-position rule of the entry of donor under the preset spec (tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.position_rule()), the rule a view of it must have been made with.

physics_donor(view_dir)

The physics donor of a view (the real cluster it replicates), from its obs_meta.json.

select_views(camp[, from_targets, strata, ...])

The selection: reuse a targets CSV, or theta500 strata (seeded), then the floor.
