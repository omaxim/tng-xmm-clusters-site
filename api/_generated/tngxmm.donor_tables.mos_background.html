tngxmm.donor_tables.mos_background¶

Corner QPB and IN/OUT soft-proton measurements of the donors’ EPIC cameras, from the cached PPS event lists.

For every donor and camera of the run (MOS1, MOS2 and, since the pn hard band leaves out the Cu lines, the pn), within the flare good time intervals of the PPS and summed over the exposures of the camera:

  1. the corner QPB scale in each PPS band and in 10-12 keV (corner_<band>_<cam>, its corner counts ncorner_<band>_<cam>) and the stage QPB factor qpb_stage_<band>_<cam> = scale / the band’s conversion of the FWC reference (fwc; tngxmm.instrument.donor_corner_qpb.corner_scale()). The reference is that of the donor’s epoch (fwc_epoch_<cam>, the first revolution of the epoch; tngxmm.instrument.fwc_state) built from the closed exposures in the normal CCD state, and the scales leave out the corners of the CCDs the fit leaves out of its images (left_out_<cam>: the MOS CCDs in the anomalous state of the soft-CCD table);

  2. the De Luca & Molendi (2004) IN/OUT 10-12 keV excess (excess_<cam>, excess_err_<cam>, R_<cam>, n_in_<cam>): the in-FOV events beyond max(1.5 R500, 3’) of the cluster and within 12’ of the pointing, per arcmin^2 of unvignetted exposure of the camera’s PPS exposure map (> 0 in that region) and per second, minus the corner-scaled FWC in-FOV rate and the CXB, and the stage’s soft-proton rate sp_rate_<cam> the excess corresponds to (tngxmm.instrument.donor_inout_sp); sp_gamma_<cam> = 0 and qpb_corr_<cam> = 1 are the neutral values of the stage’s remaining soft-proton / QPB parameters. The 10-12 keV band lies above the Cu K-alpha gap (7.8-8.2 keV) of the PPS pn hard band, so the pn is measured with the same selection as the MOS; its excess is a lower bound, because the pn corners also receive focused soft protons and photons (Marelli et al. 2021; ledger entry instrument.pn_soft_protons_inout);

  3. gti_over_img_<cam>: the on-time of the reconstructed good time intervals over the ONTIME of the PPS images (check of the flare reconstruction, 1 when they agree), n_exp_<cam> the exposures used.

The good time intervals of an exposure are reconstructed from its flare background time series (FBKTSR): the quietest bins that add up to the PPS image’s ONTIME when the image is available (gti_matching_ontime()), else the bins below the series’ optimised cut (flare_gti()). An exposure without a flare series cannot be screened and is not used; a camera with no usable exposure has no values (NaN columns), and the stage keeps its in-FOV background factors for it.

One donor is measured by measure_donor_mos_background(); measure_all() runs the donors that have no result in the work area yet (one JSON file per donor, so a run can be interrupted, resumed and sharded over processes or nodes) and assemble_table() builds the table the instrument stage reads (tngxmm.instrument.donor_corner_qpb.donor_corner_band_scales(), tngxmm.instrument.donor_inout_sp.donor_soft_protons()).

The MOS background table: one row per donor of catalogue from the stored results, columns of table_columns() (NaN for what a camera lacks, empty error_<cam> and left_out_<cam> text when there is none).

bands()

Integer (column, row) on the image grid of events with sky coordinates x, y [0.05''] of the event list header (linear sky columns X, Y -> RA, Dec through their TCRVL / TCRPX / TCDLT keywords).

image_ontime(header)

The ONTIME of a PPS image: the largest of its ONTIME / ONTIMEnn header values [s].

in_region(expmap, wcs, centre_world, ...)

Boolean map of the IN region on the grid of the exposure map expmap: exposed pixels beyond max(1.5 R500, 3') of centre_world (RA, Dec [deg]) and within 12' of pointing_world.

input_signature(donor, fwc_reference, cxb, ...)

Digest of what a donor's result depends on: the measurement version, the donor's catalogue row (position, aperture, peak pixel), the cached event lists and flare series (names and sizes), the PPS image products of the donor (the footprint and exposure maps the camera reads), the calibration the values are referred to (the rule and the per-epoch conversions of the FWC reference, the CXB rates of the CCF) and the CCDs its corner scales leave out.

left_out_text(ccds)

The CCDs left out of a camera's corner scales as table text ('4 5'; empty for none).

load_result(work_dir, obsid, signature)

A donor's stored row, or None when absent, unreadable or measured from other inputs.

measure_all(catalogue, fwc_reference, cxb, ...)

Measure the donors of catalogue (shard shard) that have no current result in work_dir, with workers processes; a status DataFrame (obsid, measured now, seconds, stored, and error_<cam> of each camera holding the error message or None) of the shard's donors.

measure_camera(donor, cam, fwc, cxb, ...[, ...])

The table values of one camera of one donor (suffixed _<cam>); {'error_<cam>': 'no event list'} or 'no flare series' when the camera has none.

measure_donor_mos_background(donor, ...[, ...])

One row of the MOS background table: obsid and the values of each camera of cameras.

read_exposure(ev_path, fbk_path, ...)

One exposure of a camera: events within the good time intervals and the IN-region measurements.

result_path(work_dir, obsid)

The JSON file of a donor's result.

screening_gti(fbk_path, ontime_image)

Good time intervals (N, 2) [s] of an exposure from its flare series: the quietest bins adding up to ontime_image when that is given and finite, else the bins at or below the series' FLCUTTHR.

select_shard(obsids, shard)

The donors of shard k of n (shard = [k, n], 0-based): every n-th obsid of the sorted list.

store_result(work_dir, obsid, signature, ...)

Write a donor's result (temporary file, then rename: an interrupted run never leaves a partial file).

table_columns(cameras)

Columns of the MOS background table, in the order of the reference table of the 195 first donors, then the FWC epoch and the CCDs left out of the corner scales (fwc_epoch_<cam>, left_out_<cam>).

Digest of what a donor’s result depends on: the measurement version, the donor’s catalogue row (position, aperture, peak pixel), the cached event lists and flare series (names and sizes), the PPS image products of the donor (the footprint and exposure maps the camera reads), the calibration the values are referred to (the rule and the per-epoch conversions of the FWC reference, the CXB rates of the CCF) and the CCDs its corner scales leave out. A stored result with another signature is measured again. donor: the catalogue row (mapping or Series).
