tngxmm.massfit.fit_hydrostatic¶

Hydrostatic M500 from XMM per-camera images with mbproj2d (2-D forward fit).

The fit has switches for seeding (seed: deterministic sampler), point-source masking, the optical axis from the header, the rebin-centre convention, per-fit provenance, the metallicity (fixed 0.3 / per population / free), the XSPEC abundance table (lodd / angr), the mean molecular weight (mbproj2d’s / full ionisation, set_molecular_weight), the PSF in the model (none / the on-axis King profile, fit_psf) and the pn out-of-time events (none / a fixed template per pn image, pn_oot). CampaignSpec massfit carries the switches; fit_switches_from_spec() maps them, and the fitfix bundle of sub-switches (FITFIX_DEFAULTS, --fitfix k=v) holds the priors, the background seeding and the optimiser stages.

For one cluster every per-camera image in four bands

2000 = 0.5-1.0 3000 = 1.0-2.0 4000 = 2.0-4.5 5000 = 4.5-12 [keV]

is loaded as a separate mbproj2d Image with its own (rmf, arf) chosen from the camera (EPN/EMOS1/EMOS2) and filter (Thin1/Medium/Thick) of the image header (fit-fix responses = 'per-observation': the RMF and on-axis ARF SAS made for the image’s own exposure, tngxmm.instrument.obs_responses), and its own exposure map. The soft bands fix the gas density; the hard bands fix the temperature (and hence the mass) through the band ratios. Band 5 (4.5-12 keV) is the thermometer (an upstream log(0) typo is worked around in patch_apec); band 1 (0.2-0.5 keV) is dropped (no temperature leverage, most exposed to N_H errors, flares and SWCX).

Model: NFW dark-matter halo parameterised directly by M500 (ProfileMassNFWfnM, overdensity 500) + hydrostatic equilibrium + a Vikhlinin density profile (mode='single', keeping the outer steepening term) + a flat metallicity (Lodders 2003 units, the abund lodd of mbproj2d’s rate tables): free with a flat prior by default, or fixed at a given value; c200 free with the flat prior in ln c over 1-10 of Sanders et al. (2018), or (cm_prior = 'dutton14') with the log-normal prior about the c200(M200, z) relation of Dutton & Maccio (2014) evaluated at the sampled mass (tngxmm.massfit.cm_prior). R500 is not fitted: it follows from M500 and the redshift through the critical density. Reported M500/R500 are TOTAL enclosed values from mbproj2d.Phys. Background (massfit.qpb_model = 'esas-fwc-corners', XMM-ESAS and X-COP; build_esas_background()): per image a fixed particle-background template whose level the observation’s corners set, a vignetted sky and the residual soft protons on the CCF flare maps, both with free levels and no prior, and the pn out-of-time template. The alternative 'mixture' is mbproj2d’s BackModelVigNoVig per image, seeded from the outer annulus with a 0.05 dex Gaussian prior (Sanders et al. 2018), widened to fallback_bg_prior_dex where the seed did not come from a clean annulus beyond 2 R500. Optimiser: two nlopt stages (backgrounds seeded from the data frozen or not in stage 1, then all released); sampler: emcee via mbproj2d (its moves, the walker start and the convergence diagnostics of every result.json: tngxmm.massfit.sample_posterior).

Randomness: every draw of a single-target fit comes from numpy’s GLOBAL RNG (mbproj2d walker starts, emcee 3.1.6 copying the global state, the randsamples shuffle); the nlopt optimisers used are deterministic. seed calls numpy.random.seed(seed) at the start of each target, so a single-target fit with nprocesses=1 is reproducible exactly.

Rate cache: mbproj2d caches XSPEC rate tables in ./mbproj2d_cache (cwd-relative, one lock directory per key). cache_dir (or $TNGXMM_MBPROJ_CACHE_DIR) points it at a PRIVATE directory; never share a cache with production or another agent (a killed lock holder hangs every other reader; check_preflight removes stale locks).

References

mbproj2d, sanders2018 and sanders2018_bgprior (method, priors), vikhlinin2006 (density profile), nfw (mass profile), emcee (sampler), nlopt (optimiser), heasoft_xspec, apec and atomdb (rate tables), xmm_ccf_sas (responses and vignetting), cosmology_planck2015 (pipeline cosmology).
