tngxmm.obs.references.parsers¶

The paper-source parsers of the literature reference-mass table.

One parser per paper reads the paper’s own table (the LaTeX source of the arXiv e-print, a machine-readable table or a catalogue FITS file) from the references area (tngxmm.obs.references.sources) and returns the rows of reference_masses.csv; build_reference_masses() runs them all and cross-matches the rows with the bank clusters. The cross-match, ref_id, independence and the writer are those of tngxmm.obs.reference_masses. The papers themselves are not in the repository: the area is fetched from arXiv and from the data package and checked against manifest.tsv beside this module.

xmm_ml_dir is the references area root (it carries catalogs/<Paper>/...) and mantz_dir is <references>/mantz; the names are those of the directories the parsers were first written against.

Module Attributes

SOURCES

per-paper conventions (columns shared by every row of the paper); the PSZ2 / ACT entries stay in the package (they are used by the cross-match) and are merged in below

NOT_PARSED

sources present on disk that carry no parsable per-cluster mass table

Functions

build_reference_masses(observations, *, ...)

The reference-mass table of the bank clusters and a build report (counts, unmatched, skipped).

load_sz_catalogues(xmm_ml_dir)

PSZ2, ACT DR6 and ACT DR5 catalogue frames (all rows; filtered by the cross-match).

parse_applegate2014(xmm_ml_dir, **_)

WtG III Table (mass_summary.txt): M(<1.5 Mpc) of the p(z) and the colour-cut method.

parse_dec(s)

Dec [deg] from +09:09:24.0, $-$06:05:35.6, --12:59:29.0, $+09^\circ09'24''$.

parse_herbonnet2020(xmm_ml_dir[, scratch_dir])

Herbonnet+20 mass table (NFW M200c, M500c; aperture R500, M500c) with BCG positions.

parse_hoekstra2015(xmm_ml_dir, **_)

H15 tab:masses (main) and tab:duffy (appendix): projected, aperture and NFW masses.

parse_lovisari2020(xmm_ml_dir[, psz2])

Lovisari+20 Table A1 (longtable tab:properties): column (3) M500, the hydrostatic mass inside its own R500; position from the PSZ2 entry nearest the Planck-ESZ-name Galactic coordinates (< 0.15 deg), as for Wicker+23.

parse_mahdavi2013(xmm_ml_dir, **_)

Mahdavi+13 table2 (M_WL, M_hydro at r500^WL) with table1 positions and obsids.

parse_mantz2010b(mantz_dir, **_)

Mantz+10b tab:mltbcs/mltreflex/mltmacs/mltmore: M500 = Mgas/fgas with r500.

parse_mantz2016(mantz_dir, **_)

Mantz+16 erratum Table 2: the WL mass inside the gas-mass r500 (1e15 Msun).

parse_martino2014(xmm_ml_dir, **_)

Martino+14 hydrostatic M2500/M1000/M500 (Chandra and XMM) with sample-table positions.

parse_okabe2016(xmm_ml_dir, **_)

Okabe & Smith 2016 tab:mass (main) and tab:mass2 (appendix, shear/contamination corrected).

parse_ra(s)

RA [deg] from 00:37:06.65 or 00$^{\rm h}$37$^{\rm m}$06.9$^{\rm s}$.

parse_value(s)

(value, err_lo, err_hi) of a LaTeX number: 11.7^{+3.0}_{-3.0}, 1.07_{-0.16}^{+0.09}, 5.2\pm0.7, 10.77 $\pm$ 4.10, 4.6; NaNs for x, \nodata, -, empty.

parse_wicker2023(xmm_ml_dir[, psz2])

Wicker+23 appendix longtable: M_tot,500 (hydrostatic, Lovisari+20); position from the PSZ2 entry nearest the Planck-name Galactic coordinates (< 0.15 deg).

source_paths(xmm_ml_dir, mantz_dir)

Every source file the table is read from (sha256 recorded in the provenance).
