tngxmm.massfit.qpb_template¶

Particle-background templates of a real observation as XMM-ESAS and X-COP build them (massfit.qpb_model = 'esas-fwc-corners'; ledger fit.particle_background_esas).

For one exposure of one EPIC camera and one PPS band this module makes, on the grid of the exposure’s PPS image:

  • the expected quiescent particle background (QPB) counts: the CCF filter-wheel-closed (FWC) events of the band (camera quality mask, PATTERN limit of the PPS images) of the closed exposures of the observation’s epoch in the normal CCD state (tngxmm.instrument.fwc_state; the epoch the corner scales refer to, fwc_epoch_<cam>), histogrammed per CCD on the ESAS detector grid (780 x 780, 50 DET units = 2.5” per pixel, DETX / DETY from -19500 to 19500), smoothed within each CCD’s area of that epoch by normalised convolution (SMOOTH_ARCMIN), divided by the CCD’s FWC exposure, multiplied by the observation’s live time of the CCD and by the observation’s corner scale (tngxmm.donor_tables.qpb_corners: the camera’s scale in the band times the relative scale of the CCD (MOS) or quadrant (pn) in the corner band), then carried to the sky;

  • the soft-proton template: the ESAS soft-proton detector map of the camera (CCF XMM_SPDETMAP, the map proton uses) on the same detector grid times the camera’s live time, carried to the sky and normalised to unit mean over the exposed pixels (its level is free in the fit).

Detector to sky: an affine map fitted by least squares to the DETX, DETY and X, Y of the exposure’s own events, X, Y then to image pixels through the image’s WCS. The events are those the image is made of: in the field of view, with a sky position (defined_sky()) and inside the good time intervals the image carries for their CCD (image_good_times(), in_good_times()). The PPS list also holds the events outside those intervals, and an exposure that starts before the telescope has settled on the target has events at another attitude there; the map is the mean attitude over the time the image integrates, the counterpart of ESAS rotdet2sky. Each detector pixel is deposited onto the image grid with 3 x 3 sub-samples, so the counts are conserved. A map that places the template worse than one image pixel (check_det_to_sky()) and a template with nothing on the exposed pixels (check_templates()) raise.

Departures from ESAS are those of the corner scales (tngxmm.donor_tables.qpb_corners).

Module Attributes

DET_NPIX

780 x 780 pixels of 50 DET units (0.05" each) from -19500 to 19500

DET_TO_SKY_EVENTS

which events the detector-to-sky map of a real template is fitted to (in_good_times()); part of the name of a cached template, so that one built from the events of the whole exposure is not reused

SMOOTH_ARCMIN

smoothing of the FWC density within a CCD [arcmin] (as instrument.donor_background.FWC_SMOOTH_ARCMIN)

SUBSAMPLE

sub-samples per detector pixel and axis when the detector grid is carried to the sky

PN_QUADRANTS

pn quadrants (CCD numbers)

ESAS_PN_SUBMODES

a Large or Small Window exposure reads part of each CCD and has no usable unexposed corners

DET_HALF_DIAGONAL

the largest distance from DET (0, 0) a template pixel has

Functions

ccd_label_image(header, wcs, shape, x, y, ccd)

The CCD number of each image pixel: the CCD with the most events (sky X, Y of the event list, header of the EVENTS extension) in the pixel; 0 where no event fell.

ccd_scales(row, cam, band, ccds)

CCD -> QPB scale of the observation in band (PPS band id): the camera's corner scale in the band times the relative scale of the CCD's unit in the corner band (unit scale over the camera's corner-band scale; a unit without corner counts takes 1).

check_det_to_sky(affine, rms, pixel_sky, label)

Raise ValueError (naming label, the observation and image) unless the detector-to-sky map places the template to within one image pixel pixel_sky [sky units].

check_templates(qpb, sp, expmap, label)

Raise ValueError (naming label) unless the QPB and soft-proton templates of a real image are finite and have a positive sum over the exposed pixels (expmap > 0).

defined_sky(header, x, y)

Whether each event of an event list (header of its EVENTS extension) has a sky position: X and Y are not the column's null value and lie within its legal range.

det_edges()

det_to_image(det_map, affine, header, wcs, shape)

Deposit a detector-grid map (values per detector pixel) onto the image grid: each detector pixel split into subsample x subsample points, each carrying its share, nearest image pixel.

esas_supported(cam, submode)

Whether the ESAS particle-background model applies to an image of cam in submode (MOS: always; pn: the full-frame modes of ESAS_PN_SUBMODES; a pn image without SUBMODE counts as full frame).

expmap_path(image_path, band)

The PPS exposure map beside a PPS image of band band.

fit_det_to_sky(detx, dety, x, y)

Affine map (2, 3) with [X, Y] = A @ [DETX, DETY, 1], least squares, and its rms residual [sky units].

flare_detmap(ccf_dir, cam, filt, band)

The soft-proton map of cam on the ESAS detector grid for PPS band band, as XMM-ESAS proton builds it (SAS 20 and later): the flare maps of the CCF XMM_FLARE for the filter (a filter without maps takes Medium, Kuntz & Snowden 2008 Sect.

fwc_band_rates(ccf_dir, cam, band_ev[, ...])

CCD -> FWC rate per detector pixel [counts / s] of the band on the ESAS detector grid (rows DETY, columns DETX), smoothed within the CCD, from the closed exposures of one epoch in the normal CCD state (tngxmm.instrument.fwc_state): the epoch that starts at revolution epoch_first (the corner table's fwc_epoch_<cam>), else that of the working CCDs ccds and revolution rev.

image_good_times(image_path)

CCD -> good time intervals (N, 2) [s, mission time] of a PPS image.

image_pixel_sky(header, img_header)

The pixel of an image (img_header, CDELT1 [deg]) in sky units of the event list header (TCDLT of X).

in_good_times(time, ccd, good)

Whether each event (time [s], CCD number ccd) lies inside the good time intervals of its CCD (good: CCD -> (N, 2), sorted by start, image_good_times()).

observation_templates(event_list, ...[, ...])

The QPB counts template and the soft-proton template of one PPS image (image_path; band id band) of a real exposure, with its own event list event_list (for the detector-to-sky map, from the events inside the image's good time intervals), the observation's corner row (tngxmm.donor_tables.qpb_corners) and the live time of each CCD within the image's good time intervals live.

reference_epoch(corner_row, cam, live[, ...])

The FWC epoch of a real exposure's template, as keyword arguments of fwc_band_rates(): for the newest FWC set the epoch its corner scales refer to (fwc_epoch_<cam> of the corner row) with the exposure's working CCDs (live > 0) to check it against, for another set the epoch of those CCDs and revolution rev.

sky_to_pixel(header, wcs, x, y)

Float (column, row) of the image grid of sky coordinates X, Y [0.05"] of the event list header.

spdetmap(ccf_dir, cam)

The ESAS soft-proton detector map of cam (CCF XMM_SPDETMAP; rows DETY, columns DETX of the ESAS grid).

template_signature(corner_row, cam, band)

Short digest of what a real template takes from the corner table (the camera's scale in the band, the unit scales, the FWC epoch) and of the FWC rule: the name of its cache file, so that a measured-again table never reuses a template of the old scales.

unit_of(cam, ccd)

The scaling unit of a CCD: the CCD number (MOS) or its pn quadrant.

tngxmm.massfit.qpb_template.defined_sky(header, x, y)[source]¶

Whether each event of an event list (header of its EVENTS extension) has a sky position: X and Y are not the column’s null value and lie within its legal range.

SAS attcalc computes X, Y from DETX, DETY with the attitude of the event’s frame; where the attitude call returns invalid pointing data “the X/Y position of the events in question are set to NULL” (attcalc, Sect. 3). In the integer X, Y columns of a PPS event list that NULL is the column’s TNULL value (-99999999); TLMIN and TLMAX give the legal range (1-51840). Such events keep their DETX, DETY and FLAG, so the in-field selection alone does not remove them. A column without the keywords falls back to finite values.

tngxmm.massfit.qpb_template.image_good_times(image_path)[source]¶

CCD -> good time intervals (N, 2) [s, mission time] of a PPS image.

SAS evselect writes the time selection of a product into the product: an image has one GTI table per CCD (HDUCLAS1 GTI, the CCD in CCDID), whose length is the image’s ONTIMEnn (to a few seconds for the pn). They are the intervals the events of the image were taken in, after the pipeline’s flare screening. Raises ValueError for an image without such a table: its time selection is not known, and the events of the whole exposure are not a substitute (see in_good_times()).

tngxmm.massfit.qpb_template.in_good_times(time, ccd, good)[source]¶

Whether each event (time [s], CCD number ccd) lies inside the good time intervals of its CCD (good: CCD -> (N, 2), sorted by start, image_good_times()). An event of a CCD without intervals is outside: the image has nothing from that CCD.

The detector-to-sky map needs this selection. DETX, DETY to X, Y is one rotation and shift only while the attitude holds; the event list of an exposure that began during the slew or before the pointing had settled has events several arcseconds to arcminutes from where the same detector pixel falls later, and the image leaves them out with the rest of the bad time.

tngxmm.massfit.qpb_template.check_det_to_sky(affine, rms, pixel_sky, label)[source]¶

Raise ValueError (naming label, the observation and image) unless the detector-to-sky map places the template to within one image pixel pixel_sky [sky units].

The criterion is geometric. DETX, DETY and X, Y are in the same unit (0.05”), so the linear part L of the map is a rotation, possibly with a reflection, and the attitude only turns and shifts the detector. Two things move a template pixel from where its events fall: the scatter of the events about the fitted map (its rms residual, the attitude’s wander about the mean attitude ESAS rotdet2sky also assumes) and a departure of L from a rotation, which at the edge of the grid moves a pixel by up to ||L - R|| times DET_HALF_DIAGONAL (R the nearest orthogonal matrix, ||.|| the largest singular value). The template is deposited on the nearest image pixel, so either displacement above one image pixel means the template is not where the events are at the resolution of the image. A sky position that is not one (the NULL of attcalc) moves the fit by millions of sky units; events from before the pointing settled, by tens to hundreds. With both left out, what still fails is an attitude that moved by more than an image pixel inside the image’s own good time, which no single template describes.

tngxmm.massfit.qpb_template.check_templates(qpb, sp, expmap, label)[source]¶

Raise ValueError (naming label) unless the QPB and soft-proton templates of a real image are finite and have a positive sum over the exposed pixels (expmap > 0). Every exposed pixel of a real image lies on a CCD that records particles and on the soft-proton map, so a zero sum means the templates fell off the image.
