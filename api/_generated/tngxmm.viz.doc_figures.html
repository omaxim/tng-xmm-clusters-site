tngxmm.viz.doc_figures¶

The figures of the documentation, made from results that exist on disk.

Every figure is one function fig_<name>(inputs, out_dir); FIGURES lists them with the files they read (INPUTS). An input is a pair (root, relative path): the root is a site variable or an INTERFACES section 7 root (TNGXMM_DATA, TNGXMM_FIXTURES, TNGXMM_DEV, TNGXMM_SITE_PROD, TNGXMM_SITE_OBS_IMAGES) or repo (this checkout), so the record names no machine path. A relative path may be a glob. Nothing here runs a simulation or a fit: the functions read tables, images and reports and plot them.

python -m tngxmm.viz.doc_figures [--only NAME ...] [--out docs/figures] writes <name>.png (and <name>.pdf for the paper candidates) and manifest.json: per figure, every input file with its root, relative path, size and sha256, the commit of the checkout, and whether it is a paper candidate. --check recomputes the sha256 of the inputs and compares them with the manifest without plotting.

The captions, the configuration each input belongs to and the caveats are on the page docs/figures.md; the numbers quoted there are printed by the functions (--verbose).

Functions

check(out_dir)

Inputs whose sha256 differs from the manifest (or that are missing).

fig_closure(files, out_dir[, log])

The closure grid of release 1.1.3: M500_fit / M500_true of idealised clusters in exact hydrostatic equilibrium (cc and ncc; M500 = 2, 4, 8e14 Msun; z = 0.06, 0.2, 0.5; three seeds) through the whole chain with the production fit (arm v1), in four data modes (clean: no sky, particle background or AGN; agnonly; bgonly; real: all of them).

fig_convergence(files, out_dir[, log])

Convergence of the projected emission with the size of the gas cube: for 20 TNG-Cluster halos x 3 views, the 90th percentile over halos and views of (left) the fraction of the 0.5-2 keV emission of the annuli inside R500c that comes from gas outside a cube of half-side X R500c, and (right) that emission relative to the median XMM background of the donors, for every annulus inside X R500c.

fig_hse_bias_fits(files, out_dir[, log])

The three masses of 43 mock views (the CHECKS D1 sample, fit group v4s1): X-ray fit (M_X), intrinsic hydrostatic mass (M_HSE, Barnes et al. 2021 estimator) and true mass, as ratios inside fixed radii of the true R500c(z_obs) (median and 16-84 per cent of the views; dashed: median of the fits not flagged by the QC rules); right, M_X / M_true at R500c against true mass, coloured by redshift; open symbols are QC-flagged fits.

fig_hse_bias_truth(files, out_dir[, log])

The intrinsic hydrostatic mass of the whole campaign population: M_HSE(< R500c) / M500c of the headline estimator (Barnes et al. 2021 with fitted slopes, non-star-forming gas, each cell's own mean molecular weight; spherical truth) against M500c and against the snapshot redshift, TNG-Cluster and the TNG300 box, and (right) the matched draw of the maximal list, the sample that passes gate P; running median and 16-84 per cent.

fig_lx_mass(files, out_dir[, log])

Soft-band luminosity inside R500 against mass: TNG-Cluster mocks (measured on the mock images with the campaign estimator, and intrinsic) against the real observations measured with the same estimator.

fig_mock_vs_real(files, out_dir[, log])

A mock and the real observation it replicates: the golden fixture view (TNG-Cluster halo 19542651, snapshot 67, view 0, release 1.1.1 configuration) observed as XMM observation 0656201501 (PSZ2 G308.33-20.21, z = 0.48), beside that observation's 0.5-2 keV EPIC mosaic.

fig_pipeline_overview(files, out_dir[, log])

The data flow of the pipeline: stages as boxes, the products between them on the arrows; the mock and the real branch meet in the same photometry and the same hydrostatic fit.

fig_realism(files, out_dir[, log])

How far the mocks are from the real observations: left, the classifier two-sample test (C2ST) AUC per feature group, one replica per donor, 465 mocks against 465 real observations (logistic regression and gradient boosting, grouped by donor; DINOv3 CLS embeddings, PCA-2/8/32, logistic); right, the AUC of each of the 17 image statistics alone (194 against 194; mock higher = AUC > 0.5) with the donor's own conditions and under the conditions-donor rule.

fig_samples(files, out_dir[, log])

The samples in the mass-redshift plane: the 638 real observations (catalogue M500 and the matching mass of the target list), the TNG-Cluster halo-snapshots with M500c >= 1e14 Msun at the nine full snapshots, and the 2,840 replica views; right: the redshift offset z_obs - z_snap of the views of the maximal campaign and the change of M500c from 500 rho_c(z_snap) to 500 rho_c(z_obs) of the same particles.

lx_truth_ratio(measured, intrinsic)

The truth ratio of a measured L_X: the median and the 16th and 84th percentiles of log10(measured / intrinsic) over the views where both are finite and positive, and their number n.

tngxmm.viz.doc_figures.lx_truth_ratio(measured, intrinsic)[source]¶

The truth ratio of a measured L_X: the median and the 16th and 84th percentiles of log10(measured / intrinsic) over the views where both are finite and positive, and their number n. It is the net ratio of the whole measurement chain of tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx.campaign_lx() (background annulus, exposure map, PSF, out-of-time events, ECF), not of one step. Its median is negative for the TNG-Cluster views of the v1.0.1 trees in all three apertures and positive in the 1.5 Mpc aperture of the box simulations (docs/validation.md, “The L_X estimator against the intrinsic luminosity”). The L_X figures quote it beside the measured luminosities.

tngxmm.viz.doc_figures.fig_lx_mass(files, out_dir, log=<built-in function print>)[source]¶

Soft-band luminosity inside R500 against mass: TNG-Cluster mocks (measured on the mock images with the campaign estimator, and intrinsic) against the real observations measured with the same estimator. The mock mass is the true M500c at z_obs, the real mass the catalogue M500 (SZ or lensing based), so the two axes are not the same quantity; L is scaled by E(z)^-2 (self-similar for a band emissivity independent of temperature). The annotation gives the truth ratio of the estimator (lx_truth_ratio()) inside R500 over the plotted mock views (lx_r500_flag == 'ok') and in the 1 Mpc aperture over the views with lx_1mpc_flag == 'ok', a different set of rows of the same tables.

The closure grid of release 1.1.3: M500_fit / M500_true of idealised clusters in exact hydrostatic equilibrium (cc and ncc; M500 = 2, 4, 8e14 Msun; z = 0.06, 0.2, 0.5; three seeds) through the whole chain with the production fit (arm v1), in four data modes (clean: no sky, particle background or AGN; agnonly; bgonly; real: all of them). The three masses of 43 mock views (the CHECKS D1 sample, fit group v4s1): X-ray fit (M_X), intrinsic hydrostatic mass (M_HSE, Barnes et al. 2021 estimator) and true mass, as ratios inside fixed radii of the true R500c(z_obs) (median and 16-84 per cent of the views; dashed: median of the fits not flagged by the QC rules); right, M_X / M_true at R500c against true mass, coloured by redshift; open symbols are QC-flagged fits.

The intrinsic hydrostatic mass of the whole campaign population: M_HSE(< R500c) / M500c of the headline estimator (Barnes et al. 2021 with fitted slopes, non-star-forming gas, each cell’s own mean molecular weight; spherical truth) against M500c and against the snapshot redshift, TNG-Cluster and the TNG300 box, and (right) the matched draw of the maximal list, the sample that passes gate P; running median and 16-84 per cent. How far the mocks are from the real observations: left, the classifier two-sample test (C2ST) AUC per feature group, one replica per donor, 465 mocks against 465 real observations (logistic regression and gradient boosting, grouped by donor; DINOv3 CLS embeddings, PCA-2/8/32, logistic); right, the AUC of each of the 17 image statistics alone (194 against 194; mock higher = AUC > 0.5) with the donor’s own conditions and under the conditions-donor rule. 0.5 = indistinguishable; the dashed line is the proposed gate AUC <= 0.75.

Convergence of the projected emission with the size of the gas cube: for 20 TNG-Cluster halos x 3 views, the 90th percentile over halos and views of (left) the fraction of the 0.5-2 keV emission of the annuli inside R500c that comes from gas outside a cube of half-side X R500c, and (right) that emission relative to the median XMM background of the donors, for every annulus inside X R500c.
