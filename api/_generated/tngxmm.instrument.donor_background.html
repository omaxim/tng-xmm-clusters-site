tngxmm.instrument.donor_background¶

The background of a donor observation, measured from its PPS images, and the donor-conditioned background model of the instrument stage.

What a PPS image holds outside the cluster is the sum of components with different spatial forms (Snowden et al. 2008, Sect. 3; Leccardi & Molendi 2008, App. B):

  • photons (sky foreground, unresolved CXB, faint sources): folded through the effective area, so they follow the X-ray vignetting of the band, i.e. the exposure map E;

  • the quiescent particle background (QPB) with its fluorescence lines: not focused, flat over the detector to first order, i.e. proportional to the UNVIGNETTED exposure F = E / V (V the vignetting of the band on the camera’s optical axis frame);

  • residual soft protons (SP): focused by the mirrors but not X-rays; their spatial distribution is less centrally concentrated than the X-ray vignetting and their spectrum is a power law not folded through the effective area.

decompose_background() fits, per camera and band, the counts of the background region as a Poisson sum a E + b F (the vignetted and the flat parts) with compact sources clipped against the Poisson expectation. b in the 4.5-12 keV band is the particle level of the observation (QPB plus the flat share of SP); measure_observation_background() runs it on every camera and band of a set of PPS-like products. The same function runs on mock products, so a donor and its replica are measured in one code path.

The instrument stage uses the measurement through the donor background table (InstrumentOptions. background = 'donor'; donor_background_record() builds a row): the camera’s FWC spectrum is scaled to the donor’s flat 4.5-12 keV level (the per-observation FWC normalisation of Snowden et al. 2008, Sect. 3.1.1, done here on the flat in-FOV component because the PPS images drop the out-of-FOV corners). The row’s sp_rate_* is 0.0: the soft-proton rate comes from the IN/OUT excess table of the EPIC cameras (donor_inout_sp) through soft_proton_events() (broken power law in counts, Leccardi & Molendi 2008, App. B). Cluster photons are not touched.

References

Snowden, S. L., Mushotzky, R. F., Kuntz, K. D. & Davis, D. S. 2008, A&A 478, 615. Leccardi, A. & Molendi, S. 2008, A&A 486, 359. Katayama, H. et al. 2004, A&A 414, 767 (EPIC-pn background; renormalising to the particle rate). Gastaldello, F. et al. 2017, Exp. Astron. 44, 321 (the particle level follows the solar cycle). De Luca, A. & Molendi, S. 2004, A&A 419, 837 (the IN over OUT diagnostic).

tngxmm.instrument.donor_background.fit_two_poisson(c, x1, x2, n_newton=50)[source]¶

Maximum-likelihood (a, b) >= 0 of counts c ~ Poisson(a x1 + b x2).

The log-likelihood is concave; Newton steps from the one-component start, halved until the likelihood rises and both parameters stay positive; the two boundary solutions (a = 0 or b = 0, each closed form) are compared with the interior one.

Fit the counts of region as Poisson(a E + b F), F = E / max(V, VIG_FLOOR).

a [counts / s / px, per unit vignetted exposure]: the vignetted (photon-like) part; b [counts / s / px, per unit unvignetted exposure]: the flat (particle-like) part. tngxmm.instrument.donor_background.fwc_density_maps(detx_arcmin, dety_arcmin, pi_ev, ccdnr, det_to_mask, shape=(900, 900), pixsize_arcmin=0.041666666666666664, smooth_arcmin=1.0, bands={'1000': (0.2, 0.5), '2000': (0.5, 1.0), '3000': (1.0, 2.0), '4000': (2.0, 4.5), '5000': (4.5, 12.0)}, ccd_exposure=None, exclude_ccds=(), gaps=None, support=None)[source]¶

Relative QPB surface density per PPS band on the stage’s detector grid from filter-wheel-closed events.

Every event is placed on the mask frame by det_to_mask (DET_TO_MASK, SAS’s DET -> sky solution of the observation behind the masks). Per CCD the events of a band are histogrammed on the grid, smoothed with a Gaussian of smooth_arcmin by normalised convolution over that CCD’s occupied pixels (so chip edges stay sharp and CCD-to-CCD level steps are kept) and divided by the CCD’s summed FWC exposure (ccd_exposure, CCD number -> s; a CCD lost during the mission has less). tngxmm.instrument.donor_background.smooth_detmap(m, smooth_arcmin=1.0, pix_arcmin=0.041666666666666664)[source]¶

A CCF flare map smoothed with a Gaussian of smooth_arcmin by normalised convolution over its pixels with data, clipped at 0. Unsmoothed, the counting noise of the map would be printed on every mock and fit template.

The flare maps are images of the flare counts minus the non-flare images scaled by the exposure times (Kuntz & Snowden 2008, Sect. 4.3), so a pixel holds a difference of counts: negative values are the noise of that difference and are data (55 per cent of the pn pixels in the field, Medium, 0.75-1.25 keV); they enter the local mean like the positive ones, which keeps it unbiased. A pixel exactly 0 holds no data: outside the field, or a chip gap or bad column of the stacked exposures (“The gap between the inner chip and the outer chips can be seen at R ~ 5.25’”). Such a pixel is not a pixel without soft protons: inside the field it gets the smoothed value of its neighbourhood, and the dead pixels of the observation enter once, through its own footprint (the mock’s efficiency map, which thins every event; the fit’s unvignetted exposure map, which multiplies the template). The pixels filled are those without data inside the half level of the smoothed occupancy (the edge of a filled region under a symmetric kernel): gaps and columns narrower than the kernel lie inside it, the space beyond the field does not. The clip at 0 is the only constraint on the result: a soft-proton rate is not negative.
