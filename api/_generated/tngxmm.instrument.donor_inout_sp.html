tngxmm.instrument.donor_inout_sp¶

Residual soft protons of a donor from the IN/OUT method of De Luca & Molendi (2004).

Soft protons are focused by the mirrors and reach only the field of view; the particle background reaches the whole CCD. At 10-12 keV, where the X-ray sky is faint, the in-FOV surface brightness of an observation over its out-of-FOV (corner) brightness, relative to the same ratio of the filter-wheel-closed data, measures the in-FOV excess, i.e. the residual soft protons (De Luca & Molendi 2004, Sect. 3 and App. B; Leccardi & Molendi 2008, App. B). Here, within the flare GTIs of the PPS images:

excess = N_IN / (A T)_IN - s_corner q_FWC,IN - CXB_IN [counts / s / arcmin^2, 10-12 keV]

with N_IN the 10-12 keV in-FOV events of the region beyond the cluster, (A T)_IN its unvignetted exposure (area times live time), s_corner the corner scale of the band (instrument.donor_corner_qpb), q_FWC,IN the FWC in-FOV rate per area of the band, and CXB_IN the cosmic X-ray background of De Luca & Molendi (2004: photon index 1.41, 11.6 photons cm^-2 s^-1 sr^-1 keV^-1 at 1 keV) through the camera’s effective area averaged over 3-12’ off axis. The excess becomes the stage’s SP rate (0.5-12 keV, tngxmm.instrument.donor_background.soft_proton_events()) through the quiescent-SP counts spectrum of Leccardi & Molendi (2008, App. B: broken power law, slopes 0.4 and 0.8, break 5 keV), spread flat over the field of view: the SP have “a much flatter radial distribution than the X-ray emission” (Kuntz & Snowden 2008). Instrument option soft_protons (SOFT_PROTONS): 'off' or 'inout', which reads the donor’s rate from the MOS background table (columns sp_rate_<cam>; the QPB is the corner level of tngxmm.instrument.donor_corner_qpb, measured separately).

The table holds the pn as well (tngxmm.donor_tables.mos_background). The pn rate is a lower bound: the pn corners are not shielded from the sky, they receive focused soft protons and photons (Marelli et al. 2021), so the corner-scaled QPB that is subtracted is too high and the excess too low. No source calibrates the pn IN/OUT excess against the soft-proton level, and the quiescent-SP spectrum is the MOS one of Leccardi & Molendi (2008); ledger entry instrument.pn_soft_protons_inout. Every camera the stage simulates needs its sp_rate_<cam> column: a table without the pn columns, such as the MOS-only table measured before the pn was added, is refused when the pn is simulated (apply_to_cameras()) instead of leaving the pn without soft protons.

A donor can have no rate for a camera: the IN region is empty when 1.5 R500 of a nearby cluster covers the 12’ circle about the pointing (112 of the 588 donors of the conditions pool), or the camera has no usable corner level, event list or flare series. Such a donor had soft protons like any other observation (98.7 per cent of the measured MOS1 rates are positive), so a rate of 0 would not be a measurement but the lowest possible value, and the donors concerned are the nearby clusters (90 per cent of the pool donors at z < 0.1, 1 per cent at z >= 0.2). Instrument option sp_rate_unmeasured (SP_RATE_UNMEASURED) says what happens instead: 'nearest-in-fold' takes the measured rate of the nearest donor of the conditions pool, in the same donor fold, that has one for the camera (nearest_measured_in_fold(): the condition-space distance of tngxmm.instrument.conditions_donor from the donor, ties by obsid), and the view records the donor that lent it (SPRFLAG/SPSUBST in the camera headers, soft_protons in obs_meta.json and the conditions record); 'refuse' stops the view (UnmeasuredSoftProtons). No rate is ever set to 0 for want of a measurement.

Functions

apply_to_background(bkg, sp)

The camera's donor-background parameters with the donor's flat SP rate set (the QPB level is untouched), and where it comes from (sp_source: 'measured' or 'substitute'; sp_substitute: the lending donor or None).

apply_to_cameras(bkg_by_cam, donor_sp, cameras)

apply_to_background() for each of cameras, the cameras the stage simulates.

cxb_band_rates(ccf_dir[, filt])

cxb_rate() of each camera with the stage's responses.

cxb_rate(arf_theta, thetas, e_mid, e_wid[, ...])

CXB count rate [counts / s / arcmin^2] in band through arf_theta (theta x energy, cm^2), averaged over theta_range with weight theta (uniform sky area).

donor_soft_protons(table, obsid[, ...])

camera -> {'sp_rate', 'source', 'substitute', 'distance'} of one donor, for the cameras the table has a column for (a camera without a column has no entry, and the stage refuses to simulate it, apply_to_cameras()); KeyError when the donor is absent.

inout_excess(n_in, area_time, qpb_in, cxb)

In-FOV 10-12 keV excess [counts / s / arcmin^2] over the corner-predicted QPB and the CXB, its Poisson error, and the IN/OUT ratio R (in-FOV over QPB + CXB; 1 for no excess).

measured_rate(row, camera)

The measured sp_rate_<cam> [counts / s / arcmin^2] of one donor's table row (0 when its excess is not positive: a measured zero), or None when the row has no finite rate (no IN region, no usable corner level, no event list or flare series).

nearest_measured_in_fold(conditions, folds, ...)

The substitute rate of a donor without a measured one for camera: {'obsid', 'sp_rate', 'distance'} of the nearest donor of the conditions pool, in the donor's fold, with a measured rate for the camera.

sp_rate_from_excess(excess[, band])

The stage's SP rate [counts / s / arcmin^2, 0.5-12 keV] whose quiescent-SP counts spectrum puts excess into band; 0 for a negative excess (no SP).

Exceptions

UnmeasuredSoftProtons

A camera of a donor has no measured IN/OUT soft-proton rate and the rule (sp_rate_unmeasured) gives it none: 'refuse', or 'nearest-in-fold' without a measured donor in the donor's fold.
