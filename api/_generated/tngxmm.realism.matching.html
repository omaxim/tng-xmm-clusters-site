tngxmm.realism.matching¶

Count matching of the replicas: the thinning factor of every view and its summary.

TNG-Cluster is brighter in soft L_X than the real clusters of the same mass, so a replica usually carries more cluster photons than the real observation it replicates. The factor f of a view is N_target / N_mock: the NET 0.5-2 keV counts (cameras summed) inside the annulus tngxmm.validation.realism.MATCH_ANNULUS_R500 (0.15-1 R500 of the donor) that the cluster must have, over those of the replica at f = 1, both measured with the same code on per-camera products.

The cluster’s physical flux is held fixed. N_target is the PHYSICS donor’s real net counts (obs_meta.json donor_package) scaled to the observing time the replica was made with: N_target = N_physics,real x t_cond / t_phys (exposure_ratio()), the ARF-weighted effective live times of the products (the conditions donor’s, jittered, under the production rule nn: obs_meta exposure_s) and of the physics donor’s REAL observation (donor_package.cameras_real_s, the times N_physics,real was counted at; the jittered times of the replica’s target row are not used), both by tngxmm.instrument.conditions_donor.effective_exposure_s(). The replica sits at the exposure and background of its observing conditions, so the data quality follows the conditions donor while the flux stays the physics donor’s. Under the rule donor (or a tree without exposure_s) the ratio is 1 and f is N_physics,real / N_mock as before. f is capped at 1 because thinning cannot add photons (tngxmm.validation.realism.count_matching_factor()). The table written here is read back by the replay (instrument.replay.thin_table), which keeps each cluster photon with probability f.

tngxmm.realism.matching.vignetted_flat_background(per_camera, ok, fov, r_r500, region=None)[source]¶

Background image (counts, cameras summed) of the 0.5-2 keV products, the rule that produced it and the inner radius of its region [R500 of the donor].

Per camera the counts of the field region are fitted as a E + b E / V (V the vignetting of the camera’s own exposure map; tngxmm.instrument.donor_background.decompose_background(): a the vignetted, photon-like part, b the flat, particle-like part, compact sources clipped), and the model is evaluated on the camera’s whole exposure map. The region is the well-exposed field (fov) outside the compact sources (ok) beyond BKG_FIELD_R500, else beyond BKG_FALLBACK_R500 when the first has fewer than BKG_MIN_PIX pixels, else the outermost BKG_OUTER_FRACTION of the well-exposed field (a cluster that fills the field: its emission is then part of the fitted background and the net counts are lower limits). The rule is chosen on the DONOR’s image (region None); the replica is given the donor’s region (rule, inner radius in R500) and uses exactly that region, so both sides are fitted over the same radii; if that region holds fewer than BKG_MIN_PIX replica pixels, or a fit fails, the rule is 'none' and there is no image. Returns (image or None, rule, inner radius [R500]; the radius is NaN for 'none').

tngxmm.realism.matching.annulus_counts(prods, cx, cy, r500_px, region=None)[source]¶

Net counts, background counts, background rule and its inner radius [R500] of the 0.5-2 keV image (cameras summed) in the matching annulus about the recentred (cx, cy); the net counts are NaN when no background could be fitted. Only the background fit leaves out compact sources; the annulus counts every exposed pixel (a bright core is not a ‘source’ and a mask that depends on the counts would bias the donor/replica ratio).

The background is the vignetted + flat model of vignetted_flat_background(), the SAME code for the real observation and the replica (a single rate over the field, which is vignetted, over- or under-subtracts: negative net counts for 36 of 117 held-out donors). region: None for the donor, which chooses the rule; the donor’s (rule, radius) for its replicas, which then use the same region in R500 units.

tngxmm.realism.matching.view_counts(view_dir, donor, z_obs, region=None)[source]¶

Net and background counts, rule and inner radius of the replica products of view_dir in the matching annulus (the aperture is the donor R500 at the view’s z_obs, the start centre the simulation centre), with the background region of the donor (region, donor_counts()). Count matching compares the replica’s flux with its physics donor’s on the generation side, so the aperture is at the redshift the photons were made at, not at the catalogue redshift drawn for the view’s fit.

The two centres differ in kind: the donor’s annulus starts at its guarded X-ray peak, the replica’s at the true cluster pixel, each recentred on its own image (annulus_counts()), where the realism features start a mock at its own first pass (tngxmm.realism.features.mock_start_centre()). The factor is a diagnostic only: the frozen evaluation runs unthinned (realism.frozen.count_matching: none, refused otherwise by tngxmm.realism.report), so the difference enters no verdict.

tngxmm.realism.matching.exposure_ratio(meta)[source]¶

t_cond / t_phys of a view: the effective live time of the products (meta['exposure_s'], the per-camera times the instrument ran with: the conditions donor’s, jittered, under the rule nn) over that of the physics donor’s REAL observation (meta['donor_package']['cameras_real_s']: the times N_physics,real was counted at), both tngxmm.instrument.conditions_donor.effective_exposure_s().

The jitter acts on the numerator only. The replica’s target row also carries jittered physics times (donor_package['cameras_s'], exposure_jittered), but the products do not use them under nn (the instrument runs with the conditions donor’s times) and the counts of the target were measured on the real observation, so they never enter the ratio. With the conditions donor equal to the physics donor and no exposure jitter the ratio is exactly 1; with the exposure jitter it is the jitter’s own scale. 1 for a view without exposure_s that was not observed under a conditions donor (the rule donor, or a tree of an earlier release); a view with a conditions record but no exposure_s, or a physics donor without real live times (a jittered package without cameras_real_s: a tree of release 1.1.1 before the review fixes), raises ValueError (the target cannot be scaled).

tngxmm.realism.matching.measure_factors(view_dirs, donors, image_root, workers=1)[source]¶

Count-matching table of the stored views (at f = 1) view_dirs, columns COLUMNS.

The donor of a view is the one of its obs_meta.json (donor_package.obsid); donors is the donor catalogue indexed by obsid. f is NaN (status no-net-counts) when the donor or the replica has no positive net counts, status no-background-region when the donor’s image has no usable background region and no-mock-background-region when the donor’s region cannot be used on the replica (recorded, never overwritten); capped is True when the replica is the fainter one (f_raw > 1, f = 1).
