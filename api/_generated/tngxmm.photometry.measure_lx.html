tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx¶

Campaign aperture Lx of a merged count image (the lx_r500/lx_1mpc/lx_1p5mpc columns).

Holds measure_lx() (plain annulus-mean aperture Lx), t_vir_keV(), emsce_mean_exposure() and ARF_BAND_MEAN_CM2 (the spec field photometry.arf_band_mean_cm2). The cosmology comes from tngxmm.core.cosmology; centring from tngxmm.photometry.find_centre.find_centre().

Convention of measure_lx():

Lx(rest 0.5-2 keV, unabsorbed) = N_net / t_eff / ECF(T, z, N_H) * 4 pi d_L^2

  • background: mean counts per pixel in the 1.1-1.6 aperture-radius annulus, restricted to the coverage map when one is given (pixels with cov <= 1 % of max are dropped); the source aperture must be >= 98 % covered;

  • no compensation factors: mocks carry (1+z) arrival and Galactic absorption like real images, so both are read with the same call;

  • t_eff = ARF-weighted per-camera live time (emsce_mean_exposure()).

The campaign tables (photometry.lx_bkg = 'masked-clipped', campaign check C6) use the same geometry, coverage rules and conversion with point sources masked in the aperture and the annulus, a sector-clipped background and no clipping of the net counts: campaign_lx() / measure_lx_flagged() write NaN plus the flags lx_<ap>_flag (ok | nondetection | masked | uncovered) and the signed lx_<ap>_signed (tngxmm.photometry.estimate_lx_background).

The ML-facing photometry uses tngxmm.photometry.measure_fixed_apertures instead (one angular background, fixed apertures, coverage flags).

References

xmm_ccf_sas : band-mean effective areas and the ECF. cosmology_planck2015 : distances (tngxmm.core.cosmology). astropy : FITS I/O and cosmology. scipy : ndimage.zoom.

tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx.emsce_mean_exposure(fits_path=None, *, header=None, settings=None)[source]¶

ARF-weighted effective exposure [s] from the EMSCE/EMSCI header keywords.

t_eff = sum_i A_i t_i / sum_j A_j, j over ALL three ECF cameras (MOS1, MOS2, PN), i over the cameras present (a missing camera enters with t = 0). Camera identity from EMSCI001..N; a header with exactly three EMSCE values and no EMSCI falls back to the (MOS1, MOS2, PN) write order; otherwise NaN. The band-mean ARFs A come from settings (the caller’s preset, tngxmm.photometry._settings.photometry_settings()), else from the production preset.

tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx.measure_lx(fits_path, apt_kpc, z_obs, exposure_s, nH_1e22, T_keV=5.0, expmap=None, pix_arcsec=None, center=None, *, settings=None)[source]¶

Background-subtracted rest-frame 0.5-2 keV Lx [erg/s] in a circle of apt_kpc.

exposure_s: effective exposure (emsce_mean_exposure()); nH_1e22: Galactic column of the field (donor N_H for mocks); expmap: coverage map on the image grid (strongly recommended); center: see tngxmm.photometry.find_centre.find_centre(); settings: the photometry numbers of the caller’s preset (tngxmm.photometry._settings.photometry_settings(); default the production preset’s). Net counts are clipped at zero (a non-detection gives 0.0). Returns NaN when unmeasurable (aperture < 98 % covered, < 200 annulus pixels, or, without coverage, an annulus beyond 0.95 of the half frame).

tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx.measure_lx_flagged(image, apt_kpc, z_obs, exposure_s, nH_1e22, T_keV=5.0, *, options=None, settings=None, inner_kpc=0.0)[source]¶

Masked-clipped aperture Lx [erg/s] of a LxImage, with its flag.

inner_kpc > 0 measures the annulus inner_kpc < r <= apt_kpc instead of the disc (a core-excised luminosity, core_excised_lx()): the source pixels, the coverage rules, the ring fill and the masked fraction are those of the annulus, and the background is the one of the disc of radius apt_kpc (its 1.1-1.6 annulus), so the whole annulus has one background from outside it.

Same geometry, coverage rules and conversion as measure_lx() (settings: the photometry numbers of the caller’s preset, tngxmm.photometry._settings.photometry_settings(); default the production preset’s); the background and the aperture sum follow tngxmm.photometry.estimate_lx_background. Returns lx (finite only when flag == 'ok'), lx_signed (the unclipped value; NaN for uncovered and for masked caused by a failed background), flag (ok | nondetection | masked | uncovered), n_net (signed net counts), bkg_per_pix, n_src_pix, n_src_masked_pix, n_bkg_pix (annulus pixels used) and n_sources_masked (listed sources whose mask reaches the aperture or the annulus), mask_frac (masked fraction of the aperture pixels) and area_corr (masked-area correction factor, see below).

Masked-area correction (ring_filled_sum()):

N_net = sum_unmasked C + sum_rings n_mask(k) <C>_k  -  B N_ap
area_corr = N_net / (sum_unmasked C - B N_unmasked)

<C>_k the mean counts of the unmasked covered aperture pixels in ring k (width lx_fill_ring_arcsec about the centre), n_mask(k) its masked covered pixels, B the background per pixel, N_ap all aperture pixels. area_corr is the factor the correction applied to the net counts of the unmasked pixels (1 with no mask; NaN when those are <= 0).

nondetection: net counts <= 0; masked: more than max_masked_frac of the aperture lies under source masks, or fewer than bkg_min_pixels annulus pixels survive masking and clipping; uncovered: the coverage rules (aperture < 98 % covered, < 200 covered annulus pixels, or without a coverage map an annulus beyond 0.95 of the half frame).

options.fill_uncovered (the seed photometry): the uncovered aperture pixels are ring-filled like the masked ones, the 98 % rule is replaced by the masked-area limit on masked plus uncovered pixels (mask_frac counts both), and an aperture more than max_masked_frac uncovered is uncovered.

tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx.view_lx_sources(merged, options, *, kind='mock', obsid=None, pps_cache=None, mock_sources=None, thresholds_path=None, sas=False)[source]¶
