tngxmm.sim.outskirts_convergence¶

Projected-emission convergence test of the uniform gas sphere (sim.gas_radius_r500).

Question. For every projected aperture of the analysis and of the realism features, what fraction of the 0.5-2 keV emission of a TNG-Cluster halo comes from gas beyond the 3D radius X R500c, and how does the emission beyond X R500c compare with the XMM background in the same aperture? The answer decides whether a sphere of 4 R500c about GroupPos (the gas and the emitting cylinder of loader = 'cutout' with gas_radius_r500 = los_depth_r500 = 4) loses emission that matters.

Decision rule (fixed before any production result; see the note on its history). A radius X is converged if, at the 90th percentile over the halos and the three views,

  • (A) in the signal annuli (SIGNAL_ANNULI: projected R/R500c < 1, the count-matching and fit core, where the cluster emission is the measured quantity), the emission from beyond X R500c is < 1 per cent of the total emission of the annulus, AND

  • (B) in every annulus (all eight), the surface brightness of the emission beyond X R500c is < 1 per cent of the XMM total background in 0.5-2 keV (the median of the real donors).

The stricter background (10th percentile of the donors) is also reported, and so is the literal rule of the request (A in every one of the eight annuli, converged_literal).

History. The rule first written applied (A) in every annulus; it cannot be met by any X <= 6: in an annulus that starts at or beyond X all the emission is beyond X by geometry, and in an annulus ending at X a large share lies beyond X along the line of sight (login test, one halo: 45 per cent in 2.5-4 R500c for X = 4). In the outer annuli the cluster emission is also far below the background (0.1-0.7 per cent of it for that halo), so the fraction of the total says nothing there and the comparison with the background (B) decides; (A) is kept where the cluster is the signal. This was changed after the login test of one halo, before the production run.

Cube cut (shape = 'cube', the box cutouts of the mock gas, sim.mock_cutout_shape = 'box'). The mock gas of the box path is ALL the gas of the axis-aligned cube of half-side H R500c about GroupPos (H = 4 in production), viewed along the simulation axes, so what it loses is the gas OUTSIDE the cube: beyond H along the line of sight and beyond H in the sky coordinates. In this shape the “radius” of a cell is its Chebyshev radius max(|x|, |y|, |z|) / R500c, binned with the same list X_R500 (now read as H); the annuli, the depth, the views, the ECF, the background reference and the decision rule are exactly those of the sphere. The histograms of the sphere run are binned by the Euclidean radius and cannot be re-binned into the Chebyshev radius, so the zooms are streamed again (4 minutes on a node for the 20 halos). Baseline: the cylinder of the sphere test, |offset along the line of sight| <= DEPTH_R500 (6) R500c and projected radius < 10 R500c, which encloses the cube of H = 6 (its corners are at projected 8.49, depth 6, 3D radius 10.39 R500c; the 3D radius of the farthest corner of the baseline is 11.7). Emission beyond H along the line of sight past the baseline depth is not in the total, so the fractions of the cube cut are lower bounds for H < 6. Where the zoom does not fill the baseline there is no gas to count: per halo r_cell_max_r500 (outermost kept cell), covers_h6_corner (it reaches the corner distance CORNER_H6_R500), and cube6_voxel_fill (fraction of the 24^3 voxels of the cube of H = 6 with at least one kept cell centre) say whether the high-resolution region covers the baseline (cells excluded as star-forming or below the temperature floor leave voxels empty, so the fill is a conservative measure). Rule of the cube run (stated before it is run; identical to the sphere rule with X -> H): at the 90th percentile over the halos and the three views, (A) the emission outside the cube of half-side H is < 1 per cent of the emission of the annulus in 0-0.15 and 0.15-1 R500c, AND (B) its surface brightness is < 1 per cent of the median XMM total background in 0.5-2 keV in all eight annuli. Annuli that start beyond H hold only emission from outside the cube by geometry, so (B) there is a statement about the cluster emission beyond H in the sky plane against the background; criterion_b_annuli_within_h (annuli ending at or inside H) is reported as information only and does not enter the verdict.

Geometry. The annuli in projected R/R500c are ANNULI_R500 (0-0.15, 0.15-1, 1-1.5, 1.5-2.5, 2.5-4, 4-6, 6-8, 8-10); the projection is along the three view axes of the campaign (generate_photons.LOS_AXIS_IDX) with the depth |offset| <= DEPTH_R500 (6) R500c about GroupPos. The total of an annulus is the emission of that cylinder, the beyond-X part is the cells with 3D radius r > X R500c. The X list is X_R500.

Gas. Every gas cell of the original zoom of the TNG-Cluster halo (loadOriginalZoom: the FoF group range and the outer-fuzz range of the offsets file, zoom_gas_ranges()), read in blocks of BLOCK_ROWS rows through the truth stage’s row reader, so the memory does not grow with the zoom (the whole gas of a zoom is up to 1.9e8 cells, ~77 GB). The cells come through tngxmm.sim.load_halo_gas.load_halo_gas() itself, so the per-cell n_e n_H V Lambda(T, Z) (rest-frame 0.5-2 keV, the TNG reference APEC table of sim.apec_version) is the one the photons use, the star-forming and heated cells are excluded and the temperature floor is sim.t_min_k. The coarse (low-resolution) cells, heavier than cutout_gas.HEAVY_CELL_FACTOR x the median gas cell mass of the zoom, are not part of the emission here (the cutout loader of the mocks KEEPS them) and are accumulated separately so the share of the emission they would carry is reported. The zoom has no gas beyond its high-resolution region, so a halo that reaches low-resolution dark matter inside the cylinder has too little emission there: r_cell_max_r500 (the outermost kept cell) is reported to judge it.

Counts. The count rate per cell is ECF(T, z_obs, N_H) L_rest / (4 pi d_L(z_obs)^2), with the EPIC per-camera energy conversion factor of the pipeline (tngxmm.instrument.compute_ecf.ecf_lookup(), ecf_table_v4: counts in the 0.5-2 keV channels per rest-frame unabsorbed 0.5-2 keV energy flux, on-axis ARF x filter x QE x RMF x TBabs(N_H), APEC at Z = 0.3 Z_sun), averaged over MOS1, MOS2 and pn, i.e. the rate of a camera of the mean effective area. This is the quantity of the realism feature log_bkg_rate (validation.realism.image_features): summed counts over summed exposure of the three cameras in 0.5-2 keV, per arcmin^2, with the vignetted exposure map (the rate per unit on-axis exposure), measured in the background region (beyond 1.5 R500 or the outer 15 per cent of the field) with the compact sources clipped. Each view takes the redshift and N_H of the campaign row of that view (the donor’s), the gas the snapshot’s physical state; the temperature and the redshift are clamped to the ECF grid (T 1-15 keV, z 0-1). The ratios of the emission beyond X to the total of an annulus, and the ratio of two rates of the same camera mix, do not depend on the absorption or the K-correction; the absolute surface brightness, and so the comparison with the background, does (N_H enters through the ECF and the redshift through the distance and the ECF).

References

tngcluster : original zooms, resolution levels (offsets OriginalZooms). illustristng : snapshot gas fields. atomdb, xmm_ccf_sas : the energy conversion factor (ARF, filter, RMF).

Module Attributes

ANNULI_R500

the aperture of the central count rate, the fit core (0.15-1), the outskirts and the background region of large clusters, and the field of small clusters (a 15 arcmin field edge is 5-10 R500c for theta500 of 1.5-3 arcmin)

X_R500

3D radii [R500c] beyond which the emission is counted

SHAPES

'sphere' (Euclidean radius, the zoom/cutout sphere) or 'cube' (Chebyshev radius, the box cutout)

CORNER_H6_R500

the baseline must reach it

N_VOX

voxels per side of the occupancy grid of the cube of half-side 6 R500c (0.5 R500c each)

SIGNAL_ANNULI

annuli where the cluster emission is the measured quantity (criterion A of the decision rule)

DEPTH_R500

half-depth of the projected cylinder [R500c]

BLOCK_ROWS

rows of a gas block read and processed at once

PERCENTILE

the percentile of the decision rule and the tolerated fraction

NH_DEFAULT

nominal Galactic column [1e22 cm^-2] when a view row carries none

ECF_FILTER

ECF table of the per-camera ECF (build_table_v4); filter of the energy conversion

BKG_COLUMN

the realism features of the real donors (log_bkg_rate column)

KPC_CM

kpc per Mpc, erg per keV, cm per kpc

SNAPS

full snapshots 99 .
