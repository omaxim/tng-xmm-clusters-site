tngxmm.obs.candidate_quality¶

Image checks of candidate observations, and the flags the grading of the added observations started from.

The archive search of 2026-11-20 (tngxmm.obs.extra_observations) left 194 candidate observations. Each was measured on its pipeline images (PPS; per camera the IMAGE_ and EXPMAP files of the catalogue bands 1-5), with the same function as the 638 observations of the bank, so a candidate is judged against the measured range of the bank. From the measurements a rule set gives every candidate a list of flags. The grade itself was then set by eye.

Three steps, and what each one is:

  1. Measurements (measure_image_set(), one observation; measure_table(), a list). Counts and exposure of bands 2 and 3 (0.5-2 keV) summed over cameras and exposures on the sky grid of the observation; exposure at the catalogue position, coverage of R500, a background rate beyond 1.3 R500, counts and signal-to-noise inside 0.5 R500, the peak of the smoothed image and the share of the net counts within 20 arcsec of it, and the band-5 (4.5-12 keV) rate beyond 1.3 R500 per camera.

  2. Flags (quality_flags(), one row; grade_table(), the list). The thresholds are the constants of this module. They are screening values chosen for the search of 2026-11-20, none of them is fitted to or derived from a result, and no flag decides alone. Three flags carry the prefix FAIL: (no images, clean exposure below 5 ks, exposure at the position below half the maximum) and one FAIL?: (signal-to-noise below 5); the prefix marks a candidate for a closer look. The hard-band flag compares with the 95th percentile of the bank’s own measurements (bank_hard_background_limits()): 0.0500 (pn), 0.0081 (MOS1) and 0.0068 (MOS2) counts/s/arcmin2 over the 558, 566 and 574 bank observations that have a region beyond 1.3 R500.

  3. Grade, by eye. Contact sheets of all candidates (contact_sheets()) were inspected, and every candidate got grade (pass, reject or hold) and, where it was not plain, a sentence in eye_note. This step is manual and has no written criteria beyond the notes: the grade is not a function of the flags. Of the 194 candidates 170 passed, 13 were rejected and 11 held; 2 of the 4 with a FAIL: flag passed (both on a clean exposure below 5 ks, 3.6 and 4.0 ks, with a signal-to-noise of 51 and 28), 9 without any FAIL flag were rejected (point sources or foreground galaxies in place of a cluster) and 9 held.

catalogue/inputs_extra/candidates_graded.csv is the table of step 2 (the candidate list joined with its measurements, exp_best_s and flags) with the two columns of step 3, grade and eye_note, added by hand, and without the local dir column. Its flags are those of grade_table() without the prefixes (plain_flags()). grade is the final grade: tngxmm.obs.extra_observations.extra_rows() takes the rows with grade == 'pass'. tngxmm obs grade-candidates runs steps 1 and 2 and, with --sheets, draws the sheets for step 3; it writes no grade.

contact_sheets(table, pps_root, out_dir[, ...])

The sheets the grade is given on: one panel per row of table (the output of grade_table()) with the smoothed, exposure-corrected 0.5-2 keV image around the catalogue position (half-width the larger of 1.6 theta500 and 4 arcmin, logarithmic between the 40th and the 99.9th percentile), circles at 0.5 and 1 theta500, and name, survey, redshift, catalogue mass, obsid and flags as the title (red with a FAIL, orange with another flag).

The candidate list joined with its measurements on obsid (candidates without a measurement row drop out), with exp_best_s (the longest of exp_PN_s, exp_M1_s, exp_M2_s) and flags (quality_flags(), joined by ;; empty when there is none).

measure_table(jobs[, workers])

One measurement row per job (obsid, directory, ra_deg, dec_deg, theta500_arcmin), in the order of the jobs, with obsid and dir.

plain_flags(flags)

A ;-joined flag string without the FAIL: and FAIL?: prefixes, as candidates_graded.csv has it.

The flags of one row: a candidate with its measurements and exp_best_s, the longest of the three camera exposures (a mapping, a pandas.Series or a named tuple).

tngxmm.obs.candidate_quality.measure_image_set(directory, ra_deg, dec_deg, theta500_arcmin, return_images=False)[source]¶

The measurements of one observation directory at a catalogue position (J2000) and angular R500.

The row (a dict) holds:

  • n_images, n_expmaps, bad_grid; cameras (M1+M2+PN), n_cameras; per camera exp_<cam>_s (sum of the EXPOSURE keywords of its band-2 images: the live time the pipeline kept), mode_<cam>, filter_<cam>, nexp_<cam>; status (ok, or no_images and nothing below);

  • pixel_arcsec, in_image; exp_at_centre_frac (median summed exposure within 0.5 arcmin of the position over its maximum) and centre_exp_frac_<cam>;

  • cover_half_r500, cover_r500: live pixels (exposure above 0.2 of the maximum) inside 0.5 and 1 theta500 over the pixel area of the full disc;

  • bg_rate_ct_s_pix, bg_blocks, bg_region: median over 8-pixel blocks of counts / exposure beyond 1.3 theta500 (bg_region none and no rate with fewer than 20 blocks);

  • counts_half_r500, bg_counts_half_r500, net_counts_half_r500, snr_half_r500 = net / sqrt(max(counts, 1)), inside 0.5 theta500 on live pixels;

  • peak_offset_arcmin: distance from the position to the maximum, inside 0.5 theta500, of smoothed counts over smoothed exposure (Gaussian, sigma 2 pixels); core20_frac: net counts within 20 arcsec of that peak over the net counts of 0.5 theta500;

  • hard_bg_<cam>_ct_s_arcmin2: the block median of the band-5 rate beyond 1.3 theta500, per camera.

With return_images the result is (row, images), images holding the summed counts and exposure, the pixel position of the catalogue position and the pixel size (None without images).

The jobs of the bank: the rows of the observation table with in_bank (all rows of a table without that column), each with the directory of the image tree that the product list of the package gives its obsid (tngxmm.obs.pps_products.read_manifest()). tngxmm.obs.candidate_quality.bank_hard_background_limits(bank_measurements, quantile=0.95)[source]¶

Per camera the quantile of hard_bg_<cam>_ct_s_arcmin2 over the measurement rows of the bank (missing values left out; linear interpolation between order statistics).

tngxmm.obs.candidate_quality.quality_flags(row, hard_bg_limits)[source]¶

The flags of one row: a candidate with its measurements and exp_best_s, the longest of the three camera exposures (a mapping, a pandas.Series or a named tuple). hard_bg_limits is bank_hard_background_limits(). In this order:

  • FAIL:no_images: status is not ok; nothing else is looked at;

  • FAIL:clean_exposure<5ks: exp_best_s below 5 ks; else short_exposure below 10 ks;

  • FAIL:centre_off_detector: exp_at_centre_frac below 0.5;

  • cameras:<present>: fewer than three cameras;

  • r500_cover<0.9: cover_r500 below 0.9;

  • no_offsource_region: no snr_half_r500 (no background rate); else FAIL?:not_detected below 5;

  • point_like_core: core20_frac above 0.6;

  • peak_far_from_position: peak_offset_arcmin above max(1 arcmin, 0.3 theta500);

  • high_particle_bg: the band-5 rate of any camera above the bank’s limit for that camera;

  • window_mode: a PN sub-mode with Window and without Full, or a MOS sub-mode with Partial.

A missing number sets no flag, except the signal-to-noise.

tngxmm.obs.candidate_quality.plain_flags(flags)[source]¶

A ;-joined flag string without the FAIL: and FAIL?: prefixes, as candidates_graded.csv has it.

tngxmm.obs.candidate_quality.grade_table(candidates, measurements, bank_measurements)[source]¶

The candidate list joined with its measurements on obsid (candidates without a measurement row drop out), with exp_best_s (the longest of exp_PN_s, exp_M1_s, exp_M2_s) and flags (quality_flags(), joined by ;; empty when there is none). The grade is not set here (module docstring, step 3).

tngxmm.obs.candidate_quality.contact_sheets(table, pps_root, out_dir, flagged_only=False, echo=<built-in function print>)[source]¶

The sheets the grade is given on: one panel per row of table (the output of grade_table()) with the smoothed, exposure-corrected 0.5-2 keV image around the catalogue position (half-width the larger of 1.6 theta500 and 4 arcmin, logarithmic between the 40th and the 99.9th percentile), circles at 0.5 and 1 theta500, and name, survey, redshift, catalogue mass, obsid and flags as the title (red with a FAIL, orange with another flag). 24 panels a sheet (all_NN.png), or with flagged_only 12 a sheet of the rows with a FAIL, core, peak, background, camera, window or exposure flag (flag_NN.png). Returns the files.
