tngxmm.instrument.donor_corner_qpb¶

Quiescent particle background (QPB) of a donor from the unexposed (out-of-field-of-view) corners of its event lists.

The EPIC CCDs extend beyond the field of view of the mirrors; their corners see no X-rays and no soft protons, only the particle background (De Luca & Molendi 2004, Sect. 3 and App. B; Kuntz & Snowden 2008). The corner count rate, divided by the corner rate of the filter-wheel-closed (FWC) event lists of the CCF on the same CCDs and in the same band, is the observation’s QPB level relative to the FWC data; the ratio of in-FOV to corner QPB is a property of the detector, not of the observation (Leccardi & Molendi 2008, App. B, Eq. B.1), so the in-FOV QPB of the donor is that ratio times the FWC in-FOV QPB. This replaces the in-FOV decomposition of tngxmm.instrument.donor_background (background = 'donor'), whose flat 4.5-12 keV level also holds residual soft protons and other flat counts and is degenerate with the vignetted part (MOS1 / MOS2 flat ratio 1.25, against 1.01 in the corners).

Selection (same for donor and FWC): out-of-FOV flag (0x10000) set, no other bit of the camera’s event-quality mask (MOS 0x766aa000, pn 0xfa000c), PATTERN <= 12 (MOS) / <= 4 (pn), PI in CORNER_BAND_EV, and for the MOS outside the regions that XMM-ESAS leaves out of the unexposed pixels (MOS_CORNER_EXCLUDE, Kuntz & Snowden 2008, Table 3: the edge of the field of view, its extension, the light leaks of CCDs 2 and 7 and, on MOS1, three edges of scattered light; De Luca & Molendi 2004, Sect. 3.1, mask the CCD 2 and 7 region “where both X-ray photons and low energy particles can reach the detectors” without giving its outline); per CCD, counts over the CCD’s live time (event-list EXPOSUnn LIVETIME; FWC: EXPTIME). pn: the out-of-time (OoT) events of the in-FOV X-rays of a column spread over the whole column, corners included; the expected OoT counts of each corner column (OoT fraction of the mode x the column’s in-FOV counts above the particle expectation, distributed over the columns as in the FWC list, x the column’s corner share, measured on the FWC list) are subtracted.

Conversion to the stage: the stage draws the xmm_simulator FWC spectrum (resp.fwc_spec) scaled by the factors of the donor background table; the conversion from the corner scale to those factors is done by the caller, not in this module.

Instrument use: InstrumentOptions.qpb_level (QPB_LEVELS): 'in-fov' (only with background = 'nominal') keeps the FWC level; 'corners' sets each PPS band’s QPB factor of every camera to that band’s corner scale (table donor_qpb_corners.csv; a camera without a usable corner level, such as a pn Large or Small Window exposure, whose corners are not read out, takes the FWC level, camera_qpb_level()) (the FWC spectra scaled to the observation band by band with the corner data, as Snowden et al. 2008, Sect. 3.1.1, do spectral bin by spectral bin), so a donor whose particle spectrum is softer or harder than the FWC keeps it; a single scale for all bands was tried and made the replicas LESS like their donors (DECISIONS, “QPB level from the out-of-FOV corners”). The corner counts are taken within the flare GTIs of the PPS images (flare_gti() from the PPS flare time series and its cut).

Functions

apply_corner_band_scales(bkg, scales)

The camera's donor-background parameters with the QPB band factors set to scales (band -> factor) and the QPB scale to the 4.5-12 keV factor; unchanged when scales is None or empty.

camera_qpb_level(bkg, scales)

The camera's background parameters with its QPB level under qpb_level = 'corners', and where the level comes from: the donor's corner scales ('corners') when it has them, else the FWC level of the CCF ('fwc': every band factor 1).

corner_excluded(cam, detx, dety)

Events inside the regions XMM-ESAS leaves out of the MOS corners (MOS_CORNER_EXCLUDE); all False for the pn (Kuntz & Snowden 2008 give no pn regions).

corner_mask(flag, pattern, pi, cam[, ...])

Events of the corner (or, with in_fov, the in-FOV) selection; band [eV] defaults to CORNER_BAND_EV.

corner_scale(events, live, fwc, cam[, ...])

The donor's corner QPB level relative to the FWC: sum over usable CCDs of corner counts / live time, over the same sum of the FWC; err from Poisson; pn: OoT counts of the in-FOV X-ray excess subtracted per column.

donor_corner_band_scales(table, obsid)

camera -> PPS band -> stage QPB factor (columns qpb_stage_<band>_<cam>; pn band 1000 takes band 2000); cameras without a finite positive 0.5-12 keV set are absent.

donor_corner_counts(table, obsid)

camera -> the counts behind the donor's corner measurement: band (PPS band -> corner counts, ncorner_<band>_<cam>; the pn band 1000 takes band 2000, as its scale does), unit (unit -> corner-band counts, nunit_<u>_<cam>) and unit_value (unit -> its corner scale, unit_<u>_<cam>).

donor_unit_factors(table, obsid)

camera -> CCD -> the QPB level of the CCD (MOS) or of its quadrant (pn) relative to the mean of the camera's units with corner counts (columns unit_<u>_<cam> of the background table, tngxmm.donor_tables.qpb_corners); cameras without unit columns are absent.

flare_gti(time, rate, timedel, threshold[, ...])

Good time intervals (N, 2) of the PPS flare screening reconstructed from the flare background time series (FBKTSR: the >10 keV single-event rate per TIMEDEL bin) and its optimised cut FLCUTTHR: bins with a finite rate at or below the cut, adjacent bins merged.

fwc_infov_rate(fwc, area_arcmin2)

CCF FWC in-FOV QPB rate [counts / s / arcmin^2] of the corner band, area-weighted over the CCDs with an area.

fwc_reference(events, exptime, cam[, band])

Per CCD of the FWC list: corner counts, in-FOV counts, exposure [s], and for the pn per (CCD, RAWX) the corner share of the column's particle events.

gti_mask(time, gti)

Events whose TIME lies in a good time interval.

gti_matching_ontime(time, rate, timedel, ontime)

Good time intervals made of the lowest-rate bins of the flare time series whose total length reaches ontime (the PPS image's ONTIME): the optimised flare cut keeps the quietest bins, so this reproduces the image's screened time when the FBKTSR cut (flare_gti()) and the image GTIs differ (attitude GTIs, rebinning).

gti_overlap(gti, start, stop)

Length [s] of the good time intervals inside [start, stop).

pattern_max(cam[, band])

PATTERN limit of the PPS images: MOS 12; pn 4, single pixels only below 0.5 keV.

pps_band_parts(cam, band)

The PI ranges [eV] of PPS image band band of camera cam (one range, or two around a gap).

simulated_corner_measurement(counts, ...)

The corner measurement of a mock camera, drawn with the counting statistics of the donor's: for every band (and every scaling unit) whose donor measurement rests on n counts, the mock's corners hold N ~ Poisson(n x live_ratio) (the mock camera's live time over the donor's), so the mock measures its QPB level as the true level times N / E with E = n x live_ratio, as the donor measured its own with an error of the same size.

tngxmm.instrument.donor_corner_qpb.simulated_corner_measurement(counts, live_ratio, rng)[source]¶

The corner measurement of a mock camera, drawn with the counting statistics of the donor’s: for every band (and every scaling unit) whose donor measurement rests on n counts, the mock’s corners hold N ~ Poisson(n x live_ratio) (the mock camera’s live time over the donor’s), so the mock measures its QPB level as the true level times N / E with E = n x live_ratio, as the donor measured its own with an error of the same size. Returns band (band -> N / E) and unit (unit -> measured relative level, the units’ values times their N / E, normalised to mean 1 over the units with counts, as donor_unit_factors() normalises them); a band or unit without counts keeps 1. No draw for counts None (both empty).
