tngxmm.massfit.fit_lists¶

Target lists drawn from the results of a fit run: what is left to fit, and two samples of finished fits.

A fit run writes one results_all.csv per work directory, a row per target with its status. Three lists are made from such tables and the fit targets they belong to, each a subcommand of tngxmm massfit fit-plan:

remaining the targets without a finished result (unfinished_targets()), largest theta500_arcmin

first: the list a further job or another machine is given.

refit-sample a sample of finished fits stratified by run time and fitted mass (stratified_refit_sample()):

the finished fits of one redshift are cut into n_time quantile classes of runtime_s and n_mass quantile classes of m500_1e14, and per_cell names are drawn from every cell with a seeded generator. Fitted again elsewhere, they compare two machines or two code states over the whole range of run times and masses.

rank-sample per group (redshift) a given number of finished fits at evenly spaced ranks of the fitted mass

(rank_spread_sample()), optionally only those with a view on disk, outside another sample and below a sampler time: a short list that spans the masses of every redshift.

A status counts as finished when it starts with one of the words given (ok, unconstrained; a fit without an R500 crossing has the status unconstrained (no R500 crossing) and is not repeated). Every other status, an error or a timeout, leaves the target in the remaining list.

The lists of 2026-11-26 were made this way from the two lanes of pass 1: 978 remaining targets; a control sample of 75 fits at z = 0.1 (5 run-time classes x 3 mass classes x 5 names, seed 20261126); a pilot list of 22 names (10, 5, 4 and 3 at z = 0.1, 0.2, 1.0 and 1.5; the other redshifts had no finished fit with a view on disk). The draw of stratified_refit_sample() depends on the order of the rows in the result table, so a table whose rows were written in another order gives another sample with the same seed; tests/unit/data/fit_lists keeps the rows the control sample was drawn from.

Nothing here selects what enters an analysis: the samples choose fits to repeat.

tngxmm.massfit.fit_lists.stratified_refit_sample(results, targets=None, *, seed, z=0.1, n_time=5, n_mass=3, per_cell=5, status='ok')[source]¶

A seeded sample of finished fits, per_cell from every cell of run time x fitted mass.

The rows of results with status (exactly) are taken; with targets only those whose name is listed there, and z is then the redshift of the target, else that of the result. z None keeps every redshift. runtime_s is cut into n_time classes and m500_1e14 into n_mass classes at their quantiles over these rows (pandas.qcut(); the two cuts are independent, so the cells are not equally filled). The cells are visited in the order (run-time class, mass class) and from each one min(per_cell, size) names are drawn without replacement by numpy.random.default_rng(seed).choice from the names of the cell in the order of results; one generator serves all cells.

Returns the drawn rows of results in its order, with the columns rt_bin and m_bin (class numbers from 0).

tngxmm.massfit.fit_lists.spread_ranks(n, k)[source]¶

k ranks spread evenly from 0 to n-1: round(linspace(0, n-1, k)) (halves to the even rank), the first and the last always among them; every rank once, so at most n.

tngxmm.massfit.fit_lists.rank_spread_sample(results, per_group, *, group='z', value='m500_1e14', among=None, exclude=None, max_sampler_h=None, status='ok')[source]¶

Per value of group the fits at evenly spaced ranks of value.

The candidates are the rows of results with status, with a name in among when given (the views on disk), not in exclude (another sample), and with a sampler time of at most max_sampler_h hours when given. The sampler time is runtime_s - runtime_optimiser_s, the part of the run time after the optimiser. For each key g of per_group the candidates with group equal to g (to 1e-8) are sorted by value and the rows at the ranks spread_ranks() (count, per_group[g]) are taken; a group without candidates gives none.

Returns the chosen rows of results in its order, with the column samp_h (sampler time in hours).
