tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions¶

The catalogue entry of every real target: its position, the published positional error, the filter scale its SZ position was measured at and the type and error of its redshift.

A real fit’s centre search starts at the catalogue position of its cluster and the fit uses the catalogue’s redshift. A mock is given what the catalogue entry of the cluster it replicates (its physics donor) would give it: a position measured the way that catalogue measures positions (position_rule(), tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre) moved by an error drawn from the entry’s published positional error (mock_catalogue_offset()), and a redshift moved by an error drawn from the entry’s published redshift error when that redshift is photometric (mock_redshift()). The entry is the one the observation’s cluster_id was made from (cluster_id_entry of observations.csv):

  • PSZ2 (Planck Collaboration 2016, Sect. 2.1, p. 3): POS_ERR (column e_pos), from the Gaussian position likelihood of the detection, read here as the radius containing the true position at 90 per cent confidence, the meaning Sect. 7.2 gives it where the paper itself uses it to accept a counterpart (the rule the centre search copies); the column note of the catalogue (p. 33, note 3) calls it a 95 per cent interval, under which sigma would be 0.88 times the value used here. For a circular Gaussian the offset is Rayleigh-distributed, so sigma = r90 / sqrt(-2 ln 0.1). The positions are peaks of maps filtered with the universal pressure profile convolved with the Planck beams (Sect. 2.1); a mock’s filter scale is the angular R500 of the entry’s mass MSZ (theta500_sz_arcmin, converted to the pipeline cosmology as observations.csv converts it), not the scale of highest S/N the detection chose. Not modelled: MMF3 positions lie on the 1.72 arcmin map pixels (Sect. 6.2: no sub-pixel positioning), 1.72’ / sqrt(12) = 0.50 arcmin rms per axis. The MMF3 POS_ERR is taken to hold it: App. C.2 says the MMF3 positional error was corrected and tested against the quality-assessment injections, and those injections measure MMF3’s accuracy on its own pixel-bound positions (Sect. 4.4, p. 8: a 67 per cent bound of 1.52’); adding it again would widen sigma by a median 10 per cent (90th percentile 21) for those entries (docs/assumptions/OPEN_ITEMS.md section P). PwS positions come from its posteriors (Sect. 2.2) and, in PSZ2, MMF1’s too (App. C.1); of the 427 PSZ2 donors, 147 have an MMF3 reference position, 144 a PwS and 136 an MMF1 one (Pipeline). PSZ2 gives one redshift per detection with its counterpart (zID) and neither types it nor gives its error (Sect. 7.6). Where the counterpart’s catalogue types its redshifts, the PSZ2 value is typed from that catalogue’s published entry (psz2_redshift()): for 40 PSZ2 donors the counterpart is an optical cluster catalogue with photometric redshifts (28 redMaPPer, 11 WHL, 1 MaxBCG), for 17 the SPT-SZ catalogue (Bleem et al. 2015, which PSZ2 matched to, Sect. 7.5.1), all read through the table --counterparts (tools/psz2_counterpart_redshifts.py), and for 3 an ACT cluster, whose ACT DR6 entry (the nearest within the 10 arcmin PSZ2 matches ACT clusters in, Sect. 7.5.2) types it. The value is spec when it is the counterpart’s spectroscopic redshift, phot when it is its photometric one, with the catalogue’s per-cluster error (redMaPPer DR8 v5.10 Z_LAMBDA_ERR, SPT-SZ e_z, ACT DR6 redshiftErr) or, where the catalogue publishes none, the error its paper gives (PHOTZ_PAPER_SIGMA), otherwise untyped (the value came from elsewhere). The other PSZ2 redshifts stay untyped because nothing published types them: an X-ray counterpart’s (MCXC, whose redshifts are those of its input catalogues, taken over without a type, Piffaretti et al. 2011, Sect. 3.1), a database counterpart’s (NED and SIMBAD names, Abell, Zwicky; PSZ2 Sect. 7.3.2 and 7.6: no type, and the least reliable of its assignments) and those of the 51 donors whose entry names no counterpart at all (Sect. 7.6: from the PSZ1 compilation and the Planck follow-up, with no record of the source). They get no drawn error, so for them the mock redshift errors are a lower bound;

  • ACT DR6 (ACT Collaboration 2025, Sect. 2.4, the Rayleigh fit to source injections): Rayleigh-distributed offsets with sigma = 1.428 arcmin / q, q the S/N at the 2.4 arcmin reference filter (fixed_SNR), from source injection. The positions come from the map filtered at that reference scale (Sect. 2.2.2). The catalogue types every redshift (redshiftType, spec or phot) and gives an uncertainty for the photometric ones (redshiftErr, Appendix A, which does not say what kind of interval it is: it is read as a Gaussian 1 sigma, an assumption). The three WaZP redshifts of the donors all carry 0.030, a value that looks nominal rather than measured per cluster; it is used as published;

  • any other position (three observations whose cluster is in neither catalogue, MACS clusters with X-ray positions): their own catalogue position, with no published error (sigma = 0) and an untyped redshift.

The centre search radius is max(theta500, r90), the rule by which PSZ2 accepts an X-ray counterpart (MCXC) of a detection: a separation is accepted when it is within the cluster’s angular size or within the Planck positional uncertainty (Planck Collaboration 2016, Sect. 7.2, p. 19, Fig. 23).

Data-package table catalogue_positions_v4.csv: obsid, catalogue, entry, ra_cat, dec_cat, snr, sigma_arcmin, r90_arcmin, theta500_sz_arcmin, z_type, z_err, z_source, z_err_source, z_rule. python -m tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions --psz2 <PSZ2.fits> --dr6 <DR6 catalogue> --counterparts <CSV> builds it from the observations table, the two published catalogue files and the counterpart redshifts (kept outside the package with the other catalogues). A table built before a column existed, or under another redshift rule (z_rule other than REDSHIFT_RULE_VERSION), is refused where it is needed (rebuild it).

catalogue_entry(obsid[, table])

default_table_path()

main([argv])

python -m tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions [--out CSV]: build the data-package table.

mock_catalogue_offset(mock_id, sigma_arcmin)

(east, north) [arcsec] of a mock's simulated catalogue error: a circular Gaussian of sigma_arcmin per axis (the published error of the replicated cluster's entry), drawn from a stream keyed by the mock's id (header VIEW_ID, else MOCKOBS), so every consumer of the view (seed, fit, realism features) and every paired study arm of a closure cell draws the same offset.

mock_redshift(z_obs, obsid, mock_id[, table])

The redshift a mock's catalogue entry would give it: z_fit = z_obs + z_offset, z_offset drawn by mock_redshift_offset() from the published error of the replicated cluster's redshift (obsid, its physics donor) when that redshift is photometric (ACT DR6 phot and the PSZ2 redshifts typed phot from their counterpart, psz2_redshift()), 0 for a spectroscopic or an untyped one (the other PSZ2 redshifts and the three other positions publish no type and no error).

mock_redshift_offset(mock_id, z_obs, z_err)

A mock's simulated photometric-redshift error: a Gaussian of z_err (the published error of the replicated cluster's photometric redshift), drawn from a stream keyed by the mock's id like mock_catalogue_offset() (its own stream), redrawn until z_obs plus the error is positive (a redshift behind the observer is not a catalogue value).

photz_paper_sigma(catalogue, z)

The photometric-redshift error the paper of counterpart catalogue publishes at the quoted redshift nearest z (PHOTZ_PAPER_SIGMA).

position_error(obsid[, table])

sigma_arcmin, r90_arcmin, ra_cat, dec_cat and catalogue of observation obsid.

position_rule(obsid[, table, spec, ...])

How the catalogue entry of observation obsid (a mock's physics donor) measured its position, the rule a mock's catalogue position is made with (tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre):

psz2_redshift(z, zid, counterparts[, act])

z_type, z_err and z_err_source of a PSZ2 redshift z whose counterpart is zid, from the counterpart's published entry (counterparts: rows of tools/psz2_counterpart_redshifts.py indexed by zid, columns catalogue, z_phot, z_phot_err, z_spec ';'-separated; act: for an ACT counterpart its ACT DR6 entry, act_counterpart(), with redshift, redshiftType and redshiftErr, None when DR6 holds none):

psz2_theta500_arcmin(msz_1e14, z_entry, z_obs)

The angular R500 [arcmin] of a PSZ2 entry's mass msz_1e14 [1e14 Msun]: MSZ re-solved in the pipeline cosmology at the entry's redshift (tngxmm.obs.mass_conversion.sz_m500_to_cosmology()) and its R500 seen at the observation's redshift z_obs, the conversion of observations.csv (theta500_arcmin of the rows whose mass is the PSZ2 one).

read_counterparts(path)

The counterpart table of tools/psz2_counterpart_redshifts.py, indexed by zid.

read_table([path])

The table, obsid as 10-digit strings.

redshift_error(obsid[, table])

z_type (spec | phot | untyped) and z_err (the published error; NaN where none is published) of the redshift of observation obsid's catalogue entry.

tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.position_rule(obsid, table=None, spec=None, *, smoothing_arcsec=None)[source]¶

How the catalogue entry of observation obsid (a mock’s physics donor) measured its position, the rule a mock’s catalogue position is made with (tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre):

  • ACT DR6: kind = 'sz', the universal-pressure-profile filter of theta500 = 2.4 arcmin (ACT_DR6_POSITION_FILTER_ARCMIN) and the f150 beam (ACT_DR6_BEAM_FWHM_ARCMIN) with a noise term of the lensed CMB plus white noise of ACT_DR6_WHITE_NOISE_UK_ARCMIN (noise = 'cmb+white'); the estimator is the S/N-weighted centre of the S/N > 3 segment (ACT_DR6_SEGMENT_SNR) that holds the detection, the map scaled so that the halo’s own maximum has the entry’s S/N (snr, fixed_SNR of the catalogue);

  • PSZ2: kind = 'sz', the same profile at the angular R500 of the entry’s MSZ (theta500_sz_arcmin) and the 143 GHz beam (PSZ2_BEAM_FWHM_ARCMIN), white noise; the estimator is the peak of the filtered map, the reference maximum of the detection’s group (the maxima are taken from the highest down, each taking those within the merging distance, PSZ2_MERGE_ARCMIN, not yet taken);

  • other (an X-ray position): kind = 'xray', the X-ray peak with the smoothing of the peak finder (smoothing_arcsec, else photometry.peak_smoothing_arcsec of spec; None: the production preset).

Every rule carries version (POSITION_RULE_VERSION) and the entry’s published positional error sigma_arcmin (0 for the X-ray positions), against which the edge test of the position is read (tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre.catalogue_position()). Raises KeyError for an observation not in the table and ValueError for a PSZ2 entry without a filter scale or an ACT entry without an S/N above the segmentation threshold (a table built before the columns existed, or an entry without MSZ).

tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.mock_catalogue_offset(mock_id, sigma_arcmin)[source]¶

(east, north) [arcsec] of a mock’s simulated catalogue error: a circular Gaussian of sigma_arcmin per axis (the published error of the replicated cluster’s entry), drawn from a stream keyed by the mock’s id (header VIEW_ID, else MOCKOBS), so every consumer of the view (seed, fit, realism features) and every paired study arm of a closure cell draws the same offset.

tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.mock_redshift_offset(mock_id, z_obs, z_err)[source]¶

A mock’s simulated photometric-redshift error: a Gaussian of z_err (the published error of the replicated cluster’s photometric redshift), drawn from a stream keyed by the mock’s id like mock_catalogue_offset() (its own stream), redrawn until z_obs plus the error is positive (a redshift behind the observer is not a catalogue value). Reading the published error as a Gaussian standard deviation is an assumption: ACT DR6 lists the column only as the uncertainty of the redshift (docs/assumptions/OPEN_ITEMS.md, section P).

tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.mock_redshift(z_obs, obsid, mock_id, table=None)[source]¶

The redshift a mock’s catalogue entry would give it: z_fit = z_obs + z_offset, z_offset drawn by mock_redshift_offset() from the published error of the replicated cluster’s redshift (obsid, its physics donor) when that redshift is photometric (ACT DR6 phot and the PSZ2 redshifts typed phot from their counterpart, psz2_redshift()), 0 for a spectroscopic or an untyped one (the other PSZ2 redshifts and the three other positions publish no type and no error).
