tngxmm.sim.compute_hse_mass¶

Intrinsic hydrostatic masses of a halo from its gas cells, and the particle M500 at any z.

The hydrostatic mass the simulated gas itself implies, from the TRUE 3-D profiles with no instrument and no fit, so that the bias of the mock fits splits into a physical part (M_HSE / M500c: non-thermal support, multiphase gas seen by X-rays) and a method part (M_fit / M_HSE).

compute_hse_truth() reads the snapshot through a HaloSource, so the fixture cutouts work too. Per halo, 48 log shells from 0.05 to 3 R500c about GroupPos (periodic):

  • true M_true(<r): gas + DM + stars + BH of the original zoom;

  • th_vw thermal HSE, -r^2/(G rho_gas) dP_th/dr, P_th = (gamma-1) rho u,

    volume-weighted shell mean over all non-star-forming gas (no mu, no T needed);

  • th_med the same with the shell MEDIAN of P and rho (clump-robust);

  • th+kin P_th + rho sigma_r^2 plus the Jeans anisotropy term -(r/G) 2

    beta sigma_r^2, velocities about the shell’s mean radial flow and the halo bulk velocity, Hubble flow added (closure test);

  • X_Tsl_nemed, X_Tmw_nemed, X_Tsl_nevw, X_Tsl_nemed_mu0.641

    “X-ray-like” -(kT r/(G mu m_p)) (dln n_e/dln r + dln T/dln r) of the hot gas (T > 5.5 MK, non-SF): n_e shell median or volume-weighted mean, T spectroscopic-like (n^2 T^-0.75 V, Mazzotta et al. 2004) or mass-weighted, mu = 0.59 or mbproj2d’s 0.6409;

  • X_Tsl_nemed_mock, X_Tmw_nemed_mock (the

    truth with the mock’s own gas selection and floor) the same estimator on the cells the mock emits from: the mock’s loader selection, T > t_min_k, non-SF, net cooling, inside the clip sphere, with the mock’s per-cell X_H.

The literature definitions, each its own column (_literature_estimators(); mu = 0.59; log-slopes by finite differences over the shell centres):

  • barnes21 Barnes et al. (2021, MNRAS 506, 2533; secs. 2.1.5,

    2.4): 3-D mass-weighted T and gas density in 50 LINEAR shells over 0-1.5 r500, thermal pressure only, NO gas cut (Barnes’s cuts belong to its synthetic images);

  • barnes21_nosf (NOSF_FD_ESTIMATOR) the same without star-forming cells

    (their temperature is the effective EOS value, not a physical one); finite-difference slopes, mu = 0.59. NOT EMITTED (too easily mistaken for the headline barnes21_nosf_fit_mu): no results column, no truth-profile dataset, not a selectable headline; computed only on request (nosf_fd=True of compute_hse_truth()) for the unit tests that compare it with the fitted headline;

  • barnes21_nosf_fit_mu THE fitted Barnes truth and the headline: Barnes et al. (2021) sec. 2.4 – 50 linear shells over 0-1.5 r500c, the shell mass-weighted quantities and the shell gas density fitted with the Vikhlinin (2006) forms and differentiated analytically (see _m_fit()), gas without star-forming cells (their temperature is the effective equation-of-state value of the TNG star-formation model) – but with the thermal pressure of each cell as simulated: the shell mass-weighted <T/mu> with each cell’s own mu (4 / (1 + 3 X_H + 4 X_H x_e), X_H of the cell from GFM_Metals where present) replaces the temperature and the fixed mu = 0.59: M = -(k r / (G m_p)) <T/mu> (dln rho/dln r + dln <T/mu>/dln r), <T/mu> fitted with the Vikhlinin T form. Since T = (gamma-1) u mu m_p / k, T/mu is the internal energy per unit mass in temperature units: the estimator is the pressure-based thermal HSE mass with fitted slopes (mu_hse_barnes21_nosf_fit_mu is NaN: no single value). hse_fit_ok_barnes21_nosf_fit_mu flags a converged pair of fits.

  • barnes21_nosf_fit (MU059_FIT_ESTIMATOR) the same fit with T and the assumed mu = 0.59. NOT EMITTED (no results column, no truth-profile dataset, not a selectable headline): it is computed only on request (mu059_fit=True of compute_hse_truth()), kept for the unit test that shows it differs from the headline only through the cells’ departure of mu from 0.59.

  • barnes21_fit_mu, barnes21_fit (S8_ESTIMATORS) the headline’s fit over every cell (star-forming cells kept), with the own mu and with mu = 0.59 (the latter is Barnes’s estimator as published). NOT EMITTED: computed only on request (s8_variants=True) for ledger test S8, which sizes the two deviations of the headline from Barnes et al. one at a time.

  • barnes21_mock the same on the mock’s own cells, with the mock’s own

    selection (load_halo_gas.emission_masks: drop T < t_min_k, SFR > 0 and heated cells, GFM_CoolingRate >= 0; loader cells inside the clip sphere);

  • barnes21_xcut with Barnes’s X-ray-image cuts (sec. 2.2.3: drop T < 1e6 K,

    SFR > 0, and cells whose net cooling rate is positive, i.e. heated): the same emission_masks rule with a 1e6 K floor. GFM_CoolingRate is negative for net cooling and positive for net heating; heated cells (>= 0) are dropped, net-cooling cells (< 0) kept;

  • barnes21_tsl the xcut cells with the spectroscopic-like T (Mazzotta et

    al. 2004, w = n^2 T^-0.75 V; valid above ~2-3 keV: the halo’s T_sl inside R500c and t_sl_valid are recorded);

  • pearce20_th, pearce20_thkin, pearce20_thkin_sig3d Pearce et

    al. (2020, MNRAS 491, 1622; sec. 3.6): 50 log shells over 0.05-2 r500 without T < 1e5 K or n_H >= 0.1 cm^-3 cells; thermal only, + rho sigma_1D^2 (sigma_3D^2 / 3 of the peculiar velocities about the shell’s mean velocity), + rho sigma_3D^2.

For each estimator: the ratio to M500c at R500c (*_atR500c), the self-consistent M500 at 500 rho_c(z_snap) beyond 0.4 R500c (*_M500self, *_R500self: the INNERMOST downward crossing of 500 rho_c by M_HSE(<r)/(4/3 pi r^3), log-interpolated between the two shells; *_ncross counts the downward crossings, NaN M500self when there is none) and the median ratio to M_true over 0.8-1.2 R500c. The headline’s count becomes the hse_crossing column (none / single / multiple).

Shell density (sim.hse_shell_density, shell_gas_density()): every estimator that pairs a shell gas density with a cell-averaged T or P uses either the selected gas mass over the geometric shell volume (geometric, the definitions as written, which assume the selected cells fill the shell) or over the selected cells’ summed volume (filling). With a FoF-member selection the outer shells of high-z groups are only partly filled by FoF members (the campaign computes its truth from all particles in a sphere, sim.truth_selection_hse = 'sphere', which fills them, and keeps the filling rule); the geometric density then falls while the cell-averaged P and T do not, and the HSE mass comes out high. filling gives the density of the gas present; shells filled below sim.hse_min_filling (0.1 in the campaign) are omitted from the profiles. The cumulative particle masses are unaffected.

The headline intrinsic mass m500c_hse_msun is the estimator the preset’s method fields select (sim.hse_pressure, hse_gas_phase_cut, hse_shell_binning, t_weighting; variant_for()). The campaign’s choice (one methodological line) is barnes21_nosf_fit_mu: Barnes et al. (2021) secs. 2.1.5 and 2.4 (Vikhlinin fits, analytic slopes, 50 linear shells to 1.5 R500c), with one deviation of ours: Barnes states no gas cut for the 3-D profiles, we drop the star-forming cells, whose temperature is the effective equation-of-state value of the TNG star-formation model, not a physical one (TNG data specifications; the same exclusion as the mock’s gas selection, Chatzigiannakis et al. 2025), with each cell’s own thermal pressure (its own mu). Every other estimator stays its own column, classified headline or diagnostic in the truth-column table of docs/dev/INTERFACES.md (truth_columns()).

m500_at_z() gives M500 at 500 rho_c(z) (any z, e.g. the view’s z_obs, the fitter’s convention) from the halo’s particle mass profile; at z_snap it reproduces the catalogue Group_M_Crit500.

References

Hydrostatic equilibrium mass (Sarazin 1988); hydrostatic bias in simulated clusters (Rasia et al. 2006); spectroscopic-like temperature (Mazzotta et al. 2004); IllustrisTNG / TNG-Cluster particle data.

tngxmm.sim.compute_hse_mass.compute_hse_truth(source, *, selection='zoom', mock_selection=None, mock_radius_ckpc_h=None, mock_clip_radius_kpc=None, mock_t_min_k=300000.0, om0=0.3089, n_grid=4000, verbose=False, profile=False, shell_density='geometric', min_filling=None, abund_table='angr', mu059_fit=False, nosf_fd=False, s8_variants=False)[source]¶

Intrinsic hydrostatic masses of the halo held by source .

selection names the particles of every truth estimator (gas, DM, stars, BH): 'zoom' (the halo’s ORIGINAL ZOOM, a fixture cutout or the snapshot through loadOriginalZoom) or 'fof' (the halo’s FoF members, loadHalo; sim.truth_selection, every simulation; the profiles beyond ~R200c then hold FoF members only, the catalogue M500c is unchanged) or 'sphere' (every particle within a sphere about GroupPos, all FoF groups and fuzz: the source of the spherical truth stage, tngxmm.sim.truth_sphere.SphereSource, sim.truth_selection_hse). Distances are minimum-image about GroupPos either way. With mock_selection (the mock’s loader, e.g. 'fof') the two *_mock estimators and the R500c temperatures of the mock’s gas are added (mock_clip_radius_kpc default 2 R200c, as the mock).

Returns {'summary': <the json fields + mock ones>, 'profiles': <the npz arrays>, 'mass_profile': (r_kpc, M(<r) Msun) on an ``n_grid-point log grid 0.05-5 R500c}``.

profile=True (sim.truth_profiles = 'on') adds 'truth_profile': the arrays of the per-halo truth-profile file (tngxmm.sim.truth_profile), binned from the cells and particles loaded here (no extra read); every other number is unchanged (additions only).

shell_density / min_filling (sim.hse_shell_density / sim.hse_min_filling): the shell gas density of every estimator that pairs it with a cell-averaged T or P (shell_gas_density()): 'geometric' (the definition as written, default) or 'filling' (the selected gas mass over the selected cells’ volume, so a FoF group that does not fill its outer shells gives the density of the gas present, consistent with its pressure); shells whose filling factor is below min_filling are omitted (NaN) from the estimators. The cumulative masses (true, M_gas(<r)) are the actual particle sums either way.

abund_table (the campaign: sim.apec_abund_table = lodd, the fit’s table): the solar units of the emission-weighted metallicities z_ew_r500c_{nonsf,mock} (total metallicity, GFM_Metallicity / Z_sun of the table) and z_fe_ew_r500c_{nonsf,mock} (iron, X_Fe / X_Fe,sun) of the summary, and of the metallicity/ profiles of the truth profile. barnes21_nosf_fit_mu (_literature_estimators()) uses each cell’s own X_H from GFM_Metals.

mu059_fit: also fit the mu = 0.59 variant (MU059_FIT_ESTIMATOR) into the summary and the profile arrays; never into the truth-profile native/ datasets or the columns. nosf_fd: the same for the finite-difference non-SF estimator (NOSF_FD_ESTIMATOR). s8_variants: the same for S8_ESTIMATORS (ledger test S8).
