tngxmm.instrument.obs_responses¶

Per-observation EPIC responses: the RMF and on-axis ARF of each exposure of the real observations, made by SAS for the observation’s date, camera, mode, filter and pattern selection.

The shared set (massfit.build_responses, MBPROJ/responses_v1) has one RMF per camera, made in 2020 with rmfgen of SAS 16.1 for one observation, and on-axis ARFs computed from the CCF tables. Generation and fit both use it, so a mock fit never meets the response of another epoch, while a real fit does. This module builds, for every exposure that a real observation’s fit images come from, the response SAS would give that exposure, and serves it to

  • the fit (massfit.responses = 'per-observation'): every image takes the RMF and ARF of its own exposure, the conditions donor’s (header DONORID) for a mock and its own (OBS_ID) for a real image;

  • the instrument stage (instrument.responses): the photons of a view are redistributed with the conditions donor’s RMF ('donor-rmf'), and with 'donor' the on-axis energy dependence of the donor’s ARF replaces that of the stage’s CCF ARF, whose off-axis (vignetting) dependence is kept.

Layout of a response set (root $TNGXMM_OBS_RESP, site key TNGXMM_OBS_RESP):

<root>/<obsid>/<CAMERA>_<EXPID>.rmf      rmfgen, the cluster region, detector-map weighted
<root>/<obsid>/<CAMERA>_<EXPID>.arf      arfgen, on axis, on the energy grid of that RMF
<root>/<obsid>/provenance.json           SAS version, CCF set and the constituents used, inputs, settings, sha256
<root>/<obsid>/logs/                     the SAS logs
<root>/manifest.csv                      one row per exposure (written by :func:`write_manifest`)

CAMERA is PN, MOS1 or MOS2, EXPID the PPS exposure identifier (S003, U002, …; header EXPIDSTR).

Per exposure (entry_command(); SAS 22.1 through the sasrun container wrapper; the SAS threads’ settings):

  1. cifbuild for the observation date (withobservationdate) with the fixed analysis date of the CCF download (tngxmm.instrument.sas_detect.CCF_ANALYSIS_DATE), once per observation;

  2. evselect: the spectrum of the cluster region of the PPS event list, PATTERN <= 4 and FLAG == 0 for the pn, PATTERN <= 12 and #XMMEA_EM for the MOS (the pattern selections of the PPS images and of the SAS threads), PI channels of 5 eV (pn, 0-20479) and 15 eV (MOS, 0-11999); and the detector image (DETX, DETY, 4 arcsec) of the same events at 0.5-7 keV;

  3. rmfgen with that image as detector map (detmaptype=dataset): the redistribution averaged over the cluster’s counts in the region, which carries the epoch (the MOS redistribution changes with time in the CCF, EMOSn_REDIST issues by validity date) and the position (MOS boresight patch, pn RAWY);

  4. arfgen on axis (sourcecoords=tel, theta = 0; flat detector map, no encircled-energy, bad-pixel or out-of-time correction: the PPS exposure maps carry the vignetting, the gaps and the dead time of an image), with the corrections of SAS 22 at their defaults written out (MOS contamination, MOS-to-pn CORRAREA, EPIC-to-NuSTAR ABSCORRAREA; ResponseSettings).

The region is the circle of the cluster centre the fit uses (obs_centres_guarded_v4.csv: the guarded X-ray peak) with radius theta500 of the table, at most REGION_MAX_ARCSEC.

Resumable: an observation is finished when its provenance.json says ok and every response it lists exists; run_queue() works off the rest, one single-core observation per core.

References

xmm_ccf_sas (SAS rmfgen, arfgen, cifbuild, evselect; CCF), xmm_cal_tn0018 (the time-dependent MOS redistribution and contamination, the SAS 22 default effective-area corrections).

Functions

adopt_entries(entries[, settings])

Take over the finished responses that an earlier centre table planned and the current one plans identically.

(resps, used): camera -> response with the conditions donor's responses (mode 'donor-rmf': its RMF; 'donor': its RMF and the on-axis energy dependence of its ARF; 'packaged': unchanged), and camera -> (rmf, arf) file names used.

arf_bin_average(arf_path, edges)

(mean SPECRESP [cm^2] over each bin of edges [keV], whether the ARF's bins cover the bin entirely): the ARF taken as constant over each of its own energy bins, as XSPEC folds it, and averaged over the overlap.

check_set([root, centres])

The digest of the set (set_digest(); FileNotFoundError without a manifest) after checking that every entry was planned from the current fit-centre table (centres, default the data package's obs_centres_guarded_v4.csv): the manifest's centres_sha256 must be that table's sha256, else ValueError.

cif_constituents(cif)

{'<SCOPE>_<TYPEID>': issue} of the response-relevant constituents of a CIF (RESPONSE_CCF_TYPES).

count_donors(entries[, conditions])

How many of the planned observations are conditions donors (rows of the package's condition table).

entry_command(entry, settings, workdir)

The shell command of one observation (run inside the SAS container in workdir): the CIF of the observation date, then every exposure with an event list; a failing exposure does not stop the others.

exposure_commands(entry, exp, settings)

The SAS command lines of one exposure, run in the entry's work directory with SAS_CCF set: spectrum and detector image of the region, rmfgen, arfgen (outputs <CAMERA>_<EXPID>.{pi,img,rmf,arf}, logs in logs/).

finished(entry)

True when <out>/provenance.json says ok and every response it lists exists.

header_obsid(header)

The observation whose response an image header takes: DONORID (a mock: its conditions donor), else OBS_ID.

image_exposures(img_root, obsid)

The per-camera exposures of the fit images of obsid: one per P<obsid><code><expid>IMAGE_2000.FTZ of the image tree (camera, expid, filter, submode, date).

manifest_digest(root)

sha256 of the sorted (obsid, camera, expid, rmf sha256, arf sha256) rows of the manifest (16 hex digits): the identity of a response set, recorded by the fits and the instrument stage that use it.

missing_responses(donors[, root, active_ccds])

{obsid: reason} for the donors whose per-observation responses a view generated under instrument.responses 'donor' (or a fit of it under 'per-observation') would not find: no entry in the set, an entry whose provenance.json status is not ok, or a camera of the entry without a response of the exposure the mock headers name (EXPIDSTR of tngxmm.instrument.sas_headers.package_sas_donor(), the same lookup as response_pair()).

onaxis_ratio(resp, arf_path)

g(E) on the stage's energy bins box_ene: the ARF of arf_path averaged over each bin (arf_bin_average(); the stage's bins under instrument.arf_model = 'fit-linear' are the RMF's, which are the bins of the donor's ARF, so the average is its own value) over the stage's on-axis area arf_theta[0], so that arf_theta[0] * g is that ARF within its energy range; beyond it g keeps its value at the last bin inside (the stage's own shape there, scaled), and where the stage has no area g is zero.

plan_entries(centres, img_root, pps_root, ...)

One entry per observation of centres (the table of the fit centres: obsid, ra_peak, dec_peak, theta500_arcsec): its image exposures, each with the PPS event list of the same exposure (evli None when the archive has none), the region and the output directory <out_root>/<obsid>.

read_arf(arf_path)

(bin-centre energies [keV], SPECRESP [cm^2]) of an OGIP ARF.

response_name(camera, expid)

<CAMERA>_<EXPID> (the stem of an exposure's RMF and ARF).

response_pair(root, obsid, camera[, expid])

(rmf, arf) of one exposure of obsid in the set.

response_root([root])

The response-set root: root > $TNGXMM_OBS_RESP > the site key TNGXMM_OBS_RESP.

run_entry(entry[, settings, work_dir, run, ...])

Build the responses of one observation into entry['out'] and write its provenance.json; returns it.

run_queue(entries, tmp, *[, cores, workers, ...])

Build the unfinished entries, one worker per core (each pinned with taskset), two attempts per entry; one JSON line per entry in progress (default <tmp>/progress.jsonl).

sas_version(log_text)

The SAS release of a SAS log ([22.1.0-a8f2c2afa-20250304]), or None.

set_digest([root])

manifest_digest() of the set root (default $TNGXMM_OBS_RESP), cached by the manifest's path and modification time: the RESPSET a mock records and a per-observation fit checks.

sky_xy(header, ra, dec)

Sky pixel (X, Y) of (ra, dec) [deg] in an event list's tangent-plane frame (REFXCRVL, REFXCRPX, REFXCDLT and the Y keywords of the EVENTS header).

unplanned_observations(entries[, observations])

The observations of the real-fit list (observations, default the package's obs/catalogue/observations.csv) without an entry in entries: a per-observation fit of each needs its own responses, donor or not.

with_observation_response(resp, rmf_path[, ...])

resp redistributed with the RMF of rmf_path (Monte-Carlo grid, channel energies, row CDFs and the response used to fold the sky background and the AGN) and, with arf_path, its effective area times onaxis_ratio() (the on-axis energy dependence of that ARF, the stage's vignetting kept).

write_manifest(root)

<root>/manifest.csv: one row per exposure of every <root>/<obsid>/provenance.json (obsid, camera, expid, date, filter, submode, status, files and sha256, SAS version, CIF sha256, the REDIST, QUANTUMEF and CONTAMINATION issues of the camera, and the sha256 of the centre table the region was planned from).

tngxmm.instrument.obs_responses.adopt_entries(entries, settings=None)[source]¶

Take over the finished responses that an earlier centre table planned and the current one plans identically.

A centre table that gains rows (observations added to the bank) has another sha256, although the rows of the observations it already held are the same; their responses need no rebuild. For every plan entry whose <out>/provenance.json is finished under another centres_sha256: when the set, the region (ra, dec, radius_arcsec), the exposures (camera, exposure id, event list) and, if given, the settings of the record equal the plan’s, the record is rewritten with the plan’s centres_sha256 and the earlier one appended to centres_sha256_built; an entry that is a link into another set becomes a directory of links to that set’s files with its own record (the other set is not touched; the directory is prepared beside the link and takes its place in one rename, and run_entry() removes such links before it builds). An entry that differs in any of these, or whose record lacks one, is left as it is and listed, for run_queue() to rebuild after its directory is removed. Without settings the settings are not compared.

Returns {"adopted": [...], "current": [...], "differs": [...], "unfinished": [...]} (obsids).

tngxmm.instrument.obs_responses.check_set(root=None, centres=None)[source]¶

The digest of the set (set_digest(); FileNotFoundError without a manifest) after checking that every entry was planned from the current fit-centre table (centres, default the data package’s obs_centres_guarded_v4.csv): the manifest’s centres_sha256 must be that table’s sha256, else ValueError. The rule: a set whose cluster regions came from another centre table is rebuilt before it is used, since a mock and a real fit would otherwise take responses of regions the fit does not use. Called where the set is used: the planners (donor_tables.missing_donors, the closure grid’s coverage) and the per-observation fit (fit_hydrostatic.response_record).
