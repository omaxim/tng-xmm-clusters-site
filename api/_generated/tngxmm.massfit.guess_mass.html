tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass¶

First-guess M500 and c200 of a hydrostatic fit (optimiser start and the fit region; never the fitted quantity).

The first-guess R500 sets the fit’s crop (massfit.crop_r500 R500), its pixel size, the model’s outer radius and the centre search of a mock, so it is taken from a published OBSERVED relation and the image itself, the same way for mocks and real observations:

  • relation: Bulbul et al. (2019), SPT-selected clusters observed with XMM-Newton, soft-band luminosity in the rest frame 0.5-2 keV inside R500, core included, against the SZ-based M500 (their eq. 26 and the values on p. 22):

    L_X = 4.12e44 erg/s (M500 / 6.35e14 Msun)^1.89 (E(z) / E(0.45))^2 ((1 + z) / 1.45)^-0.20,  sigma_lnL = 0.27

    in the cosmology of that paper (flat LCDM, H0 = 67.74, Omega_M = 0.304), the pipeline’s to within 0.005 in Omega_M, so no conversion is applied. The band and aperture are those of the photometry here (measure_lx.seed_lx), so no band correction enters;

  • iteration: R500 follows from the mass, the luminosity is measured on the image inside that R500 (with the ECF at the virial temperature of the trial mass) and inverted through the relation, until the mass changes by less than ITER_RTOL in ln. The procedure is the one of the MCXC (Piffaretti et al. 2011, Sect. 3.4), which iterates L500 and R500 from an L-M relation; it starts at the relation’s pivot mass. The fixed point is the root of g(ln M) = ln M_rel(L(R500(M))) - ln M, which falls with M wherever the measured luminosity grows more slowly than M^1.89 (always, for a cluster profile); the steps are those of the plain iteration while they stay inside the bracket of the root the signs of g have established so far, and bisect the bracket otherwise (an image whose noise or substructure makes the plain iteration oscillate);

  • aperture beyond the field: the seed photometry fills the uncovered part of the aperture with the azimuthal mean of its covered pixels at the same radius, the ring fill that already replaces masked pixels (tngxmm.photometry.estimate_lx_background.ring_filled_sum()), up to the same limit of half the aperture;

  • fallback: a trial mass whose luminosity is not measurable (flags nondetection, masked, uncovered) bounds the search: before any trial was measurable the mass steps down a decade (its R500 shrinks into the field), after one it bisects back towards the last measurable trial; a root beyond what the image can measure keeps the largest measurable mass (field-edge); an image with no measurable trial keeps the pivot mass (pivot);

  • c200 start: the median of Dutton & Maccio (2014) at the first-guess mass (c200_dutton14()); the fit’s prior on c200 is flat (Sanders et al. 2018), so the start only places the optimiser.

References

bulbul2019 (relation), piffaretti2011 (iteration), dutton2014 (c200), nfw (M500 / M200), cosmology_planck2015 (E(z)).

Functions

Ez(z)

E(z) of the pipeline's flat LCDM (Om = 0.3089).

c200_dutton14(m500_1e14, z[, h])

Median NFW c200 of Dutton & Maccio (2014) for a (relaxed) halo of M500c = m500_1e14 [1e14 Msun] at z.

c200_guess(m500_1e14[, z])

Start of c200: the Dutton & Maccio (2014) median at M500 = m500_1e14 [1e14 Msun] and z.

centre_and_seed(merged, image, nominal_xy, ...)

The centre and the first guess of a fit target, mock or real, in one procedure: (1) the seed (seed_lx_iterated()) at the nominal position nominal_xy (a real cluster's catalogue position; a mock's catalogue position, its detection on its Compton-y map filtered as the replicated cluster's catalogue filters (tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre), or an X-ray position for the three entries of neither SZ catalogue, moved by an error drawn from the published positional error of that entry, tngxmm.photometry.find_centre.mock_nominal()); z_obs is the catalogue redshift (a mock's carries the drawn error of a photometric one, tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.mock_redshift()); (2) the guarded X-ray peak of image (the merged 0.5-2 keV counts, its listed point sources masked outside the catalogue's error circle, peak_search_image()) within max(first-guess theta500, r90_arcsec) of the nominal position, the radius within which PSZ2 accepts an X-ray counterpart (tngxmm.photometry.find_centre.find_peak_centre_guarded()); (3) the seed again at that peak, the centre the fit uses.

iterate_m500(measure, z[, rtol, max_iter])

First-guess M500 of one image: measure(m500_msun, r500_kpc) -> L_X [erg/s] (0.5-2 keV, inside R500, NaN when unmeasurable) inverted through m500_of_lx500() until the mass is self-consistent (module docstring: the plain iteration from the pivot mass, safeguarded by bisection).

lx500_of_m500(m500_msun, z)

L_X(0.5-2 keV, < R500, core included) [erg/s] of Bulbul et al. (2019) at M500 [Msun] and z.

m500_of_lx500(lx_erg_s, z)

M500 [Msun] of a luminosity L_X(0.5-2 keV, < R500) [erg/s] through the inverted relation (NaN if L <= 0).

m500_over_m200_nfw(c200)

M500c / M200c of an NFW halo of concentration c200 (solves the 500 / 200 density ratio).

peak_search_image(merged, image, nominal_xy, ...)

image with the listed point sources set to zero over their mask radius (the photometry's rule), for the peak search: an AGN or a star is not taken for the cluster.

r500_kpc(m500_msun, z)

R500c [proper kpc] of M500 [Msun] at z (the pipeline cosmology).

seed_data_files()

The data files the procedure reads besides each target's own images, exposure maps and source lists: the catalogue positions and their errors (tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions), the ECF table (tngxmm.instrument.compute_ecf.resolve_table_path()) and the per-donor source thresholds that mask a mock's simulated sources under massfit.source_list = 'default' (tngxmm.massfit.build_source_thresholds).

seed_lx_iterated(merged, z_obs, exposure_s, ...)

The seed of a fit target, mock or real, from its own image: iterate_m500() with the luminosity inside each trial R500 measured by tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx.seed_lx() (the campaign estimator: masked point sources, sector-clipped local annulus, ECF at the virial temperature of the trial mass).

tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.iterate_m500(measure, z, rtol=0.001, max_iter=50)[source]¶

First-guess M500 of one image: measure(m500_msun, r500_kpc) -> L_X [erg/s] (0.5-2 keV, inside R500, NaN when unmeasurable) inverted through m500_of_lx500() until the mass is self-consistent (module docstring: the plain iteration from the pivot mass, safeguarded by bisection). A trial whose aperture is unmeasurable bounds the search: before any trial was measurable the mass steps down a decade at a time (a smaller R500 that fits the field); after, the search bisects back towards the last measurable trial. Returns m500_seed_msun, r500_seed_kpc, lx500_seed (the luminosity at that R500), seed_n_iter and seed_status: converged; field-edge (the relation asks for a larger R500 than the image can measure: the largest measurable trial is kept); pivot (no trial measurable: the pivot mass) or not-converged (the last measured mass is kept).

tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.seed_lx_iterated(merged, z_obs, exposure_s, nh_1e22, expmap=None, *, spec, **kw)[source]¶

The seed of a fit target, mock or real, from its own image: iterate_m500() with the luminosity inside each trial R500 measured by tngxmm.photometry.measure_lx.seed_lx() (the campaign estimator: masked point sources, sector-clipped local annulus, ECF at the virial temperature of the trial mass). kw goes to seed_lx (sources, centre, kind, obsid, pps_cache). Returns the seed_lx columns at the final R500 (lx_0p35mpc, lx_r500 = the luminosity inside the first-guess R500, lx_1mpc, flags) and the iteration’s columns. No catalogue or true mass enters.

tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.peak_search_image(merged, image, nominal_xy, protect_arcsec, *, spec, pix_arcsec=4.0, sources=None, sources_xy=None, kind='mock', obsid=None, pps_cache=None, **_)[source]¶

image with the listed point sources set to zero over their mask radius (the photometry’s rule), for the peak search: an AGN or a star is not taken for the cluster. The list is the one the seed photometry masks with (the SAS edetect_chain list of the image under massfit.source_list = 'sas'). Sources within protect_arcsec of the nominal position (the catalogue’s 90 per cent error circle, at least the PSF half-energy width) are kept: there the catalogue places the cluster, and its core can be detected as point-like (Pacaud et al. 2016).

tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.centre_and_seed(merged, image, nominal_xy, z_obs, exposure_s, nh_1e22, expmap=None, *, spec, pix_arcsec=4.0, r90_arcsec=0.0, **kw)[source]¶

The centre and the first guess of a fit target, mock or real, in one procedure: (1) the seed (seed_lx_iterated()) at the nominal position nominal_xy (a real cluster’s catalogue position; a mock’s catalogue position, its detection on its Compton-y map filtered as the replicated cluster’s catalogue filters (tngxmm.sim.catalogue_centre), or an X-ray position for the three entries of neither SZ catalogue, moved by an error drawn from the published positional error of that entry, tngxmm.photometry.find_centre.mock_nominal()); z_obs is the catalogue redshift (a mock’s carries the drawn error of a photometric one, tngxmm.photometry.catalogue_positions.mock_redshift()); (2) the guarded X-ray peak of image (the merged 0.5-2 keV counts, its listed point sources masked outside the catalogue’s error circle, peak_search_image()) within max(first-guess theta500, r90_arcsec) of the nominal position, the radius within which PSZ2 accepts an X-ray counterpart (tngxmm.photometry.find_centre.find_peak_centre_guarded()); (3) the seed again at that peak, the centre the fit uses. Every number comes from spec (its photometry settings, estimator options, source-list rule and constants), never from another preset. Returns the seed columns of step 3, m500_seed1_msun (step 1), centre_x, centre_y (0-indexed pixels of image), centre_flag and search_arcsec.

tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.DM14_SCATTER_DEX = 0.11¶

log10 c200 = a + b log10(M200 / 1e12 h^-1 Msun)

Type:

Dutton & Maccio (2014, eqs. 10-11, Planck cosmology, NFW c200)

tngxmm.massfit.guess_mass.c200_dutton14(m500_1e14, z, h=0.6774)[source]¶

Median NFW c200 of Dutton & Maccio (2014) for a (relaxed) halo of M500c = m500_1e14 [1e14 Msun] at z.

log10 c200 = a + b log10(M200 / (1e12 h^-1 Msun)), a = 0.520 + (0.905 - 0.520) exp(-0.617 z^1.21), b = -0.101 + 0.026 z (relaxed haloes, Planck cosmology); the scatter is DM14_SCATTER_DEX = 0.11 dex. M200 follows from M500 through the NFW halo of the relation itself (a short fixed-point iteration).
