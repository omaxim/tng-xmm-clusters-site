The data package, bundles and fixtures¶

The data package ($TNGXMM_DATA)¶

Heavy and frozen inputs are not in git (data/ is ignored; only the placeholder data/manifest.json is tracked; the package is found through $TNGXMM_DATA). $TNGXMM_DATA holds one directory per asset (tngxmm.core.paths.DATA_ASSETS):

asset

content

tables

frozen tables: the donor split, core offsets, APEC tables, results CSVs, and the per-donor tables built by the donor_tables pipeline

legacy_v3, legacy_v2

byte-identical copies of the external input tables the package reads (ECF table, APEC tables, fit target lists, catalogue snapshot) at the same relative path

obs

band 2+3 mosaics and PPS exposure maps of the 638 observations of the bank

mosaics-1, mosaics-2

bank mosaics

embeddings

DINOv3 embeddings

fits

hydrostatic fit trees

regression

stored regression products

fixtures

the test fixtures (below)

Every asset carries a SHA256SUMS; tngxmm data verify checks them all. tngxmm data freeze-legacy copies the external input tables from their source trees into the package; data/manifest.json is a placeholder (its release is null).

Two packages are in the release files of the repository (tngxmm data fetch, Running tngxmm on a laptop). data_v1p1 holds the tables of the bank of 638 observations and goes with the response set responses_obs_v1; pass 1 was generated and fitted with them. data_v1p2 and responses_obs_v2 hold the tables and responses of the 808 observations of src/tngxmm/obs/catalogue/observations.csv: the bank and the 170 observations added on 2026-11-25 (in_bank False; inputs under src/tngxmm/obs/catalogue/inputs_extra/). The rows and files of the 638 are identical in both: tngxmm data compare-tables <data_v1p1>/tables/<name>.csv <data_v1p2>/tables/<name>.csv reads the two builds of a table as text, groups the rows by obsid (--key) and fails when a row of the earlier build is changed or gone; the rows of new keys are counted as added (--shared-columns when the new build has a further column). In data_v1p2 the conditions table tables/donor_conditions.csv has two flags: pool, the pool of the conditions rule nn, which holds observations of the bank only, and lends, set for every observation that can lend its conditions to a view paired with it (700 observations: 548 of the bank and 152 added). Donor folds are assigned on the bank only.

Tables added at the integration of 2026-10-30¶

  • tables/donor_sky_rass.csv: the Local Hot Bubble and Galactic-halo levels of every donor field, fitted to its RASS spectrum in the 1-2 deg annulus about the cluster (python -m tngxmm.donor_tables.rass_sky, login node; read under instrument.sky_model = 'donor-rass').

  • tables/donor_qpb_corners.csv: the corner QPB level of every EPIC camera per PPS band, per MOS CCD and pn quadrant, for the 638 reference observations (job 1890054, tngxmm.donor_tables.qpb_corners), with the MOS columns of donor_mos_background.csv beside them (identical values). The instrument stage takes every camera’s particle level from it (Instrument background of the mocks); provenance in donor_qpb_corners.provenance.json. The copy of 2026-10-30 was measured against the FWC reference of every epoch and CCD state and its sidecar records neither the commit’s state nor the measurement version, so the stage and the real fit refuse it; it is built again, with its sidecar, by kedro run --pipeline donor_tables --nodes donor_tables.build_fwc_reference,donor_tables.build_qpb_corners.

Files a view is made from¶

Every file of the data package a mock view reads is checked before the view and recorded in its obs_meta.json (data_inputs: path and sha256; tngxmm.sim.data_inputs). The files a builder of this package writes carry a provenance sidecar and are read only when it is current (Instrument background of the mocks, “Donor tables measured about the cluster”); the APEC tables, the frozen ECF table and the SFD reddening table come from outside the package and are pinned by their sha256 in src/tngxmm/sim/data_manifest.csv, part of the spec hash, together with the AtomDB files the photon spectra are drawn from (root atomdb: $TNGXMM_APEC_ROOT, else $ATOMDB). Every pinned row is checked. A new copy of a pinned file is a commit of the manifest (and a new spec hash). A closure cell records the same in its truth.json, and a stored view or cell is reused only when its record is the current one.

Column conventions of the results tables¶

  • lx_intr_*: rest-frame 0.5-2 keV luminosity of the gas in the aperture [erg/s].

  • lx_*_tngref: the temet-exact value of the official TNG X-ray products, which is the rest-frame luminosity divided by (1 + z) of the snapshot. Multiply by (1 + z_snap) before comparing with lx_intr_*; otherwise the ratio drifts from +0.003 dex at z = 0 to +0.46 dex at z = 1.5. The columns keep temet’s convention so that they can be compared with the published TNG products directly.

A fit batch writes <work>/fits/results_all.csv, one row per target of the batch with the keys of result.json (the fit_results table, section 4.3 of INTERFACES: the contract between the stages). A view, a detection or a fit stopped by a full disk has the status interrupted: disk in the log and is in no table.

Two keys of result.json belong to the concentration prior massfit.cm_prior = 'dutton14':

  • c200_prior_range: the hard range of c200 the fit had, (0.5, 20). The QC flag prior_edge_c200 reads it.

  • c200_prior_offset_sd: the median over the chain of the offset of ln c200 from the Dutton & Maccio (2014) relation at the sample’s own M200, in standard deviations of the prior. It is reported and cuts nothing.

A fit with the flat prior has neither key.

Local bundles¶

tngxmm data bundle --list / --make <dir> --stage ... copies the subset of the package an analysis stage needs (src/tngxmm/core/bundle.yml, tngxmm.core.bundle), so the stages after the simulation run on a workstation. The items, their sizes and the use of a bundle are in Running tngxmm on a laptop.

Counterpart redshifts of the PSZ2 donors¶

catalogue_positions_v4.csv is built with the published redshifts of the optical and SPT counterparts PSZ2 names (redMaPPer, WHL, MaxBCG, SPT-SZ; --counterparts); its ACT counterparts are typed from the ACT DR6 file. That input, psz2_counterpart_redshifts.csv, and the NED and VizieR replies it was made from (cache/) are kept in the references area, $TNGXMM_SITE_REFS/psz2_counterparts/, not in the data package; tools/psz2_counterpart_redshifts.py rebuilds the CSV from that cache without the services. The builder takes only the pinned PSZ2, ACT DR6 and counterpart files (catalogue_positions.INPUT_PINS): a rebuilt counterpart table changes its pin first.

Light tables in the repository¶

The observation tables (src/tngxmm/obs/catalogue/) and the donor target lists (campaigns/<campaign>/, campaigns/<campaign>_max/) are committed: they are the inputs the campaign reads and the outputs of the obs and donors nodes, at the paths the CLI writes. A Kedro run of those nodes rewrites them in place and git diff shows any change. inputs/literature_values.csv is the public table of every literature value the package uses.

Fixtures¶

The fixtures make the pipeline testable without the cluster: two TNG-Cluster halos cut out of the snapshots, the golden products of the v1 preset made from one of them (golden_v1.1.1), and two public XMM-Newton observations (2.6 GB unpacked, not in git; tngxmm data fetch <root> --item fixtures). Tests find them through TNGXMM_FIXTURES; python tests/fixtures/check_fixtures.py checks the manifest, the sha256 sums and that every file opens. The contents, the producing scripts and the halo table:

The fixtures make the pipeline testable without the cluster: two TNG-Cluster halos cut out of the snapshots, the golden products of the v1 preset made from one of them (golden_v<release>/: this version reads golden_v1.1.3/, not made yet, STATUS G1; the newest made is golden_v1.1.1/), and two public XMM-Newton observations. Tests find them through the environment variable TNGXMM_FIXTURES (on Vera the site file sets it):

export TNGXMM_FIXTURES=<directory of the fixtures>
python tests/fixtures/check_fixtures.py          # manifest, sha256, open every file
python tests/fixtures/check_fixtures.py --quick  # skip sha256
pytest tests/fixtures/check_fixtures.py          # same, skips if the variable is unset

Elsewhere, restore them from the fixtures asset of the data package (the fixtures directory of the shared data package) and point TNGXMM_FIXTURES at the result.

Scripts in this directory¶

  • make_tng_cutout.py: cuts one halo (needs the TNG-Cluster output and illustris_python). About 35 s and 3.6 GB peak memory per halo.

  • collect_fixtures.py: copies the observed products and writes provenance.json per view and manifest.json (--manifest-only rewrites only the manifest).

  • make_temet_reference.py: freezes temet’s own X-ray output on the fixture halos (tests/e2e/golden/temet_reference.npz, the target of tests/e2e/test_temet_exact.py).

  • check_fixtures.py: the loader test (no TNG code needed).

TNG cutout format (format_version = "tngxmm-fixture-1")¶

The layout is that of a TNG public-API cutout (one HDF5 file with a Header group and one group per particle type, fields under their snapshot names in native code units), extended so that the file can stand in for both the snapshot and the group catalogue.

Header¶

Every attribute of the snapshot header (snap_<snap>.0.hdf5), notably BoxSize (680000 ckpc/h), HubbleParam (0.6774), Redshift, Time (scale factor a), MassTable (MassTable[1] is the high-resolution DM particle mass, which has no Masses field), Omega0, OmegaBaryon, OmegaLambda, UnitLength_in_cm, UnitMass_in_g, UnitVelocity_in_cm_per_s, Git_commit. As in API cutouts, NumPart_ThisFile and NumPart_Total count the particles in this file and NumFilesPerSnapshot is 1; the snapshot totals are kept in NumPart_Total_Snapshot and NumPart_Total_HighWord_Snapshot. The groups Config and Parameters copy the snapshot’s run-time configuration attributes.

Group and Subhalo¶

Group holds one dataset per field of the halo’s group-catalogue row, exactly as illustris_python.groupcat.loadSingle(base, snap, haloID=halo_id) returns it (scalars as 0-d datasets, vectors such as GroupPos, GroupMassType, GroupLenType with their natural shape): all 32 fields, including GroupOrigHaloID, GroupPrimaryZoomTarget, Group_M_Crit500/200, Group_R_Crit500/200, Group_M_Mean200, Group_M_TopHat200, GroupFirstSub, GroupNsubs, GroupContaminationFracByMass. Read it back with {k: f['Group'][k][()] for k in f['Group']} to get the same dict. Subhalo holds the row of subhalo GroupFirstSub (the central) in the same way (53 fields); its attribute subhalo_id is that index.

PartType0 … PartType5¶

Particles of the halo’s original zoom simulation (TNG-Cluster stores 352 zoom runs side by side; illustris_python.snapshot.loadOriginalZoom returns the FoF particles of all groups of the zoom followed by its outer fuzz) whose periodic distance to GroupPos is at most cutout_radius_ckpc_h. Particles of other zoom runs that overlap the volume are excluded, which is what makes the particle M(<R500c) equal the catalogue M500c.

PartType3 (tracers) is not included. Datasets are gzip level 4 with shuffle, readable by any h5py.

Three properties a reader can rely on:

  1. Raw coordinates. Coordinates are the snapshot values: not recentred, not wrapped. A consumer computes d = x - GroupPos; d -= BoxSize * round(d / BoxSize) exactly as on the snapshot (neither fixture halo is near the box edge).

  2. Snapshot order. Particles keep their order within the original zoom.

  3. InFoF is 1 for the members of this halo’s FoF group. Selecting InFoF == 1 reproduces illustris_python.snapshot.loadHalo element for element. Every FoF member of both halos lies inside the cut (the farthest at 3.5 and 2.9 R500c), so the InFoF subset is the complete FoF group.

Velocities are in the snapshot convention (multiply by sqrt(a) for peculiar km/s). Units: lengths ckpc/h, masses 1e10 Msun/h, Density (1e10 Msun/h)/(ckpc/h)^3, InternalEnergy (km/s)^2, StarFormationRate Msun/yr, GFM_CoolingRate erg cm^3 s^-1.

The interface (schemas/signatures.py has the Protocol the code uses):

class HaloSource(Protocol):
    snap: int
    halo_id: int
    def header(self) -> dict: ...            # Header attrs as a dict
    def group(self) -> dict: ...             # loadSingle(haloID) dict
    def subhalo(self) -> dict: ...           # loadSingle(subhaloID=GroupFirstSub) dict
    def particles(self, ptype: int, fields: list[str], selection: str = 'fof',
                  radius_ckpc_h: float | None = None) -> dict: ...
        # dict of arrays plus 'count', like illustris_python; ptype 1 has no
        # 'Masses' field: callers use header()['MassTable'][1]
def open_halo_source(path_or_base: str, snap: int | None = None,
                     halo_id: int | None = None) -> HaloSource: ...
        # a file ending in .hdf5 -> CutoutHaloSource, a directory -> SnapshotHaloSource

Because selection and order are preserved, load_gas on top of either backend gives bit-identical arrays, and so do the per-view photon lists under the same seeds.

Golden products (golden_v1.1.3/, not made yet; golden_v1.1.1/)¶

Made by bash tools/make_v1_golden.sh <out_dir> on a compute node (the quick fits need the massfit stack, XSPEC and a private rate cache), then copied to $TNGXMM_FIXTURES/golden_v1.1.3 (STATUS G1: the products of 1.1.3 are not made yet). The directory names are versioned with the package and named in ONE place, tests/fixtures/golden_layout.py (the tests, check_fixtures.py, collect_fixtures.py and the script read it): this version uses golden_v1.1.3/, golden_v1.1.3_fits/ and golden_v1.1.3.SHA256SUMS; release 1.1.1 reads golden_v1.1.1/; release 1.1.0 reads golden_v1.1/ (its products, under the physics donor’s own conditions) and release 1.0.1 (main) golden_v1/; each set stays unchanged beside the others in the same root and manifest.json lists every file of all of them (a checkout scans the whole root and fails on an unlisted file). The script runs campaign run --preset v1 --snap 67, campaign truth-sphere and campaign check on the cutout tng/067_19542651_r100.hdf5 (after campaign mock-cutout, which cuts the cube of the mock gas; up to 1.1.1 the 7.2 R500c cutout), then ESA’s source detection on the three views (instrument sas-detect, from 1.1.3), with the three replica rows of conf/fixtures/targets/targets_fixture.csv (the view ids keep the stream key of the rows), and a quick fit (seed 20261007) of the views. Since release 1.1.1 the preset observes under the conditions donor (donors.observing_conditions {rule: nn, n: 5, ...}): each view directory also holds conditions.json and stage_events.h5 (the photon list retargeted to the conditions donor), obs_meta.json has the keys observing_conditions and conditions and the donor package the key replica_k, and the cluster sits at the conditions donor’s core offset plus the pointing jitter. The photon list is sized for the longest jittered camera time of the conditions donor (texp_gen_s, the exp_time of events_v<k>_abs.h5). In obs_meta.json and the results row exposure_*_s, filter, pn_mode, nh_1e22 and posangle_deg are those of the products (the conditions donor’s; obs_meta.exposure_s the per-camera times), and physics_* (physics_nh_1e22: the absorption of events_v<k>_abs.h5, while stage_events.h5 is absorbed with the conditions donor’s N_H) and conditions_obsid name the two observations. Regenerated after the review fixes of 1.1.1 (the products of the first 1.1.1 run, commit 17a175b, were sized for the physics donor’s exposure and described the physics donor; they are not kept in the root). Regenerated again after the second review round (the conditions rule is fold_restricted, so the conditions donors differ; donor_package has cameras_real_s, physics_exposure_*_s are the real times; conditions.json has fold_restricted and fold); the previous set (889671a) is kept outside the root. Observations (obs/)¶

Both obsids carry the per-camera (PN, MOS1, MOS2) count images and exposure maps for PPS bands 1–5 (15 + 15 files, so the 12 + 12 of bands 2–5 used by the fits are present), the combined EPIC band-8 image, and two EPIC mosaics *_built.FITS that this project built from the per-camera images (not XSA products). Abell 1835 also has the PPS source list P0551830101_OBSMLI.FTZ.
