P2b

2026-11-03

No tuned factors: every number that enters a mock or a fit comes from first principles, calibration data or a published value with its source; the last fitted or hand-set numbers (TNG-fitted first-guess relations, the count-threshold priors, the background seed priors, the xmm_simulator sky norms, the AGN N_H cap, the FWC registration) are replaced

a result that rests on a number chosen on the mocks cannot be defended; each replacement is a published relation, a calibration file or the data of the donor field

No tuned factors

2026-11-03

Mocks get no information a real observation lacks: the truth leaks found in an independent read of the code were removed (the QPB template, the true centre and its search radius, the true source lists, the catalogue position as an SZ detection with its published error) or bound them by a closure arm

the mock-real comparison measures the method only if both sides go through the same procedure with the same knowledge

The fit, the pilot and the production run¶

date

decision

reason

record

2026-11-10

The fit model is averaged over each pixel within 24 fit pixels of the centre (massfit.model_pixel_average = 8), for mocks and real observations alike

a pixel holds the integral of the surface brightness over its area; read at the pixel centre, a cool core is too peaked on coarse pixels

The model averaged over the pixel

2026-11-10

A real fit is compared with the published mass of the same cluster by the tension between the two 68 per cent intervals, not by a percentage band about 1. Since 2026-11-11 the tension is read together with the common offset and the scatter, and every cluster beyond 3 sigma is traced

a band says nothing about the errors of either side; independent analyses of the same data differ by more than their statistical errors, so overlap of every pair is not asked for

The pilot run before the freeze, “How the result is read”

2026-11-11

The closure grid is reported as the measured error of the method, with its verdict as it came out (FAIL), and no longer gates the freeze; the freeze is judged on the pilot. An amendment made knowing the result

the grid cannot measure the agreement of real fits with published masses or the ratio of the fit to the hydrostatic and true mass on simulated gas

Pre-registered rules of 2026-11-04 (DESIGN v4), section 1; The pilot run before the freeze, amendment of 2026-11-11

2026-11-11

The hydrostatic reference masses are read from Table A1 of Lovisari et al. (2020), not from the copy in Wicker et al. (2023)

the copy differs in 20 of 120 rows (13 truncated, 7 with a digit of the lower error lost)

The pilot run before the freeze, “Conventions of the published masses”; CHANGELOG

18 of 67 in-domain pilot fits ended at a bound of the flat prior; the relation and its scatter are the published ones, and the ledger says how they differ from the concentration of the reference analysis

The concentration prior; ledger fit.cm_prior

2026-11-12
