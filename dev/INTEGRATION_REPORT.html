Can TNG-Cluster be brought onto the observed L_X trend?¶

No, not by a defensible means.

  • Through our own pipeline FLAMINGO L1_m8 is 0.33 dex fainter than TNG-Cluster in L_X(1 Mpc) at matched true mass (193 pairs; 0.35 dex intrinsic) and sits on the real pool (-0.04 dex on true mass, +0.03 on 3-D hydrostatic mass), while TNG-Cluster sits +0.31 / +0.41 dex above it. An independent recomputation gives -0.36 dex, temet against SOAP at catalogue level -0.42 dex. About 60 per cent is the gas fraction, the rest the emission measure of the 0.15-1 R500c shell.

  • The audit (LXGAP_INVESTIGATION/REPORT.md) found no step that raises TNG-Cluster luminosities by more than 0.022 dex (the legacy abundance table, now replaced by full).

  • The gas exploration found no physically defensible change of the emission model that removes more than about 0.06 dex; removing the full excess needs deleting cool-core gas that a telescope records.

Not removed, with the reason:

  • FWC_REGISTRATION (flip, rotation, shift of the FWC event density onto the packaged detector mask, by cross-correlation): it places a calibration map in our detector frame and moves no level or rate. Proposal: derive it from the CCF (LINCOORD CCD positions and orientation, the chain the CCD labels and the telescope axis already use) and check it against the cross-correlation (IoU 0.96-0.99 now). This removes the last fitted number of the background geometry.

  • fwc.band_conv: the ratio of the stage’s FWC spectrum to the CCF FWC event rate, two calibration data sets.

  • Selection thresholds (anomalous MOS CCDs, footprint, matching costs): they classify, they do not scale.

Found on 2026-10-30 and left for the author’s decision (STATUS Q2-Q4, Q6). All four were settled by 2026-11-03: the table of the last section says how, and src/tngxmm/config.py has neither bg_seed = 'typical-epic' nor cm_prior = 'auto'.

  • massfit.bg_seed = 'typical-epic' (production default): a typical background level from 54 closure cells used as the centre of a 0.1 dex Gaussian prior where no background annulus exists (z near 0.06). It is a mock-calibrated value inside a prior. Replacement: the observation’s corner level plus the published sky model.

  • cm_prior = 'auto' uses the Dutton & Maccio (2014) prior centred on a first-guess mass from an L-M relation fitted on TNG mocks (guess_mass.M0, SLOPE), below 20,000 net counts. Replacement: a flat prior, or the same prior centred on a published observed relation.

  • The count matching of the realism evaluation (replica thinned to the donor’s net counts; median factor 0.54).

  • The sky-model norms taken from xmm_simulator: their provenance is not documented.

Proposals (not implemented)¶

  • Tie the sky and soft-proton levels across bands, the X-COP way. Now each fitted image has its own background level and vignetted fraction. X-COP fixes the spectral shape of each component (Ghirardini et al. 2019, Sect. 2.4.2; Eckert et al. 2017): one normalisation per sky component and one soft-proton normalisation per camera, with the band levels following from the spectra and the responses. Effect: from 24 free background parameters to about six; the sky can no longer trade with the cluster band by band, which is the degeneracy behind the background-cell deficit. Needs a hashed fit switch and a closure run.

  • Start the MCMC inside the bounds. The walkers start in a ball about the optimiser’s best fit; walkers that start outside a flat prior begin at zero probability. Draw the start ball inside the bounds (or reflect it in) and record how many walkers were moved. Effect: no change of the posterior, fewer wasted steps, and the burn-in diagnostics no longer see stuck walkers. Needs a test on the closure cells.

Kedro: one graph for every simulation¶

register_pipelines() takes no preset; the preset enters as params:preset. tests/unit/test_single_graph.py builds the pipelines under v1, v1_tng300, v1_flamingo, v1_flamingo_l1m8, v1_simba and v1_eagle and asserts identical nodes (name, inputs, outputs, function, tags), identical catalog types, no simulation name in a dataset name, ingest.build_canonical_cubes as the only node in tngxmm.ingest, and no module outside the ingest stage that branches on the simulation (a pinned allowlist of TNG target-list builders). Fixed on the way: the plan filtered snapshots without targets only for three named TNG boxes (now by descriptor kind), the analysis mock table had the tng-cluster folder hard-coded, and the index-route gas loader bypassed the SIMBA and EAGLE rules. The L1_m8 cubes appear in the graph through the ingest node, which checks them against their manifests (pre-step and sample rule documented in Kedro pipelines: the map and Simulations and the ingest stage).

SIMBA and EAGLE¶

Loaders tngxmm.ingest.simba and tngxmm.ingest.eagle with the interface of the other families (halo table, canonical gas with the simulation’s rules, ingest of a list of halos, python -m entry), on the shared TNG-format reader; registry tngxmm.ingest.loaders; 20 unit tests. Smoke test on the login node: SIMBA snapshot 140, halos 7 and 2 (2.7e6 and 7.0e6 gas cells in their 5.66 R500c cubes; the canonical gas equals an independent read of the FoF gas field by field). EAGLE: the full box pass for one cube takes about 30 minutes and was not run; the loader’s canonical gas equals the descriptor-driven result on all 1.5e7 FoF cells of halo 4 at snapshot 26. Both stay internal: the conversion’s author has not been contacted. Presets v1_simba and v1_eagle existed already.

After the integration: its open points and the reviews (to 2026-11-03)¶

The pn soft protons from the IN/OUT excess and the pn out-of-time energies from the CTI calibration were merged into integration first; then every branch was merged into multisim, main was moved to it (2fec78e) and the last tuned numbers were replaced. Nothing was pushed. The record is in Decision record, “No tuned factors: the last replacements” and “Independent review of the replacements, round 1” to “Independent review, round 4”.

Four rounds of independent review followed, looking for truth leaks and mock/real asymmetries rather than constants:

  • Round 1 (changes in 74fb587). A mock’s QPB template is built as a real one is, from its own simulated corner measurement and the half of the FWC events the draw did not use. Mocks are centred and seeded by the two-pass procedure of the real targets, from a nominal position instead of the true centre. Production absorbs the photons with the conditions donor’s column; the seed photometry masks with the SAS source list on both sides; the core protection is the half-energy width of the on-axis pn PSF, 16.487 arcsec; the RASS fits hold the CXB at the Moretti total (halo norms -0.8 per cent at the median); the closure gate is decided on v1sas; the first-guess iteration keeps its bracket (30 of 638 real targets had not converged, 62 had an R500 aperture off the field); the cluster photons get the King PSF in energy and off-axis angle.

  • Round 2 (changes in 3117a07). The centre search covers max(first-guess theta500, r90), r90 the catalogue’s 90 per cent position radius (catalogue_positions_v4.csv), and a mock starts at its true centre moved by an error drawn from the published one. The first guess steps down from an unmeasurable pivot; every closure cell is detected and seeded; new arm v1_qpbfree. Rebuilt real tables: seeds 616 converged, 21 field-edge, 1 without a source list, none at the pivot (45 before); catalogue-to-centre offsets above 5 arcmin: 2 (13 before).

  • Round 3 (changes in c4cea94). 36 per cent of the ACT DR6 centres lie beyond the noise-only 90 per cent radius, against 10 per cent expected, so a mock’s catalogue position now starts from its own thermal-SZ centre (header SZCENX/SZCENY) plus the drawn error. The seed hash covers the procedure and the catalogue table; the gate arm masks every cell’s SAS list; v1_qpbfree measures the cost of a free QPB level, and the bound on a wrong fixed level is planned from real fits with the level free (fit-fix qpb_scale). The real seed table came out byte-identical to round 2’s.

  • Round 4 (changes in 184be38). The SZ centre is taken within the scale each catalogue reports positions at (2.4 arcmin for ACT DR6, R500 for PSZ2; ledger fit.centre), and the seed hash covers the source of the seed procedure’s modules.
