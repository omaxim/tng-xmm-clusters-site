The PSF on the mask window and the projection in one product: what changes, by how much, how fast¶

Written on 2026-11-24 on the branch fastfit (2ef14e5, merged since). Two fit-fix switches, both off by default:

switch

values

what it does

psf_eval

image (default), window

the off-axis King PSF computed on the bounding box of each image’s mask instead of the whole padded grid (massfit/psf_window.py)

projection

groups (default), matrix

all node groups of an evaluation projected in one triangular matrix product instead of one matrix-vector product each (massfit/band_response.py)

Neither changes the model. projection = matrix returned the same log posterior at every point tested in 85 of 86 fits. psf_eval = window changes the model image by the rounding of a single-precision FFT of another length, and this page measures what that does to a fit. The fits of pass 1 finished by 2026-11-24 were made with both off.

Adopted since. The preset v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass2_extra sets massfit.psf_eval = 'window' and massfit.projection = 'matrix' (2026-11-25), so the pass-2 views of the list with the added observations are fitted with both. The last 978 fits of pass 1 were made with both on 2026-11-26, on a second cluster, after a control sample: 75 finished z = 0.1 mocks refitted with the two switches gave ln M500 (switched minus production) mean +0.001 +- 0.006, median +0.0005, 38 up and 37 down; two of the 75 moved by more than 0.25. The other fits of pass 1 (about 4,000, on the home cluster) and the 623 fits of the real observations used the evaluation on the whole grid.

1. What is computed¶

KingAnglePSF (massfit/fit_psf.py) convolves the cluster image S of every evaluation as

model = sum_j  C_j (x) [w_j S]   +   sum_j  T_j (*) B[w_j S]

with w_j the hat weight of off-axis node j, C_j the node’s core kernel (reach h pixels, cyclic on the padded grid), B the b x b block sums and T_j the tail transfer on the coarse grid. The transforms run on the whole padded grid: 234 x 234 pixels for a z = 0.1 fit at 12 arcsec, of which a quarter is inside the mask.

The likelihood reads the model inside the mask only (mbproj2d.fast.calcPoissonLogLikelihoodMasked). With O the bounding box of the mask, WindowedAnglePSF computes the same sums on O:

  • Core. An output pixel takes source pixels within h of it, so the output on O needs the source on O grown by h. The weight of a node is non-zero on an annulus about the optical axis. Consecutive nodes are grouped, and a group is transformed on a buffer that holds the rows and columns where its weights are non-zero, cut to O grown by h. For input rows a..b and output rows u1..u2 the offsets output minus input span u1 - b .. u2 - a; a cyclic length N >= max(u2 - a, b - u1) + h + 1 keeps every offset larger than h away from the kernel, so the cyclic convolution on N equals the linear one at every output row. The same holds for the columns.

  • Tail. The block sums A..B of a group’s input are convolved with the transfer at the offsets Y1 - B .. Y2 - A for the output blocks Y1..Y2 of O; N >= (B - A + 1) + (Y2 - Y1 + 1) - 1 keeps them apart.

  • Lengths. The smallest even 2^a 3^b 5^c at or above these minima. For the 234 px grid the four groups run on 60, 100, 144 and 180 px (core) and 64, 80, 90 and 128 blocks (tail), two nodes each.

  • Unchanged. The kernel values (KingPSF.resample), the tail transfer (tail_transfer), the weights, the single-precision products w_j S and the order of the block sums. The spectra of a group are summed and inverted once, as _fourier_sum does for all nodes.

Two things follow from the construction. No part of the kernel is dropped, so there is no truncation error: the only difference is rounding. And the model outside O is not computed (it is set to the floor value), so the window may be used only where the mask is the only reader; the fit switches back to the image-grid PSF after the sampler.

An image whose mask box grown by h reaches the edge of the grid keeps KingAnglePSF: there the cyclic wrap of the grid is part of the model. In the 86 fits of section 2 this is every image of the fits at z >= 0.25 and of most fits at z = 0.2; all images are windowed in 34 of 36 fits at z <= 0.15 (25 of 25 mocks, 9 of 11 real).

The grouping is chosen by counted work (n^2 log2 n per transform, n^2 per element-wise pass) and the plans are made with FFTW_ESTIMATE, so nothing depends on timing (section 5).

projection = matrix: Radii.project of mbproj2d multiplies the 1056 x 1056 projection matrix by one emissivity vector at a time, 61 times an evaluation for a 12-image fit. The matrix is upper triangular, so the emissivities are stacked as columns and multiplied once with BLAS dtrmm, in double precision, then cast to single as before.

2. The log posterior at fixed points¶

86 finished fits (57 mocks, 29 real), 100 points of each fit’s stored chain. At each point the log posterior was computed four ways: as fitted, with the window, with the matrix projection, with both. The per-image terms are single precision in mbproj2d; they were summed in double precision here, so that the comparison is not limited by the last addition. A second sum takes the model images and forms the Poisson term in double precision.

Projection. Identical at all 100 points in 85 of 86 fits. One real fit differs by at most 1.9e-5. The product is the same sum in another order, so identity is observed and not guaranteed.

A constant offset of the log posterior changes nothing. What could move a mass is a difference that varies with the parameters, and the reweighting measures exactly that: weights exp(difference) on the chain points move the mean of ln M500 by less than a hundredth of a per cent of M500.

In the fits where no image is windowed (z >= 0.25 and most of z = 0.2) psf_eval = window changes nothing: the difference is zero at every point.

The third run has no meaning other than to show what a difference in the sixth digit of one start value does to the path of a derivative-free optimiser. 5. Reproducibility and the FFTW plans¶

Pass-1 fits are not reproducible to the bit: of 10 mocks rerun with the unchanged code, 4 gave another chain. The cause is in the image-grid PSF. mbproj2d and the tail of KingAnglePSF make their FFTW plans with FFTW_MEASURE, which times candidate algorithms when the fit is set up and keeps the fastest. On a busy node the timing differs from run to run, another plan sums in another order, and the last digit of the model differs. Repeats of one PSF evaluation in new processes on the node matched the first in 1 of 10; with the PSF switched off 10 of 10; with the window 9 of 9 and 7 of 7.

WindowedAnglePSF plans with FFTW_ESTIMATE and chooses its groups by counted work, so the same input gives the same bits. In job 1902121 the six repeated fits with every image windowed reproduced their chains exactly; of the five repeated fits without a windowed image one did.

FFTW_ESTIMATE costs speed: in separate processes a 234 px transform took 0.335 ms against 0.292 ms with a measured plan, a 280 px one 0.433 against 0.262. The window is faster than the image grid in spite of that. A plan measured once and stored as FFTW wisdom, imported by every fit, would give measured speed and fixed bits for both paths; that is not written.

6. Speed¶

One evaluation (login node, one core, cache off): 106.5 ms to 50.4 ms in mocks at z <= 0.15 (median factor 2.09, range 1.70 to 2.20), 1.98 in real fits with every image windowed, 97.7 to 81.0 ms at z = 0.2, and 1.11 to 1.18 where no image is windowed (the projection alone).

Stage 2 of the optimiser with the cache, same start (section 4.1, on node cores next to running fits): 2.29 times less time per evaluation, 2.41 in total.

Two cautions. Between 03:30 and 04:00 of the job other processes of this test held more cores than were free (load 75 to 77 on 72 cores), so fits running then lost a few per cent. And the non-windowed fits gained more on the node (1.38 over the cache) than one evaluation gains on the login node (1.11 to 1.18): the single product moves about 540 MB less memory per evaluation, which matters when 72 fits share the memory bus, but this was not measured separately.

7. Limits¶

  • The window needs the mask box grown by the kernel reach inside the grid. At z = 0.2 the masks reach the edge in most mocks (7 of 9 here), and those fits take 23 per cent of the campaign time. Padding the buffer instead of refusing would extend the window to them; the cyclic wrap of the image grid would then be absent from the model, which is a change of the model at the edge and not of the rounding, and needs its own test.

  • The fit does not record how many images were windowed; it prints the count. It should go into result.json.

  • A windowed PSF fills the mask box only. The fit puts the image-grid PSF back after the sampler; code that reads model images during the fit outside the mask would get the floor value.

  • Mixed fits (some images windowed) and fits without a windowed image keep FFTW_MEASURE plans for the rest and are not reproducible to the bit.

  • About 4,000 fits of pass 1 were made with both switches off and the last 978 with both on (2026-11-26). A sample fitted partly with and partly without them is one method to within the numbers above; the switch is recorded per fit (fitfix in result.json).

  • Not tested: more than 12 images, pixel sizes other than 8 to 24 arcsec, the fast-fit presets.

8. How to reproduce¶

Unit tests: tests/unit/test_massfit_psf_window.py (window against image grid inside the mask for three geometries, bit identity of two windows, edge masks, switch defaults) and tests/unit/test_massfit_band_response.py (matrix against group projection).

A fit with the switches:

python -m tngxmm.massfit.fit_hydrostatic ... --fitfix psf_eval=window,projection=matrix

The measurements of this page were made with scripts kept outside the package, in psfwin/ of the campaign’s development directory: surface.py and surface_summary.py (section 2), refcheck.py and ref_summary.py (section 3), opt_pairs_node.py and opt_summary.py (4.1), pairs.py and pairs_split.py with nwin.py (4.2 and 6), repro.py and fftplan.py (5), campaign.py (6). The fits are those of jobs 1902090 (plain, cache) and 1902121 (new, rep), plan FIT_PLAN_fastfit_test2.txt.
