Validation status¶

What has been tested, against which rule, with which result, and on which configuration. The mock-making and the provenance are checked in detail; the three tests a reader needs before trusting a mock hydrostatic bias are not passed. The mass fit is biased low by about 15 per cent once a realistic background is present, the replica sample does not match the real sample in mass, and a classifier separates mocks from real observations. The pre-registered realism evaluation has not been run. Terms: Glossary; quantities: Science definitions; figures: Figures.

Every number below names the configuration it was measured on. Most come from release 1.1.1 (198b80e9d4563eec) or older, and the last section lists which. None was measured on the configuration of the integration of 2026-10-30 with the pn out-of-time energies from the CTI calibration (spec hash 12641be3938b849d of preset v1: full emission abundances, the corner QPB level of every camera, the pn Cu-line gap, no RGA dipole, the out-of-time energies from EPN_CTI instead of the removed factor; Instrument background of the mocks).

The configuration has changed further since (Changelog): the MOS vignetting carries the RGA obscuration of the calibration since 2026-11-03 (instrument.mos_rga_vignetting = 'ccf'), and the spec hash of v1 is c2e6b282f6aab356 since 2026-11-12. Two measurements exist on the newer configurations: the closure grid of 2026-11-10 and the pilot of 60 real and 60 mock fits. They are in the section “The closure grid and the pilot of November 2026”. No other number on this page has been measured again. Pass 1 of the pairing on the fit mass (5,001 FLAMINGO L1_m8 views) has been generated and fitted since; its fits use short chains and serve the pairing (A production run from the target list to the masses), and no number of this page comes from them.

The closure test¶

Idealised clusters in exact hydrostatic equilibrium (cool-core and non-cool-core profiles; M500 = 2, 4, 8e14 Msun; z = 0.1 and 0.6) are put through the whole chain and fitted with the production fit (the cell’s own SAS source list, arm v4sas). Data modes: clean (cluster only), agnonly, bgonly (sky and particle background) and real (all of them). 120 fits, 2026-10-17, figure “closure”:

  • The rule registered in PIPELINE.md before the grid was run, 1.00 +- 0.03 in every cell, fails in every data mode. After the results were seen the gate was moved to the clean cells and to the catastrophic tail of the real cells (the certification rules R4_clean and R4_real_tail); the change is recorded with its date in the decision log (“Fit crop stays 3 R500; closure background bias carried as a measured calibration”).

  • The deficit of the real cells is not the fit crop (0.845 / 0.854 / 0.845 at crops of 3 / 5 / 8 R500), the spatial shape of the background, the SAS detection, the AGN light or the optimiser; it needs the sky and particle background. The AGN-only cells are lower still, which points at the background model of the fit when the data hold no background (the background-free fit of the clean cells gives 1.032).

  • The deficit is a measured property of the method. It is not subtracted from any mock or real result on this site. Its dependence on redshift, mass, profile and S/N is in the figure; with 24 fits per mode it is not measured well enough to be a calibration. An M_X / M_true of a mock is therefore the product of the hydrostatic bias of the gas and a method bias of about 10-15 per cent that depends on the background.

  • The grid was run before the release 1.1 instrument changes (donor background components, footprint, Galactic column). It has to be rerun on the current configuration (slurm/closure_grid.sh, about 3 node-hours).

  • The rerun (STATUS B1) is judged by DESIGN v4, written on 2026-11-04 before any of its fits (Pre-registered rules of 2026-11-04 (DESIGN v4)): the clean cells gate the fitter (pooled median within 0.95-1.05, every redshift within 0.90-1.10), the real cells gate the catastrophic tail (at most 5 per cent outside 0.67-1.5), and the real-cell median (within 0.92-1.08, every redshift within 0.85-1.15, DESIGN v2’s rule) decides whether a mock M_X / M_true may be quoted as a hydrostatic bias without the method term. Every study arm is judged by one paired rule at 0.03 in ln M. The per-cell rule above is kept as a report.

The closure grid and the pilot of November 2026¶

Two runs on the configurations after the integration. Both used the flat concentration prior; the prior was changed afterwards (below). The fits of pass 1 ran under the new prior.

The closure grid (job 1896730, 2026-11-10; gate arm with the model averaged over the pixel). Under the rule registered on 2026-11-04 the grid fails: the pooled ratio of the usable clean fits is 1.046 (27 usable of 54, interval about 1.010 to 1.084; the 54 ok fits give 1.015), and the real-background cells give 0.89. The rule was not changed. On 2026-11-11 the author decided, knowing this result, that the grid is reported as the measured error of the method on clusters in exact equilibrium and no longer gates the freeze (Pre-registered rules of 2026-11-04 (DESIGN v4), section 1). Nothing is subtracted for it.

The pilot (The pilot run before the freeze): 60 real observations with a hydrostatic M500 of Lovisari et al. (2020) and 60 FLAMINGO L1_m8 views, fitted with the configuration that is to be frozen, quick chains.

  • A real fit is read by the tension between the fit’s 68 per cent interval and the published one, with the published mass and its errors carried to our cosmology, not by a percentage band about 1. Since the amendment of 2026-11-11 three things are read together: the common offset inside the domain, the scatter against the scatter between published analyses of the same clusters, and every cluster beyond 3 sigma traced and listed.

  • About half of each list lies outside the domain of the fit (a flag known before the fit: the centre, a cluster that fills the field, a first guess limited by the field or missing). Those fits are reported apart.

  • Real fits inside the domain and without a flag from the fit’s own result: 22 fits, median M_fit / M_published 1.020 (0.878 to 1.185), scatter 0.32 in ln, 9 beyond 2 sigma and 5 beyond 3 sigma. With every ok fit inside the domain: 39 fits, median 0.978, intervals overlapping for 14, 19 beyond 2 sigma and 12 beyond 3 sigma. The size of the scatter between published analyses has not been entered in the ledger yet, so the pilot’s scatter has no verdict. The fit intervals come from quick chains and may be too narrow; a pull is then too large.

  • One cluster was traced. MACS J0717.5+3745 (pull -10.7) had been fitted without a particle background because its templates were empty. After the fix it gives 11.87 (11.28-12.42) e14 Msun against 12.91 (12.22-13.46), pull -1.2. The counts above still contain the old fit.

  • The mock fits lie above the simulation’s own hydrostatic mass, where the reading asks for a ratio of 1. The cause is not established (Open work (2026-11-04, updated 2026-11-26), B15), so no M_fit / M_HSE or M_fit / M_true of the pilot is quoted here as a bias.

  • 18 of the 67 in-domain fits ended at a bound of the flat concentration prior. On 2026-11-12 the prior became the Dutton & Maccio (2014) relation (ledger fit.cm_prior). The fits that were not at a bound move by a median of 0.3 per cent (real) and 0.0 per cent (mock) when their chains are weighted by the new prior, at most 10 per cent; the 18 fits at a bound are not refitted.

The freeze of the configuration is judged on the pilot. The pilot is not the science result: 60 views of one projection are too few for a bias to two digits, and its views were not paired on the fit mass. Pass 1 of that pairing has been run since; the pass-2 list with the 170 added observations is not built yet (Samples and selection).

Hydrostatic bias of the mocks (preview)¶

Two measurements exist; neither is on the current configuration and neither is a result yet.

  • The intrinsic hydrostatic bias of the whole population (no X-ray fit, figure “hse_bias_truth”): M_HSE(< R500c) / M500c has a median of 0.883 (16-84 per cent 0.781-1.008) over 2,460 TNG-Cluster halo-snapshots and 0.914 over 742 TNG300 halo-snapshots, nearly flat in mass and redshift (v1.0.1 trees; the truth stage has not changed since).

  • X-ray fits of 43 mock views (the development sample “D1”, V4 configuration, figure “hse_bias_fits”): at the true R500c, M_X / M_true = 0.825, M_HSE / M_true = 0.866, M_X / M_HSE = 0.847 (medians of all 43). Without the 12 fits flagged by the QC rules the medians are 0.835, 0.881 and 0.931. The QC flags (posterior at a prior edge, runaway outer pressure, centre, background fallback) select on the outcome, and the method-bias median moves by 8 per cent when they are applied, so a result has to be given with and without them. Three re-seeds of the same views give ln(M_X / M_HSE) medians of -0.072, -0.064 and -0.089 without the flagged fits (a 7-8 per cent method bias).

  • Three numbers on this page measure the bias of the method, and they are not the same quantity: the closure ratio M_fit / M_true of idealised clusters with a realistic background (0.845; exact hydrostatic equilibrium, smooth gas), and M_X / M_HSE of TNG mocks with every fit (0.847) or without the QC-flagged fits (0.931), where M_HSE is itself an estimate from the 3-D profiles. None is adopted as a correction: the closure grid has to be rerun on the current configuration first, and the QC choice has to be fixed before the result is seen. The closure ratio is the method bias without clumping and non-thermal pressure; the mock ratio M_X / M_HSE is the one the paper’s claim C refers to. What the mocks represent of the physics behind the bias (thermal against total pressure, clumping, temperature weighting, the absorbing column, the epoch) is listed in Science definitions (“Scope of the bias measurement”).

The population sample¶

See Samples and selection. The replica list fails gate P in mass (the mocks are lighter) and in coverage. Population statistics therefore use the matched draw from the maximal list (3,111 draws), which passes gate P strictly when recomputed with the current code (matching mass D = 0.026 against 0.067, coverage 0.978). On the intrinsic hydrostatic bias the difference between the two samples is under 1 per cent (median M_HSE / M500c 0.877 against 0.885).

Realism¶

A classifier can tell a mock from the real observation it replicates. The rule AUC <= 0.75 was proposed before any machine-learning result on real clusters (method.realism_domain_gap); it is a requirement for claim D, not for the hydrostatic bias. Claim D is not part of the first paper; there the realism tests are reported as a description of how far the mocks are from the real observations.

  • background features: 0.718 / 0.796; gas features alone (surface-brightness shape and band ratios): 0.664 / 0.677; counts, point sources and footprint: 0.53-0.57;

So the separation comes from both sides. The background and field statistics point at the instrument and sky model (the fixed sky foreground, instrument.sky_background; the pn soft background, which is not measured per donor). The gas statistics point at TNG itself: TNG-Cluster halos are brighter than real clusters of the same mass (next section), and the band ratio of a mock relative to its donor grows as 0.46 ln(T_sl,mock / T_donor) (465 donors, 17 sigma; instrument.galactic_column_total), i.e. the mock clusters are hotter than the real clusters they are matched to. These are properties of the simulation, not of the instrument model.

The frozen evaluation (84 held-out donors never used to tune the instrument stage, every setting registered in realism.frozen, code that refuses a deviating run; The realism evaluation and frozen mode) has not been run. It needs one node (slurm/realism_frozen.sh).

The luminosity of TNG-Cluster¶

At fixed true M500c (5e14 Msun, z = 0.2) the soft-band luminosity of TNG-Cluster is +0.28 dex above observed relations (Bulbul et al. 2019 +0.41, Lovisari et al. 2020 +0.28, Pratt et al. 2009 +0.20; the references differ by +-0.1 dex among themselves; each 0.1 of hydrostatic bias in the observed masses adds about +0.09 dex). About 70 per cent is explained by the gas fraction, clumping, the density-profile shape and the cool-core mix (+0.19 +- 0.06 stat +- 0.07 lensing-mass systematic); the remainder, +0.09 +- 0.09 dex, is consistent with zero. The pipeline is ruled out as the cause (its intrinsic L_X matches temet’s to +0.0095 dex). The excess is reported as a property of the simulation and is not calibrated away. Figure “lx_mass” shows the measured L_X of the mocks and of the real observations (+0.31 dex at 4-7e14 Msun, with the true mass on one side and the catalogue mass on the other). In the median a measured mock L_X is below the intrinsic one: by 0.077 dex inside R500 and 0.071 dex in L_X(1 Mpc) (6,634 and 6,967 TNG-Cluster views of the v1.0.1 trees, an earlier configuration; 0.052 dex in L_X(1 Mpc) on the 193 TNG-Cluster views of the L_X gap test), though not in every aperture and sample (Science definitions, L_X). The next section gives its steps. Whether it cancels between mocks and real data is not measured.

FLAMINGO L1_m8, put through the same mock pipeline in 193 pairs matched in true mass, donor, redshift and line of sight, is 0.33 dex fainter than TNG-Cluster in L_X(1 Mpc) (0.35 dex intrinsic; the estimator reads FLAMINGO 0.041 dex and TNG-Cluster 0.052 dex below their intrinsic L_X(1 Mpc), medians over the 193 pairs) and sits on the real pool (-0.04 dex on true mass, +0.03 on its 3-D hydrostatic mass), while TNG-Cluster sits +0.31 and +0.41 dex above it. An independent recomputation (-0.36 dex) and a catalogue-level comparison of temet and SOAP luminosities (-0.42 dex) confirm the difference; about 60 per cent of it is the gas fraction. No step of the pipeline raises the TNG-Cluster luminosities by more than 0.022 dex. The pipeline therefore does not create the excess (Simulations and the ingest stage).

Consequence for the mass fits: a mock has about twice the net counts of its real counterpart (the count-matching factor has a median of 0.54), and no mock is thinned. The S/N of a mock fit (QC exclusions, prior-edge flags) therefore differs from that of the real fit of the same observation; the priors themselves are the same at every signal level (the concentration-mass relation for c200, flat for the metallicity).

Other simulations (branch multisim)¶

FLAMINGO, SIMBA and EAGLE go through the same graph after the ingest stage. Measured so far:

  • Ingest: every gas particle that SIMBA’s original snapshots and the TNG-format conversion share is bit-identical (two halos); the originals add the decoupled wind particles, which do not emit either way. EAGLE agrees with its conversion to rounding; its electron abundance of full ionisation changes L_X by +0.0027 dex (one halo). Campaign checks C1-C17 passed on the fixture halos of FLAMINGO L1_m10 and L1_m8, and of SIMBA and EAGLE as read from the conversions.

  • Emission: our L_X(0.5-2 keV, R500c) of FLAMINGO L1_m8 (legacy emission) is +0.028 dex (16-84 per cent +0.018, +0.040) above the sum of FLAMINGO’s own per-particle luminosities (20 clusters, z = 0.2).

  • Luminosity through the mock pipeline (199 pairs of TNG-Cluster and FLAMINGO L1_m8 views at matched true M500c, each pair under the same donor, z = 0.1-0.5): the median log10 of L_FLAMINGO / L_TNG-Cluster is -0.325 [95 per cent: -0.375, -0.292] for L_X(1 Mpc) measured on the images, -0.351 for the intrinsic L_X in R500c, and grows in size with mass (-0.24 at 1-2e14 Msun, -0.39 above 4e14 Msun); full emission on both sides. Indicative only: against the median real L_X(1 Mpc) of the observations within 0.1 dex in catalogue M500 and 0.1 in z, with the mocks at their true M500c, TNG-Cluster lies +0.31 dex and FLAMINGO L1_m8 -0.04 dex from the real data (the real masses are mostly SZ-based catalogue masses; no conversion between the definitions is applied).

  • Resolution: FLAMINGO L1_m10 represents the integrated quantities (luminosity, spectral temperature, hydrostatic truth) and image statistics at the scale of R500c above about 2e14 Msun, but not structure below about 100 kpc. L1_m8 (gas particles of 1.3e8 Msun) is the production FLAMINGO source.

Of these simulations only FLAMINGO L1_m8 has hydrostatic fits: the 60 views of the pilot and the 5,001 views of pass 1 (short chains). No fit of SIMBA or EAGLE has been run.
