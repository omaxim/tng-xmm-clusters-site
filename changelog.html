Changelog¶

The history of the pipeline, one entry per version, with the substantive decisions as one-line items. The reasoning and the measurements behind each decision are in docs/dev/DECISIONS.md (the section is named in brackets) and in the commit messages (git log); the planning notes of the port are in docs/dev/archive/. The code itself carries no version history: a docstring says what the code does now.

Script logic moved into the package (script-logic, 2026-11-26, not released)¶

  • tngxmm.obs.candidate_quality and tngxmm obs grade-candidates: the image measurements and the flag rules the grading of the 194 candidate observations started from, with the thresholds as named constants (docs/samples.md). From the stored measurements the rules give the stored flags of all 194 rows; 15 image sets measured again agree with the stored numbers to 7e-13. The grade itself was given by eye on contact sheets (grade, eye_note of inputs_extra/candidates_graded.csv) and has no written criteria.

  • The 170 added observations are in obs/catalogue/pps_products.csv (5,735 files under extra/; 27,062 files of 813 observations in all), so tngxmm obs fetch-pps-products fetches and checks them like the bank’s. tngxmm obs stage-extra-images puts graded image sets into the tree and lists them; obs fetch-sky-columns, obs build-extra-inputs and obs fetch-pps-archive are the commands of functions that had none.

  • slurm/viper_fits.sh runs every stream in pieces with new processes (memory of long-lived fit workers); slurm/viper_views.sh generates the views of a target list on Viper, runs the source detection and builds the fit targets (tested on seven pass-2 views, 2026-11-27).

  • tngxmm massfit fit-plan streams writes the plans of slurm/viper_fits.sh with the dealing of assign_jobs; tools/split_fit_plan.py is removed. fit-plan remaining, refit-sample and rank-sample (massfit/fit_lists.py) make the lists of 2026-11-26: the control sample of 75 fits is drawn again name for name from the rows it was drawn from.

  • tngxmm data compare-tables: the rows of an earlier data-package table in a rebuilt one.

  • citations/sim.yml: AtomDB 3.1.3 for the mock emission (it said 3.0.9).

Pass 2 with six projections (pass2-six-views, 2026-11-25, not released)¶

  • Preset v1_flamingo_l1m8_pass2: the pass-1 preset with the six icosahedral projections (sim.projection = 'icosahedral6', views 0 to 5) and its own random streams (v4_flamingo_l1m8_pass2).

  • tngxmm donors pair-projections (donors/pair_six_views.py): every halo-snapshot with an ok pass-1 fit is paired by pair_projections with up to six real observations on the fit mass of both sides (fixed window, nearest first, repeated in turn when fewer), and the target list has its six views, each under its observation and at that observation’s redshift. A halo-snapshot without an observation in the window is kept under its nearest observations and flagged paired False.

  • An observation with a camera that has an exposure and no corner counts (a pn window mode: 5 of 638) cannot lend a particle background under qpb_units = 'corners': donor_tables.qpb_unit_gaps; the two pairing commands leave it out with that reason, and campaign run names it before a view is made.

  • No two views of a halo-snapshot are observed under the same observation. Where the physics donor repeats, the view takes its conditions from the nearest observation no other view of the halo-snapshot uses, from outside the window (pair_six_views.distinct_conditions; target columns pass2_physics_obsid, pass2_conditions_in_window); its redshift stays the physics donor’s.

  • On the fits of 2026-11-25: 3,993 halo-snapshots, 23,958 views, 3,950 paired, window 0.091 dex; 3,840 have six observations inside the window, and 334 views of the other 110 are observed under an observation outside it. Twelve views of two halos generated as a test, all ok.

The products of the real observations fetched and rebuilt by the package (obs-products, 2026-11-25, not released)¶

  • tngxmm.obs.pps_products with the list obs/catalogue/pps_products.csv (21,327 files of 644 observations with size and sha256; the pixel sha256 of the built mosaics). tngxmm obs fetch-pps-products and the nodes obs.fetch_pps_products and obs.build_pps_mosaics fetch the pipeline products from the public archive, accept a file only with the listed sha256, copy the rest from another copy of the tree, and sum the two project-built EPIC mosaics again. The nodes read the tree as it is unless obs.pps_products.download is set.

  • The mosaic sum is the one of the script that first built them (the exposures of the archive’s band-8 mosaic): all 1,286 stored mosaics are reproduced pixel for pixel. Three observations fetched from the archive into an empty directory on 2026-11-25 gave 93 of 93 files with the listed sha256 and six mosaics with the listed pixels.

  • Not ported: the choice of the observations (archive queries by cluster position, one observation per cluster); its result stays the committed obs/catalogue/inputs.

The reference masses rebuilt inside the package (obs-references, 2026-11-25, not released)¶

  • The paper parsers of reference_masses.csv are in the package again (tngxmm.obs.references.parsers, moved back unchanged from the private references area) with the fetcher of their sources (tngxmm.obs.references.sources): the arXiv e-prints are downloaded, the catalogue files copied from another copy of the area, every file accepted only with the sha256 of manifest.tsv. No paper is in the repository.

  • Commands tngxmm obs fetch-references and tngxmm obs build-reference-masses --verify | --out; nodes obs.fetch_reference_sources and obs.rebuild_reference_masses, which read the area as it is unless obs.references.download is set and keep the committed table where the area is absent or incomplete.

  • The rebuilt table is byte-identical to the committed one (3,598 rows), from the area on disk and from an area restored from arXiv into an empty directory.

  • The rule that the package reads no paper source now holds for everything outside tngxmm.obs.references (tests/unit/test_no_paper_sources.py); a second test checks that the package holds no paper file.

Running without the cluster (local-setup, 2026-11-25, not released)¶

  • tngxmm data fetch <root> and the pipeline setup (node setup.fetch_release_data) download what a machine away from the cluster needs from the release files of the repository, by item or by stage (fit, mock, analysis; catalogue src/tngxmm/core/fetch.yml): they check the sha256 lists, unpack, re-point the cluster paths of the stored tables and fit-image links, and write a site file, an activate.sh and a sasrun wrapper that mounts SAS at the path it was installed under. --list gives the download and disk size of every item.

  • The xspec wrapper takes the directories the container may read from TNGXMM_CONTAINER_BINDS; the default is the cluster’s.

  • The RMFs of the instrument stage come from the installed xmm_simulator where no legacy tree is configured (the same files).

  • Run from empty directories on 2026-11-25 (docs/LOCAL.md, “Without the cluster”): a fit of a stored mock, and for one halo the stream of its gas, the view, the SAS source list, the fit target and the fit, each identical to the production product.

FLAMINGO L1_m8 cubes fetched by the ingest node on request (stream-on-demand, 2026-11-25, not released)¶

  • ingest.stream_missing (default false). With it the node ingest.build_canonical_cubes streams, for the descriptor flamingo_l1m8, the planned halos that the cube file does not hold from the public FLAMINGO data service before it checks them, with the SOAP table fetched when absent (ingest.soap_dir, ingest.soap_m500_min). The cubes are written by the function of the separate streaming step, so a halo fetched this way is the halo of the production cubes: snapshot 48, halo 24039133 has the same sha256 in all 20 datasets.

  • For runs away from the cluster, where the 576 GB of production cubes are not at hand: the cube root starts empty and holds the targets of the runs made. Production on the cluster is unchanged (compute nodes have no network).

The PSF on the mask window, the projection in one product (fastfit, 2026-11-24, not released)¶

  • Fit-fix entry psf_eval (image, the default | window). The likelihood reads the model inside the mask only, so massfit/psf_window.py computes the off-axis King PSF on the bounding box of the mask, on buffers long enough that the cyclic convolution equals the linear one there. No part of the kernel is dropped; the model differs by the rounding of a shorter single-precision FFT. An image whose mask reaches the edge of the grid keeps the image-grid PSF. The plans are made with FFTW_ESTIMATE, so a fully windowed fit gives the same bits every time.

  • Fit-fix entry projection (groups, the default | matrix). All node groups of an evaluation are projected with one triangular matrix product (BLAS dtrmm) in place of one product per group.

  • Measured on 86 finished fits and in 51 paired reruns (docs/dev/PSF_WINDOW_VALIDATION.md): the reweighted mean of ln M500 moves by at most 6e-5; fits end 1 to 3 per cent away in M500, as reruns of the unchanged code do, with no direction; a fit with every image windowed takes a third of the time of the unchanged code.

  • Both are off by default and recorded per fit. The fits of pass 1 were made without them.

The optimiser keeps the work of unchanged images (fastfit, 2026-11-23, not released)¶

  • The optimiser takes about 70 per cent of a fit’s time, and most of its steps move one to three parameters; close to half move only the sky or soft-proton level of one image. massfit/like_cache.py keeps the cluster images (after PSF and exposure map) and the Poisson term of every image between evaluations, and recomputes only what the changed parameters enter. What is recomputed runs through the same code on the same numbers in the same order as mbproj2d’s Likelihood, so every evaluation returns the same value bit for bit and the optimiser walks the same path. The sampler is unchanged.

  • Fit-fix entry like_cache (image, the default | off, mbproj2d’s evaluation at every point). A fit records it only when it is off, so the fits made before keep their option record.

  • A model whose background levels are shared between images (the soft protons tied per camera, the mixture background) is not split and runs on mbproj2d’s evaluation.

  • Checked on rebuilt production fits: 600 evaluations of a z = 1.0 mock and 300 of Abell 1835 (single levels, cluster parameters, values on the bounds, the PSF switched on and off), all equal to mbproj2d’s value.

Real templates placed with the events of the image (multisim, 2026-11-23, not released)¶

The detector-to-sky map of a real QPB and soft-proton template is now fitted to the events the image is made of: in the field, with a sky position, and inside the good time intervals the PPS image carries for their CCD (qpb_template.image_good_times, in_good_times). Until now it used every placed in-field event of the exposure’s list. A list also holds the events of the bad time, and when an exposure starts before the telescope has settled on the target those lie at another attitude. In the fits of the real observations (job 1901872, 634 targets) seven stopped at the one-pixel check of the map for this reason, always on a MOS image; the pn exposure starts later.

Over the 1916 exposures of the bank that have an event list and a 0.5-1 keV image, 21 fail the check with the old selection and 9 with the new one; none fails only with the new one, and every image has a good-time table with at least three events inside. Twelve MOS exposures of six observations pass now (0300210201, 0605000901, 0650381001, 0674490501, 0677182201, 0827011301): the rms residual of their maps falls from 94-1131 sky units to 5-21, one image pixel being 80, and the map moves by 17-176 units. The nine that still fail are the three cameras of 0404520201, whose attitude wanders by up to about 100 arcsec for some 2500 s inside the good time (rms 522-543), and of 0161560101 and 0747420101, which were not among the fit targets. No single template describes those images and they stay refused. For the 1895 exposures that pass either way the map moves by 0.4 units in the median, 17 at the 99th percentile and 36 at most, under half an image pixel; 342 have the same events in both selections.

The CCD label of the image pixels still uses all placed in-field events: a heavily screened image has too few events of its own to reach every exposed pixel, and the label is the majority CCD of a pixel. A cached real template now carries the event selection in its file name, so one built from the whole exposure is not reused; the templates of a real observation are built again at its next fit. The 621 finished fits of job 1901872 used the old maps and were not run again. Mocks take their templates from the stage and are not affected; no preset and no hash changes. The source for the per-CCD time selection of a product is the evselect manual (ledger fit.particle_background_esas).

Pairing command for a pass-2 trial (multisim, 2026-11-19, not released)¶

tngxmm donors pair-fit-mass (donors.pair_fit_mass) reads the fit table of real observations and the pass-1 fit tables and gives every observation one mock halo: at the snapshot nearest to the observation’s redshift, the halo-snapshot whose pass-1 M500 is nearest in log to the observation’s fitted M500. Only fits with status ok enter, pairs are taken in order of increasing distance, and no halo (halo group, zoom_target_id) is given to two observations. It writes the pairing table, the observations left out with the reason, and the target list of the paired views under their observations’ own conditions at the observations’ redshifts (build_pass1_targets with the observation in place of the reference), which the pass-1 preset generates as it is. Fit batches prepare their targets on all workers (multisim, 2026-11-16, not released)¶

massfit fit-hydrostatic built the job of every target (source list, fit centre) on one core before the first fit started: about 4 s a target, so close to 3 h for a lane of 2,500 with the other 71 workers waiting, and again at every resubmission. On the nodes that was not enough: two lanes of 2,500 targets still took 100 minutes to prepare on 72 workers each. find_images globbed <image root>/*/*/P<obsid>* for each of the four bands at every call, a listing of all 5,001 view directories each time, and the file system could not serve 144 processes doing that at once.
